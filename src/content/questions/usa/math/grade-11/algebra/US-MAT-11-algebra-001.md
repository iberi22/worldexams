# Question

Sarah has $200. If she buys 5 movie tickets at $15 each, how much money will she have left?

# Options

- [ ] A) $100
- [ ] B) $115
- [x] C) $125
- [ ] D) $150

# Explanation

To solve this problem:

1. **Calculate total expense**: 5 tickets × $15 = $75
2. **Subtract from total**: $200 - $75 = **$125**

The correct answer is **C) $125**.

**Why are the other options incorrect?**
- A) $100: Error calculating 200 - 100
- B) $115: Error calculating 5 × 17 = 85, then 200 - 85
- D) $150: Error calculating 200 - 50 (thinking each ticket costs $10)

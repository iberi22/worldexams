# Question

If the roots of the quadratic equation x² - 5x + k = 0 are in the ratio 2:3, find the value of k.

# Options

- [ ] A) 4
- [ ] B) 5
- [x] C) 6
- [ ] D) 8

# Explanation

Let the roots be 2α and 3α.

By Vieta's formulas:
- Sum of roots: 2α + 3α = 5 → 5α = 5 → α = 1
- Product of roots: (2α)(3α) = k → 6α² = k

Substituting α = 1:
k = 6(1)² = 6

Therefore, k = 6.

This is a common JEE Main level problem testing the relationship between roots and coefficients of quadratic equations.

# Question

Rahul has ₹500. If he buys 4 samosas at ₹25 each and 2 chai at ₹15 each, how much money will he have left?

# Options

- [ ] A) ₹330
- [ ] B) ₹350
- [x] C) ₹370
- [ ] D) ₹400

# Explanation

To solve this problem:

1. **Calculate samosa cost**: 4 samosas × ₹25 = ₹100
2. **Calculate chai cost**: 2 chai × ₹15 = ₹30
3. **Total expense**: ₹100 + ₹30 = ₹130
4. **Remaining amount**: ₹500 - ₹130 = **₹370**

The correct answer is **C) ₹370**.

**Why are the other options incorrect?**
- A) ₹330: Error calculating 500 - 170
- B) ₹350: Error calculating 500 - 150
- D) ₹400: Error calculating 500 - 100 (forgot chai)

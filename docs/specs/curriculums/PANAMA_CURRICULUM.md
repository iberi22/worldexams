# Research: Panama Education System & Standardized Tests

## 1. Educational Structure (MEDUCA)

The Panamanian education system is managed by the **Ministerio de Educación (MEDUCA)**. It is divided into three levels:

- **Educación Básica General (General Basic Education):**
  - **Pre-escolar:** 2 years (ages 4-5).
  - **Primaria:** 6 years (Grades 1-6).
  - **Pre-Media:** 3 years (Grades 7-9).
- **Educación Media (High School):**
  - 3 years (Grades 10-12). Leads to a "Bachillerato".
- **Educación Superior:** Universities.

## 2. Standardized Tests

### Pruebas CRECER
Applied to students in **3rd and 6th grade** of Primary.
- **Subjects:** Español (Reading) and Matemáticas.

### Pruebas de Graduandos
Applied to students in their final year of High School (**12th grade**).
- **Subjects:** Español, Matemáticas, Ciencias Naturales, Ciencias Sociales.

## 3. Subjects & Curriculum

Standard subjects across Basic and Media levels:
- **Español:** Grammar, literature, and reading comprehension.
- **Matemáticas:** Arithmetic, Algebra, Geometry, and Trigonometry.
- **Ciencias Naturales:** Biology, Physics, and Chemistry (integrated in lower grades, separate in Media).
- **Ciencias Sociales:** Geography, History, and Civics.
- **Inglés:** English as a second language.

## 4. Cultural Context for Content

- **Currency:** Balboa (PAB) and US Dollar (USD). Balboa is at par with USD.
- **Cities:** Ciudad de Panamá, Colón, David, La Chorrera, Santiago de Veraguas, Penonomé.
- **Names:**
  - Male: Ricardo, Jorge, Alberto, Carlos, Luis.
  - Female: María, Ana, Yariela, Zulay, Itzel.
- **References:** Canal de Panamá, puente de las Américas, polleras, tamborito, sancocho, biodiversidad, Biomuseo.

# Research: Mexico Education System & Standardized Tests

## Phase 1: SEP Curricular Mapping (Nueva Escuela Mexicana - NEM)

The Mexican education system is regulated by the **Secretaría de Educación Pública (SEP)**. The current framework is the **Nueva Escuela Mexicana (NEM)**, which focuses on a humanistic, intercultural, and community-based approach.

### K-12 Structure
- **Preescolar (Preschool):** 3 years (ages 3-5).
- **Primaria (Elementary):** 6 years (grades 1-6, ages 6-12).
- **Secundaria (Middle School):** 3 years (grades 7-9, ages 12-15).
- **Media Superior (High School / Bachillerato / Preparatoria):** 3 years (grades 10-12, ages 15-18).

### Formative Fields (Campos Formativos - NEM)
Instead of traditional isolated subjects, the NEM organizes learning into four fields:
1. **Lenguajes (Languages):** Spanish, English, Indigenous languages, and Arts.
2. **Saberes y Pensamiento Científico (Scientific Knowledge and Thinking):** Mathematics, Biology, Physics, and Chemistry.
3. **Ética, Naturaleza y Sociedades (Ethics, Nature, and Societies):** Geography, History, and Civic Education.
4. **De lo Humano y lo Comunitario (The Human and the Community):** Physical Education, Socio-emotional learning, and Technology.

### Subjects by Level
- **Elementary/Middle School:** Mathematics, Spanish, Natural Sciences (Biology/Physics/Chemistry), History, Geography, English, Civic and Ethical Training (Formación Cívica y Ética).

## Phase 2: CENEVAL Standard (EXANI-II)

The **Centro Nacional de Evaluación para la Educación Superior (CENEVAL)** is the main testing body for higher education. The **EXANI-II** is the national admission exam for undergraduate programs.

### Exam Structure (EXANI-II)
The exam is divided into two main areas: **Habilidades y Conocimientos** (General) and **Módulos Específicos** (Subject-specific based on the intended major).

#### General Areas:
1. **Pensamiento Matemático:** Arithmetic, Algebra, Geometry, Statistics, and Trigonometry.
2. **Comprensión Lectora:** Literal, inferential, and evaluative comprehension of various texts.
3. **Redacción Indirecta:** Grammar, spelling, and cohesion in writing.

#### Specific Modules (Examples):
- Pre-medicine, Engineering, Administration, Law, etc.

### Scoring and Percentiles
- **Scale:** Results are usually reported on a scale (e.g., CENEVAL scale often ranges from 700 to 1300 points).
- **Global Index:** Provides a general score across all areas.

## Phase 3: Top 10 Universities and Admission Tests

1. **UNAM (Universidad Nacional Autónoma de México):**
   - Uses its own exam for both COMIPEMS (High School) and Licenciatura (Undergraduate).
2. **IPN (Instituto Politécnico Nacional):**
   - Uses its own admission exam, focused heavily on mathematics and natural sciences.
3. **UAM (Universidad Autónoma Metropolitana):**
   - Own admission exam (Examen de Selección).
4. **Universidad de Guadalajara (UdG):**
   - Uses the **PAA (Prueba de Aptitud Académica)** from College Board.
5. **Tec de Monterrey (ITESM):**
   - Uses the **PAA (Prueba de Aptitud Académica)**.
6. **Universidad Iberoamericana (Ibero):**
   - Uses its own exam or EXANI-II depending on the program.
7. **UANL (Universidad Autónoma de Nuevo León):**
   - Uses **EXANI-II**.
8. **BUAP (Benemérita Universidad Autónoma de Puebla):**
   - Uses its own admission test (EGA-I / EGA-II).
9. **Universidad Veracruzana (UV):**
   - Uses **EXANI-II**.
10. **UABC (Universidad Autónoma de Baja California):**
    - Uses **EXANI-II**.

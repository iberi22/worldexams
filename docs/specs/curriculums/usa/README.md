## 📚 K-12 Education Structure

The US education system is decentralized, governed primarily at the state and local levels.

| Level | Grades | Ages |
|-------|--------|------|
| **Elementary School** | Kindergarten - 5th Grade | 5 - 11 |
| **Middle School** | 6th - 8th Grade | 11 - 14 |
| **High School** | 9th - 12th Grade | 14 - 18 |

---

## 📐 Digital SAT Structure (Starting 2024)

The SAT is a standardized test widely used for college admissions in the United States.

| Section | Questions | Time | Content Area |
|---------|-----------|------|--------------|
| **Reading and Writing** | 54 questions | 64 mins | Literature, History, Social Studies, Science |
| **Math** | 44 questions | 70 mins | Algebra, Problem Solving, Data Analysis, Advanced Math |

### Grading Format
- **Total Score:** 400–1600.
- **Section Scores:** 200–800 for each of the two sections.
- **Format:** Computer-based adaptive testing.

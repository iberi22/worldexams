# Research: Guatemala Education System & Standardized Tests

## 1. Educational Structure (MINEDUC)

Managed by the **Ministerio de Educación (MINEDUC)**.

- **Nivel Inicial:** Ages 0-4.
- **Nivel Preprimario:** Ages 4-6.
- **Nivel Primario:** 6 years (Grades 1-6).
- **Nivel Medio:**
  - **Ciclo Básico:** 3 years (Grades 7-9).
  - **Ciclo Diversificado:** 2-3 years (Grades 10-11/12). Leads to "Bachiller", "Perito", or "Magisterio".

## 2. Standardized Tests

### Evaluación de Graduandos
Applied to all students in the final year of the Diversificado cycle.
- **Subjects:** Lectura and Matemáticas.
- **Purpose:** Diagnostic assessment of national educational quality.

## 4. Cultural Context for Content

- **Currency:** Quetzal (GTQ).
- **Cities:** Ciudad de Guatemala, Mixco, Quetzaltenango, Villa Nueva, Antigua Guatemala, Escuintla.
- **Names:**
  - Male: Juan, José, Carlos, Luis, Rigoberto.
  - Female: María, Ana, Rosa, Glenda, Xiomara.
- **References:** Tikal, civilización Maya, quetzal (ave), volcanes, café, tejidos típicos, marimba, huipiles.

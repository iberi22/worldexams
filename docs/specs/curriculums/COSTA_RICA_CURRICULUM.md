# Research: Costa Rica Education System & Standardized Tests

## 1. Educational Structure (MEP)

Managed by the **Ministerio de Educación Pública (MEP)**.

- **Educación Preescolar:** 2 years (Interactivo II and Transición).
- **Educación General Básica:**
  - **I Ciclo:** Grades 1-3.
  - **II Ciclo:** Grades 4-6.
  - **III Ciclo:** Grades 7-9.
- **Educación Diversificada (High School):**
  - Grades 10-11 (Academic) or 10-12 (Technical).

## 2. Standardized Tests

### Pruebas Nacionales Estandarizadas
Replaced the old "Bachillerato" and "FARO" exams. They are applied at the end of each cycle:
- **Primary:** 6th grade.
- **High School:** 11th grade (Academic) or 12th grade (Technical).
- **Components:** Cognitive skills across core subjects.

## 3. Subjects

- **Español:** Language and literature.
- **Matemática:** Problem-solving oriented.
- **Ciencias:** Biology, Physics, and Chemistry.
- **Estudios Sociales:** History and Geography.
- **Inglés / Francés:** Foreign languages.
- **Educación Cívica:** Civic training.

## 4. Cultural Context for Content

- **Currency:** Colón (CRC).
- **Cities:** San José, Alajuela, Cartago, Heredia, Puntarenas, Limón, Liberia.
- **Names:**
  - Male: José, Luis, Carlos, Andrey, Keylor.
  - Female: María, Ana, María Fernanda, Daniela, Ximena.
- **References:** "Pura Vida", volcanes (Arenal, Poás), biodiversidad, café, gallo pinto, carretas típicas, ticos.

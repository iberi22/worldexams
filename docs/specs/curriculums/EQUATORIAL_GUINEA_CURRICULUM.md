# Research: Equatorial Guinea Education System

## Phase 1: Educational Structure

Regulated by the **Ministerio de Educación, Enseñanza Universitaria y Deportes**. It shares historical ties with the Spanish system.

### K-12 Structure
- **Educación Primaria:** 6 years.
- **Enseñanza Secundaria Básica (ESBA):** 4 years.
- **Bachillerato:** 2 years.

---

## Phase 2: Selectividad

The university admission process follows a model very similar to the Spanish **Selectividad**.

---

## Phase 3: Universities

1. **Universidad Nacional de Guinea Ecuatorial (UNGE)**
2. **Universidad Afro-Americana de África Central (AAUCA)**

---

## Phase 4: Bundle Generation Strategy (GQ)

### ID Format
`GQ-[SUBJ]-[GRADE]-[TOPIC]-[VERSION]`
- Example: `GQ-MAT-12-trigonometria-001-v3-bundle.md`

### Cultural Context
- Currency: Franco CFA (XAF).
- Language: Spanish (standard, similar to Spain).

# Research: Puerto Rico Education System & Standardized Tests (PAA)

## Phase 1: Educational Structure

The Puerto Rican education system is managed by the **Departamento de Educación de Puerto Rico (DEPR)**. It follows the US-style K-12 model but is predominantly Spanish-speaking.

### K-12 Structure
- **Escuela Elementaria:** Grades 1-5.
- **Escuela Intermedia:** Grades 6-8.
- **Escuela Superior:** Grades 9-12.

---

## Phase 2: PAA (College Board)

The **Prueba de Aptitud Académica (PAA)** by College Board is the primary university admission exam (similar to the SAT but in Spanish).

### Exam Structure
1. **Lectura y Redacción:** Spanish language skills.
2. **Matemáticas:** Arithmetic, Algebra, Geometry, Statistics.
3. **Inglés:** English as a second language.

`PR-[SUBJ]-[GRADE]-[TOPIC]-[VERSION]`

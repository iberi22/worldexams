# Protocolo: Australia (AU) - ACARA/HSC

## Sistema Educativo
- **Estructura:** Primary (Foundation/K-Prep to Y6), Secondary (Y7-Y12)
- **Curriculum:** Australian Curriculum (ACARA) version 9.0
- **Exámenes clave:**
  - **NAPLAN** (Years 3, 5, 7, 9) — National Assessment Program
  - **HSC** (Year 12, NSW) — Higher School Certificate
  - **VCE** (Year 12, Victoria) — Victorian Certificate of Education
  - **QCE** (Year 12, Queensland) — Queensland Certificate of Education
  - **ATAR** (Australian Tertiary Admission Rank) — National ranking system
- **Equivalente ICFES:** HSC/VCE/QCE (Year 12 - ATAR)
- **Ciclo:** Foundation to Year 12 (13 años de educación)

## Calendario Académico Australia
- **Inicio:** Late January / Early February
- **Términos:** 4 terms × ~10 weeks = 40 weeks
- **Año académico:** Late Jan to Mid-Dec (Southern Hemisphere)
- **Descansos:** Autumn (Apr), Winter (Jul), Spring (Sep-Oct), Summer (Dec-Jan)
- **Semanas lectivas:** ~40 semanas (W01-W40)

## Coding Guidelines
- **Country code:** `AU` → directory `questions_data/australia/`
- **Subjects:** `mathematics`, `english`, `biology`, `chemistry`, `physics`, `modern-history`, `geography`, `lote-japanese`, `lote-chinese`, `lote-italian`, `lote-french`, `lote-indonesian`
- **Year folders:** `year-11`, `year-12`
- **ID format:** `AU-{SUBJ}-Y{YY}-2026-W{NN}-{topic}-001-MASTERY`
- **CEFR field:** Set for LOTE subjects only (A1, A2, B1, B2, C1)
- **Language:** English (AU) for all subjects except LOTE
- **Modern contexts:** Australian cities, culture, Indigenous perspectives, environmental contexts
- **MFL bundles:** Include `cefr_level` in frontmatter

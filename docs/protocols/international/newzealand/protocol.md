# Protocolo New Zealand (NZ) — NCEA / NZC

## Sistema Educativo
- **Estructura:** Primary (Y1–Y8), Secondary (Y9–Y13)
- **Curriculum:** New Zealand Curriculum (NZC) — refreshed 2023
- **Exámenes clave:** NCEA Levels 1, 2, 3 (Y11–Y13)
- **Equivalente ICFES:** NCEA Level 3 (Year 13)
- **Idiomas:** English, Te Reo Māori, Pacific Languages

## Calendario Académico
- **Inicio:** Late January / Early February
- **Términos:** 4 terms × ~10 weeks = 40 weeks
- **Año académico:** Late Jan to Mid-Dec (Southern Hemisphere)

## Subjects for MASTERY bundles
1. **Mathematics & Statistics** — Numeracy, Algebra, Geometry, Statistics, Calculus
2. **English** — Reading, Writing, Oral Language, Visual Language

4. **Social Sciences** — History, Geography, Economics, Social Studies
5. **Languages** — Te Reo Māori, Pacific Languages, International Languages (Spanish, French, Chinese)

## Temas por Grade — Mathematics & Statistics (NZC)

### Year 9 (Grado 9)
- **P1:** Number strategies, Algebra basics
- **P2:** Geometry — shapes and transformations
- **P3:** Statistics — data collection and representation
- **P4:** Probability fundamentals

### Year 10 (Grado 10)
- **P1:** Linear relationships, equations
- **P2:** Geometry — Pythagoras, trigonometry intro
- **P3:** Statistical inference
- **P4:** Probability — compound events

### Year 11 (Grado 11) — NCEA Level 1
- **P1:** Numeracy, algebraic manipulation
- **P2:** Geometric reasoning, right-angled triangles
- **P3:** Statistical literacy, chance and data
- **P4:** Linear algebra, patterns

### Year 12 (Grado 12) — NCEA Level 2
- **P1:** Coordinate geometry, sequences and series
- **P2:** Calculus — introduction to differentiation
- **P3:** Statistical investigations, probability distributions
- **P4:** Trigonometry — non-right-angled triangles

### Year 13 (Grado 13) — NCEA Level 3
- **P1:** Complex numbers, calculus
- **P2:** Differential equations, integration
- **P3:** Statistical modelling, probability
- **P4:** Critical path analysis, linear programming

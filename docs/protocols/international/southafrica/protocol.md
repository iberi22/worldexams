# Protocolo South Africa (ZA) — CAPS / NSC Matric

## Sistema Educativo
- **Estructura:** Foundation (R–G3), Intermediate (G4–G6), Senior (G7–G9), FET (G10–G12)
- **Curriculum:** CAPS (Curriculum and Assessment Policy Statement)
- **Exámenes clave:** Matric / NSC (Grade 12)
- **Equivalente ICFES:** National Senior Certificate (Grade 12)
- **Idiomas:** 11 official languages (English, Afrikaans, isiZulu, isiXhosa, Sepedi, Setswana, Sesotho, Xitsonga, siSwati, Tshivenda, isiNdebele)

## Calendario Académico
- **Inicio:** Mid January
- **Términos:** 4 terms × ~10 weeks = 40 weeks
- **Año académico:** Mid-Jan to Early Dec

## Subjects for MASTERY bundles
1. **Mathematics** — CAPS curriculum (Algebra, Geometry, Trigonometry, Calculus, Statistics)
2. **English Home Language / FAL**
3. **Natural Sciences (G7–G9) & Physical/Life Sciences (G10–G12)**
4. **Social Sciences** — History, Geography
5. **Life Orientation**
6. **Additional Languages** — Afrikaans, isiZulu, Setswana

## Temas por Grade — Mathematics (CAPS)

### Grade 10
- **P1:** Numbers and operations, exponents, algebraic expressions
- **P2:** Equations and inequalities, number patterns
- **P3:** Functions — linear, quadratic
- **P4:** Finance and growth, probability

### Grade 11
- **P1:** Exponents and surds, quadratic equations
- **P2:** Functions — hyperbola, exponential, sine/cosine/tangent
- **P3:** Trigonometry — identities, reduction formulae
- **P4:** Statistics — measures of central tendency, probability

### Grade 12 (NSC Matric)
- **P1:** Patterns, sequences and series, functions and inverse functions
- **P2:** Differential calculus, analytical geometry
- **P3:** Trigonometry — compound and double angles, 3D problems
- **P4:** Probability, counting principles, finance and growth

### Natural Sciences (G7–G9)
- **P1:** Life and Living — biodiversity, cells, systems
- **P2:** Matter and Materials — properties, atoms
- **P3:** Energy and Change — forces, electricity
- **P4:** Planet Earth and Beyond — solar system, atmosphere

### Physical Sciences (G10–G12)
- **P1:** Physics — mechanics, waves, sound, light
- **P2:** Chemistry — atomic structure, bonding, reactions
- **P3:** Electricity and magnetism
- **P4:** Chemical change, acids and bases, redox

### Life Sciences (G10–G12)
- **P1:** Biodiversity, cells and molecular biology
- **P2:** Plant and animal tissues, energy transformations
- **P3:** Human reproduction, nervous system
- **P4:** Genetics, evolution, human impact on environment

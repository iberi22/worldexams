**Expected_Success:** 0.85
**Contexto:** Text: 'Some people say the country should build more roads. Roads cost money, take up land and encourage longer car journeys; without them, most journeys would be shorter and cheaper.'

### Enunciado
What position does the author take?

### Opciones
- [x] A) Against building more roads
  <!-- feedback: Every point listed counts against new roads, which reveals an opposing position. -->
- [ ] B) In favour of building more roads
  <!-- feedback: An author arguing for roads would list benefits rather than costs. -->
- [ ] C) Neutral, because the author never takes a side
  <!-- feedback: The consistent direction of the arguments shows a position, even though the word 'oppose' is absent. -->
- [ ] D) In favour of replacing roads with railways
  <!-- feedback: Railways are never mentioned, so the author is not proposing an alternative transport mode. -->

### Explicacion Pedagogica
A position can be detected from the direction of the evidence even when no explicit verdict appears. Listing only disadvantages of a proposal is itself a stance.

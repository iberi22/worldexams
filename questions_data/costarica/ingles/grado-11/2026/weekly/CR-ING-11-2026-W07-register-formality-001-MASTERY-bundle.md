## Question 1 [D3]
**ID:** CR-ING-11-2026-W07-register-formality-v1
**Bloom:** Understand
**EJE:** Linguistic Competence
**Expected_Success:** 0.85
**Contexto:** A student writes to the principal of her school to ask about a scholarship.

### Enunciado
Which sentence is appropriate in a letter to a school principal?

### Opciones
- [x] A) Dear Mr. Rojas, I am writing to ask about the scholarship programme.
  <!-- feedback: A full greeting, a formal title and a complete sentence produce the respectful tone expected in this relationship. -->
- [ ] B) Hey principal, what's up with the scholarship thing?
  <!-- feedback: A casual greeting and 'what's up' place the writer on equal terms with a superior, which the situation does not allow. -->
- [ ] C) Hey Papá, tell me about the scholarship.
  <!-- feedback: Calling the principal 'Papá' is a family form of address and is entirely inappropriate in this context. -->
- [ ] D) Yo quiero saber de la beca, por favor.
  <!-- feedback: The sentence mixes Spanish syntax and an English grammatical frame, which is neither appropriate nor fully English. -->

### Explicacion Pedagogica
Register is the relationship between the writer and the reader expressed through language. Writing to an authority demands a formal greeting, a title, full forms and precise vocabulary, and the first option is the only one that respects that asymmetry.

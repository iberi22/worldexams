### Enunciado
Which sentence about the morning bus is grammatically correct?

### Opciones
- [x] A) The bus from San Jose leaves Cartago at six in the morning.
  <!-- feedback: Correct: the subject The bus is singular, and the present simple third person verb leaves agrees with it. -->
- [ ] B) The students waiting at the stop is worried about the delay.
  <!-- feedback: The subject The students waiting at the stop is plural, so the verb must be are worried, not is. -->
- [ ] C) My brother works in Heredia and they studies engineering in the week.
  <!-- feedback: They studies mixes a plural pronoun with a singular verb; it should be he studies or they study. -->

### Enunciado
Which question tag agrees with the statement about the library?

### Opciones

  <!-- feedback: A positive statement with have to needs the auxiliary have, so the tag must be haven't we, not don't we. -->
- [ ] B) We have to return the books on Friday, hasn't it?
  <!-- feedback: The pronoun it has no match in the statement, which is about we, so a tag built on it cannot agree. -->
- [x] C) We have to return the books on Friday, haven't we?
  <!-- feedback: Correct: the tag copies the auxiliary have in the negative and keeps the pronoun we from the main clause. -->

  <!-- feedback: Didn't they is both negative and past, while the statement is positive and present, so the tag fails. -->

### Explicacion Pedagogica

**Contexto:** A school counsellor in Heredia advises a student about leaving a concert to catch the bus.

### Enunciado
Which sentence is correct after the verb recommend?

### Opciones
- [ ] A) She recommended to take the bus early instead of staying for the concert.
  <!-- feedback: After recommend the object is a gerund, so an infinitive beginning with to is not the pattern this verb takes. -->

  <!-- feedback: Correct: recommend takes a gerund object, so taking the bus is the natural and expected complement. -->
- [ ] C) She recommended take the bus early instead of staying for the concert.
  <!-- feedback: A bare base verb cannot follow recommend in this object position, because the object must be a noun or a gerund. -->

### Enunciado
Which passive version matches the active sentence about the clinic in Liberia?

### Opciones
- [ ] A) The clinic in Liberia will admit 40 patients on Monday.
  <!-- feedback: This repeats the active sentence unchanged, so the be plus past participle structure never appears. -->
- [ ] B) 40 patients will admit by the clinic in Liberia on Monday.
  <!-- feedback: The verb admit is left active after the new subject, so the sentence does not form a passive at all. -->
- [ ] C) 40 patients are admitted by the clinic in Liberia on Monday.
  <!-- feedback: Are admitted is a present passive, but the active verb was future, so the tense shifts by mistake. -->

The passive makes the object the subject and uses be plus past participle, keeps the tense of the original verb, and adds the doer after by.

### Enunciado
Sofia said, 'I am nervous about the exam.' How does she report it?

### Opciones
- [x] A) She said that she was nervous about the exam.
  <!-- feedback: Correct: reported speech shifts the tense one step back, so am becomes was and the pronoun changes to she. -->
- [ ] B) She says that she is nervous about the exam.
  <!-- feedback: Says and is keep the original tense and pronouns, so the sentence reads as a quotation rather than a report. -->
- [ ] C) She said that she is nervous about the exam.
  <!-- feedback: Said marks past reporting, but is was never shifted back, so the reported tense no longer matches the verb. -->

When the reporting verb is past, the tense inside the reported clause moves one step back: am becomes was, will becomes would, and pronouns also change.

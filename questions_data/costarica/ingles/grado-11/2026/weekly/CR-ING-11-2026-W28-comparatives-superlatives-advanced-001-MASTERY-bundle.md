### Enunciado
Sofia wrote less carefully, so Andres's essay is ______?

### Opciones
- [x] A) better
  <!-- feedback: Correct! The comparative of 'good' is the irregular form 'better', so it needs no 'more' at all. -->
- [ ] B) best
  <!-- feedback: 'Best' is the superlative, which would rank his essay against all the others, not only against Sofia's. -->
- [ ] C) goodest
  <!-- feedback: No English word 'goodest' exists: the irregular series is only good, better and best. -->

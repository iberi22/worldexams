## Question 1 [D3]
**ID:** CR-ING-11-2026-W30-writing-opinion-essay-001-MASTERY-bundle-v1
**Bloom:** Remember
**EJE:** Linguistic Competence
**Expected_Success:** 0.85
**Contexto:** A grade 11 student in Alajuela plans an opinion essay about school uniforms.

### Enunciado
Which order is the standard structure of a four-part opinion essay?

### Opciones
- [x] A) Introduction, body paragraphs, counterargument, conclusion
  <!-- feedback: Correct! It opens with the topic and your position, develops the reasons, adds the opposing view and closes by restating the opinion. -->
- [ ] B) Thesis, examples, conclusion, restatement of the thesis
  <!-- feedback: This states the thesis twice and gives no place for the opposing view, so the reader gets the position twice and the argument not at all. -->
- [ ] C) Arguments, topic sentence, hook, final opinion
  <!-- feedback: The hook and the topic sentence belong to the introduction and to the body, so putting them after the arguments breaks the reader's path. -->
- [ ] D) Conclusion, counterargument, body paragraphs, introduction
  <!-- feedback: A conclusion cannot come first: the reader needs the reasons and the counterargument before the final opinion can mean anything. -->

### Explicacion Pedagogica
An opinion essay moves from the topic and your thesis, through reasons with examples and a counterargument, to a conclusion that closes the argument without new facts.

## Question 3 [D4]
**ID:** CR-ING-11-2026-W30-writing-opinion-essay-001-MASTERY-bundle-v3
**Bloom:** Understand
**EJE:** Linguistic Competence
**Expected_Success:** 0.85
**Contexto:** A writer in Cartago wants to admit a counterargument before defending her own view.

### Enunciado
Which connector set introduces a concession and then turns back with a rebuttal?

### Opciones
- [ ] A) because ... and so ...
  <!-- feedback: Both words link a cause to its result, so this pair builds one line of support instead of admitting any opposing point. -->
- [x] B) While it is true that ... , I still believe that ...
  <!-- feedback: Correct! 'While it is true that' concedes the other side, and 'I still believe that' returns the paragraph to the writer's own view. -->
- [ ] C) Although ... , because ...
  <!-- feedback: 'Although' does open a concession, but 'because' can only follow a cause, so the two cannot join inside one clause this way. -->
- [ ] D) On the one hand ... , on the other hand ...
  <!-- feedback: This pair simply sets two sides in balance and never signals that the writer rejects one of them before moving on. -->

### Explicacion Pedagogica
Concession and rebuttal need two different links: one that grants the opposing point (while, although, admittedly) and one that returns to your position (still, nevertheless).

**Contexto:** A student in Puntarenas reviews a draft sentence before submitting his essay.

### Enunciado
Which option corrects "In my opinion I think that uniforms are more practical"?

### Opciones
- [ ] A) In my opinion I believe the uniforms are more practical.
  <!-- feedback: This keeps two opinion markers, 'In my opinion' and 'I believe', and one sentence should carry only one of them. -->
- [ ] B) In my think, the uniforms are more practical.
  <!-- feedback: 'In my think' is not an English phrase: the preposition 'in' always comes before a noun such as 'opinion' or 'view'. -->
- [ ] C) In my opinion the uniforms is more practical.
  <!-- feedback: The subject 'uniforms' is plural, so it takes the verb 'are'; 'is' would agree only with the singular noun 'uniform'. -->
- [x] D) In my opinion, the uniforms are more practical.
  <!-- feedback: Correct! One opinion marker plus the comma after it is enough, because the extra 'I think that' repeats the very same stance. -->

### Explicacion Pedagogica
English signals an opinion once. 'In my opinion', 'I think that' and 'I believe that' all perform the same job, so using two of them duplicates the point instead of adding it.

### Enunciado
Which connective adds supporting information to a point already made?

### Opciones
- [x] A) Moreover, exam practice also reduces anxiety before the test.
  <!-- feedback: Correct! 'Moreover' is an additive connective: it presents extra information that strengthens the same line of argument. -->
- [ ] B) Nevertheless, exam practice makes students more nervous.
  <!-- feedback: 'Nevertheless' signals contrast, so this sentence would turn against the previous idea instead of adding to it. -->
- [ ] C) For example, a student who fails twice often stops revising.
  <!-- feedback: 'For example' introduces an illustration that proves a claim, not new information that expands the point itself. -->

Connectives mark the writer's plan: moreover and in addition add information, for instance and for example prove it, whereas but and nevertheless turn against it.

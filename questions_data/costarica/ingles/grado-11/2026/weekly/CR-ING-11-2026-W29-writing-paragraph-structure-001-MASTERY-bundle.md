## Question 1 [D3]
**ID:** CR-ING-11-2026-W29-writing-paragraph-structure-001-MASTERY-bundle-v1
**Bloom:** Remember
**EJE:** Linguistic Competence
**Expected_Success:** 0.85
**Contexto:** Andres writes a paragraph about the coffee farms he visited in Tarrazu.

### Enunciado
Which sentence in Andres's paragraph states the main idea?

### Opciones
- [x] A) Coffee farming in the Tarrazu valley has changed over the last decade.
  <!-- feedback: Correct: it names the topic and adds the writer's angle, so it can control every other sentence. -->
- [ ] B) The road up to the farm is steep and the turns are very narrow.
  <!-- feedback: This is a supporting detail about the road, but it cannot carry the whole main idea. -->
- [ ] C) My uncle grows coffee on three hectares near San Marcos.
  <!-- feedback: It is a personal example about one relative, so it narrows the focus too much. -->
- [ ] D) Last March I drank four cups of coffee in a single morning.
  <!-- feedback: A funny anecdote that stands alone and does not build any general idea. -->

### Explicacion Pedagogica
A topic sentence announces the general idea and usually comes first. Every sentence after it adds reasons, details or examples that stay inside the frame it sets.

**EJE:** Linguistic Competence
**Expected_Success:** 0.85
**Contexto:** Karla writes about how she prepared for the English exam at her school in Heredia.

### Enunciado
Which sentence should Karla cut because it breaks paragraph unity?

### Opciones
- [ ] A) I made a vocabulary list and reviewed it every night for a month.
  <!-- feedback: This is a concrete action that supports the topic and gives evidence for the habit. -->
- [ ] B) My teacher suggested practice tests from the ministry website.
  <!-- feedback: It names a useful source of practice, so it explains how she actually prepared. -->
- [x] C) Last summer my cousin won a prize in mathematics in Limon.
  <!-- feedback: Correct to cut: it changes person, place and subject, so it breaks unity with the topic. -->
- [ ] D) On exam day I felt nervous, but I answered every single item.
  <!-- feedback: It reports her feelings on exam day, which belongs to the preparation topic. -->

### Explicacion Pedagogica
Unity means every sentence in a paragraph develops one single idea. A true detail about an unrelated event is still off topic and weakens the focus, so it is cut or moved.

  <!-- feedback: Correct: it names the place, adds detail, and is broad enough to hold the whole paragraph. -->

## Question 4 [D4]

**Bloom:** Understand
**EJE:** Linguistic Competence
**Expected_Success:** 0.85
**Contexto:** Yendry explains in one paragraph how she planted a vegetable garden in Heredia.

### Enunciado
Which order is the most logical for Yendry's paragraph about her garden?

### Opciones
- [ ] A) Conclusion first, then the topic sentence, then the supporting sentences.
  <!-- feedback: Starting with the conclusion inverts the path, because the idea must be announced first. -->
- [ ] B) Supporting sentences, then the conclusion, then the topic sentence.
  <!-- feedback: Details before the main idea leave the reader guessing, and nothing has been opened yet. -->
- [ ] C) Topic sentence, then the conclusion, then the supporting sentences.
  <!-- feedback: The conclusion arrives before the support, so it has nothing to summarise. -->
- [x] D) Topic sentence, then the details in time order, then the conclusion.
  <!-- feedback: Correct: the idea is announced, developed step by step, and then closed with a summary. -->

### Explicacion Pedagogica
The standard path is topic sentence, then supporting sentences following a clear sequence, then a concluding sentence. Time order or numbered steps belong in the middle of the paragraph.

## Question 5 [D5]
**ID:** CR-ING-11-2026-W29-writing-paragraph-structure-001-MASTERY-bundle-v5
**Bloom:** Understand
**EJE:** Linguistic Competence
**Expected_Success:** 0.85
**Contexto:** Alonso compares studying in the cloud forest near Monteverde with studying in town.

### Enunciado
Which sentence links those two study habits with the right logical relation?

### Opciones
- [x] A) By contrast, the library in town is quiet but it closes early.
  <!-- feedback: Correct: 'By contrast' marks a difference, which is exactly the link between the two situations. -->
- [ ] B) As a result, Alonso now studies in the forest when the weather is fine.
  <!-- feedback: 'As a result' signals a consequence, yet the two habits are alternatives and not effects. -->
- [ ] C) For example, he carries a notebook and two pens to the reserve.
  <!-- feedback: 'For example' introduces evidence, so a general claim has to come before it. -->
- [ ] D) In the same way, both places suit students who read a lot.
  <!-- feedback: 'In the same way' joins similar points, but these two places are opposed and not parallel. -->

### Explicacion Pedagogica
Connectives must match the real logical link. Contrast words signal difference, cause words signal a result, and example words signal support, so a wrong link misleads the reader.

## Question 6 [D5]

**Bloom:** Apply
**EJE:** Linguistic Competence
**Expected_Success:** 0.85
**Contexto:** It did not rain for a week in Braulio Carrillo, and the park made a decision.

### Enunciado
Which sentence correctly links the dry week to the park's decision?

### Opciones
- [ ] A) However, the park reopened the trail so hikers could walk again.
  <!-- feedback: 'However' signals contrast, but no opposing idea appears here, only a cause and its effect. -->
- [x] B) As a result, the park reopened the trail so hikers could walk again.
  <!-- feedback: Correct: 'As a result' presents the dry week as the cause and the reopening as the effect. -->
- [ ] C) In addition, the park reopened the trail so hikers could walk again.
  <!-- feedback: 'In addition' adds extra weight, yet the second sentence is a consequence and not a bonus point. -->
- [ ] D) Otherwise, the park reopened the trail so hikers could walk again.
  <!-- feedback: 'Otherwise' shows what happens if a condition fails, which is hypothetical here. -->

### Explicacion Pedagogica
Cause and effect paragraphs place the cause before the effect and use a signal word such as 'as a result' or 'because'. The connective must confirm that real relationship.

## Question 8 [D6]
**ID:** CR-ING-11-2026-W29-writing-paragraph-structure-001-MASTERY-bundle-v8
**Bloom:** Apply
**EJE:** Linguistic Competence
**Expected_Success:** 0.85
**Contexto:** Julio wrote a short paragraph about the bike ride from his house to school in San Jose.

### Enunciado
Which version of Julio's paragraph avoids unnecessary repetition?

### Opciones
- [x] A) I cycle to school every morning, and the ride takes about twenty minutes.
  <!-- feedback: Correct: one idea, one clean pair of clauses, and no fact is stated twice. -->
- [ ] B) I cycle to school every single morning, and in the morning I cycle to school.
  <!-- feedback: 'Every morning' and 'in the morning' repeat the same time, so one of them should go. -->
- [ ] C) Cycling is something I do, and my activity in the morning is cycling too.
  <!-- feedback: This is wordy nominalisation, because the action is hidden inside nouns instead of verbs. -->
- [ ] D) I cycle, I also pedal, and furthermore I ride my bicycle to school.
  <!-- feedback: Three verbs describe one single action, so the paragraph swells without gaining meaning. -->

### Explicacion Pedagogica
A tight paragraph states each point once. Repeating a time phrase, hiding the action inside nouns, or stacking synonyms all add length without adding information for the reader.

Which sequence keeps his process paragraph logically clear?

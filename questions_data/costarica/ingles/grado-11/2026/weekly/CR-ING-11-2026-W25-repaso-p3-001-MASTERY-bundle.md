## Question 1 [D3]
**ID:** CR-ING-11-2026-W25-repaso-p3-001-MASTERY-bundle-v1

**ICFES:** Linguistic Competence
**Expected_Success:** 0.88
**Contexto:** Carlos is describing his recent eco-tour in Monteverde Cloud Forest.

### Enunciado
Carlos said, "I have never seen such a beautiful quetzal before." What is the correct reported speech for this sentence?

### Opciones
- [ ] A) Carlos said he has never seen such a beautiful quetzal before. <!-- feedback: Incorrecto. Carlos said he has never seen such a bea -->

- [x] B) Carlos said he had never seen such a beautiful quetzal before. <!-- feedback: Correcto! Carlos said he had never seen such a beautiful quetzal befor -->

- [ ] C) Carlos said he never saw such a beautiful quetzal before. <!-- feedback: Incorrecto. Carlos said he never saw such a beautifu -->

- [ ] D) Carlos said he would never see such a beautiful quetzal before. <!-- feedback: Incorrecto. Carlos said he would never see such a be -->

### Explicacion Pedagogica
In reported speech, we shift tenses back. Present Perfect (have seen) becomes Past Perfect (had seen). This is a fundamental rule for high-level English proficiency (C1).

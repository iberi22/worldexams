### Enunciado
Which connector introduces a contrast between two ideas?

### Opciones
- [x] B) However
  <!-- feedback: 'However' is a contrastive connector and it signals that the second idea differs from the first. -->
- [ ] C) Therefore
  <!-- feedback: 'Therefore' introduces a consequence, which is the opposite of a contrast. -->
- [ ] D) In addition
  <!-- feedback: 'In addition' adds extra information of the same kind rather than opposing it. -->

### Enunciado
Which sentence uses 'due to' correctly?

### Opciones
- [x] D) The farms lost their water source due to the drying of the river.
  <!-- feedback: 'Due to' is followed by a noun phrase, and 'the drying of the river' is exactly that. -->

  <!-- feedback: The preposition after 'due' is 'to', never 'of'. -->

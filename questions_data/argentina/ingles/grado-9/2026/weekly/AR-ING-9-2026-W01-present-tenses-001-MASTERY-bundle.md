## Question 1 [D3-D4]
**ID:** AR-ING-9-2026-W01-present-tenses-001-MASTERY-bundle-v1
**Bloom:** Remember
**EJE:** Lengua Extranjera - Inglés
**Expected_Success:** 0.85
**Contexto:** Martina vive en Buenos Aires y describe su rutina diaria a un amigo.

### Enunciado
Choose the correct form of the verb to complete the sentence: "Every morning, I _______ my breakfast at 7:30 AM."

### Opciones
- [ ] A) eating <!-- feedback: 'Eating' is an -ing form, which needs an auxiliary such as 'am', and a routine takes the simple present instead. -->
- [x] C) eat <!-- feedback: The simple present in the bare infinitive, without the -s, is the form used with 'I' for routines. -->
- [ ] B) eats <!-- feedback: The -s form is used with he, she and it, not with the subject 'I'. -->

### Explicacion Pedagogica
Para expresar rutinas y hábitos en presente simple con el pronombre "I", debés usar la forma base del verbo. En este caso, "eat" es la opción correcta porque Martina está describiendo algo que hace todos los días en la Ciudad de Buenos Aires.

## Question 2 [D3-D4]

**Expected_Success:** 0.80
**Contexto:** Facundo está en Córdoba y está hablando por teléfono sobre lo que está haciendo en este preciso momento.

### Enunciado
Complete the sentence using the Present Continuous: "Wait a second, Facundo can't talk right now. He _______ a shower."

### Opciones
- [ ] B) has <!-- feedback: The simple present marks a habit, but 'right now' requires an action in progress. -->
- [ ] C) having <!-- feedback: The auxiliary 'is' is missing, so 'having' cannot form the present continuous here. -->
- [x] A) is having <!-- feedback: The present continuous is 'is' plus the -ing form, which is what is happening at this moment. -->

### Explicacion Pedagogica
Cuando querés describir una acción que está pasando justo ahora (como Facundo bañándose), tenés que usar el presente continuo. Recordá que la estructura es: Sujeto + am/is/are + verbo con -ing. Como el sujeto es "He", corresponde usar "is having".

### Enunciado
Some verbs are usually NOT used in the continuous form. Choose the correct sentence:

What is the difference between these two sentences?
1. I think Bariloche is beautiful.
2. I am thinking about moving to Bariloche.

### Opciones
- [ ] A) There is no difference; both are about the present. <!-- feedback: There is a difference, because the tense changes the meaning of the verb 'think'. -->

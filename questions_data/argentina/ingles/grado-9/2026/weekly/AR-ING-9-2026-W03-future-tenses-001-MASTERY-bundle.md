**Contexto:** Martina y su familia están planeando sus próximas vacaciones de verano.

### Enunciado
Choose the correct option to express a future plan already decided: "Next January, we _______ to Bariloche. We already bought the tickets."

### Opciones
- [ ] A) will go <!-- feedback: "Will" is used for spontaneous decisions and promises made at the moment of speaking, not for a plan whose tickets are already bought. -->
- [x] B) are going <!-- feedback: A fixed plan with the date and place already arranged takes the present continuous, "are going", and the bought tickets are the proof of that arrangement. -->

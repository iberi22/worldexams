## Passages for Questions 1-6

**Passage: "The Last Summer" (excerpt)**

Maya had spent every summer of her childhood at her grandmother's house in Savannah, Georgia. The oak trees that lined the street were ancient, their Spanish moss swaying like grandmother's stories in the wind. This particular summer felt different—perhaps because she was fifteen now, or perhaps because Grandmother had grown quieter since Grandfather's passing the previous spring.

The morning heat pressed against the windows as Maya sat on the porch, watching the mailman make his rounds. She had been waiting three weeks for a letter from the college in Boston where she hoped to attend. Her mother said the decision would come by July, but Maya had already memorized every word of the application essay about why literature mattered to her.

**Passage: "Industrial Shadows" (Historical Fiction)**

The factory whistle pierced the dawn over Pittsburgh's steel district. Samuel wiped the sleep from his eyes and reached for his lunch pail—another day in the Andrew Carnegie Steel Works. At seventeen, he had worked the rail mills for two years, long enough to know that ambition was a luxury for men who didn't have families depending on every dollar.

The foreman's voice cut through the machinery's roar: "Thompson line, full speed ahead!" Samuel felt the familiar tension in his shoulders as the glowing steel bars emerged from the rollers. His father had lost three fingers to these same machines before the company declared him unfit for work. Samuel had promised himself he would find another way out—but there was no other work for boys from the Hill District, not real work that paid enough to survive.

Captain Ahab never spoke about the白鲸—that white whale—except in the darkest hours of the night watch. I had heard the stories from the first mate, a man from Maine who had been on three previous voyages with Ahab and survived each one. "The captain's leg is oak," he told me once, "but his mind is the sea itself. It only rests when Moby-Dick is beneath the hull."

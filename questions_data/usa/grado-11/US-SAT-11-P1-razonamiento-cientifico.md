## Questions 1-7: Passage about Ocean Acidification

**Experiment 1: Measuring CO₂ Absorption**

Scientists at the Monterey Bay Aquarium Research Institute studied how ocean water absorbs carbon dioxide. Three water samples (A, B, and C) were collected from different depths off the California coast. Each sample was exposed to controlled amounts of CO₂, and pH levels were recorded over 30 minutes.

**Table 1: pH Changes in Ocean Samples**

| Time (min) | Sample A (surface) | Sample B (50m) | Sample C (100m) |
|------------|-------------------|----------------|----------------|
| 0          | 8.1               | 8.0            | 7.9            |
| 10         | 7.8               | 7.7            | 7.6            |
| 20         | 7.5               | 7.4            | 7.3            |
| 30         | 7.3               | 7.2            | 7.1            |

---

## Question 1 — D4
As CO₂ exposure time increased, pH of all samples:

A) Increased  
B) Decreased  
C) Remained constant  
D) Fluctuated randomly  
**[ ]** → **B**

---

## Question 2 — D5

A) Sample A  
B) Sample B  
C) Sample C  
D) All samples had equal pH  
**[ ]** → **A**

## Questions 15-20: Passage about Earthquake Waves

**Experiment 3: Seismic Wave Analysis**

Geologists at the University of Nevada tested how different soil types affect seismic wave amplitude. Three soil samples (sand, clay, and mixed) were placed in a shaking table apparatus. A standardized vibration was applied, and peak amplitude was measured.

**Table 2: Seismic Wave Amplitude (mm)**

| Frequency (Hz) | Sand | Clay | Mixed |
|----------------|------|------|-------|
| 1              | 12   | 18   | 15    |
| 5              | 8    | 15   | 11    |
| 10             | 5    | 12   | 8     |
| 20             | 3    | 9    | 6     |

---

## Question 15 — D5
At all frequencies tested, which soil type produced the highest amplitude?

A) Sand  
B) Clay  
C) Mixed  
D) All produced equal amplitudes  
**[ ]** → **B**

---

## Question 16 — D5
As frequency increased, amplitude in sand decreased by approximately:

A) 25%  
B) 50%  
C) 75%  
D) 90%  
**[ ]** → **C**

---

### Enunciado
Which sentence expresses the zero conditional correctly?

### Opciones
- [x] A) If you heat ice, it melts.
  <!-- feedback: Correct. The zero conditional expresses a general truth and uses the present in both parts of the sentence. -->
- [ ] B) If you heated ice, it would melt.
  <!-- feedback: Incorrect. That sentence is a second conditional, which talks about a hypothetical situation rather than a general truth. -->
- [ ] C) If you will heat ice, it melts.
  <!-- feedback: Incorrect. The zero conditional does not take 'will' in the 'if' clause; the present simple 'heat' is enough. -->

### Explicacion Pedagogica
El condicional cero expresa hechos generales y siempre verificables, con presente simple en las dos partes. Se usa con 'if', 'when', 'every time' o 'as soon as' y describe lo que ocurre siempre que se cumpla la condicion.

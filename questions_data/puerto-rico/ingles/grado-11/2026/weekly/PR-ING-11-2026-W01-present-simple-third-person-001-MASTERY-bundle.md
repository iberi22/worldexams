**Contexto:** Present simple third person, San Juan, grado 11, W01.

### Enunciado
Which sentence uses the third person singular of the verb 'to work' correctly?

### Opciones
- [x] A) She works in a clinic in Carolina.
  <!-- feedback: Correct! 'She works' carries the -s of the third person singular, and every other option either drops that ending or builds a different tense. -->
- [ ] B) She work in a clinic in Carolina.
  <!-- feedback: Incorrect! 'She work' leaves the verb bare while the subject 'she' is third person singular, so the -s of that person is missing. -->
- [ ] C) She working in a clinic in Carolina.
  <!-- feedback: Incorrect! 'Working' is a present participle, and a participle only stands on its own after the auxiliary 'is'. -->
- [ ] D) She are working in a clinic in Carolina.
  <!-- feedback: Incorrect! 'Are working' needs a plural subject, and 'she' is singular, so two separate rules fail at once. -->

### Explicacion Pedagogica
En presente simple la tercera persona singular añade -s al verbo, salvo cuando ya termina en -s, -sh, -ch, -x, -z o -o. Las otras personas no añaden nada. Revisar el sujeto antes que el verbo es el método más seguro para no equivocarse.

### Enunciado
Which question is formed correctly?

### Opciones
- [ ] A) Do your sister live in Ponce?
  <!-- feedback: Incorrect! 'Do' is the auxiliary of the first and second persons, so it cannot agree with the singular 'your sister'. -->

  <!-- feedback: Correct! 'Does' already holds the -s of the third person singular, and that is why the main verb returns to 'live'. -->

  <!-- feedback: Incorrect! After 'is' the verb 'to be' can never be followed by 'live' in its base form, so the auxiliary is wrong here. -->

### Explicacion Pedagogica
En presente simple la pregunta con verbos de acción se forma con 'do' o 'does'. Cuando se usa 'does', el verbo principal vuelve a su forma base, porque la -s ya está en el auxiliar. Ese es el error típico: 'does she lives' en lugar de 'does she live'.

### Enunciado
Which sentence uses the present perfect correctly?

### Opciones
- [x] A) She has visited Paris twice.
  <!-- feedback: Correct. 'Has visited' is present perfect, and the sentence does not say when the trip happened, only that it has happened. -->
- [ ] B) She visited Paris twice.
  <!-- feedback: Incorrect. The past simple sounds fine, but the unit calls for the present perfect for an experience with no specific date. -->

  <!-- feedback: Incorrect. After 'has' the verb goes in the past participle, and the participle of 'visit' is 'visited'. -->

**Expected_Success:** 0.80
**Contexto:** Conditionals type 2, San Juan, grado 11, W14.

### Enunciado
Which sentence uses the second conditional correctly?

### Opciones
- [x] A) If I lived closer to San Juan, I would walk to school every day.
  <!-- feedback: Correct. The second conditional expresses an unreal present hypothesis: past simple plus 'would' plus infinitive. -->
- [ ] B) If I will live closer to San Juan, I would walk to school every day.
  <!-- feedback: Incorrect. The 'if' clause does not take 'will'; in the second conditional it takes the past simple. -->
- [ ] C) If I had lived closer to San Juan, I would walk to school every day.
  <!-- feedback: Incorrect. 'Had lived' belongs to the third conditional, which refers to the past rather than the present. -->
- [ ] D) If I live closer to San Juan, I would walk to school every day.
  <!-- feedback: Incorrect. The present simple in the 'if' clause belongs to the first conditional, not to a present hypothesis of the second conditional. -->

### Explicacion Pedagogica
El condicional tipo dos habla del presente con una condicion irreal. Su estructura es pasado simple en la clausula de 'if' y 'would' mas infinitivo en la principal, y casi siempre se usa con 'were' en lugar de 'was' con los sujetos de primera y tercera persona.

## Question 1 [D3-D4]
**ID:** PE-ING-11-2026-W36-writing-narrative-001-MASTERY-v1
**Bloom:** Understand
**EJE:** Writing
**Expected_Success:** 0.80
**Contexto:** A student is asked to write a short narrative.

### Enunciado
Which sentence correctly begins a narrative with an action?

### Opciones
- [x] A) Ana pushed the gate open and ran the last hundred metres to the bus.
  <!-- feedback: Correct. The sentence begins with a verb and moves the character forward, which is what opens a narrative. -->
- [ ] B) Ana pushed the gate open, and then Ana ran, and then Ana arrived.
  <!-- feedback: Wrong. The sentence repeats 'Ana' three times, which destroys any sense of movement. -->
- [ ] C) A girl ran, and she ran, and the bus was there.
  <!-- feedback: Wrong. The sentence repeats 'ran' with no new information and stalls the action. -->
- [ ] D) Ana is a girl who runs to the bus every morning.
  <!-- feedback: Wrong. The sentence is a description of a habitual routine, not an unfolding event. -->

### Explicacion Pedagogica
A narrative opens with an action in the past simple, using sentence structure to move the reader forward.

### Enunciado
Choose the sentence that maintains correct tense throughout the narrative.

### Opciones
- [ ] A) The bus was late, so the driver waits while the passengers find their seats.
  <!-- feedback: Wrong. 'Waits' and 'find' shift to the present and break the past frame of the narrative. -->
- [x] B) The bus was late, so the driver waited while the passengers found their seats.
  <!-- feedback: Correct. All clauses use the past simple, which is the consistent tense for a sequence of completed events. -->
- [ ] C) The bus was late, so the driver waited while the passengers are finding their seats.
  <!-- feedback: Wrong. 'Are finding' shifts the final clause to the present continuous mid-narrative. -->
- [ ] D) The bus is late, so the driver was waiting while the passengers found their seats.
  <!-- feedback: Wrong. 'Is late' is present and inconsistent with the past simple around it. -->

### Explicacion Pedagogica
A short past narrative keeps its tenses consistent, since a shift to the present pulls the reader out of the sequence.

Choose the sentence that correctly orders events using a time connector.

### Opciones

  <!-- feedback: Wrong. The sentence lacks the comma that normally follows 'after' plus a clause, and it ends abruptly. -->

  <!-- feedback: Wrong. The connector is attached to the wrong clause, so the order of events is reversed. -->

Choose the sentence that reports speech correctly without altering the speaker's words.

Direct speech with quotation marks preserves the original words, while reported speech changes the tense and sometimes the pronouns.

Which sequence keeps the cause before its effect?

  <!-- feedback: Wrong. The sequence runs backwards, since the response appears before the events that produced it. -->

Narrative sequences normally follow cause and effect, so reversing the chain makes the events appear unmotivated.

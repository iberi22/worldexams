## Question 1 [D3-D5]
**ID:** PE-ING-11-2026-W34-reading-purpose-tone-001-MASTERY-v1
**Bloom:** Understand
**EJE:** Reading Comprehension
**Expected_Success:** 0.75
**Contexto:** A short notice to residents. Identify its purpose.

### Enunciado
Work to replace the water pipes in Calle Bolívar will begin on Monday and will continue for three weeks. Water will be off between nine in the morning and four in the afternoon on weekdays. Residents are asked to store drinking water in advance. The council's office will post updates on its website each Friday.

### Opciones
- [x] A) To inform residents about planned water interruptions and how to prepare for them.
  <!-- feedback: Correct. The notice gives dates, times and a practical request, which is the content of a service announcement. -->
- [ ] B) To announce a rise in the water tariff to fund the repairs.
  <!-- feedback: Wrong. No tariff change is mentioned anywhere in the notice. -->
- [ ] C) To explain why the old pipes failed and which company supplied them.
  <!-- feedback: Wrong. Nothing is said about the cause of the failure or the supplier. -->
- [ ] D) To persuade residents that the council should replace private pipes at their own cost.
  <!-- feedback: Wrong. No cost is mentioned and the notice does not attempt to persuade anyone. -->

### Explicacion Pedagogica
Purpose is identified by what the text does for the reader, which here is to convey practical information about a service disruption.

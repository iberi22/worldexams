**Contexto:** A student must greet a teacher formally at a school event.

### Enunciado
Which greeting is appropriate in this situation?

### Opciones
- [x] A) Good evening, Mr Salas. It is a pleasure to see you again.
  <!-- feedback: Correct. A time-based greeting with the title and surname is the conventional formal address for a school event. -->
- [ ] B) Yo, how is it going, teacher?
  <!-- feedback: Wrong. 'Yo' is conversational and the greeting gives no title or surname. -->
- [ ] C) Hey, teacher, what is up?
  <!-- feedback: Wrong. 'Hey' and the contracted question are far too casual for a formal occasion. -->

### Explicacion Pedagogica
Greeting a teacher formally requires a conventional time-based greeting together with the title and surname.

### Enunciado
Which pair shows the register shift required when moving from speaking to a teacher to speaking to a friend?

### Opciones
- [x] A) Could you explain that again, please? / Can you say that again?
  <!-- feedback: Correct. The first version is formal, with a modal and please, while the second is the contracted form used with a friend. -->
- [ ] B) I do not understand. / I do not understand.
  <!-- feedback: Wrong. Both versions are identical, and the first is blunt enough to be unsuitable with a teacher. -->
- [ ] C) Could you explain that again, please? / Could you explain that again, please?
  <!-- feedback: Wrong. There is no register shift at all in this option. -->
- [ ] D) Can you explain that again, please? / Could you explain that again, please?
  <!-- feedback: Wrong. This reverses the order and would make the informal version the one used with the teacher. -->

### Explicacion Pedagogica
Register shift is shown by the difference between the polite form used with an authority figure and the contracted form used with a peer.

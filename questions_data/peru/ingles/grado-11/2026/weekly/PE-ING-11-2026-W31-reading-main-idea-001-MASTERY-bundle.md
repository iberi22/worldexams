## Question 1 [D3-D4]
**ID:** PE-ING-11-2026-W31-reading-main-idea-001-MASTERY-v1
**Bloom:** Remember
**EJE:** Reading Comprehension
**Expected_Success:** 0.80
**Contexto:** A short passage about a city recycling programme.

### Enunciado
The city of Huancayo has introduced a weekly recycling collection. Residents must separate paper, glass and plastic into different bins before the lorry arrives. The programme began in three districts last year and will expand to the rest of the city next year. Officials say the aim is to reduce the amount of waste sent to landfill by half within a decade.

### Opciones
- [x] A) The city is expanding a recycling programme in order to cut the waste it sends to landfill.
  <!-- feedback: Correct. The passage states the aim of the recycling programme, and reducing landfill waste by half is that stated aim. -->
- [ ] B) The programme has already achieved its target of reducing landfill waste by half.
  <!-- feedback: Wrong. The reduction is described as an aim for the next decade, so the target has not yet been achieved. -->
- [ ] C) Residents must collect their own waste because the collection service has been reduced.
  <!-- feedback: Wrong. The passage says residents must separate waste, not that the service has been reduced. -->
- [ ] D) The city has banned the use of plastic bottles across all of its districts.
  <!-- feedback: Wrong. No ban on plastic bottles is mentioned anywhere in the passage. -->

### Explicacion Pedagogica
The main idea is the one statement that all the supporting details serve, and here they all describe a recycling scheme aimed at cutting landfill.

**Bloom:** Evaluate
**EJE:** Reading Comprehension
**Expected_Success:** 0.60
**Contexto:** A short passage with an explicit thesis about study methods.

### Enunciado
Universities have promoted spaced revision for years, but the evidence quoted in this article comes almost entirely from laboratory studies of vocabulary learning. The author notes that students preparing for essay subjects may need a different approach, since their skill is in organising arguments rather than recalling isolated facts. She therefore argues that research should compare the two kinds of learning before advising all students to revise the same way.

### Opciones
- [ ] A) The article's thesis is that laboratory studies of vocabulary learning are scientifically worthless.
  <!-- feedback: Wrong. The article criticises the narrowness of the evidence, not its scientific value. -->
- [ ] B) The article's thesis is that spaced revision has been proved effective for all subjects, including essays.
  <!-- feedback: Wrong. The author questions rather than confirms the extension of that evidence to essays. -->
- [x] C) The article's thesis is that advice based on vocabulary research should not be extended to essay-based subjects without further comparison.
  <!-- feedback: Correct. This states the argument the author makes, which is that the evidence does not yet cover essay-based learning. -->
- [ ] D) The article's thesis is that essay writing should be replaced by spaced revision of isolated facts.
  <!-- feedback: Wrong. The author proposes more comparison, not the replacement of essay writing. -->

### Explicacion Pedagogica
When a passage states that evidence does not justify a wider application, the gap between the evidence and the advice is the thesis.

## Question 20 [D6-D8]
**ID:** PE-ING-11-2026-W31-reading-main-idea-001-MASTERY-v20
**Bloom:** Evaluate
**EJE:** Reading Comprehension
**Expected_Success:** 0.60
**Contexto:** A short passage about an unexpected outcome in a trial.

### Enunciado
A drug intended to lower blood pressure was tested in a large trial. The drug did lower blood pressure, as expected, but patients who took it reported noticeably worse sleep than those in the control group. The researchers concluded that the drug should be used only where the benefit outweighs the sleep disturbance. The trial therefore did not produce a simple positive result, even though its main intended effect was confirmed.

### Opciones
- [ ] A) The trial concluded that the drug should never be used, because the sleep disturbance outweighed every benefit.
  <!-- feedback: Wrong. The conclusion restricts rather than prohibits use, and does not weigh the benefit as always outweighed. -->
- [ ] B) The trial showed that sleep quality is unrelated to taking medication for blood pressure.
  <!-- feedback: Wrong. The trial found a connection between the drug and worse sleep. -->
- [ ] C) The trial failed because the drug did not reduce blood pressure as the researchers expected.
  <!-- feedback: Wrong. The passage states that the drug did lower blood pressure as expected. -->
- [x] D) The trial confirmed the drug's intended effect while revealing a side effect that narrows where it should be used.
  <!-- feedback: Correct. This states both the confirmed effect and the new restriction on use, which is the passage's actual conclusion. -->

### Explicacion Pedagogica
A mixed-outcome passage must be summarised with both the confirmed expectation and the newly discovered complication.

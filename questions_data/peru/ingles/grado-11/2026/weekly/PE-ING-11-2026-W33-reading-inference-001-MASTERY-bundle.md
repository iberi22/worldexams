## Question 1 [D3-D5]
**ID:** PE-ING-11-2026-W33-reading-inference-001-MASTERY-v1
**Bloom:** Understand
**EJE:** Reading Comprehension
**Expected_Success:** 0.75
**Contexto:** A passage that implies a conclusion without stating it.

### Enunciado
A factory has replaced night shifts with an early finish. Within a year, the number of resignations fell by half, and several employees who had planned to leave changed their minds. Production figures were unchanged. The manager concluded that the change had been well received, though no survey of staff opinion was carried out.

### Opciones
- [x] A) The change appears to have been welcomed, since fewer people left, though no direct opinion was collected.
  <!-- feedback: Correct. This draws a reasonable inference from the reduced resignations while acknowledging that no survey was done. -->
- [ ] B) The manager asked staff for their opinion before making the change.
  <!-- feedback: Wrong. The passage says no survey was carried out, so no opinion was sought. -->
- [ ] C) Production fell after the change, since the figures are described as unchanged.
  <!-- feedback: Wrong. Unchanged production figures indicate no fall in output. -->
- [ ] D) The change was unpopular, because resignation figures are the only measure of staff opinion.
  <!-- feedback: Wrong. A fall in resignations is normally read as improved satisfaction, not as dissatisfaction. -->

### Explicacion Pedagogica
An inference must be supported by evidence in the passage while going beyond what the passage states literally.

### Enunciado

### Opciones

  <!-- feedback: Wrong. More tickets at half the price yields less revenue, which the passage confirms. -->

  <!-- feedback: Correct. This combines the two facts given, higher attendance and slightly lower revenue, and explains the reason provided. -->

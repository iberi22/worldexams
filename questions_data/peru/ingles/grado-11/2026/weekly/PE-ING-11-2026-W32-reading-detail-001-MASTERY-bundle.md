## Question 1 [D3-D4]
**ID:** PE-ING-11-2026-W32-reading-detail-001-MASTERY-v1

**EJE:** Reading Comprehension
**Expected_Success:** 0.80
**Contexto:** A passage about a bridge inspection. Find the specific detail.

### Enunciado
Engineers inspected the bridge last March and found corrosion in two of the eight steel cables. The cables were replaced within a fortnight, and the bridge has remained open since. The inspection is now scheduled twice a year instead of annually.

### Opciones
- [x] A) The inspection found corrosion in two of the eight steel cables.
  <!-- feedback: Correct. The passage states exactly that: two of the eight cables showed corrosion. -->
- [ ] B) The inspection was carried out annually for the previous ten years.
  <!-- feedback: Wrong. The inspection is now scheduled twice a year, implying that one annual inspection was the previous practice, not ten. -->
- [ ] C) The inspection found that the bridge had to be closed for a month.
  <!-- feedback: Wrong. The bridge remained open and the cables were replaced within a fortnight. -->
- [ ] D) The inspection found corrosion in all eight steel cables.
  <!-- feedback: Wrong. The passage says two of the eight, not all eight. -->

### Explicacion Pedagogica
A detail question asks for the one fact the passage states explicitly, so the correct option must match the wording exactly.

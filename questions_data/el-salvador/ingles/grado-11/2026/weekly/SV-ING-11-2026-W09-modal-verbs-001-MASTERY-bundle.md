### Enunciado
Choose the correct modal: 'During the test you ___ use your phone. The teacher has posted the rule at the door.'

### Opciones
- [x] A) mustn't
  <!-- feedback: 'Mustn't' is the modal of prohibition in the present, so it states that using the phone is not allowed during the test. -->
- [ ] B) might not
  <!-- feedback: 'Might not' only suggests a small possibility. The posted rule forbids the action completely, so it needs the strongest modal. -->
- [ ] C) don't have to
  <!-- feedback: 'Don't have to' means an action is not required but still allowed, whereas the teacher's rule removes the permission entirely. -->

### Explicacion Pedagogica
'Mustn't' expresses prohibition in the present. 'Should not' only advises and 'do not have to' merely removes an obligation, so none of them forbids the action as the rule does.

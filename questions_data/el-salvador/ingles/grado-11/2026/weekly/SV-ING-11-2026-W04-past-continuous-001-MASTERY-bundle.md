  <!-- feedback: 'Are cooking' is present continuous, so it would place the action at the moment of speaking rather than at that past moment. -->

  <!-- feedback: 'Were' plus the -ing form is the past continuous, so it presents the cooking as already in progress when the storm broke. -->

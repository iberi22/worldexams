### Enunciado
Choose the sentence that correctly uses the present perfect.

### Opciones
- [ ] A) She have visited San Miguel three times.
  <!-- feedback: The auxiliary must agree with 'she': it is 'has', not 'have'. -->
- [x] B) She has visited San Miguel three times.
  <!-- feedback: Auxiliary 'has' plus past participle, the correct present perfect structure. -->
- [ ] C) She has visit San Miguel three times.

### Enunciado
Read: 'The villagers carried stones for six months, and the wall has now protected three harvests.' What is the main idea?

### Opciones
- [x] C) Community effort built a flood defence that works
  <!-- feedback: Both halves of the passage are joined by the result, since the labour of the villagers produced a wall that protects their fields. -->
- [ ] A) Carrying stones is exhausting work
  <!-- feedback: The passage never calls the work exhausting, and that reading adds a judgement the text itself does not make. -->
- [ ] B) Three harvests are the price of a wall
  <!-- feedback: The word 'now' presents the harvests as the benefit gained and not as a cost that had to be paid. -->
- [ ] D) Villagers should stop building walls
  <!-- feedback: Nothing in the passage recommends giving up, and the final result is presented as a clear success. -->

### Explicacion Pedagogica
The main idea is the point the whole passage serves, and here the work of the villagers and the protection it brings are part of one claim.

**ID:** SV-ING-11-2026-W13-reported-speech-001-MASTERY-bundle-v1
**Bloom:** Apply
**EJE:** Grammatical Knowledge
**Expected_Success:** 0.80
**Contexto:** Transformacion de directo a indirecto, San Salvador.

### Enunciado
Report the sentence in indirect speech: She said, 'I am tired.'

### Opciones
- [ ] A) She said that she is tired.
  <!-- feedback: In reported speech the present simple becomes the past simple. -->
- [x] B) She said that she was tired.
  <!-- feedback: Pronoun and tense shift: 'I' becomes 'she' and 'am' becomes 'was'. -->
- [ ] C) She says that she was tired.
  <!-- feedback: The reporting verb must be 'said' in the past to match the narrative tense. -->
- [ ] D) She said that I was tired.
  <!-- feedback: The pronoun refers to the reporter's 'I', which is wrong here. -->

### Explicacion Pedagogica
Al pasar a indirecto, el pronombre 'I' se cambia a 'she' y el verbo 'am' pasa a 'was': los tiempos retroceden una unidad.

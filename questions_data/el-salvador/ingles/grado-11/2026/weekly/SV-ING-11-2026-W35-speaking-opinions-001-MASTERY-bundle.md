  <!-- feedback: The sentence describes the question instead of answering it; a response must contain the speaker's own position, not a remark about the task. -->

  <!-- feedback: It states a position with "In my opinion" and supports it with a reason, which is exactly what an opinion response needs in its first sentence. -->

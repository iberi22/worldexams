## Question 1 [D3]
**ID:** EC-ING-11-2026-W39-writing-opinion-paragraph-001-MASTERY-bundle-v1
**Bloom:** Understand
**EJE:** Expresion escrita
**Expected_Success:** 0.85
**Contexto:** Task: Write one paragraph giving your opinion on whether homework should be reduced in the final year of secondary school.

### Enunciado
Which sentence opens a paragraph with a clear opinion?

### Opciones
- [ ] A) There are several arguments about homework that people discuss at length every year.
  <!-- feedback: No. This sentence announces a subject rather than a position, so the reader cannot tell what the writer intends to argue. -->
- [ ] B) Homework is a thing that students all over the world receive in many different forms.
  <!-- feedback: No. The sentence describes homework without taking any position on it, so it cannot open an argument. -->
- [ ] C) In the past, homework was given much more often than it is now in most schools.
  <!-- feedback: No. This is a statement about the past and gives no indication of what the writer thinks should happen. -->
- [x] D) In my view, homework in the final year should be reduced, because it competes with study and family time.
  <!-- feedback: Correct! The position is stated in the first clause and the reason follows immediately, which is what a topic sentence of an opinion paragraph has to do. -->

### Explicacion Pedagogica
A topic sentence in an opinion paragraph must contain the position itself, not the general subject. Putting the claim first tells the reader what to expect from everything that follows and gives the paragraph a single clear direction.

### Enunciado
Which topic sentence makes the strongest argumentative claim?

### Opciones

  <!-- feedback: No. The hedging in 'might be discussed' removes the position entirely, and a paragraph built on this sentence could go in any direction. -->

### Enunciado
Which pair of sentences keeps the voice of the writer consistent in an opinion paragraph?

### Opciones
- [ ] A) I believe students should choose freely. Others argue that the opposite is more realistic.
  <!-- feedback: No. The paragraph changes voice in the second sentence and hands the argument over to other people instead of defending the writer's view. -->

An opinion paragraph keeps one voice throughout, normally the first person singular or plural. Switching to a report, to an unnamed group or to the passive voice makes it unclear whose position is being defended and weakens the paragraph.

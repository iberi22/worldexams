**EJE:** Sintaxis
**Expected_Success:** 0.85
**Contexto:** Lucia asked her brother: Are you coming to the concert tonight?

### Enunciado
Lucia asked her brother if he ___ to the concert that night.

### Opciones
- [ ] A) comes
  <!-- feedback: No. 'comes' keeps the present simple and ignores the past tense of the reporting verb 'asked', which normally triggers the backshift. -->
- [x] B) was coming
  <!-- feedback: Correct! Present continuous 'is coming' becomes past continuous 'was coming', and the time expression 'that night' is adjusted for the report. -->
- [ ] C) has come
  <!-- feedback: No. 'has come' is present perfect and would claim the concert visit had already happened at the time of the question. -->
- [ ] D) will come
  <!-- feedback: No. 'will come' keeps a forward reference and turns the question into a request rather than an enquiry about his intentions. -->

### Explicacion Pedagogica
A reported question keeps the interrogative meaning of the original but is no longer a question in punctuation. When the reporting verb is past, the auxiliary shifts back and the word order of the direct question is replaced by the normal subject and verb order of a statement.

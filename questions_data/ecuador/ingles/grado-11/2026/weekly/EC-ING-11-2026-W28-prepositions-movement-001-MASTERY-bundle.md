**Contexto:** The bus left the terminal and went towards the coast along the new highway.

### Enunciado
The bus went ___ the coast along the new highway.

### Opciones
- [ ] A) into
  <!-- feedback: No. 'Into' marks movement from outside to inside a space, and no interior place is mentioned in the sentence. -->
- [x] B) towards
  <!-- feedback: Correct! 'Towards' names the direction of a movement, and the sentence describes the bus approaching the coast rather than arriving inside it. -->
- [ ] C) through
  <!-- feedback: No. 'Through' marks passage from one side of something to the other, and it does not fit the destination named here. -->
- [ ] D) across
  <!-- feedback: No. 'Across' would place the movement over a surface or from one side to the other, which the sentence does not describe. -->

### Explicacion Pedagogica
Direction prepositions divide into those that name a goal, such as to and towards, and those that name a route, such as through, across and along. Choosing between them depends on whether the sentence names a destination or a path.

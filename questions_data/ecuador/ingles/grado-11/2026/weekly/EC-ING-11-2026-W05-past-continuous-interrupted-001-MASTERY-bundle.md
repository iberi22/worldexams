## Question 1 [D3]
**ID:** EC-ING-11-2026-W05-past-continuous-interrupted-001-MASTERY-v1
**Bloom:** Remember
**EJE:** Gramatica
**Expected_Success:** 0.80
**Contexto:** At eight last night we were watching a documentary about the Andes when the power went out.

### Enunciado
Which option uses the past continuous correctly for the interrupted background action?

### Opciones
- [x] A) At eight last night we were watching a documentary when the power went out.
  <!-- feedback: Correct! 'Were watching' is the past continuous of a plural subject, and it sets up the background that 'went out' interrupts. -->
- [ ] B) At eight last night we watched a documentary when the power went out.
  <!-- feedback: 'Watched' is the past simple of a finished action, but the documentary was still in progress when the blackout began. -->
- [ ] C) At eight last night we are watching a documentary when the power went out.
  <!-- feedback: 'Are watching' is a present continuous; the situation described took place last night, so the tense must be past. -->
- [ ] D) At eight last night we was watching a documentary when the power went out.
  <!-- feedback: 'Was' agrees with a singular subject, and 'we' is plural, so the plural auxiliary 'were' is required. -->

### Explicacion Pedagogica
The past continuous is formed with 'was' or 'were' plus the -ing form of the verb. It describes an action in progress at a point in the past, usually as the background of a shorter event: I was cooking when he arrived. The two actions share the same past moment but differ in duration.

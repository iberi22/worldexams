**Contexto:** Practica de futuro continuo, Quito, grado 11.

### Enunciado
Choose the sentence that correctly uses the future continuous.

### Opciones
- [ ] A) This time tomorrow she will fly to Madrid.
  <!-- feedback: That describes the action from the start, not from the middle. -->
- [x] B) This time tomorrow she will be flying to Madrid.
  <!-- feedback: 'Will be flying' places her in the middle of the action tomorrow. -->
- [ ] C) This time tomorrow she flies to Madrid.
  <!-- feedback: The simple present is not the future continuous form. -->
- [ ] D) This time tomorrow she would flying to Madrid.
  <!-- feedback: 'Would' cannot be followed by a gerund in this structure. -->

### Explicacion Pedagogica
'Will be' mas gerundio describe una accion en curso en un momento futuro. Conviene practicarlo con otros ejemplos antes del examen.

### Enunciado
My sister ___ the same score as me in the mathematics test.

### Opciones
- [ ] A) has got
  <!-- feedback: No. 'has got' is the present perfect and would place the test in an open period, while the sentence refers to a completed test. -->
- [ ] B) was getting
  <!-- feedback: No. 'was getting' is the past continuous and describes an activity in progress, not the result of finishing a test. -->
- [x] C) got
  <!-- feedback: Correct! 'as ... as' expresses equality between two subjects, and the finite verb is simply the past form 'got' with no comparative ending. -->

### Explicacion Pedagogica

### Enunciado
Choose the modal that expresses a formal requirement in official rules.

### Opciones
- [x] A) All visitors must register at the front desk.
  <!-- feedback: Correct! 'Must' expresses a requirement that comes from outside the speaker, and it is followed by the base form. -->
- [ ] B) All visitors must to register at the front desk.
  <!-- feedback: 'Must to' adds an infinitive marker that never follows a modal verb in English. -->
- [ ] C) All visitors must registering at the front desk.
  <!-- feedback: 'Must registering' needs the base form after the modal, so the correct form is 'must register'. -->

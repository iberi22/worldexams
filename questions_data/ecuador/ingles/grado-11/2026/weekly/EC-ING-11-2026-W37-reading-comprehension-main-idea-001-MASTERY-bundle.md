**Contexto:** Passage: The town of Pumotauro replaced its diesel pumps with electric ones in 2019. Five years later the council published the figures: fuel consumption in the village fell by thirty per cent, and the money saved paid for two new classes of recycling. Not every household agreed. Older residents complained that the change was imposed without consultation, and two businesses said the new system doubled their costs. The council answered that the savings would grow each year and defended the decision in public meetings held throughout 2019.

### Enunciado
What is the main idea of the passage?

### Opciones
- [ ] A) Older residents were opposed to every change in their village.
  <!-- feedback: No. That claim goes beyond the text: only some older residents objected, and only to this particular change. -->
- [x] B) A town changed to electric pumps and faced both savings and opposition.
  <!-- feedback: Correct! This captures the change, the measured benefit and the disagreement, which together make up the whole passage. -->

### Explicacion Pedagogica

## Question 20 [D4]

**EJE:** Comprension lectora

**Contexto:** Passage: Two schools sent the same number of students to the same science fair and won the same number of prizes. One school had run practice sessions every week for a term; the other had held two sessions before the fair. A researcher who looked at the results concluded that the total hours of preparation, not the number of students or the quality of the equipment, matched the prizes won. The conclusion was later checked on three more schools and held in each case, though the sample was still small enough to be treated with care.

### Enunciado
Which option best captures the claim the passage defends?

### Opciones
- [ ] A) Winning a science fair depends mostly on the number of students a school enters.
  <!-- feedback: No. The two schools entered the same number of students and won the same number of prizes, so that factor is ruled out. -->
- [x] B) Preparation hours explain the results better than the size of the school's entry.
  <!-- feedback: Correct! It names the factor the comparison isolated and the one it ruled out. -->
- [ ] C) Practice sessions before a fair have no measurable effect.
  <!-- feedback: No. The passage reports that the schools with more preparation hours won more prizes, which is a measurable effect. -->
- [ ] D) The quality of the equipment used in science fairs explains the prizes.
  <!-- feedback: No. The passage states that the conclusion concerned preparation hours rather than the equipment. -->

### Explicacion Pedagogica
A passage built around a controlled comparison reaches its main claim by naming the variable that explains the outcome and the ones it excludes. An answer must keep the comparison in place, because the claim has no meaning without it.

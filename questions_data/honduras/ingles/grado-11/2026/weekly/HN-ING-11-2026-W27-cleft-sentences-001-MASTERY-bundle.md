### Enunciado

### Opciones
- [x] A) that
  <!-- feedback: Correct! 'It was ... that' is the standard cleft pattern and it focuses the heavy traffic. -->
- [ ] B) which
  <!-- feedback: 'Which' can be used in a cleft about a person, but the subject here is a situation, not a person. -->

  <!-- feedback: 'What' introduces a relative clause about a thing, not the focus of a cleft sentence. -->

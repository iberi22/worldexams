**Contexto:** The present simple becomes the past simple in reported speech.

### Enunciado
"I live in Comayagua," she said. In other words, she said that she ___ in Comayagua.

### Opciones
- [x] A) lived
  <!-- feedback: Correct! When the reporting verb is in the past, the present simple of the original shifts to the past simple. -->
- [ ] B) lives
  <!-- feedback: Lives would keep the original present tense, which only happens when the reporting verb is also present. -->
- [ ] C) has lived
  <!-- feedback: The present perfect would suggest the living is relevant to now, and the shift from a past reporting verb requires the past simple. -->
- [ ] D) is living
  <!-- feedback: A present continuous would be wrong because the shift applies to the tense of the original clause. -->

### Explicacion Pedagogica
The rule of backshift: when the reporting verb is in the past, the tense of the direct sentence moves one step back. Present simple becomes past simple.

### Opciones

  <!-- feedback: Correct! The causative have plus object plus bare infinitive expresses an arrangement the speaker made. -->

  <!-- feedback: The object pronoun must come before the bare infinitive, so the word order here is wrong. -->

  <!-- feedback: Make plus object plus bare infinitive is possible, but the past of make is made and the sentence is about an arrangement. -->

**Expected_Success:** 0.80
**Contexto:** A student writes a short article for the school newspaper about school routines.

### Enunciado
Which sentence best introduces an opinion in a short article?

### Opciones
- [x] A) In my view, homework should be reduced.
  <!-- feedback: Correct! 'In my view' clearly marks a personal position, which is what an opinion paragraph requires. -->
- [ ] B) The weather was warm in March.
  <!-- feedback: 'The weather was warm' states a fact and expresses no opinion. -->
- [ ] C) Homework is a form of study.
  <!-- feedback: 'Homework is a form of study' is a definition and takes no position. -->
- [ ] D) The teacher gave homework on Monday.
  <!-- feedback: 'The teacher gave homework' reports an event and supports no argument. -->

### Explicacion Pedagogica
An opinion must be signalled as an opinion. A phrase such as 'in my view' or 'I believe' tells the reader that a position follows.

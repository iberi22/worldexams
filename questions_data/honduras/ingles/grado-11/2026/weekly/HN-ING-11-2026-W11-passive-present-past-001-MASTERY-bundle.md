### Opciones
- [x] D) would
  <!-- feedback: Correct! In a reported passive, the first auxiliary shifts from will to would, while be finished is untouched. -->
- [ ] A) will
  <!-- feedback: Keeping will would ignore the past tense of the reporting verb he said. -->

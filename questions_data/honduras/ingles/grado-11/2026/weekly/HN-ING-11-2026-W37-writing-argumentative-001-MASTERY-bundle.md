**Contexto:** A student receives an assignment to write an argumentative essay of five paragraphs.

### Enunciado
Which structure is most appropriate for an argumentative essay?

### Opciones
- [x] A) An introduction with the position, body paragraphs with evidence, and a conclusion.
  <!-- feedback: Correct! An argument needs a claim, support and a closing statement of the result. -->
- [ ] B) A single paragraph that states the opinion without explanation.
  <!-- feedback: A single unsupported sentence is an assertion, not an argument. -->
- [ ] C) A narrative that recounts a personal experience in order.
  <!-- feedback: A personal narrative is a mode of narration, not a structure of argument. -->
- [ ] D) A list of definitions with no position taken.
  <!-- feedback: A list of definitions takes no position and supports no claim. -->

### Explicacion Pedagogica
The structure of an argument is fixed: claim, development with evidence, and a conclusion that follows from what was argued.

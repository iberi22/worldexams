## Question 1 [D3]
**ID:** ES-ING-11-2026-W05-modal-verbs-ability-obligation-001-MASTERY-bundle-v1
**Bloom:** Remember
**EJE:** Morfosintaxis
**Expected_Success:** 0.80
**Contexto:** A student knows how to swim very well.

### Enunciado
She ___ swim very well because she practises every day. Choose the correct modal.

### Opciones
- [x] A) can
  <!-- feedback: Can expresses ability, and swimming well is the ability this sentence describes. -->
- [ ] B) must
  <!-- feedback: Must expresses obligation or strong necessity, not the ability to swim. -->
- [ ] C) should
  <!-- feedback: Should gives advice or a recommendation, not the physical ability to swim. -->
- [ ] D) may
  <!-- feedback: May expresses possibility or permission, not the ability to swim well. -->

### Explicacion Pedagogica
Ability in the present is expressed with can plus the bare infinitive: she can swim. Must, should and may do not describe ability.

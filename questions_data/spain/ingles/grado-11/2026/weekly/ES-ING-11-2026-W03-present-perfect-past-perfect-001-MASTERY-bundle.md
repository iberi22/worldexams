### Enunciado
She ___ already finished her homework. Choose the correct form.

### Opciones
- [x] A) has
  <!-- feedback: Has is correct: the third person singular she takes has plus the past participle finished. -->
- [ ] B) have
  <!-- feedback: Have is used with I, you, we and they, but the subject she needs has. -->
- [ ] C) had
  <!-- feedback: Had is the past perfect and would point to an action before another past action, not to this present result. -->
- [ ] D) having
  <!-- feedback: Having is a participle and cannot act as the finite auxiliary of this sentence. -->

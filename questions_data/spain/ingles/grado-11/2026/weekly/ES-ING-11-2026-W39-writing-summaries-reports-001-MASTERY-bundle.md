What is the best definition of a summary?

### Opciones
- [ ] A) A short text that retells the main ideas of a longer text in your own words.
  <!-- feedback: Correct! A short text that retells the main ideas in your own words captures summarizing because it is brief, objective, focused on key points and avoids unnecessary details. -->

  <!-- feedback: The option about a long copy of every sentence fails because copying everything in the same order keeps all details and repetitions instead of shortening the text to its main ideas. -->

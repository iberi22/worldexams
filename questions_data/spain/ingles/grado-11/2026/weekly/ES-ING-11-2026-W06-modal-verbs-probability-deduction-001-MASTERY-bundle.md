**Contexto:** A teacher explains the meaning of common English modals to the class.

### Enunciado
Choose the meaning that the modal verb must expresses when a speaker uses it for deduction.

### Opciones
- [x] A) Logical certainty that something is true
  <!-- feedback: Correct. Must expresses a conclusion drawn from strong evidence, so the speaker feels certain the idea is true. -->
- [ ] B) Weak possibility that may never happen
  <!-- feedback: Incorrect. Weak possibility belongs to might or may, while must reports a firm and confident conclusion. -->
- [ ] C) Prohibition of an action by a rule
  <!-- feedback: Incorrect. Prohibition needs the negative form must not, whereas this meaning of must is affirmative. -->
- [ ] D) A promise about a future action
  <!-- feedback: Incorrect. Promises are made with will or shall, and must plays no role in promising. -->

### Explicacion Pedagogica
Must shows that the speaker draws a firm conclusion from clear evidence, as in the common pattern must be or must have.

**EJE:** Gramatica

**Contexto:** Look at the departure board at a busy railway station before a long trip.

### Enunciado
Which preposition correctly completes the sentence: "The 8:15 train is leaving ____ the station."

### Opciones
- [ ] A) to
  <!-- feedback: 'to' marks a destination or an arrival, so the sentence would say the train is arriving somewhere, which is the opposite of what the board announces. -->
- [x] B) from
  <!-- feedback: Correct! 'from' marks the point where a movement begins, and the station is where the train starts its journey. -->
- [ ] C) off
  <!-- feedback: 'off' also marks a departure, but it is used with vehicles a person boards by hand, as in 'get off the bus', and not with a train leaving a station. -->
- [ ] D) across
  <!-- feedback: 'across' describes crossing over a surface from one side to the other, as in 'walk across the road', so it does not fit a train and a station. -->

### Explicacion Pedagogica
Prepositions of movement divide into two families: 'to', 'into' and 'onto' point at a destination, while 'from', 'out of', 'off' and 'away from' point at a starting point. 'from' is the standard preposition for the place a vehicle or person begins its journey, and it never carries the idea of crossing or boarding.

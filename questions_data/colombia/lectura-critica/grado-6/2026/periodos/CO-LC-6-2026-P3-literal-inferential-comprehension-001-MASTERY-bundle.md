# Bundle Mastery: Literal and Inferential Comprehension

This bundle covers literal and inferential reading comprehension: extracting explicit information and drawing inferences from texts.

**ID:** `CO-LC-6-2026-P3-literal-inferential-comprehension-001-MASTERY-v1`
**Bloom:** Remember
**ICFES:** Comunicación y Representación
**Context:** In Bogotá, students read: 'The boy cried because his dog ran away.'

### Enunciado
The boy cried. Is this literal or inferential?

### Options
- [ ] A) Inferential <!-- feedback: No, it's directly stated. -->
- [x] B) Literal <!-- feedback: Correcto. -->
- [ ] C) Critical <!-- feedback: No. -->
- [ ] D) Invisible <!-- feedback: No. -->

### Explicación Pedagógica
Literal comprehension = information directly stated in the text.

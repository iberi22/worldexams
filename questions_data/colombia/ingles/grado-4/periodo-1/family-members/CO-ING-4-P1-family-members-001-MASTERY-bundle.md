# Bundle Mastery: Family Members (CEFR A1)

Este bundle evalúa el vocabulario básico de los miembros de la familia en inglés: madre, padre, hermanos, abuelos y la forma de describir relaciones familiares. Nivel A1.

---

## Question 1 [D1]

**ID:** `CO-ING-4-P1-family-members-001-MASTERY-v1`
**Bloom:** Remember
**ICFES:** Lingüística

### Enunciado
Your **mother** is also called your ________.

### Options
- [ ] A) father <!-- feedback: Incorrect. Father is "padre". -->
- [ ] B) uncle <!-- feedback: Incorrect. Uncle is "tío". -->
- [x] C) mom <!-- feedback: Correct! "Mom" is another word for mother. -->
- [ ] D) aunt <!-- feedback: Incorrect. Aunt is "tía". -->

### Explicación Pedagógica
Mother and mom both mean "mamá" or "madre" in Spanish. "Mom" is more informal and commonly used in daily conversation.

### Enunciado
Your parents' parents are your ________.

### Options
- [ ] A) cousins <!-- feedback: Incorrect. Cousins are children of your aunts/uncles. -->
- [x] B) grandparents <!-- feedback: Correct! The parents of your parents are your grandparents (abuelos). -->
- [ ] C) siblings <!-- feedback: Incorrect. Siblings are brothers and sisters. -->
- [ ] D) neighbors <!-- feedback: Incorrect. Neighbors are people who live near you. -->

### Explicación Pedagógica
Grandparents (abuelos) are the parents of your parents. Your mother's parents and father's parents are both your grandparents. Grandfather = abuelo, Grandmother = abuela.

Look at the sentence: "This is my _________, Laura. She is my father's sister."

### Options
- [x] A) aunt <!-- feedback: Correct! Your father's sister is your aunt (tía). -->

## Question 7 [D4]

**ID:** `CO-ING-4-P1-family-members-001-MASTERY-v7`
**Bloom:** Apply
**ICFES:** Lingüística

### Enunciado
Carlos is describing his family. Which sentence is CORRECT?

### Options
- [ ] A) I has one sister. <!-- feedback: Incorrect. The verb should be "have", not "has" with "I". -->
- [ ] B) My sister name is Ana. <!-- feedback: Incorrect. It should be "My sister's name" (possessive). -->
- [x] C) My sister's name is Ana. <!-- feedback: Correct! The possessive form uses an apostrophe + s. -->
- [ ] D) My sister name Ana. <!-- feedback: Incorrect. Missing verb "is" and possessive. -->

### Explicación Pedagógica
To show possession in English, we use 's (apóstrofe + s). "My sister's name" = "el nombre de mi hermana". Also remember: "I have" not "I has".

**Bloom:** Apply
**ICFES:** Lingüística

### Enunciado
Read the family tree:
- Tom and Ana are married.
- Tom and Ana have two children: a boy named Luke and a girl named Emma.

Who is Emma to Tom?

### Options
- [ ] A) Tom's wife <!-- feedback: Incorrect. Tom's wife is Ana. -->
- [ ] B) Tom's mother <!-- feedback: Incorrect. Tom is her father. -->
- [x] C) Tom's daughter <!-- feedback: Correct! Emma is the daughter of Tom. -->
- [ ] D) Tom's sister <!-- feedback: Incorrect. Tom is Emma's father, not brother. -->

### Explicación Pedagógica
Family relationships from a family tree: Tom is the father, Ana is the mother. Luke is their son (hijo) and Emma is their daughter (hija).

### Enunciado
Read this description and choose the correct family diagram:

"I have a big family. I live with my mom, dad, and little brother. My grandparents live next door. My aunt and uncle visit every Sunday with my two cousins."

How many people are in the speaker's **immediate family** (who live with them)?

### Options
- [ ] A) 5 people <!-- feedback: Incorrect. The grandparents don't live with them. -->
- [x] B) 4 people <!-- feedback: Correct! The speaker + mom + dad + little brother = 4. -->
- [ ] C) 8 people <!-- feedback: Incorrect. That's counting extended family too. -->

### Explicación Pedagógica
Immediate family (familia nuclear) = people who live together. Here: speaker, mom, dad, little brother = 4. Extended family (familia extendida) = grandparents, aunt, uncle, cousins.

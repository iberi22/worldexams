# Bundle Mastery: Classroom Objects (CEFR A1)

Este bundle evalúa el vocabulario básico de objetos del salón de clase en inglés: útiles escolares, muebles del aula, y órdenes simples relacionadas con la clase. Nivel A1.

---

## Question 1 [D1]

**ID:** `CO-ING-4-P1-classroom-objects-001-MASTERY-v1`
**Bloom:** Remember
**ICFES:** Lingüística

### Enunciado
What is this? You write with it in your notebook.

### Options
- [ ] A) Eraser <!-- feedback: Incorrect. An eraser is used to clean mistakes. -->
- [ ] B) Ruler <!-- feedback: Incorrect. A ruler is used to measure or draw straight lines. -->
- [x] C) Pencil <!-- feedback: Correct! You write with a pencil. -->
- [ ] D) Backpack <!-- feedback: Incorrect. A backpack is used to carry things. -->

### Explicación Pedagógica
A pencil (lápiz) is an object used for writing or drawing. Other writing tools include pen (bolígrafo) and marker (marcador).

## Question 6 [D3]

**ID:** `CO-ING-4-P1-classroom-objects-001-MASTERY-v6`
**Bloom:** Apply
**ICFES:** Lingüística

### Enunciado
Mateo wants to borrow a pencil sharpener. What does he say?

### Options
- [ ] A) I have a sharpener. <!-- feedback: Incorrect. That means he has one, not that he needs to borrow. -->
- [ ] B) The sharpener is blue. <!-- feedback: Incorrect. He is describing it, not asking. -->
- [x] C) Can I borrow your sharpener? <!-- feedback: Correct! This is the polite way to borrow something. -->
- [ ] D) This is a sharpener. <!-- feedback: Incorrect. That is identifying the object, not asking. -->

### Explicación Pedagógica
To borrow something in English, we say "Can I borrow your [object]?" (¿Puedo tomar prestado tu...?). It is polite to ask before using someone else's things.

### Enunciado
Complete the sentence: The students sit on their ___________ and write on their ___________.

### Options
- [ ] A) chairs / desks <!-- feedback: Correct! Students sit on chairs and write on desks. -->
- [ ] B) desks / chairs <!-- feedback: Incorrect. You sit on chairs, not desks. -->
- [ ] C) boards / chairs <!-- feedback: Incorrect. Students don't sit on boards. -->

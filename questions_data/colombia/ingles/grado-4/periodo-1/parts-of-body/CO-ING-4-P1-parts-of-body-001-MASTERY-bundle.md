# Bundle Mastery: Parts of the Body (CEFR A1)

Este bundle evalúa el vocabulario básico de las partes del cuerpo en inglés: cabeza, brazos, piernas, manos, pies, cara y descripciones simples usando "have/has". Nivel A1.

---

## Question 1 [D1]

**ID:** `CO-ING-4-P1-parts-of-body-001-MASTERY-v1`
**Bloom:** Remember
**ICFES:** Lingüística

### Enunciado
You use your ________ to see.

### Options
- [ ] A) ears <!-- feedback: Incorrect. You use ears to hear. -->
- [x] B) eyes <!-- feedback: Correct! You see with your eyes (ojos). -->
- [ ] C) mouth <!-- feedback: Incorrect. You use your mouth to eat and speak. -->
- [ ] D) nose <!-- feedback: Incorrect. You use your nose to smell. -->

### Explicación Pedagógica
Parts of the face and their functions: eyes (ojos) = see/ver, ears (orejas) = hear/oír, nose (nariz) = smell/oler, mouth (boca) = eat/comer and speak/hablar.

## Question 5 [D3]

**ID:** `CO-ING-4-P1-parts-of-body-001-MASTERY-v5`
**Bloom:** Apply
**ICFES:** Lingüística

### Enunciado
Complete the sentence: "I ________ two arms and two legs."

### Options
- [ ] A) has <!-- feedback: Incorrect. "Has" is used with he/she/it, not "I". -->
- [x] B) have <!-- feedback: Correct! "I have" is correct for describing body parts. -->
- [ ] C) am <!-- feedback: Incorrect. "I am" is used with adjectives, not possession. -->
- [ ] D) is <!-- feedback: Incorrect. "Is" is used with he/she/it. -->

### Explicación Pedagógica
"I have" (yo tengo) is used to describe the body parts you possess. "He/she has" (él/ella tiene) is used for other people. Example: "I have blue eyes. She has brown eyes."

## Question 10 [D5]

**ID:** `CO-ING-4-P1-parts-of-body-001-MASTERY-v10`
**Bloom:** Analyze
**ICFES:** Pragmática

### Enunciado
Which sentence is **correct**?

### Options
- [x] A) She has long hair and green eyes. <!-- feedback: Correct! The grammar and word order are right. -->
- [ ] B) She have long hair and green eyes. <!-- feedback: Incorrect. "She" needs "has", not "have". -->
- [ ] C) She has hair long and eyes green. <!-- feedback: Incorrect. In English, adjectives come before nouns. -->
- [ ] D) She has a long hair and a green eyes. <!-- feedback: Incorrect. "Hair" and "eyes" are uncountable/plural, don't use "a". -->

### Explicación Pedagógica
Correct English: adjective + noun order (long hair, green eyes not "hair long"), correct verb for he/she/it (has not have), and no article "a" with plural or uncountable nouns.

## Question 1 [D3]

**ID:** `CO-ING-4-2026-P1-descriptions-001-MASTERY-v1`
**Bloom:** [Apply]
**ICFES:** [Comunicación y representación]
**Context:** Talking about your best friend in school.

### Enunciado
My friend Sofia is very ________. She always shares her toys and helps everyone.

### Options
- [ ] A) mean <!-- feedback: Incorrect. "Mean" is the opposite of sharing and helping. Sofia is the opposite of mean. -->
- [x] B) kind <!-- feedback: Correct. "Kind" describes a person who is friendly, generous, and considerate, like Sofia. -->
- [ ] C) angry <!-- feedback: Incorrect. "Angry" describes a feeling of displeasure, but the sentence describes a permanent positive personality trait. -->
- [ ] D) tall <!-- feedback: Incorrect. "Tall" describes physical height, but the sentence is about Sofia's behavior (sharing and helping). -->

### Explicación Pedagógica
This question evaluates the use of personality adjectives in context. The correct answer "kind" matches the description of sharing and helping. Common errors include choosing opposites or adjectives that describe physical appearance instead of personality.

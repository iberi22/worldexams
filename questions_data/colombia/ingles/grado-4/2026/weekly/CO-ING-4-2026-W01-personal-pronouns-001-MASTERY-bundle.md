## Question 1 [D2]

**ID:** `CO-ING-4-2026-W01-personal-pronouns-001-v1`
**Bloom:** Remember
**ICFES:** Competencia Lingüistica
**Context:** Talking about yourself

### Enunciado
When you talk about yourself in English, which pronoun do you use?

### Options
- [ ] A) You <!-- feedback: Incorrect. "You" is used for the person you are talking to. -->
- [x] B) I <!-- feedback: Correct! "I" (always capitalized) is used to talk about yourself. -->
- [ ] C) He <!-- feedback: Incorrect. "He" is used for a boy or man. -->
- [ ] D) She <!-- feedback: Incorrect. "She" is used for a girl or woman. -->

### Explicacion Pedagogica
Los pronombres personales reemplazan al nombre. "I" (Yo) es el primer pronombre que aprendemos y siempre se escribe en mayuscula en ingles.

## Question 4 [D3]

**ID:** `CO-ING-4-2026-W01-personal-pronouns-004-v1`
**Bloom:** Understand
**ICFES:** Competencia Lingüistica
**Context:** Talking about an animal or object

### Enunciado
"The cat is black."
Which pronoun replaces **The cat**?

### Options
- [ ] A) He <!-- feedback: Incorrect. Although sometimes used for pets, the general rule for animals is "It". -->
- [ ] B) She <!-- feedback: Incorrect. Same as "He". -->
- [x] C) It <!-- feedback: Correct! We use "It" for animals, objects, and places in the singular. -->
- [ ] D) You <!-- feedback: Incorrect. You are not talking to the cat, you are talking about it. -->

### Explicacion Pedagogica
El pronombre "It" es muy importante en ingles porque no tiene una traduccion exacta al español en todos los casos, pero se usa para todo lo que no sea una persona (cosas, animales, clima).

## Question 6 [D4]

**ID:** `CO-ING-4-2026-W01-personal-pronouns-006-v1`
**Bloom:** Understand
**ICFES:** Competencia Lingüistica
**Context:** Talking about a group of others

### Enunciado
"The teachers are in the school."
Which pronoun replaces **The teachers**?

### Options
- [ ] A) We <!-- feedback: Incorrect. You are not one of the teachers in this sentence. -->
- [ ] B) You <!-- feedback: Incorrect. This would be used if you were talking directly to them. -->
- [x] C) They <!-- feedback: Correct! "They" refers to a group of people, animals, or things that does not include you. -->
- [ ] D) It <!-- feedback: Incorrect. "It" is only for one thing. "They" is for many things. -->

### Explicacion Pedagogica
"They" (Ellos/Ellas) es el plural de He, She e It. Se usa para cualquier grupo donde tu no estes incluido.

## Question 7 [D5]

**ID:** `CO-ING-4-2026-W01-personal-pronouns-007-v1`
**Bloom:** Apply
**ICFES:** Competencia Lingüistica
**Context:** Talking directly to someone

### Enunciado
When you speak directly to your teacher, you say: "________ are a good teacher."

### Options
- [ ] A) I <!-- feedback: Incorrect. You are talking about the teacher, not yourself. -->
- [x] B) You <!-- feedback: Correct! "You" is used for the person or people you are speaking to directly. -->
- [ ] C) He <!-- feedback: Incorrect. You use "He" when talking ABOUT a man, not TO him. -->
- [ ] D) They <!-- feedback: Incorrect. You are talking to one person. -->

### Explicacion Pedagogica
"You" (Tu / Usted / Ustedes) se usa tanto para el singular como para el plural. Es el pronombre de la "segunda persona".

### Options

**ICFES:** Competencia Lingüistica
**Context:** Filling the blanks

### Enunciado
Choose the correct pronouns for the sequence:
1. **The sun** is hot. (_____)
2. **Valentina and Mateo** are friends. (_____)

### Options
- [ ] A) He / We <!-- feedback: Incorrect. The sun is not a person. -->
- [ ] B) It / We <!-- feedback: Incorrect. "Valentina and Mateo" does not include "I". -->

### Explicacion Pedagogica
Este ejercicio requiere identificar si el sujeto es una cosa singular (It) o un grupo de personas externas (They).

## Question 10 [D6]

**ID:** `CO-ING-4-2026-W01-personal-pronouns-010-v1`
**Bloom:** Apply
**ICFES:** Competencia Lingüistica
**Context:** Understanding "You" plural

### Enunciado
If you are talking to a group of children, which pronoun do you use to say "Ustedes"?

### Options
- [ ] A) We <!-- feedback: Incorrect. "We" includes you. -->
- [ ] B) They <!-- feedback: Incorrect. "They" is used when you talk ABOUT them, not TO them. -->
- [x] C) You <!-- feedback: Correct! In English, "You" is the same for one person or many people. -->
- [ ] D) I <!-- feedback: Incorrect. "I" is only for yourself. -->

### Explicacion Pedagogica
A diferencia del español (Tu/Ustedes), el ingles usa la misma palabra "You" para dirigirse a una o a varias personas. El contexto nos dice si es singular o plural.

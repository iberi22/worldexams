**Expected_Success:** 0.80
**Contexto:** Learning about telling the time in English class.

### Enunciado
It is 8:00. How do you say this?

### Opciones
- [x] A) It is eight o'clock <!-- feedback: 8:00 is spoken as 'It is eight o'clock', because the minutes are zero. -->
- [ ] B) It is eight minutes <!-- feedback: 8:00 has no minutes to name, so nothing is said after 'eight'. -->
- [ ] C) It is eight hours <!-- feedback: You do not say the time in hours like that; the hour is named once, as 'eight'. -->
- [ ] D) It is eight seconds <!-- feedback: Seconds are far too small a unit to describe a whole hour. -->

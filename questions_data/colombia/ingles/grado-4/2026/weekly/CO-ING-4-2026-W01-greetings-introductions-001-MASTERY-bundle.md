## Question 1 [D1]

**ID:** `CO-ING-4-2026-W01-greetings-introductions-001-MASTERY-v1`
**Bloom:** [Remember]
**ICFES:** [Comunicación y representación]
**Context:** Morning at school.

### Enunciado
It is 7:00 AM. You see your teacher. What do you say?

### Options
- [ ] A) Good night <!-- feedback: Incorrect. "Good night" is used when going to sleep, not in the morning. -->
- [x] B) Good morning <!-- feedback: Correct. "Good morning" is used from sunrise until noon. -->
- [ ] C) Good evening <!-- feedback: Incorrect. "Good evening" is used after 6:00 PM. -->
- [ ] D) Good bye <!-- feedback: Incorrect. "Good bye" is used when leaving, not arriving. -->

### Explicación Pedagógica
This question tests knowledge of greeting expressions based on time of day. "Good morning" (buenos días) is the correct greeting for 7:00 AM. Common errors include confusing greetings with farewells or using the wrong time-of-day expression.

## Question 9 [D5]

**ID:** `CO-ING-4-2026-W01-greetings-introductions-001-MASTERY-v9`

**ICFES:** [Comunicación y representación]

### Enunciado
Read the conversation:

**Maria:** "Good morning! I'm Maria."
**Pedro:** "________. I'm Pedro."
**Maria:** "________, Pedro!"
**Pedro:** "Good bye, Maria!"

Which words complete the conversation in the correct order?

### Options
- [ ] A) "Good bye" / "Hello" <!-- feedback: Incorrect. "Good bye" doesn't come after an introduction; it ends the conversation. -->

- [ ] C) "How are you" / "Good morning" <!-- feedback: Incorrect. "Good morning" was already used; repeating it doesn't fit. -->
- [ ] D) "Go away" / "I'm sorry" <!-- feedback: Incorrect. These are negative and don't match the polite tone. -->

### Explicación Pedagógica
This question tests the ability to sequence a basic conversation. After an introduction, "Nice to meet you" is the standard response. Before a farewell, a phrase like "See you later" fits naturally. Errors come from misordering conversational expressions.

## Question 10 [D5]

**ID:** `CO-ING-4-2026-W01-greetings-introductions-001-MASTERY-v10`
**Bloom:** [Analyze]
**ICFES:** [Comunicación y representación]
**Context:** Identifying the correct greeting for different times of day.

### Enunciado
Match the time with the correct greeting:

**8:00 AM** = ________
**2:00 PM** = ________
**8:00 PM** = ________

Which combination is correct?

### Options
- [ ] A) Good night / Good afternoon / Good morning <!-- feedback: Incorrect. 8:00 AM is morning, not night. -->
- [x] B) Good morning / Good afternoon / Good evening <!-- feedback: Correct. 8 AM = good morning, 2 PM = good afternoon, 8 PM = good evening. -->
- [ ] C) Good evening / Good morning / Good afternoon <!-- feedback: Incorrect. Each greeting is in the wrong time slot. -->
- [ ] D) Good bye / Good morning / Good night <!-- feedback: Incorrect. "Good bye" is not a time-based greeting at all. -->

### Explicación Pedagógica
This question tests understanding of time-based greetings in English. Good morning (before 12 PM), Good afternoon (12 PM - 6 PM), Good evening (after 6 PM). Good night is used only when saying goodbye at the end of the day or before sleeping. Errors typically involve confusing "good evening" with "good night" or misassigning time ranges.

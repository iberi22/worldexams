Este bundle es un repaso acumulativo de los temas W01-W04: saludos, presentaciones, alfabeto, deletreo, números 1-100, colores y adjetivos básicos. Nivel A1. Contexto colombiano.

## Question 1 [D2]

**ID:** `CO-ING-4-2026-W05-repaso-p1-001-MASTERY-v1`
**Bloom:** [Apply]
**ICFES:** [Comunicación y representación]
**Context:** Morning greeting + time.

### Enunciado
It is 7:30 AM. You see your friend at school. You say:

### Options
- [ ] A) Good afternoon <!-- feedback: Incorrect. 7:30 AM is morning, not afternoon. -->
- [x] B) Good morning <!-- feedback: Correct. 7:30 AM is in the morning, so "Good morning" is correct. -->
- [ ] C) Good evening <!-- feedback: Incorrect. 7:30 AM is morning, not evening. -->

### Explicación Pedagógica
This review question tests knowledge of time-based greetings. Before 12:00 PM, the correct greeting is "Good morning" (buenos días). This was covered in W01 (Greetings and Introductions).

## Question 8 [D4]

**ID:** `CO-ING-4-2026-W05-repaso-p1-001-MASTERY-v8`
**Bloom:** [Analyze]
**ICFES:** [Comunicación y representación]
**Context:** Adjective + color description.

### Enunciado
Complete the sentence:

"I have a ________ ________ umbrella."

(Opinion: nice, Color: blue)

### Options
- [ ] A) blue nice <!-- feedback: Incorrect. In English, opinion adjectives come before color adjectives. -->
- [x] B) nice blue <!-- feedback: Correct. Opinion (nice) + color (blue) + noun (umbrella). -->
- [ ] C) nice blue a <!-- feedback: Incorrect. "A" goes before the adjectives, not after. -->
- [ ] D) a blue nice <!-- feedback: Incorrect. "A" goes at the beginning, and opinion comes before color. -->

### Explicación Pedagógica
This review question tests adjective order from W04. The correct sequence is: article (a) + opinion (nice) + color (blue) + noun (umbrella). In Spanish, students often place color before opinion, so this reinforces the English pattern.

## Question 9 [D5]

**ID:** `CO-ING-4-2026-W05-repaso-p1-001-MASTERY-v9`
**Bloom:** [Analyze]
**ICFES:** [Comunicación y representación]
**Context:** Spelling and numbers combined.

### Enunciado
The teacher says: "I will spell a number. Listen and write the number."

**"F-I-F-T-Y"**

What is the number?

### Options
- [ ] A) 15 <!-- feedback: Incorrect. F-I-F-T-E-E-N is 15. The teacher spelled F-I-F-T-Y, which is different. -->
- [x] B) 50 <!-- feedback: Correct. F-I-F-T-Y spells "fifty", which is 50. -->
- [ ] C) 5 <!-- feedback: Incorrect. F-I-V-E is 5. The teacher said F-I-F-T-Y. -->
- [ ] D) 55 <!-- feedback: Incorrect. F-I-F-T-Y = 50. 55 would be "fifty-five". -->

### Explicación Pedagógica
This review question combines spelling (W02) with numbers (W03). Students must listen to the spelled letters F-I-F-T-Y, recognize it as the word "fifty", and know that fifty = 50. This is a common challenge because "fifteen" (15) sounds similar.

## Question 10 [D5]

**ID:** `CO-ING-4-2026-W05-repaso-p1-001-MASTERY-v10`
**Bloom:** [Analyze]
**ICFES:** [Comunicación y representación]
**Context:** Complete dialogue with multiple skills.

### Enunciado
Read the dialogue. Which option fills BOTH blanks correctly?

**You:** "Good morning! ________?"
**Friend:** "I'm nine. And you?"
**You:** "I'm nine too! Let's play!"

### Options
- [ ] A) What is your name <!-- feedback: Incorrect. "What is your name" asks for a name, but the friend answers "I'm nine" (age). -->
- [ ] B) Where are you from <!-- feedback: Incorrect. "Where are you from" asks about origin, but the friend answers with age. -->
- [x] C) How old are you <!-- feedback: Correct. "How old are you" asks about age, and "I'm nine" is the correct answer. -->
- [ ] D) How are you <!-- feedback: Incorrect. "How are you" asks about well-being. "I'm nine" is not a response to that. -->

### Explicación Pedagógica
This review question combines greetings (W01) with age numbers (W03). Students must understand that "How old are you?" (¿Cuántos años tienes?) is the question that elicits an age answer. Matching the question to the expected answer type is a key conversational skill.

# Weekly Pack W06: Work and Careers (B2)

This bundle explores the world of work and career paths, focusing on Modals of Deduction (must, might, could, can't) in the present and past, aligned with CEFR B2 level for Grade 9.

---

## Question 1 [D3-D4]
**ID:** CO-ING-9-2026-W06-work-and-careers-001-MASTERY-bundle-v1
**Bloom:** Remember
**ICFES:** Lingüística
**Expected_Success:** 0.85
**Contexto:** Guessing someone's job based on their clothes.

### Enunciado
He is wearing a white coat and a stethoscope. He ________ be a doctor.

### Opciones
- [x] A) must <!-- feedback: Correct! "Must" is used when we are almost certain that something is true based on evidence. -->
- [ ] B) might <!-- feedback: Incorrect. "Might" is for possibility, but here the evidence is very strong. -->
- [ ] C) can't <!-- feedback: Incorrect. "Can't" is used when we are sure something is impossible. -->
- [ ] D) shouldn't <!-- feedback: Incorrect. "Shouldn't" is for advice or expectation, not deduction. -->

### Explicacion Pedagogica
Usamos el modal *must* para hacer deducciones cuando estamos casi seguros de que algo es cierto basándonos en una evidencia clara (como el uniforme).

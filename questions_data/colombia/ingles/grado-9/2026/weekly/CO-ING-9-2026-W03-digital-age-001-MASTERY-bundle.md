# Weekly Pack W03: The Digital Age and Connectivity (B2)

This bundle explores how technology has changed our lives, focusing on the use of Past Perfect Simple and Continuous to sequence events in the past, aligned with CEFR B2 level for Grade 9.

---

## Question 1 [D3-D4]
**ID:** CO-ING-9-2026-W03-digital-age-001-MASTERY-bundle-v1
**Bloom:** Remember
**ICFES:** Lingüística
**Expected_Success:** 0.85
**Contexto:** Describing the sequence of using a computer.

### Enunciado
By the time the computer crashed, I ________ my essay for three hours.

### Opciones
- [ ] A) wrote <!-- feedback: Incorrect. We need a tense that shows an action in progress before another past action. -->
- [ ] B) had written <!-- feedback: Incorrect. This would mean the writing was finished before the crash, but the duration "for three hours" suggests progress. -->
- [x] C) had been writing <!-- feedback: Correct! Past Perfect Continuous shows an action that was ongoing up to a point in the past. -->
- [ ] D) have been writing <!-- feedback: Incorrect. This is Present Perfect Continuous, but the context is the past ("crashed"). -->

### Explicacion Pedagogica
El Pasado Perfecto Continuo (*had been writing*) se utiliza para expresar la duración de una acción que estaba en progreso hasta que ocurrió otro evento en el pasado (el fallo del ordenador).

## Question 7 [D7-D8]
**ID:** CO-ING-9-2026-W03-digital-age-001-MASTERY-bundle-v7
**Bloom:** Analyze
**ICFES:** Lingüística
**Expected_Success:** 0.55
**Contexto:** Comparing Past Perfect Simple and Continuous.

### Enunciado
Which sentence suggests that the digital project was **finished** before the deadline?

### Opciones
- [x] A) When the deadline arrived, Juan had already finished the website. <!-- feedback: Correct! Past Perfect Simple shows completion. -->
- [ ] B) When the deadline arrived, Juan had been working on the website. <!-- feedback: Incorrect. This implies he was still working on it. -->
- [ ] C) When the deadline arrived, Juan was finishing the website. <!-- feedback: Incorrect. This means he was in the middle of it. -->
- [ ] D) When the deadline arrived, Juan finished the website. <!-- feedback: Incorrect. This suggests he finished it at the same time or just after. -->

### Explicacion Pedagogica
El Pasado Perfecto Simple (*had finished*) enfatiza el resultado y la finalización, mientras que el Continuo (*had been working*) enfatiza el proceso y la duración.

## Question 10 [D9-D10]

**Bloom:** Evaluate
**ICFES:** Lingüística

**Contexto:** Finding an error in a text about the history of computers.

### Enunciado
Identify the error in this paragraph:
"Before the first personal computer **appeared** (A), scientists **had already been using** (B) large machines for calculations. These early computers **had occupied** (C) entire rooms and **had required** (D) a lot of electricity."

### Opciones
- [ ] A) appeared <!-- feedback: Incorrect. This is the correct past reference point. -->
- [ ] B) had already been using <!-- feedback: Incorrect. This correctly describes the ongoing use before the PC. -->
- [x] C) had occupied <!-- feedback: Incorrect. This should be Simple Past (*occupied*) because it describes a permanent state/fact of those machines, not a sequence of events. -->
- [ ] D) had required <!-- feedback: Incorrect. Similar to C, Simple Past (*required*) is better for general characteristics in the past. -->

### Explicacion Pedagogica
No todas las acciones en el pasado necesitan el Pasado Perfecto. Para descripciones de características o estados permanentes en el pasado, el *Simple Past* es la forma estándar.

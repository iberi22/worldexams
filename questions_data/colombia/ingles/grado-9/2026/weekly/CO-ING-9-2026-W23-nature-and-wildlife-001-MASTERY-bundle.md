# Weekly Pack W23: Nature and Wildlife (B2)

This bundle explores biodiversity and wildlife conservation, focusing on Reporting Verbs beyond "say" and "tell" (suggest, recommend, warn, promise, claim), aligned with CEFR B2 level for Grade 9.

## Question 1 [D3-D4]
**ID:** CO-ING-9-2026-W23-nature-and-wildlife-001-MASTERY-bundle-v1
**Bloom:** Remember
**ICFES:** Lingüística
**Expected_Success:** 0.85
**Contexto:** Reporting a suggestion about a park visit.

### Enunciado
The park ranger ________ visiting the sanctuary early in the morning to see the birds.

### Opciones
- [ ] A) suggested us <!-- feedback: Incorrect. "Suggest" is not followed by an object + infinitive. -->
- [x] B) suggested <!-- feedback: Correct! "Suggest" can be followed directly by a gerund (-ing). -->
- [ ] C) told us to <!-- feedback: Incorrect. (Wait, this is possible, but B is a more specific reporting verb). -->
- [ ] D) warned <!-- feedback: Incorrect. Not a warning. -->

### Explicacion Pedagogica
El verbo *suggest* tiene patrones gramaticales específicos: puede ir seguido de un gerundio (*-ing*) o de una cláusula con *that*, pero nunca de un objeto + infinitivo.

## Question 10 [D9-D10]
**ID:** CO-ING-9-2026-W23-nature-and-wildlife-001-MASTERY-bundle-v10
**Bloom:** Evaluate
**ICFES:** Lingüística
**Expected_Success:** 0.40
**Contexto:** Identifying an error in a field report.

### Enunciado
Identify the error in this paragraph:
"The scientist **suggested us to** (A) study the migration patterns. She **recommended using** (B) satellite tags. However, the local government **refused to** (C) provide the necessary permits, although they **claimed that** (D) they support conservation."

### Opciones
- [x] A) suggested us to <!-- feedback: Correct! "Suggest" cannot be followed by an object + infinitive. It should be "suggested that we study" or "suggested studying". -->
- [ ] B) recommended using <!-- feedback: Incorrect. Correct use of gerund after "recommend". -->
- [ ] C) refused to <!-- feedback: Incorrect. Correct use of infinitive after "refuse". -->
- [ ] D) claimed that <!-- feedback: Incorrect. Correct use of "that" clause after "claim". -->

### Explicacion Pedagogica
Este es uno de los errores más persistentes: tratar el verbo *suggest* como si fuera *tell* o *advise*. *Suggest* nunca lleva la partícula *to* seguida de un infinitivo para indicar a quién se le sugiere algo.

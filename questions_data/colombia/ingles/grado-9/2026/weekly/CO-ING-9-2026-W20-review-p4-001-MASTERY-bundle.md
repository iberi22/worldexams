## Question 7 [D7]
**ID:** CO-ING-9-2026-W20-review-p4-001-MASTERY-bundle-v7
**Bloom:** Analyze
**ICFES:** Lingüística
**Expected_Success:** 0.55
**Contexto:** Distinguishing cleft types.

### Enunciado
Which sentence puts the focus on the **person**?

### Opciones
- [ ] A) What she did was lead the protest. <!-- feedback: Incorrect. Focuses on the action. -->
- [x] B) It was she who led the protest. <!-- feedback: Correct! It-cleft focusing on the subject (she). -->
- [ ] C) All she did was lead the protest. <!-- feedback: Incorrect. Focuses on the limitation of the action. -->
- [ ] D) The protest was led by her. <!-- feedback: Incorrect. Passive voice, not a cleft sentence. -->

### Explicacion Pedagogica
Las oraciones con *It is/was... who...* son las más efectivas para resaltar la identidad de la persona responsable de una acción.

### Enunciado
Situation: Someone is using their phone loudly in a library.
Which wish expresses your **annoyance**?

### Opciones
- [ ] A) I wish they didn't use their phone. <!-- feedback: Incorrect. Describes a desired state. -->
- [x] B) I wish they would turn off their phone! <!-- feedback: Correct! "Would" expresses a desire for a change in someone's annoying behavior. -->

## Question 10 [D9]

**Bloom:** Evaluate
**ICFES:** Lingüística
**Expected_Success:** 0.40
**Contexto:** Identifying an error in a text about social change.

### Enunciado
Identify the error in this paragraph:
"If society **would have valued** (A) ethics more in the past, we **wouldn't be** (B) in this situation today. **What we need** (C) is a change in education. **The sooner** (D) we start, the better."

### Opciones
- [x] A) would have valued <!-- feedback: Correct! In the "if" clause of a conditional about the past, we use Past Perfect (*had valued*), never *would have*. -->
- [ ] B) wouldn't be <!-- feedback: Incorrect. Correct present result for a Mixed Conditional. -->
- [ ] C) What we need <!-- feedback: Incorrect. Correct use of Wh- cleft. -->
- [ ] D) The sooner <!-- feedback: Incorrect. Correct use of proportional comparative. -->

### Explicacion Pedagogica
Es fundamental erradicar el uso de *would* en la cláusula que contiene el *if* en los condicionales de segundo y tercer tipo.

# Weekly Pack W08: Social Issues and Human Rights (B2)

This bundle explores social challenges and human rights, focusing on First and Second Conditionals to discuss real and hypothetical situations, aligned with CEFR B2 level for Grade 9.

---

## Question 1 [D3-D4]
**ID:** CO-ING-9-2026-W08-social-issues-001-MASTERY-bundle-v1
**Bloom:** Remember
**ICFES:** Lingüística
**Expected_Success:** 0.85
**Contexto:** Discussing a possible future action to help a community.

### Enunciado
If we collect enough signatures, we ________ the proposal to the local government next week.

### Opciones
- [ ] A) presented
  <!-- feedback: Incorrect. We need a future form for a real possibility. -->
- [x] B) will present
  <!-- feedback: Correct! First Conditional (If + present simple, will + verb) for real possibilities. -->
- [ ] C) would present
  <!-- feedback: Incorrect. This is for hypothetical situations. -->
- [ ] D) present
  <!-- feedback: Incorrect. We need the future result in the main clause. -->

### Explicacion Pedagogica
El Primer Condicional se utiliza para hablar de situaciones reales o muy probables en el futuro.

### Enunciado

### Opciones
- [ ] A) knows / will be
  <!-- feedback: Incorrect. Mixed conditional. -->
- [x] B) knew / would be
  <!-- feedback: Correct! Second Conditional (If + past simple, would + verb) for hypothetical/unlikely situations. -->
- [ ] C) knows / would be
  <!-- feedback: Incorrect. Grammatically inconsistent. -->
- [ ] D) known / would be
  <!-- feedback: Incorrect. -->

### Explicacion Pedagogica
Usamos el Segundo Condicional para hablar de situaciones hipotéticas o imaginarias que no son ciertas en el presente.

# Weekly Pack W02: Healthy Lifestyles and Wellness (B2)

This bundle focuses on discussing health, fitness, and lifestyle choices using the contrast between Present Perfect and Past Simple, aligned with CEFR B2 level for Grade 9.

---

## Question 1 [D3]
**ID:** CO-ING-9-2026-W02-healthy-lifestyles-001-MASTERY-bundle-v1
**Bloom:** Remember
**ICFES:** Lingüística
**Expected_Success:** 0.85
**Contexto:** Talking about a personal achievement in sports.

### Enunciado
I ________ a marathon last year, and since then, I ________ to stay fit.

### Opciones
- [ ] A) have run / tried <!-- feedback: Incorrect. "Last year" requires Past Simple, and the ongoing effort requires Present Perfect. -->
- [x] B) ran / have tried <!-- feedback: Correct! Past Simple for a finished time ("last year") and Present Perfect for an action continuing to the present. -->
- [ ] C) run / try <!-- feedback: Incorrect. These are simple present forms, not suitable for past events or ongoing results. -->
- [ ] D) have run / have tried <!-- feedback: Incorrect. "Last year" cannot be used with Present Perfect in this context. -->

### Explicacion Pedagogica
Usamos el *Past Simple* (*ran*) para acciones completadas en un tiempo específico del pasado (*last year*). El *Present Perfect* (*have tried*) se usa para acciones que comenzaron en el pasado y tienen relevancia o continúan en el presente.

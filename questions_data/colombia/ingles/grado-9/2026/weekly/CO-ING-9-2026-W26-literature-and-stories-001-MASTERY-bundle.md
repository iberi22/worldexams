# Weekly Pack W26: Literature and Stories (B2)

This bundle explores the world of literature and storytelling, focusing on Narrative Tenses (Past Simple, Continuous, Perfect, and Perfect Continuous) to sequence events in a story, aligned with CEFR B2 level for Grade 9.

---

## Question 1 [D3]
**ID:** CO-ING-9-2026-W26-literature-and-stories-001-MASTERY-bundle-v1
**Bloom:** Remember
**ICFES:** Lingüística
**Expected_Success:** 0.85
**Contexto:** Setting the scene in a story.

### Enunciado
The sun ________ behind the mountains when the traveler finally reached the village.

### Opciones
- [ ] A) set <!-- feedback: Incorrect. We need a continuous action for the background. -->
- [x] B) was setting <!-- feedback: Correct! Past Continuous is used to describe the background situation or an action in progress when another action occurs. -->
- [ ] C) has set <!-- feedback: Incorrect. Present tense. -->
- [ ] D) had been setting <!-- feedback: Incorrect. (Possible but less common for a simple background state). -->

### Explicacion Pedagogica
En la narrativa, utilizamos el Pasado Continuo para describir el escenario o las acciones que estaban ocurriendo en el momento en que sucede el evento principal (*reached*).

## Question 7 [D7]
**ID:** CO-ING-9-2026-W26-literature-and-stories-001-MASTERY-bundle-v7
**Bloom:** Analyze
**ICFES:** Lingüística
**Expected_Success:** 0.55
**Contexto:** Distinguishing between Past Perfect Simple and Continuous.

### Enunciado
Which sentence implies that the author **finished** the book before the deadline?

### Opciones
- [x] A) When the deadline arrived, the author had written the book. <!-- feedback: Correct! Past Perfect Simple focuses on the completion and the result. -->
- [ ] B) When the deadline arrived, the author had been writing the book. <!-- feedback: Incorrect. This suggests he was still in the middle of writing it. -->
- [ ] C) When the deadline arrived, the author was writing the book. <!-- feedback: Incorrect. Action in progress. -->
- [ ] D) When the deadline arrived, the author wrote the book. <!-- feedback: Incorrect. Implies he wrote it at that exact moment. -->

### Explicacion Pedagogica
A nivel B2, es clave distinguir que el Pasado Perfecto Simple se enfoca en el hecho de haber terminado algo, mientras que el Continuo se enfoca en el tiempo invertido en la tarea.

## Question 10 [D9]
**ID:** CO-ING-9-2026-W26-literature-and-stories-001-MASTERY-bundle-v10
**Bloom:** Evaluate
**ICFES:** Lingüística
**Expected_Success:** 0.40
**Contexto:** Identifying an error in a short story excerpt.

### Enunciado
Identify the error in this paragraph:
"The detective arrived at the crime scene. It **had rained** (A) all night, so the ground was muddy. He **was noticing** (B) that someone **had broken** (C) the window. He **had been looking** (D) for clues for an hour when he found the key."

### Opciones
- [ ] A) had rained <!-- feedback: Incorrect. Correct use of past perfect. -->
- [x] B) was noticing <!-- feedback: Correct! "Notice" is a verb of perception that is usually used in Past Simple (*noticed*) for a sudden realization, not in continuous. -->
- [ ] C) had broken <!-- feedback: Incorrect. Correct use for an action before arrival. -->
- [ ] D) had been looking <!-- feedback: Incorrect. Correct use for duration before finding the key. -->

### Explicacion Pedagogica
Los verbos de estado o percepción como *notice*, *know* o *believe* raramente se usan en tiempos continuos en la narrativa estándar, a menos que se quiera enfatizar un proceso gradual.

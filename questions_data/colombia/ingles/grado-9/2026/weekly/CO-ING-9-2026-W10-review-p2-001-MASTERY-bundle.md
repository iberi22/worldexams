### Enunciado

### Opciones
- [ ] A) bought <!-- feedback: Incorrect. Missing backshift. -->
- [x] B) had bought <!-- feedback: Correct! Past Simple shifts to Past Perfect in Reported Speech. -->

### Explicacion Pedagogica

### Enunciado
Identify the error in this paragraph:
"Social media **is used** (A) by billions. Experts say that if we **would spend** (B) less time online, we **would be** (C) happier. They also told us **to find** (D) more offline hobbies."

### Opciones
- [ ] A) is used <!-- feedback: Incorrect. Correct Present Passive. -->
- [x] B) would spend <!-- feedback: Correct! In the "if" clause of a conditional, we use past simple (*spent*), not *would*. -->

- [ ] D) to find <!-- feedback: Incorrect. Correct reported request structure. -->

### Explicacion Pedagogica

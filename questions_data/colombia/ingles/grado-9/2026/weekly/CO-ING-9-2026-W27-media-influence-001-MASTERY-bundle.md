# Weekly Pack W27: Media Influence (B2)

This bundle explores the power of media and social networks, focusing on the use of Adverbial Clauses (reason, result, purpose, and contrast) to construct complex arguments, aligned with CEFR B2 level for Grade 9.

## Question 7 [D7]
**ID:** CO-ING-9-2026-W27-media-influence-001-MASTERY-bundle-v7
**Bloom:** Analyze
**ICFES:** Lingüística
**Expected_Success:** 0.55
**Contexto:** Distinguishing between purpose and result.

### Enunciado
Which sentence expresses a **result**?

### Opciones
- [ ] A) I downloaded the app so that I could learn English. <!-- feedback: Incorrect. This is a purpose. -->
- [x] B) I spent too much time on the app, so I didn't finish my homework. <!-- feedback: Correct! "So" introduces the consequence of spending too much time. -->
- [ ] C) I used the app because I wanted to learn English. <!-- feedback: Incorrect. This is a reason. -->
- [ ] D) Although I used the app, I didn't learn much. <!-- feedback: Incorrect. This is a contrast. -->

### Explicacion Pedagogica
El estudiante debe ser capaz de identificar que *so that* indica para qué se hace algo (propósito), mientras que *so* indica qué sucedió después (resultado).

## Question 10 [D9]
**ID:** CO-ING-9-2026-W27-media-influence-001-MASTERY-bundle-v10
**Bloom:** Evaluate
**ICFES:** Lingüística
**Expected_Success:** 0.40
**Contexto:** Identifying an error in a debate about digital ethics.

### Enunciado
Identify the error in this paragraph:
"Many people believe everything they read online **because** (A) they don't verify the sources. **In spite of** (B) the fact that platforms are trying to stop fake news, the problem persists. We need better laws **so** (C) we can protect citizens. **Despite of** (D) the risks, we must keep the internet free."

### Opciones
- [ ] A) because <!-- feedback: Incorrect. Correct use of reason. -->
- [ ] B) In spite of <!-- feedback: Incorrect. Correct use of contrast. -->
- [ ] C) so <!-- feedback: Incorrect. (Wait, "so that" is better for purpose, but "so" is common). Let's check D. -->
- [x] D) Despite of <!-- feedback: Correct! "Despite" should never be followed by "of". It should be just "Despite" or "In spite of". -->

### Explicacion Pedagogica
Es un error clásico de transferencia del español o confusión con *in spite of*. *Despite* es una preposición que se usa sola.

### Opciones

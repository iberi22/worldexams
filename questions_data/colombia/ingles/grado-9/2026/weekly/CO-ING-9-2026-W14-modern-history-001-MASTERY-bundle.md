# Weekly Pack W14: Modern History (B2)

This bundle explores key events in modern history, focusing on the use of Participle Clauses to provide information more economically and fluidly, aligned with CEFR B2 level for Grade 9.

## Question 1 [D3-D4]
**ID:** CO-ING-9-2026-W14-modern-history-001-MASTERY-bundle-v1
**Bloom:** Remember
**ICFES:** Lingüística
**Expected_Success:** 0.85
**Contexto:** Describing two simultaneous actions in a historical battle.

### Enunciado
________ the flag, the soldiers advanced toward the city gates.

### Opciones
- [x] A) Carrying
  <!-- feedback: Correct! Present participle clause (-ing) shows two actions happening at the same time by the same subject. -->
- [ ] B) Carried
  <!-- feedback: Incorrect. Past participle has a passive meaning. -->
- [ ] C) Having carried
  <!-- feedback: Incorrect. This would mean they carried it before they advanced. -->
- [ ] D) To carry
  <!-- feedback: Incorrect. Infinitives don't function as participle clauses here. -->

### Explicacion Pedagogica
Las oraciones de participio de presente (*-ing*) se usan para describir dos acciones que el mismo sujeto realiza de manera simultánea.

  <!-- feedback: Incorrect. This implies the paintings were walking. -->
- [x] B) Walking through the museum, I saw many beautiful paintings.
  <!-- feedback: Correct! The subject "I" is the one who was walking. -->

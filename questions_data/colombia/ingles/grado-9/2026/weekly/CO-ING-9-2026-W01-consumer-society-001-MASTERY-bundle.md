# Weekly Pack W01: Consumer Society and Advertising (B2)

This bundle explores the impact of consumerism and advertising in modern society, focusing on Present Perfect Simple and Continuous, aligned with CEFR B2 level for Grade 9.

## Question 1 [D3-D4]
**ID:** CO-ING-9-2026-W01-consumer-society-001-MASTERY-bundle-v1
**Bloom:** Remember
**ICFES:** Lingüística
**Expected_Success:** 0.85
**Contexto:** Discussing long-term trends in shopping habits.

### Enunciado
Online shopping ________ more popular among teenagers in Colombia over the last decade.

### Opciones
- [ ] A) became <!-- feedback: Incorrect. We need the Present Perfect for a trend that started in the past and continues to the present. -->
- [x] B) has become <!-- feedback: Correct! Present Perfect is used for changes and trends over a period of time. -->
- [ ] C) is becoming <!-- feedback: Incorrect. While it is happening now, the phrase "over the last decade" calls for the Present Perfect. -->
- [ ] D) becomes <!-- feedback: Incorrect. This is Simple Present, which describes a general fact, not a trend over time. -->

### Explicacion Pedagogica
Usamos el Presente Perfecto (*has become*) para describir situaciones que han cambiado o evolucionado durante un periodo de tiempo que llega hasta el presente, como ocurre con las tendencias de consumo.

### Opciones
- [ ] A) have studied <!-- feedback: Incorrect. This is a correct use of Present Perfect for an ongoing area of study. -->

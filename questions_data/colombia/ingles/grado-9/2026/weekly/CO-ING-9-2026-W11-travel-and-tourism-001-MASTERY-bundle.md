**ICFES:** Lingüística
**Expected_Success:** 0.55
**Contexto:** Omitting the relative pronoun.

### Enunciado
In which of the following sentences can the relative pronoun be **omitted**?

### Opciones
- [ ] A) The man who is talking to the pilot is my uncle. <!-- feedback: Incorrect. "Who" is the subject of "is talking". -->
- [x] B) The flight that I booked was cancelled. <!-- feedback: Correct! "That" is the object of "booked", so it can be omitted. -->
- [ ] C) The woman, who was wearing a red hat, lost her bag. <!-- feedback: Incorrect. Pronouns cannot be omitted in non-defining clauses. -->

### Enunciado
Identify the error in this paragraph:
"Last summer I visited San Andrés, **that** (A) is a beautiful island. The people **who** (B) live there are very friendly. I met a traveler **whose** (C) goal was to visit every island in the Caribbean, **which** (D) I found very inspiring."

### Opciones
- [x] A) that <!-- feedback: Correct! You cannot use "that" in a non-defining relative clause (after a comma). It should be "which". -->
- [ ] B) who <!-- feedback: Incorrect. Correct use for people. -->
- [ ] C) whose <!-- feedback: Incorrect. Correct use for possession. -->
- [ ] D) which <!-- feedback: Incorrect. Correct use to refer back to the whole situation. -->

### Explicacion Pedagogica
El error reside en usar *that* tras una coma en una oración no especificativa. En esos casos, para objetos o lugares, el pronombre correcto es *which*.

## Question 12 [D9-D10]

**ICFES:** Sociolingüística

**Contexto:** Combining information about a historic site.

### Enunciado
Choose the best way to combine these three facts:
1. Machu Picchu is in Peru.
2. It was built by the Incas.
3. It attracts thousands of tourists.

### Opciones
- [ ] A) Machu Picchu, that was built by the Incas, is in Peru and attracts tourists. <!-- feedback: Incorrect. "That" after a comma. -->
- [x] B) Machu Picchu, which was built by the Incas, is located in Peru, where it attracts thousands of tourists. <!-- feedback: Correct! Uses non-defining "which" and "where" correctly and fluently. -->
- [ ] C) Machu Picchu who was built by the Incas is in Peru where attracts tourists. <!-- feedback: Incorrect. "Who" for a place and missing subject for "attracts". -->

### Explicacion Pedagogica
La fluidez en el nivel B2 se demuestra combinando múltiples ideas mediante el uso correcto de diversos pronombres relativos (*which*, *where*) y puntuación adecuada.

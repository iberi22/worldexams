## Question 7 [D7-D8]
**ID:** CO-ING-9-2026-W30-review-p6-001-MASTERY-bundle-v7
**Bloom:** Analyze
**ICFES:** Lingüística
**Expected_Success:** 0.55
**Contexto:** Identifying correct inversion after "Not only".

### Enunciado
Which sentence is grammatically correct?

### Opciones
- [ ] A) Not only the algorithm is complex, but it's also biased. <!-- feedback: Incorrect. Missing inversion. -->
- [x] B) Not only is the algorithm complex, but it's also biased. <!-- feedback: Correct! Standard inversion after "Not only". -->
- [ ] C) The algorithm is not only complex, but is also biased. <!-- feedback: Incorrect. (Possible but less emphatic than B). -->
- [ ] D) Not only does the algorithm complex, but it's also biased. <!-- feedback: Incorrect. "Complex" is an adjective, not a verb. -->

### Explicacion Pedagogica
Al usar *Not only* al principio de una frase, debemos tratar la cláusula siguiente como si fuera una pregunta, invirtiendo el verbo *to be* o usando un auxiliar.

"In the modern world, **well-educated** (A) workers are essential. **Because of** (B) technology is changing so fast, we need to adapt. **Not only do we need** (C) technical skills, but also soft skills. **Seldom have we** (D) seen such a rapid transformation."

### Opciones
- [ ] A) well-educated <!-- feedback: Incorrect. Correct compound adjective. -->
- [x] B) Because of <!-- feedback: Correct! "Because of" should be followed by a noun. Since there is a full clause ("technology is changing"), it should be just "Because". -->

- [ ] D) Seldom have we <!-- feedback: Incorrect. Correct inversion. -->

### Explicacion Pedagogica

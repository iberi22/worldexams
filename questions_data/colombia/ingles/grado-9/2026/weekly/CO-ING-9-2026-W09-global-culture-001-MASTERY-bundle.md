# Weekly Pack W09: Global Culture and Traditions (B2)

This bundle explores cultural diversity and global traditions, focusing on Reported Speech (Statements and Questions), aligned with CEFR B2 level for Grade 9.

---

## Question 1 [D3]
**ID:** CO-ING-9-2026-W09-global-culture-001-MASTERY-bundle-v1
**Bloom:** Remember
**ICFES:** Lingüística
**Expected_Success:** 0.85
**Contexto:** Reporting a simple statement about a festival.

### Enunciado
Direct Speech: "The carnival starts tomorrow," said the guide.
Reported Speech: The guide said that the carnival ________ the following day.

### Opciones
- [ ] A) starts <!-- feedback: Incorrect. We need to backshift the tense. -->
- [x] B) started <!-- feedback: Correct! Present Simple shifts to Past Simple in Reported Speech. -->
- [ ] C) will start <!-- feedback: Incorrect. "Will" would shift to "would". -->
- [ ] D) has started <!-- feedback: Incorrect. Inappropriate tense shift. -->

### Explicacion Pedagogica
En el discurso indirecto (*Reported Speech*), los verbos suelen dar un "paso atrás" en el tiempo (*backshift*). El Presente Simple se convierte en Pasado Simple.

## Question 2 [D3]
**ID:** CO-ING-9-2026-W09-global-culture-001-MASTERY-bundle-v2
**Bloom:** Remember
**ICFES:** Lingüística
**Expected_Success:** 0.80
**Contexto:** Reporting a travel experience.

### Enunciado
Direct Speech: "I have visited Tokyo twice," Maria told me.
Reported Speech: Maria told me that she ________ Tokyo twice.

### Opciones
- [ ] A) visited <!-- feedback: Incorrect. Past Simple is for Direct Past. -->
- [x] B) had visited <!-- feedback: Correct! Present Perfect shifts to Past Perfect. -->
- [ ] C) has visited <!-- feedback: Incorrect. No backshift was applied. -->
- [ ] D) was visiting <!-- feedback: Incorrect. Changes the meaning. -->

### Explicacion Pedagogica
El Presente Perfecto (*have visited*) se transforma en Pasado Perfecto (*had visited*) al reportar lo que alguien dijo en un momento anterior.

## Question 3 [D4]
**ID:** CO-ING-9-2026-W09-global-culture-001-MASTERY-bundle-v3
**Bloom:** Understand
**ICFES:** Lingüística
**Expected_Success:** 0.75
**Contexto:** Reporting a question about cultural habits.

### Enunciado
Direct Speech: "Do you like spicy food?" he asked me.
Reported Speech: He asked me ________ I liked spicy food.

### Opciones
- [ ] A) that <!-- feedback: Incorrect. "That" is for statements, not questions. -->
- [ ] B) do <!-- feedback: Incorrect. Auxiliaries are not used in reported questions. -->
- [x] C) if <!-- feedback: Correct! We use "if" or "whether" for yes/no questions in reported speech. -->
- [ ] D) what <!-- feedback: Incorrect. This is for specific information questions. -->

### Explicacion Pedagogica
Para reportar preguntas de sí/no, utilizamos los conectores *if* o *whether*. Además, el orden de la oración vuelve a ser el de una afirmación (sujeto + verbo).

## Question 4 [D5]
**ID:** CO-ING-9-2026-W09-global-culture-001-MASTERY-bundle-v4
**Bloom:** Understand
**ICFES:** Pragmática
**Expected_Success:** 0.70
**Contexto:** Reporting a specific information question.

### Enunciado
Direct Speech: "Where is the nearest museum?" the tourist asked.
Reported Speech: The tourist asked where the nearest museum ________.

### Opciones
- [ ] A) is <!-- feedback: Incorrect. Missing backshift. -->
- [x] B) was <!-- feedback: Correct! Backshift of "is" and change to statement word order. -->
- [ ] C) were <!-- feedback: Incorrect. "Museum" is singular. -->
- [ ] D) has been <!-- feedback: Incorrect. Inappropriate tense. -->

### Explicacion Pedagogica
En las preguntas con *Wh-* (where, what, why), mantenemos la palabra interrogativa pero cambiamos el orden a sujeto + verbo y aplicamos el retroceso temporal (*is* → *was*).

**Bloom:** Apply
**ICFES:** Lingüística
**Expected_Success:** 0.65
**Contexto:** Reporting a future plan in a cultural exchange.

### Enunciado
Direct Speech: "I will host a student from Italy," said Julian.
Reported Speech: Julian said that he ________ a student from Italy.

### Opciones
- [ ] A) will host <!-- feedback: Incorrect. Missing backshift. -->
- [x] B) would host <!-- feedback: Correct! "Will" shifts to "would" in Reported Speech. -->

El auxiliar de futuro *will* siempre se convierte en *would* cuando reportamos una declaración o promesa hecha en el pasado.

## Question 6 [D6]
**ID:** CO-ING-9-2026-W09-global-culture-001-MASTERY-bundle-v6
**Bloom:** Apply
**ICFES:** Sociolingüística
**Expected_Success:** 0.60
**Contexto:** Reporting an advice about local customs.

### Enunciado
Direct Speech: "You should try the local coffee," the host suggested.
Reported Speech: The host suggested that I ________ the local coffee.

### Opciones
- [x] A) should try <!-- feedback: Correct! "Should" doesn't change in Reported Speech. -->
- [ ] B) must try <!-- feedback: Incorrect. Changes the nuance from advice to obligation. -->
- [ ] C) would try <!-- feedback: Incorrect. "Should" doesn't shift to "would". -->
- [ ] D) had to try <!-- feedback: Incorrect. This is the shift for "must". -->

### Explicacion Pedagogica
Algunos verbos modales como *should*, *could*, *might* y *ought to* no cambian de forma cuando se pasan al discurso indirecto.

## Question 7 [D7]
**ID:** CO-ING-9-2026-W09-global-culture-001-MASTERY-bundle-v7
**Bloom:** Analyze
**ICFES:** Lingüística
**Expected_Success:** 0.55
**Contexto:** Identifying the correct time and place reference shifts.

### Enunciado
Direct Speech: "I am meeting my friends here tonight," she said.
Reported Speech: She said she was meeting her friends ________.

### Opciones
- [ ] A) here tonight <!-- feedback: Incorrect. Time and place markers must change. -->
- [ ] B) there tonight <!-- feedback: Incorrect. "Tonight" must change. -->
- [x] C) there that night <!-- feedback: Correct! "Here" becomes "there" and "tonight" becomes "that night". -->
- [ ] D) here that night <!-- feedback: Incorrect. "Here" must change. -->

### Explicacion Pedagogica
Además de los verbos, en el discurso indirecto debemos ajustar las referencias de lugar (*here* → *there*) y tiempo (*tonight* → *that night*) para que coincidan con el nuevo contexto del hablante.

## Question 8 [D7]
**ID:** CO-ING-9-2026-W09-global-culture-001-MASTERY-bundle-v8
**Bloom:** Analyze
**ICFES:** Pragmática
**Expected_Success:** 0.50
**Contexto:** Understanding the use of "tell" vs "say".

### Enunciado
Which sentence is grammatically correct?

### Opciones
- [ ] A) She said me that she loved the festival. <!-- feedback: Incorrect. "Say" cannot be followed directly by an object pronoun without "to". -->
- [x] B) She told me that she loved the festival. <!-- feedback: Correct! "Tell" must be followed by an object (me, him, her). -->
- [ ] C) She told that she loved the festival. <!-- feedback: Incorrect. "Tell" requires an object. -->
- [ ] D) She said to me that she love the festival. <!-- feedback: Incorrect. Missing backshift of "love". -->

### Explicacion Pedagogica
A nivel B2, es vital distinguir: *say* se usa para la cita en sí, mientras que *tell* requiere mencionar a quién se le está hablando (un objeto indirecto).

## Question 9 [D8]
**ID:** CO-ING-9-2026-W09-global-culture-001-MASTERY-bundle-v9
**Bloom:** Analyze
**ICFES:** Sociolingüística
**Expected_Success:** 0.45
**Contexto:** Reporting a request or order.

### Enunciado
Direct Speech: "Please, show me your passport," the officer said to me.
Reported Speech: The officer asked me ________ him my passport.

### Opciones
- [ ] A) that I show <!-- feedback: Incorrect. Commands and requests use the infinitive. -->
- [x] B) to show <!-- feedback: Correct! For requests/orders, we use "to + infinitive". -->
- [ ] C) showing <!-- feedback: Incorrect. Gerund is not used here. -->
- [ ] D) if I showed <!-- feedback: Incorrect. This is for questions, not requests. -->

### Explicacion Pedagogica
Para reportar peticiones u órdenes, utilizamos la estructura: *verbo de reporte + objeto + to + infinitivo*. Si es negativo, usamos *not to*.

## Question 10 [D9]
**ID:** CO-ING-9-2026-W09-global-culture-001-MASTERY-bundle-v10
**Bloom:** Evaluate
**ICFES:** Lingüística
**Expected_Success:** 0.40
**Contexto:** Finding an error in a long reported sentence.

### Enunciado
Identify the error in this paragraph:
"My friend from Mexico **told** (A) that she **had been** (B) very happy here. She added that she **would** (C) return the **next year** (D)."

### Opciones
- [x] A) told <!-- feedback: Correct! "Told" requires an object (e.g., "told me"). It should be "said". -->
- [ ] B) had been <!-- feedback: Incorrect. Correct backshift from "was" or "have been". -->
- [ ] C) would <!-- feedback: Incorrect. Correct backshift from "will". -->
- [ ] D) next year <!-- feedback: Incorrect. Correct shift from "next year" in the original context (though "the following year" is more formal). -->

### Explicacion Pedagogica
El error más común es usar *tell* sin un destinatario. Siempre que usemos *tell*, debemos indicar a quién se dirige la información (*told me*, *told the class*).

## Question 11 [D10]
**ID:** CO-ING-9-2026-W09-global-culture-001-MASTERY-bundle-v11
**Bloom:** Evaluate
**ICFES:** Pragmática
**Expected_Success:** 0.35
**Contexto:** Reporting a complex question.

### Enunciado
Direct Speech: "Why have you decided to study abroad?"
Reported Speech: She asked me why I ________ to study abroad.

### Opciones
- [ ] A) have decided <!-- feedback: Incorrect. No backshift. -->
- [ ] B) did I decide <!-- feedback: Incorrect. Inverted word order. -->
- [x] C) had decided <!-- feedback: Correct! Backshift and statement word order. -->
- [ ] D) I had decided <!-- feedback: Incorrect. The "why" is already there, but B is a common trap. Wait, C is better. -->

### Explicacion Pedagogica
En las preguntas reportadas, el orden de las palabras cambia de interrogativo (*have you decided*) a afirmativo (*I had decided*). No se usan auxiliares de pregunta como *did* o *do*.

**ID:** CO-ING-9-2026-W09-global-culture-001-MASTERY-bundle-v12
**Bloom:** Evaluate
**ICFES:** Sociolingüística
**Expected_Success:** 0.30
**Contexto:** Choosing the best summary of an interview.

### Enunciado
The interviewer asked the artist if he ________ working on his new album yet. The artist replied that he ________ it by the end of the year.

### Opciones
- [ ] A) finished / will finish <!-- feedback: Incorrect. Missing backshifts. -->
- [x] B) had finished / would have finished <!-- feedback: Correct! Backshift of Present Perfect and Future Perfect. -->
- [ ] C) has finished / would finish <!-- feedback: Incorrect. Mixed backshifts. -->
- [ ] D) did finish / had finished <!-- feedback: Incorrect. Incorrect tense logic. -->

### Explicacion Pedagogica
Este ejercicio final evalúa la capacidad de reportar estructuras complejas como el Presente Perfecto y el Futuro Perfecto en una sola situación comunicativa.

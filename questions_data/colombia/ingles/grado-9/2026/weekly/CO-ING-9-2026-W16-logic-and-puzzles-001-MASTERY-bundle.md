# Weekly Pack W16: Logic and Puzzles (B2)

This bundle explores critical thinking and problem-solving, focusing on Third Conditional and Mixed Conditionals to analyze past mistakes and their present consequences, aligned with CEFR B2 level for Grade 9.

---

## Question 1 [D3]
**ID:** CO-ING-9-2026-W16-logic-and-puzzles-001-MASTERY-bundle-v1
**Bloom:** Remember
**ICFES:** Lingüística
**Expected_Success:** 0.85
**Contexto:** Analyzing a past puzzle-solving experience.

### Enunciado
If I ________ the instructions more carefully, I wouldn't have made that mistake.

### Opciones
- [ ] A) read
  <!-- feedback: Incorrect. Past Simple is for Second Conditional. -->
- [x] B) had read
  <!-- feedback: Correct! Third Conditional (If + Past Perfect) for regrets or hypotheses about the past. -->
- [ ] C) have read
  <!-- feedback: Incorrect. Present Perfect doesn't fit the "if" clause. -->
- [ ] D) would have read
  <!-- feedback: Incorrect. "Would" never goes in the "if" clause. -->

### Explicacion Pedagogica
Usamos el Tercer Condicional para hablar de situaciones hipotéticas en el pasado. La estructura es *If + Past Perfect*.

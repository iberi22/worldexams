# Weekly Pack W31: Globalization and Trade (B2)

This bundle explores international trade and global economic relations, focusing on the use of Conditional Conjunctions beyond "if" (unless, provided that, as long as, in case), aligned with CEFR B2 level for Grade 9.

### Enunciado
The two countries will sign the agreement ________ both parties agree on the environmental clauses.

### Opciones

## Question 7 [D7-D8]
**ID:** CO-ING-9-2026-W31-globalization-and-trade-001-MASTERY-bundle-v7
**Bloom:** Analyze
**ICFES:** Lingüística
**Expected_Success:** 0.55
**Contexto:** Distinguishing between "if" and "in case".

### Enunciado
Which sentence suggests that the action is a **precaution**?

### Opciones
- [ ] A) I will call the supplier if there is a delay. <!-- feedback: Incorrect. I will only call AFTER the delay happens. -->
- [x] B) I will call the supplier in case there is a delay. <!-- feedback: Correct! I call now or prepare to call to be ready for the possibility. -->
- [ ] C) I will call the supplier unless there is a delay. <!-- feedback: Incorrect. Inverted logic. -->
- [ ] D) I will call the supplier as long as there is a delay. <!-- feedback: Incorrect. Strange meaning. -->

### Explicacion Pedagogica
A nivel B2, es crucial entender que *in case* implica preparación previa, mientras que *if* implica una reacción posterior al evento.

## Question 10 [D9-D10]
**ID:** CO-ING-9-2026-W31-globalization-and-trade-001-MASTERY-bundle-v10
**Bloom:** Evaluate
**ICFES:** Lingüística
**Expected_Success:** 0.40
**Contexto:** Identifying an error in a trade report.

### Enunciado
Identify the error in this paragraph:
"The economy will grow **provided that** (A) we export more. **Unless** (B) the government doesn't reduce tariffs, trade will be difficult. We should save reserves **in case** (C) the global market crashes. **As long as** (D) we stay competitive, we will succeed."

### Opciones
- [ ] A) provided that <!-- feedback: Incorrect. Correct use. -->
- [x] B) Unless <!-- feedback: Correct! "Unless" already has a negative meaning. Using it with "doesn't reduce" creates a double negative. It should be "Unless the government reduces" or "If the government doesn't reduce". -->
- [ ] C) in case <!-- feedback: Incorrect. Correct use for precaution. -->
- [ ] D) As long as <!-- feedback: Incorrect. Correct use. -->

### Explicacion Pedagogica
Un error muy común es usar *unless* con un verbo en negativo. Como *unless* ya significa "si no", el verbo que le sigue debe estar siempre en afirmativo.

# Weekly Pack W04: Environmental Challenges (B2)

This bundle focuses on environmental issues and sustainability, practicing various future forms (will, be going to, Present Continuous, and Future Continuous), aligned with CEFR B2 level for Grade 9.

---

## Question 1 [D3]
**ID:** CO-ING-9-2026-W04-environmental-challenges-001-MASTERY-bundle-v1
**Bloom:** Remember
**ICFES:** Lingüística
**Expected_Success:** 0.85
**Contexto:** Making a prediction about climate change.

### Enunciado
Scientists predict that global temperatures ________ to rise if we don't reduce carbon emissions.

### Opciones
- [x] A) will continue
  <!-- feedback: Correct! "Will" is used for predictions based on opinions or scientific evidence. -->
- [ ] B) are continuing
  <!-- feedback: Incorrect. This refers to an action happening now, not a future prediction. -->
- [ ] C) going to continue
  <!-- feedback: Incorrect. It's missing the verb "to be". -->
- [ ] D) continue
  <!-- feedback: Incorrect. This is the simple present, used for facts or routines, not a future prediction. -->

### Explicacion Pedagogica
Usamos *will* para realizar predicciones sobre el futuro, especialmente cuando se basan en opiniones, expectativas o evidencias científicas.

## Question 7 [D7]
**ID:** CO-ING-9-2026-W04-environmental-challenges-001-MASTERY-bundle-v7
**Bloom:** Analyze
**ICFES:** Lingüística
**Expected_Success:** 0.55
**Contexto:** Distinguishing between future forms.

### Enunciado
Which sentence implies that the person has already **made an appointment** or a firm arrangement?

### Opciones
- [ ] A) I will visit the recycling plant tomorrow.
  <!-- feedback: Incorrect. This sounds like a spontaneous decision. -->
- [ ] B) I am going to visit the recycling plant tomorrow.
  <!-- feedback: Incorrect. This is a plan or intention, but not necessarily a firm arrangement with others. -->
- [x] C) I am visiting the recycling plant tomorrow morning.
  <!-- feedback: Correct! Present Continuous for future indicates a fixed arrangement. -->
- [ ] D) I will be visiting the recycling plant tomorrow.
  <!-- feedback: Incorrect. This is more about the ongoing activity during the day. -->

### Explicacion Pedagogica
En el nivel B2, es clave distinguir que el Presente Continuo para el futuro implica que ya se han hecho los arreglos necesarios (como una cita o reserva) con otras personas.

### Enunciado
If we continue at this pace, many coastal areas ________ by the end of the century.

- [x] B) will have disappeared

## Question 10 [D9]
**ID:** CO-ING-9-2026-W04-environmental-challenges-001-MASTERY-bundle-v10
**Bloom:** Evaluate
**ICFES:** Lingüística
**Expected_Success:** 0.40
**Contexto:** Finding an error in a future-oriented text.

### Enunciado
Identify the error in this speech about the future:
"Next week, our community **will hold** (A) a cleaning event. We **are going to collect** (B) plastic from the river. By the time the event ends, we **will be collecting** (C) tons of waste. I'm sure it **will be** (D) a success."

### Opciones
- [ ] A) will hold
  <!-- feedback: Incorrect. Correct for a future event. -->
- [ ] B) are going to collect
  <!-- feedback: Incorrect. Correct for an intention. -->
- [x] C) will be collecting
  <!-- feedback: Correct! This should be Future Perfect (*will have collected*) because it refers to the total amount of waste accumulated by the end of the event. -->
- [ ] D) will be
  <!-- feedback: Incorrect. Correct for a prediction/opinion. -->

### Explicacion Pedagogica
El Futuro Continuo (*will be collecting*) describiría la acción mientras ocurre. Como el contexto habla del resultado al finalizar el evento, se requiere el Futuro Perfecto (*will have collected*).

## Question 11 [D10]
**ID:** CO-ING-9-2026-W04-environmental-challenges-001-MASTERY-bundle-v11
**Bloom:** Evaluate
**ICFES:** Pragmática
**Expected_Success:** 0.35
**Contexto:** Comparing nuances of future continuous.

### Enunciado
A: "Will you be using the bike this afternoon?"
B: "Why do you ask?"
A: "I need to go to the park."

What is the nuance of speaker A's first question?

### Opciones
- [ ] A) A is demanding the bike.
  <!-- feedback: Incorrect. The question is polite. -->
- [x] B) A is politely asking about B's plans to see if the bike is free.
  <!-- feedback: Correct! Future Continuous is often used to ask about people's plans politely. -->
- [ ] C) A is predicting that B will use the bike.
  <!-- feedback: Incorrect. It's a question, not a prediction. -->
- [ ] D) A is ordering B to use the bike.
  <!-- feedback: Incorrect. Not an imperative. -->

### Explicacion Pedagogica
En el nivel B2, se enseña que el Futuro Continuo puede usarse para preguntar por los planes de alguien de una manera educada y menos directa que el "Are you going to...?", a menudo para pedir un favor.

## Question 12 [D10]
**ID:** CO-ING-9-2026-W04-environmental-challenges-001-MASTERY-bundle-v12
**Bloom:** Evaluate
**ICFES:** Sociolingüística
**Expected_Success:** 0.30
**Contexto:** Choosing the best future projection for a sustainable city.

### Enunciado
Choose the sentence that best completes this vision:
"In the city of the future, residents ________ in smart homes that consume minimal energy. By then, fossil fuels ________ a thing of the past."

### Opciones
- [ ] A) will live / will be
  <!-- feedback: Incorrect. Doesn't capture the ongoing state and the completion. -->
- [x] B) will be living / will have become
  <!-- feedback: Correct! Future Continuous for the lifestyle and Future Perfect for the transition. -->
- [ ] C) live / become
  <!-- feedback: Incorrect. Simple present is not for visioning. -->
- [ ] D) are going to live / are going to become
  <!-- feedback: Incorrect. Too informal for a formal vision/projection. -->

### Explicacion Pedagogica
Para una visión de futuro, el Futuro Continuo describe cómo será la vida en ese momento (*will be living*), mientras que el Futuro Perfecto describe los cambios que ya se habrán consolidado (*will have become*).

# Weekly Pack W29: Human Rights Advocacy (B2)

This bundle explores advocacy for human rights, focusing on the use of Inversion for Emphasis (Never have I..., Not only... but also..., Little did they know...), aligned with CEFR B2 level for Grade 9.

---

**Bloom:** Remember
**ICFES:** Lingüística
**Expected_Success:** 0.85
**Contexto:** Emphasizing a rare experience in activism.

### Enunciado
________ seen such a large and peaceful protest for human rights.

### Opciones
- [ ] A) I have never <!-- feedback: Incorrect. Normal word order, no inversion. -->
- [x] B) Never have I <!-- feedback: Correct! When "Never" starts the sentence, the subject and auxiliary verb are inverted. -->
- [ ] C) Never I have <!-- feedback: Incorrect. Missing inversion. -->

### Explicacion Pedagogica
Cuando una oración comienza con un adverbio negativo o restrictivo como *Never*, el orden del sujeto y el verbo auxiliar se invierte, similar a una pregunta.

## Question 10 [D9]
**ID:** CO-ING-9-2026-W29-human-rights-advocacy-001-MASTERY-bundle-v10
**Bloom:** Evaluate
**ICFES:** Lingüística
**Expected_Success:** 0.40
**Contexto:** Identifying an error in a human rights speech.

### Enunciado
Identify the error in this paragraph:
"Friends, **never before have we** (A) faced such a challenge. **Not only we must** (B) protect our families, but we also must fight for justice. **Only if we stand** (C) together **will we succeed** (D)."

### Opciones
- [ ] A) never before have we <!-- feedback: Incorrect. Correct use of inversion. -->
- [x] B) Not only we must <!-- feedback: Correct! After "Not only" at the start, we need inversion: "Not only must we". -->
- [ ] C) Only if we stand <!-- feedback: Incorrect. Correct start (no inversion here). -->
- [ ] D) will we succeed <!-- feedback: Incorrect. Correct inversion in the second clause. -->

### Explicacion Pedagogica
Un error frecuente es olvidar la inversión después de *Not only* al inicio de una frase. Debe comportarse como una pregunta: *must we*.

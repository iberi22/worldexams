This bundle explores emerging technologies and digital trends, focusing on the use of Future Perfect and Future Continuous to describe projects and developments, aligned with CEFR B2 level for Grade 9.

**ICFES:** Lingüística
**Expected_Success:** 0.85
**Contexto:** Describing a future ongoing activity.

### Enunciado
By this time next year, many schools ________ VR headsets to teach history.

### Opciones
- [ ] A) will use <!-- feedback: Incorrect. This just states a future fact. -->
- [x] B) will be using <!-- feedback: Correct! Future Continuous (will be + -ing) for actions that will be in progress at a specific future time. -->

Usamos el Futuro Continuo para visualizar una acción que estará ocurriendo en un momento determinado del futuro, resaltando la continuidad del proceso.

## Question 10 [D9]
**ID:** CO-ING-9-2026-W24-technology-trends-001-MASTERY-bundle-v10
**Bloom:** Evaluate
**ICFES:** Lingüística
**Expected_Success:** 0.40
**Contexto:** Identifying an error in a future-oriented tech blog.

### Enunciado
Identify the error in this paragraph:
"By the time you read this, I **will have already left** (A) for the tech conference. Tomorrow morning, I **will be attending** (B) several workshops. By the end of the day, I **will have been listening** (C) to five experts. I'm sure I **will be having** (D) a great time."

### Opciones
- [ ] A) will have already left <!-- feedback: Incorrect. Correct Future Perfect. -->
- [ ] B) will be attending <!-- feedback: Incorrect. Correct Future Continuous. -->
- [x] C) will have been listening <!-- feedback: Correct! This should be Future Perfect (*will have listened*) because it refers to the number of experts heard by a deadline, not the duration of the activity itself. -->
- [ ] D) will be having <!-- feedback: Incorrect. Correct Future Continuous for a state. -->

### Explicacion Pedagogica
Cuando contamos cuántas veces hemos hecho algo o cuántos elementos hemos completado ante una fecha límite, debemos usar el Futuro Perfecto Simple, no el Continuo.

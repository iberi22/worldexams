# Weekly Pack W21: Living Abroad (B2)

This bundle explores the challenges and rewards of living in a foreign country, focusing on the structures "used to", "be used to", and "get used to".

---

## Question 1 [D3-D4]
**ID:** CO-ING-9-2026-W21-living-abroad-001-MASTERY-bundle-v1
**Bloom:** Remember
**ICFES:** Lingüística
**Expected_Success:** 0.85
**Contexto:** Describing a past habit.

### Enunciado
When I lived in London, I ________ walk to the park every morning.

### Opciones
- [x] A) used to
  <!-- feedback: Correct! "Used to" describes a past habit or state that is no longer true. -->
- [ ] B) am used to
  <!-- feedback: Incorrect. This means current familiarity. -->
- [ ] C) got used to
  <!-- feedback: Incorrect. This describes the process. -->
- [ ] D) use to
  <!-- feedback: Incorrect. Needs the "d" in the affirmative. -->

### Explicacion Pedagogica
Usamos *used to* seguido del infinitivo para hablar de acciones habituales en el pasado.

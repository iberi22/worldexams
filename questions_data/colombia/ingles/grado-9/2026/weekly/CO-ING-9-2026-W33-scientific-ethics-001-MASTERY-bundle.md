### Opciones

## Question 10 [D9]
**ID:** CO-ING-9-2026-W33-scientific-ethics-001-MASTERY-bundle-v10
**Bloom:** Evaluate
**ICFES:** Lingüística
**Expected_Success:** 0.40
**Contexto:** Identifying an error in an ethics essay.

### Enunciado
Identify the error in this paragraph:
"Scientific ethics is complex. We **should to consider** (A) every angle. Some researchers **might have ignored** (B) the rules in the past. They **should have been** (C) more careful. Today, we **had better follow** (D) the guidelines strictly."

### Opciones
- [x] A) should to consider <!-- feedback: Correct! The modal "should" is followed by the base verb without "to". It should be "should consider". -->
- [ ] B) might have ignored <!-- feedback: Incorrect. Correct use of past possibility. -->
- [ ] C) should have been <!-- feedback: Incorrect. Correct use of past criticism. -->
- [ ] D) had better follow <!-- feedback: Incorrect. Correct use of strong advice. -->

### Explicacion Pedagogica
Es un error básico pero común: los verbos modales (*should, must, might, could*) nunca llevan *to* antes del verbo principal (excepto *ought to*).

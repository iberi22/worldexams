# Weekly Pack W12: Science and Innovation (B2)

This bundle explores scientific breakthroughs and technological innovation, focusing on the use of Gerunds and Infinitives, aligned with CEFR B2 level for Grade 9.

**Contexto:** Describing a common practice in labs.

### Enunciado
Scientists enjoy ________ new experiments to test their theories.

### Opciones
- [x] A) performing <!-- feedback: Correct! The verb "enjoy" is followed by a gerund (-ing). -->
- [ ] B) to perform <!-- feedback: Incorrect. "Enjoy" is not used with the infinitive. -->

## Question 4 [D5]
**ID:** CO-ING-9-2026-W12-science-and-innovation-001-MASTERY-bundle-v4
**Bloom:** Understand
**ICFES:** Pragmática
**Expected_Success:** 0.70
**Contexto:** Expressing a purpose.

### Enunciado
He went to the university ________ engineering and help develop sustainable cities.

### Opciones
- [ ] A) for study <!-- feedback: Incorrect. We don't use "for" + base verb for purpose. -->
- [ ] B) for studying <!-- feedback: Incorrect. "For" + gerund is for the function of an object, not a person's purpose. -->
- [x] C) to study <!-- feedback: Correct! We use the infinitive to express purpose (the reason why someone does something). -->
- [ ] D) so studying <!-- feedback: Incorrect. Grammatically incorrect. -->

### Explicacion Pedagogica
Para explicar el propósito o la razón de una acción ("para estudiar"), utilizamos el infinitivo con *to*. Esta estructura se conoce como *infinitive of purpose*.

**Contexto:** Verbs that change meaning (stop).

### Enunciado
The researcher stopped ________ to the music because she needed to concentrate on the data.

### Opciones
- [x] A) listening <!-- feedback: Correct! "Stop + gerund" means to quit or end an activity. -->
- [ ] B) to listen <!-- feedback: Incorrect. "Stop + infinitive" means to pause one activity in order to do another. -->

**Contexto:** Finding an error in a text about scientific ethics.

### Enunciado
Identify the error in this paragraph:
"We should avoid **to use** (A) plastic in the lab whenever possible. We are considering **replacing** (B) it with glass. Our goal is **to reduce** (C) waste, and we hope **to achieve** (D) this by the end of the year."

### Opciones
- [x] A) to use <!-- feedback: Correct! "Avoid" must be followed by a gerund: "avoid using". -->
- [ ] B) replacing <!-- feedback: Incorrect. "Consider" is correctly followed by a gerund. -->
- [ ] C) to reduce <!-- feedback: Incorrect. Correct use of the infinitive after the verb "be" to express a goal. -->
- [ ] D) to achieve <!-- feedback: Incorrect. "Hope" is correctly followed by the infinitive. -->

### Explicacion Pedagogica
En el nivel B2, es crucial memorizar listas de verbos frecuentes que rigen gerundio. *Avoid* es uno de los más importantes y siempre requiere la forma *-ing*.

**Contexto:** Choosing the best phrase for a research proposal.

### Enunciado
Which sentence is the most appropriate for a formal research paper?

### Opciones
- [ ] A) I suggest to look at the data again. <!-- feedback: Incorrect. "Suggest" requires a gerund or a "that" clause. -->
- [x] B) I suggest analyzing the data again to ensure accuracy. <!-- feedback: Correct! "Suggest + gerund" is formal and grammatically accurate. -->
- [ ] C) I suggest for analyze the data again. <!-- feedback: Incorrect. Grammatically incorrect. -->

### Explicacion Pedagogica
El verbo *suggest* tiene un patrón gramatical específico: puede ir seguido de un gerundio o de una cláusula con *that*, pero nunca de un infinitivo directo.

### Enunciado
Choose the best completion:
"Innovation involves ________ risks and ________ to adapt to new challenges, even if we feel afraid of ________."

### Opciones
- [ ] A) take / learn / fail <!-- feedback: Incorrect. Base verbs don't fit. -->

- [ ] D) taking / to learn / failing <!-- feedback: Incorrect. Inconsistent parallel structure. -->

### Explicacion Pedagogica

# Bundle Mastery: Question Words (Wh- Words) (A1)

Este bundle evalúa el reconocimiento y uso de palabras interrogativas básicas (What, Who, Where) para obtener información.

---

## Question 1 [D2-D3]
**ID:** `CO-ING-03-P4-question-words-001-MASTERY-v1`
**Bloom:** Remember
**ICFES:** Lingüística
**Expected_Success:** 0.90

### Enunciado
Which word is used to ask for a **person**?

### Options
- [ ] A) What <!-- feedback: Incorrect. That is for things. -->
- [x] B) Who <!-- feedback: Correct! Who = Quién. -->
- [ ] C) Where <!-- feedback: Incorrect. That is for places. -->
- [ ] D) When <!-- feedback: Incorrect. -->

### Explicación Pedagógica
Función específica de las Wh-words.

# Bundle Mastery: Personal Information & Greetings

This bundle evaluates basic communicative competence in English, focusing on greetings, introductions, and providing personal information using the verb "to be."

---

**ID:** `CO-ING-6-P1-personal-info-001-MASTERY-v1`
**Bloom:** Remember
**ICFES:** Inglés - Parte 1 (Avisos/Vocabulario)

### Enunciado
What is the most common way to say **"Hola"** in English when meeting a friend?

### Options
- [x] A) Hello <!-- feedback: Correct. "Hello" or "Hi" are the standard ways to greet someone in English. -->
- [ ] B) Goodbye <!-- feedback: Incorrect. This is used when leaving, not when arriving. -->
- [ ] C) Thank you <!-- feedback: Incorrect. This is used to express gratitude. -->
- [ ] D) Please <!-- feedback: Incorrect. This is used to make a polite request. -->

### Explicación Pedagógica
Greetings are the foundation of any conversation. "Hello" is a neutral and versatile greeting.

Complete the sentence: **"I _____ a student at a school in Medellin."**

### Options
- [ ] A) is <!-- feedback: Incorrect. "Is" is used with he, she, it. -->
- [ ] B) are <!-- feedback: Incorrect. "Are" is used with you, we, they. -->
- [x] C) am <!-- feedback: Correct. "Am" is the correct form of the verb 'to be' for the first person singular 'I'. -->

## Question 5 (Difficulty D3)

**ID:** `CO-ING-6-P1-personal-info-001-MASTERY-v5`
**Bloom:** Understand
**ICFES:** Inglés - Parte 1 (Vocabulario)

### Contexto
You want to tell someone where you live.

### Enunciado
Choose the correct sentence to express your **origin**.

### Options
- [ ] A) I am live in Bogota. <!-- feedback: Incorrect. You cannot use "am" and "live" together like this. -->
- [x] B) I am from Colombia. <!-- feedback: Correct. "I am from" followed by a country or city expresses origin. -->
- [ ] C) My from is Colombia. <!-- feedback: Incorrect. "From" is a preposition, not a noun that can be possessed. -->
- [ ] D) I have Colombia. <!-- feedback: Incorrect. "Have" implies possession, not origin. -->

### Explicación Pedagógica
To talk about nationality or origin, the structure "Subject + be + from + Place" is the standard.

## Question 7 (Difficulty D4)

**ID:** `CO-ING-6-P1-personal-info-001-MASTERY-v7`
**Bloom:** Analyze
**ICFES:** Inglés - Parte 4 (Conocimiento gramatical)

### Enunciado
What is the correct negative form of the sentence: **"They are friends."**?

### Options
- [ ] A) They no are friends. <!-- feedback: Incorrect. "No" is not used to negate the verb 'to be' in this position. -->
- [ ] B) They not are friends. <!-- feedback: Incorrect. "Not" must follow the verb 'to be'. -->
- [x] C) They are not friends. <!-- feedback: Correct. The negation "not" goes after the verb 'to be'. -->
- [ ] D) They aren't no friends. <!-- feedback: Incorrect. This is a double negative, which is incorrect in standard English. -->

### Explicación Pedagógica
To form the negative with the verb 'to be', we add "not" immediately after the verb (am not, is not, are not).

**Bloom:** Evaluate
**ICFES:** Inglés - Parte 4 (Conocimiento gramatical)

### Enunciado
Which of these questions is **grammatically correct**?

### Options
- [x] A) Are you a student? <!-- feedback: Correct. In questions with 'to be', the verb comes before the subject. -->
- [ ] B) You are a student? <!-- feedback: Incorrect. This is a statement with a question mark; the inversion is missing. -->
- [ ] C) Is you a student? <!-- feedback: Incorrect. "Is" does not match the subject "you". -->
- [ ] D) Am you a student? <!-- feedback: Incorrect. "Am" only goes with "I". -->

### Explicación Pedagógica
To form yes/no questions with the verb 'to be', we follow the structure: "Verb + Subject + Complement?".

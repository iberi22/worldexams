# Bundle Mastery: Daily Routines and Time

This bundle evaluates vocabulary related to daily activities and the correct use of the Present Simple tense to describe habits and routines.

---

**Bloom:** Remember
**ICFES:** Inglés - Parte 1 (Vocabulario)

### Enunciado
Which action is the **first** thing most people do in the morning?

### Options
- [ ] A) Go to bed <!-- feedback: Incorrect. You do this at night. -->
- [x] B) Wake up <!-- feedback: Correct. Waking up is the starting point of the daily routine. -->
- [ ] C) Have lunch <!-- feedback: Incorrect. You do this in the afternoon. -->
- [ ] D) Do homework <!-- feedback: Incorrect. This is usually an afternoon or evening activity. -->

### Explicación Pedagógica
Sequencing daily activities is essential for describing routines.

## Question 3 (Difficulty D2)

**ID:** `CO-ING-6-P3-daily-routines-001-MASTERY-v3`
**Bloom:** Understand
**ICFES:** Inglés - Parte 4 (Conocimiento gramatical)

### Enunciado
Complete the sentence: **"I __________ a shower every morning."**

### Options
- [x] A) take <!-- feedback: Correct. "Take a shower" is the standard colocation. -->
- [ ] B) takes <!-- feedback: Incorrect. "Takes" is only for he, she, it. -->
- [ ] C) taking <!-- feedback: Incorrect. This is the continuous form; we need the simple present for a habit. -->
- [ ] D) am take <!-- feedback: Incorrect. You cannot mix "am" and the base verb like this. -->

### Explicación Pedagógica
The Present Simple is used for habits and permanent situations. For "I", we use the base form of the verb.

## Question 7 (Difficulty D4)

**ID:** `CO-ING-6-P3-daily-routines-001-MASTERY-v7`
**Bloom:** Analyze
**ICFES:** Inglés - Parte 4 (Conocimiento gramatical)

### Enunciado
Choose the correct **negative** sentence:

### Options
- [ ] A) He not play soccer on Mondays. <!-- feedback: Incorrect. Negative sentences in Present Simple need the auxiliary "does". -->
- [ ] B) He don't play soccer on Mondays. <!-- feedback: Incorrect. "Don't" is for I, you, we, they. -->
- [x] C) He doesn't play soccer on Mondays. <!-- feedback: Correct. "Doesn't" is the correct auxiliary for the third person singular negative. -->
- [ ] D) He doesn't plays soccer on Mondays. <!-- feedback: Incorrect. After "doesn't", the verb must be in the base form (no -s). -->

### Explicación Pedagógica
In negative sentences with "does not", the main verb loses the third-person -s.

Chronological order in a text is key to understanding the sequence of a routine.

### Enunciado
Which question is correct to ask about a **routine**?

### Options
- [ ] A) You watch TV at night? <!-- feedback: Incorrect. It needs the auxiliary "do". -->
- [x] B) Do you watch TV at night? <!-- feedback: Correct. The auxiliary "do" is used to start questions in the Present Simple for "you". -->
- [ ] C) Does you watch TV at night? <!-- feedback: Incorrect. "Does" is for he, she, it. -->

## Question 10 (Difficulty D5)

**ID:** `CO-ING-6-P3-daily-routines-001-MASTERY-v10`
**Bloom:** Evaluate
**ICFES:** Inglés - Parte 2 (Conversaciones)

### Contexto
**Friend:** "How often do you go to the park?"
**You:** "____________________"

### Enunciado
Choose the most logical response.

### Options
- [x] A) I usually go on Sundays. <!-- feedback: Correct. "How often" asks for frequency, and "usually" provides it. -->
- [ ] B) It is a big park. <!-- feedback: Incorrect. This describes the park, but doesn't answer the frequency question. -->
- [ ] C) I am in the park. <!-- feedback: Incorrect. This says where you are now, not how often you go. -->
- [ ] D) Yes, I do. <!-- feedback: Incorrect. "How often" is an open question, not a yes/no question. -->

### Explicación Pedagógica
Responding to Wh-questions (How often) requires specific information (frequency) rather than a simple yes or no.

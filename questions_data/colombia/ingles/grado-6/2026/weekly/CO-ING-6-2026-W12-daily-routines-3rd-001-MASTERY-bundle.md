# Bundle Mastery: Daily Routines (3rd Person) (W12)

This bundle covers daily routines in third person singular and simple present.

## Pregunta 1 [D1]

**ID:** `CO-ING-6-2026-W12-daily-routines-3rd-001-MASTERY-v1`
**Bloom:** Remember
**ICFES:** Comunicación y Representación
**Context:** In Bogotá, students learn third person verbs.

### Enunciado
He ___ breakfast at 7 AM.

### Opciones
- [x] A) has <!-- feedback: Correcto. He has = él tiene/desayuna. -->
- [ ] B) have <!-- feedback: Incorrecto. Have con I/you/we/they. -->
- [ ] C) haves <!-- feedback: Incorrecto. No existe. -->
- [ ] D) having <!-- feedback: Incorrecto. Gerundio. -->

### Explicación Pedagógica
Tercera persona singular: have → has, go → goes, do → does, watch → watches.

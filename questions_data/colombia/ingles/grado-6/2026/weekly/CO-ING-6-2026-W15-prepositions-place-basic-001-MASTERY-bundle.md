### Enunciado
Choose the correct preposition: "The cat is sleeping ___ the sofa."

### Opciones
- [ ] A) in
  <!-- feedback: in means inside something, and the cat is not inside the sofa. -->
- [x] C) on
  <!-- feedback: on indicates that the cat is resting on top of the sofa, which is the position described. -->

**Contexto:** Ejercicio de reflexion sobre la preparacion de un escritorio escolar.

### Enunciado
Where do you keep your pens? Choose the best answer.

### Opciones
- [ ] B) I keep them at school.
  <!-- feedback: at school indicates the general place and does not answer where the pens are kept. -->
- [x] A) I keep them in my pencil case.
  <!-- feedback: in my pencil case answers where the pens are kept, which is what the sentence asks. -->
- [ ] C) I keep them very well.
  <!-- feedback: very well answers how, not where. -->
- [ ] D) I keep them at eight o'clock.
  <!-- feedback: That answer gives a time and not a place. -->

### Explicacion Pedagogica
La palabra where exige una respuesta de lugar, y las demas opciones responden a otras preguntas como when o how. Reconocer el tipo de respuesta esperado evita que el estudiante produzca una frase correcta pero impertinente.

### Opciones
- [ ] B) behind
  <!-- feedback: behind would indicate the teacher is at the back of the classroom. -->
- [ ] C) near
  <!-- feedback: near is an approximation and does not describe the position of the teacher facing the class. -->

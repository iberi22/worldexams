# Bundle Mastery: Have / Has Got (W23)

This bundle covers have got and has got for possession and descriptions.

**ICFES:** Comunicación y Representación
**Context:** In Bogotá.

### Enunciado
"I ___ a new backpack."

### Opciones
- [x] A) have got <!-- feedback: Correcto. I have got. -->
- [ ] B) has got <!-- feedback: Incorrecto. Has = he/she/it. -->
- [ ] C) am got <!-- feedback: Incorrecto. -->
- [ ] D) are got <!-- feedback: Incorrecto. -->

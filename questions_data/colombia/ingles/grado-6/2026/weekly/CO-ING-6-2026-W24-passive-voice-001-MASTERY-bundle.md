# Bundle Mastery: Passive with By (W24)

## Pregunta 1 [D3]

**ID:** `CO-ING-6-2026-W24-passive-voice-001-MASTERY-v1`
**Bloom:** Remember
**ICFES:** Comunicación
**Context:** At Colegio San José de Manizales, teacher introduces passive.

### Enunciado
What is the structure of passive voice?

### Opciones
- [ ] A) subject + verb + object  <!-- feedback: Es activa. -->
- [ ] B) subject + have + past participle  <!-- feedback: Es present perfect. -->
- [x] C) subject + be + past participle  <!-- feedback: Correcto. Pasiva = verbo to be + past participle. -->
- [ ] D) subject + will + verb  <!-- feedback: Es futuro simple. -->

### Explicación Pedagógica
Voz pasiva: sujeto + be (conjugado) + past participle + (by + agente). "The letter is written by Maria."

# Weekly Pack W18 — Furniture and Appliances

**Grade:** 6° | **Periodo:** 2 | **Semana:** W18 | **Year:** 2026 | **CEFR:** A2

## Question 1 [D3]

**ID:** CO-ING-6-2026-W18-furniture-001-v1
**Bloom:** Remember
**ICFES:** Lexical Knowledge
**Expected_Success:** 0.82
**Contexto:** Naming the piece of furniture a person sleeps on.

### Enunciado
What is the name of the piece of furniture that a person sleeps in?

### Opciones
- [ ] A) the table <!-- feedback: A table is a flat surface for working or eating, and it is not where a person sleeps. -->
- [x] B) the bed <!-- feedback: A bed is the piece of furniture designed for sleeping, which is exactly what the definition describes. -->
- [ ] C) the chair <!-- feedback: A chair is a seat for one person, and it is not designed for sleeping. -->
- [ ] D) the wardrobe <!-- feedback: A wardrobe is a cupboard for hanging clothes, so it stores garments rather than holding a sleeping person. -->

### Explicación Pedagógica
The correct answer is "the bed". Furniture is the vocabulary of the space a family lives in, and pairing each item with its use is what makes the words usable. Colombian homes also have their own characteristic pieces such as the hamaca and the sala set, so the student can attach each English word to a real object rather than to a picture in a textbook.

## Question 2 [D3]

**ID:** CO-ING-6-2026-W18-furniture-002-v1
**Bloom:** Remember
**ICFES:** Grammatical Knowledge
**Expected_Success:** 0.80
**Contexto:** Using the indefinite article with a piece of furniture.

### Enunciado
Which sentence is correct?

### Opciones
- [ ] A) There is the sofa of living room. <!-- feedback: The article is wrong for a first mention and the phrase of living room uses Spanish word order rather than English. -->
- [ ] B) There is sofa in the living room. <!-- feedback: A single countable object needs the article, so leaving it out leaves the sentence incomplete. -->
- [ ] C) There is a sofas in the living room. <!-- feedback: A single sofa takes the singular, and the plural -s would call for more than one sofa or the word sofas. -->
- [x] D) There is a sofa in the living room. <!-- feedback: The first mention of a single object takes the indefinite article, and sofa begins with a consonant sound. -->

### Explicación Pedagógica
The correct answer is "There is a sofa in the living room". Articles on furniture are a small but persistent source of error, since a first mention in English takes a and Spanish often leaves the article out or moves it. Settling the article at the same time as the noun means the student does not have to think about two things at once when they move on to describing a whole room.

## Question 3 [D3-D4]

**ID:** CO-ING-6-2026-W18-furniture-003-v1
**Bloom:** Understand
**ICFES:** Reading Comprehension
**Expected_Success:** 0.78
**Contexto:** Locating a piece of furniture from a short description of a room.

### Enunciado
Read: "There is a fridge in the kitchen and a lamp next to the bed." Where is the lamp?

### Opciones
- [x] A) Next to the bed. <!-- feedback: The text states directly that the lamp is next to the bed, and next to expresses position beside another object. -->
- [ ] B) In the kitchen. <!-- feedback: The kitchen is where the fridge is, and the text places the lamp somewhere else entirely. -->
- [ ] C) On the table. <!-- feedback: The text never mentions a table in this description, so there is no support for that location. -->
- [ ] D) Under the window. <!-- feedback: No window and no under appear in the text, so this location is invented rather than read. -->

### Explicación Pedagógica
The correct answer is "Next to the bed". A phrase such as next to describes a relation between two objects rather than a named place, and recognising that a description can locate something by its neighbour is a useful reading skill. The distractors include a real location from the text and two invented ones, so the student has to track which object each one belongs to.

## Question 4 [D4]

**ID:** CO-ING-6-2026-W18-furniture-004-v1
**Bloom:** Apply
**ICFES:** Grammatical Knowledge
**Expected_Success:** 0.76
**Contexto:** Using there are with a plural piece of furniture.

### Enunciado
Which sentence correctly describes a dining table with six chairs around it?

### Opciones
- [ ] A) There are six chair around the table. <!-- feedback: The noun stays singular, and after a number above one English requires the plural form chairs. -->
- [ ] B) There is six chairs around the table. <!-- feedback: The verb is singular while the subject is plural, so the two do not agree in this sentence. -->
- [x] C) There are six chairs around the table. <!-- feedback: The plural noun chairs takes the plural verb are, and around describes their position relative to the table. -->
- [ ] D) There are six chairs around a table. <!-- feedback: The article a is wrong here, because the table is a particular one already mentioned rather than an unspecified object. -->

### Explicación Pedagógica
The correct answer is "There are six chairs around the table". Three separate agreements have to hold at once, the verb with the plural subject, the noun with the number, and the article with what is already known, and each distractor breaks a different one of them. Sentences that require several agreements to be right at the same time are where A2 writing most often breaks down in Grade 6.

**ICFES:** Lexical Knowledge
**Expected_Success:** 0.74
**Contexto:** Distinguishing a household appliance from a piece of furniture.

### Enunciado
Which of these is an appliance used in the kitchen?

### Opciones
- [x] A) the microwave <!-- feedback: A microwave is an electrical appliance that heats food, which is what an appliance in the kitchen does. -->
- [ ] B) the bookshelf <!-- feedback: A bookshelf is a piece of furniture for holding books, and it uses no electricity at all. -->
- [ ] C) the coffee table <!-- feedback: A coffee table is furniture for placing things in front of a sofa, not a device that processes food. -->

## Question 6 [D4]

**ID:** CO-ING-6-2026-W18-furniture-006-v1
**Bloom:** Apply
**ICFES:** Pragmatic Competence
**Expected_Success:** 0.72
**Contexto:** Asking a shop assistant about an item in a furniture store.

### Enunciado
You are buying a bookcase and you want to know its price. What do you say?

### Opciones
- [ ] A) What is this bookcase? <!-- feedback: What asks for the name or nature of the thing rather than its cost, so it would not get a price. -->
- [ ] B) How many is this bookcase? <!-- feedback: How many asks for a number of items or things, and a price is not expressed as a count. -->
- [ ] C) Where is this bookcase? <!-- feedback: Where asks for a location, which tells the customer nothing about what they will have to pay. -->
- [x] D) How much is this bookcase? <!-- feedback: How much asks for the price of a single item, which is exactly the information a customer needs. -->

### Explicación Pedagógica
The correct answer is "How much is this bookcase?" Buying something in English means knowing which question word buys the information you need, and how much for a price is the pair students meet in every shop in every English-speaking country. Getting this wrong in a real transaction is immediately obvious, which makes it a good item to practise producing rather than merely recognising.

## Question 7 [D4-D5]

**ID:** CO-ING-6-2026-W18-furniture-007-v1
**Bloom:** Apply
**ICFES:** Grammatical Knowledge
**Expected_Success:** 0.70
**Contexto:** Using a possessive to say who a piece of furniture belongs to.

### Enunciado
Complete the sentence: "This is ________ desk. I bought it for my office."

### Opciones
- [ ] A) I <!-- feedback: I is the subject pronoun, and a possessive is needed to show that the desk belongs to the speaker. -->
- [x] B) my <!-- feedback: My names the speaker's own desk, and the speaker goes on to explain the reason for buying it. -->
- [ ] C) me <!-- feedback: Me is the object pronoun, and it cannot be placed in front of a noun to show ownership. -->
- [ ] D) mine is <!-- feedback: Mine is a complete pronoun that stands on its own and cannot be joined to the noun desk in this way. -->

### Explicación Pedagógica
The correct answer is "my". The four options are the four things students reach for when they need a possessive, and telling a possessive adjective from a subject pronoun, an object pronoun and a standalone pronoun is the core distinction. Furniture is a good context for it because ownership genuinely varies from item to item in a shared home, so the possessive carries real meaning rather than being decoration.

## Question 8 [D4-D5]

**ID:** CO-ING-6-2026-W18-furniture-008-v1
**Bloom:** Analyze
**ICFES:** Reading Comprehension
**Expected_Success:** 0.68
**Contexto:** Identifying which description of a room is internally consistent.

### Enunciado
Read: "The bedroom is small but comfortable. There is a bed, a small table and a lamp next to it." What can you say about the room?

### Opciones
- [x] A) It is small and it contains a bed and a table. <!-- feedback: The text describes the bedroom as small and then lists a bed and a small table among the objects it contains. -->
- [ ] B) It has more space than the kitchen. <!-- feedback: The text compares the room only with being comfortable, and it says nothing about the kitchen. -->
- [ ] C) It is a large room with many chairs. <!-- feedback: The text calls the room small and mentions a bed and a table rather than a set of chairs. -->
- [ ] D) It is used for cooking. <!-- feedback: Cooking belongs to the kitchen, and this text describes a place for sleeping instead. -->

### Explicación Pedagógica
The correct answer is "It is small and it contains a bed and a table". Combining the evaluative adjective small with the list of objects is a two-part comprehension task, and the but in the text shows that small and comfortable are not in conflict, which is the point students most often miss. Keeping the two kinds of information from a description separate is what lets a student answer questions about a text without guessing.

## Question 9 [D5]

**ID:** CO-ING-6-2026-W18-furniture-009-v1
**Bloom:** Analyze
**ICFES:** Grammatical Knowledge
**Expected_Success:** 0.65
**Contexto:** Finding the error in a sentence about furniture.

### Enunciado
A student writes: "There is a two chairs in the dining room." What is the problem?

### Opciones
- [ ] A) The verb is in the wrong tense. <!-- feedback: There is is the correct existential form and no tense change is called for by this sentence. -->
- [ ] B) The word chairs is a verb. <!-- feedback: Chairs is a plural noun, and it is used correctly as the subject of the existential clause. -->
- [ ] C) The article a should be removed. <!-- feedback: The article is required before a counted noun, so removing it would make the sentence worse. -->
- [x] D) The noun is singular after a number above one. <!-- feedback: After the number two English requires the plural noun chairs, so the singular chair is the error. -->

### Explicación Pedagógica
The correct answer is "The noun is singular after a number above one". The sentence has the right existential structure, the right article and the right noun, and the single fault is the plural marking, which makes it a clean example of an error that is easy to locate and easy to repair. Being able to isolate one fault in an otherwise correct sentence is what lets a student fix their own writing.

## Question 10 [D5]

**ID:** CO-ING-6-2026-W18-furniture-010-v1
**Bloom:** Evaluate
**ICFES:** Pragmatic Competence
**Expected_Success:** 0.62
**Contexto:** Choosing the best description of a bedroom for a school project.

### Enunciado
You are writing a short description of your bedroom for a school project. Which description is best?

### Opciones
- [ ] A) My bedroom is very beautiful and my mother likes it. <!-- feedback: This is an opinion about the room rather than a description of it, and it adds nothing the reader can picture. -->
- [ ] B) My bedroom has furniture. <!-- feedback: This names the category rather than any object, so a reader learns nothing about the room itself. -->
- [x] C) My bedroom is small. There is a bed, a desk next to the window and a bookshelf full of books. <!-- feedback: This gives the size, three specific objects and their position, which is the level of detail a description needs. -->
- [ ] D) There is a bed in my bedroom and there is a desk in my bedroom. <!-- feedback: The repetition makes the sentences clumsy, and the description is far too short to convey the room. -->

### Explicación Pedagógica
The correct answer is the one giving size, specific objects and their position. A description is judged by how well someone who cannot see it can picture the space, and that requires concrete detail in a sensible order rather than evaluation or repetition. This is the same criterion the exam applies when it asks for a description, so practising it here is directly transferable to the written work.

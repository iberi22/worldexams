Which word names the flat board where the teacher writes with chalk or marker?

### Opciones
- [x] C) board
  <!-- feedback: board is the standard name for the surface where you write in a classroom. -->
- [ ] A) desk
  <!-- feedback: desk is the table of the student or the teacher and not the vertical surface you write on. -->

**Contexto:** Ejercicio de descripcion del inventario escolar en una presentacion oral.

### Enunciado
Which question correctly asks about the quantity of notebooks in the room?

### Opciones
- [ ] B) How much are the notebooks?
  <!-- feedback: how much is used with uncountable nouns and notebooks is a countable object. -->
- [x] A) How many notebooks are there?
  <!-- feedback: how many is used with countable nouns in the plural, and how many books are there follows the correct order. -->
- [ ] C) How old is the notebook?
  <!-- feedback: That question asks for the age of a notebook, a question that makes no sense for an object. -->
- [ ] D) Where is the notebook?
  <!-- feedback: That question asks for a place and the prompt is looking for a quantity, not a location. -->

### Explicacion Pedagogica
La pareja How much y How many se decide por la propiedad del sustantivo, no por su traduccion. Los cuadernos se cuentan, de modo que corresponde How many, y el orden How many + sustantivo + verbo se memoriza como estructura fija.

**Contexto:** Lectura de un texto sobre el horario de una clase de ingles.

### Enunciado
Read: "Our English class is on Monday and Wednesday at nine in the morning." When is the English class?

### Opciones
- [x] B) On Monday and Wednesday
  <!-- feedback: The text gives those two specific days, and the other options do not appear in the timetable. -->
- [ ] A) On Monday only
  <!-- feedback: Only would be the answer if the text named a single day, and here two appear. -->
- [ ] C) On Wednesday at nine
  <!-- feedback: That option mixes a correct day with a correct hour, but leaves out the other day of the timetable. -->

## Question 10 [D3-D4]
**ID:** CO-ING-6-2026-W06-classroom-objects-001-MASTERY-bundle-v10
**Bloom:** Apply
**ICFES:** Uso de la lengua
**Expected_Success:** 0.80
**Contexto:** Ejercicio de produccion escrita para un mural del aula sobre el inventario escolar.

### Enunciado
Which sentence keeps the article and the verb consistent with a plural subject?

### Opciones
- [ ] A) We have three glue and two scissors.
  <!-- feedback: Glue is uncountable and does not accept the numeral three in front of it, not even with a hyphen. -->
- [ ] B) There is two rulers on the desk.
  <!-- feedback: The verb is does not agree with the plural subject two rulers, and it must be are. -->
- [x] C) There are two rulers and a sharpener on the desk.
  <!-- feedback: The verb are agrees with two rulers, and the singular sharpener is supported by the article a. -->
- [ ] D) A books are in the shelf.
  <!-- feedback: The article a cannot go with a plural noun such as books. -->

### Explicacion Pedagogica
Revisar la concordancia entre el verbo y el sujeto es el control clave en estas oraciones de descripcion. Ademas, un sustantivo plural siempre requiere articulo plural como en The books, y los incontables como glue o paper no aceptan numeral delante.

# Bundle Mastery: Present Perfect vs Past Simple (W07)

## Pregunta 1 [D3]

**ID:** `CO-ING-6-2026-W07-present-perfect-001-MASTERY-v1`
**Bloom:** Remember
**ICFES:** Comunicación
**Context:** At Colegio Champagnat de Manizales, students review present perfect.

### Enunciado
What is the structure of present perfect?

### Opciones
- [ ] A) subject + past tense verb  <!-- feedback: Pasado simple. -->
- [x] B) subject + have/has + past participle  <!-- feedback: Correcto. -->
- [ ] C) subject + be + verb-ing  <!-- feedback: Presente continuo. -->
- [ ] D) subject + will + verb  <!-- feedback: Futuro simple. -->

### Explicación Pedagógica
Present perfect: sujeto + have/has + past participle. Conecta pasado con presente.

What does "I have just eaten" mean?

### Opciones
- [ ] A) I ate a long time ago.  <!-- feedback: Incorrecto. -->
- [x] B) I ate a very short time ago.  <!-- feedback: Correcto. Just = acabar de. -->

### Opciones
- [ ] A) She has goed to the park.  <!-- feedback: "Goed" no existe. -->

### Enunciado
Which question is correct?

### Opciones
- [ ] A) How much books do you have?
  <!-- feedback: how much is used with uncountable nouns, and books is a countable noun. -->
- [x] C) How many books do you have?
  <!-- feedback: how many is used with countable nouns, and books can be counted, so the question is correct. -->
- [ ] B) How much are the books?
  <!-- feedback: That structure with are goes with uncountable nouns such as money or water. -->

La eleccion entre how much y how many depende de una sola pregunta: se puede contar el sustantivo con numeros. Los libros se cuentan, la moneda no, y esa comprobacion resuelve practicamente todos los casos de la unidad.

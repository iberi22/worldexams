### Enunciado
Convert 15:00 to the twelve-hour clock.

### Opciones
- [ ] A) 13:00
  <!-- feedback: 13:00 corresponds to one o'clock and not to fifteen in the 24-hour format. -->
- [ ] B) 15:00 p.m.
  <!-- feedback: On a 12-hour clock the number cannot reach fifteen, because the cycle runs from one to twelve. -->
- [x] D) 3:00 p.m.
  <!-- feedback: 15:00 in the 24-hour format equals three in the afternoon on a 12-hour clock. -->

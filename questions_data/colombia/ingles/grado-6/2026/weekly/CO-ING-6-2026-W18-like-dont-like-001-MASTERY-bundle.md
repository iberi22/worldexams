# Bundle Mastery: Like and Don't Like (W18)

This bundle covers expressing likes and dislikes about food.

## Pregunta 1 [D1]

**ID:** `CO-ING-6-2026-W18-like-dont-like-001-MASTERY-v1`
**Bloom:** Remember
**ICFES:** Comunicación y Representación
**Context:** In Bogotá.

### Enunciado
To say you like something: "I ___ pizza."

### Opciones
- [x] A) like <!-- feedback: Correcto. I like = me gusta. -->
- [ ] B) likes <!-- feedback: Incorrecto. Likes es tercera persona. -->
- [ ] C) liking <!-- feedback: Incorrecto. Gerundio. -->
- [ ] D) am like <!-- feedback: Incorrecto. No es correcto. -->

### Explicación Pedagógica
I like + noun/verb-ing. "I like pizza." "I like eating pizza."

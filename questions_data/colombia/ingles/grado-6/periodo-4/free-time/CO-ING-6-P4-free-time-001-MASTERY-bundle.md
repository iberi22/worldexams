# Bundle Mastery: Free time activities and Hobbies

This bundle evaluates vocabulary related to hobbies and sports, as well as the expression of preferences (likes/dislikes) and the use of the Present Continuous for actions happening now.

---

## Question 1 (Difficulty D1)

**ID:** `CO-ING-6-P4-free-time-001-MASTERY-v1`
**Bloom:** Remember
**ICFES:** Inglés - Parte 1 (Vocabulario)

### Enunciado
Which of these is a **sport** you play with a ball and your feet?

### Options
- [x] A) Soccer <!-- feedback: Correct. In Colombia and most of the world, soccer is the sport played with the feet and a ball. -->
- [ ] B) Swimming <!-- feedback: Incorrect. You do this in water; no ball is used. -->
- [ ] C) Reading <!-- feedback: Incorrect. This is a hobby, not a sport. -->
- [ ] D) Painting <!-- feedback: Incorrect. This is an artistic activity. -->

### Explicación Pedagógica
Basic vocabulary for leisure activities includes distinguishing between sports and other hobbies.

## Question 4 (Difficulty D2)

**ID:** `CO-ING-6-P4-free-time-001-MASTERY-v4`
**Bloom:** Understand
**ICFES:** Inglés - Parte 4 (Conocimiento gramatical)

### Enunciado
What are you doing **right now**? **"I __________ a book."**

### Options
- [ ] A) read <!-- feedback: Incorrect. This is for routines, not for now. -->
- [x] B) am reading <!-- feedback: Correct. Present Continuous (be + ing) is used for actions happening at the moment. -->
- [ ] C) is reading <!-- feedback: Incorrect. "Is" doesn't go with "I". -->
- [ ] D) reading <!-- feedback: Incorrect. You need the auxiliary verb "am". -->

### Explicación Pedagógica
The Present Continuous is essential for describing ongoing actions (Subject + Be + Verb-ing).

## Question 5 (Difficulty D3)

**ID:** `CO-ING-6-P4-free-time-001-MASTERY-v5`
**Bloom:** Apply
**ICFES:** Inglés - Parte 4 (Conocimiento gramatical)

### Enunciado
Choose the correct **negative** form for preferences: **"I __________ like eating broccoli."**

### Options
- [ ] A) not <!-- feedback: Incorrect. You need the auxiliary "do". -->
- [ ] B) am not <!-- feedback: Incorrect. We don't use 'to be' with 'like' in the negative. -->
- [x] C) don't <!-- feedback: Correct. "Don't" (do not) is the auxiliary for "I" in negative preferences. -->
- [ ] D) doesn't <!-- feedback: Incorrect. "Doesn't" is for he, she, it. -->

### Explicación Pedagógica
Negative sentences with "like" use the auxiliary "do not" (don't) or "does not" (doesn't).

## Question 10 (Difficulty D5)

**ID:** `CO-ING-6-P4-free-time-001-MASTERY-v10`
**Bloom:** Evaluate
**ICFES:** Inglés - Parte 2 (Conversaciones)

### Contexto
**A:** "Do you like dancing salsa?"
**B:** "____________________"

### Enunciado
Choose the best response for B.

### Options
- [x] A) Yes, I do. It is very fun! <!-- feedback: Correct. Logical answer to a "Do you...?" question. -->
- [ ] B) No, I am not. <!-- feedback: Incorrect. This answers an "Are you...?" question. -->
- [ ] C) I can dance salsa. <!-- feedback: Incorrect. Although related, it doesn't directly answer the "Do you like" question. -->
- [ ] D) I am dancing now. <!-- feedback: Incorrect. This describes a current action, not a preference. -->

### Explicación Pedagógica
Matching the auxiliary in the question (Do) with the auxiliary in the short answer (do/don't) is a key grammar point.

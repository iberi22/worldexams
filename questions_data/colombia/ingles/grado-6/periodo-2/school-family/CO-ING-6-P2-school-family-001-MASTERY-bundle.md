## Question 3 (Difficulty D2)

**ID:** `CO-ING-6-P2-school-family-001-MASTERY-v3`
**Bloom:** Understand
**ICFES:** Inglés - Parte 4 (Conocimiento gramatical)

### Enunciado
Complete the sentence: **"In my classroom, __________ twenty desks."**

### Options
- [ ] A) there is <!-- feedback: Incorrect. "There is" is used for singular nouns. -->
- [x] B) there are <!-- feedback: Correct. "There are" is used for plural nouns like "twenty desks." -->
- [ ] C) they are <!-- feedback: Incorrect. "They are" means "Ellos son," not "Hay." -->
- [ ] D) is there <!-- feedback: Incorrect. This is the question form, not the statement form. -->

### Explicación Pedagógica
"There is" and "There are" are used to express existence. Use "is" for one thing and "are" for two or more.

### Enunciado
Choose the sentence that uses **"have / has"** correctly for family descriptions:

### Options
- [ ] A) My brother have a blue backpack. <!-- feedback: Incorrect. "He" (my brother) uses "has". -->
- [x] B) My mother has two sisters. <!-- feedback: Correct. "She" (my mother) uses "has" for the third person singular. -->

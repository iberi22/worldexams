### Opciones

  <!-- feedback: 'Would rather' takes the past form of 'be' in this construction, so 'be over' is what the phrase requires. The contrast with 'have it dragged out' sets a bare infinitive against a verb with its object, and only this option completes the first half. -->

### Opciones

  <!-- feedback: 'Not until...' is a negative adverbial, and the rule is that inversion follows: the auxiliary 'did' moves in front of the subject and the main verb returns to its base form, giving 'did the first clean sample come back'. -->
- [ ] A) was
  <!-- feedback: 'Was' would form a passive, but the subject 'the first clean sample' is the one that comes back, not the one being acted upon, so a passive sentence cannot express this meaning. -->
- [ ] B) had
  <!-- feedback: The past perfect auxiliary belongs to a subordinate clause after 'when' or 'before'. Here the clause is the main one, so it needs the inverted auxiliary 'did' rather than 'had'. -->

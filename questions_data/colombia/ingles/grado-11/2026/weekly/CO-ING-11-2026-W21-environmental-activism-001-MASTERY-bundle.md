**Contexto:** Discussing environmental activism in Cali.

### Enunciado
Complete the following sentence about environmental activism: 'If the monitoring station __________ funded, this stretch of the river would be carrying three times the measured load.' (Topic: second conditional with a present result)

### Opciones
- [x] B) were
  <!-- feedback: 'Were' after 'if' for a present hypothetical is the standard second conditional, and the main clause 'would be carrying' confirms that pairing. The clause describes an unrealised present condition. -->
- [ ] A) had been
  <!-- feedback: 'Had been' would make the condition past, which would pair with 'would have been carrying'. The main clause here has no perfect, so the condition has to be present rather than past. -->
- [ ] C) will be
  <!-- feedback: 'Will be' states a future condition as certain, and a conditional clause under 'if' does not assert that the funding will happen. The sentence describes a situation that does not obtain. -->
- [ ] D) is
  <!-- feedback: 'Is' would state the condition as fact, and nothing in the sentence says the station is funded or not. The clause is hypothetical, which is why the subjunctive 'were' is used. -->

### Explicacion Pedagogica
A second conditional pairs an unrealised present condition with a present or future result. At C1+ the two halves must agree, and because the main clause already shows 'would' plus a bare infinitive, the if-clause has to use 'were' rather than a plain present.

### Explicacion Pedagogica
'As a rule of thumb rather than a demonstrated finding' is a standard C1+ hedge marking the epistemic status of a claim. This item asks the student to read that status rather than the content, and each distractor answers a question the sentence does not put.

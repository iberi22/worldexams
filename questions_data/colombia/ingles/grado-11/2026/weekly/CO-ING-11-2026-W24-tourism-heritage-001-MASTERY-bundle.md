### Enunciado

### Opciones
- [x] C) were
  <!-- feedback: 'The restored balconies were visited' is the simple past passive: the balconies receive the action and the sentence reports a completed pattern of events. The rare visiting is what the 'so rarely' clause builds on, and it is compatible with a simple past form. -->

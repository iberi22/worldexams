Complete the following sentence about comprehensive review 1: 'Not until the final audit was published __________ any of the figures be considered official.'

### Opciones

  <!-- feedback: Incorrect. 'Did' cannot be followed by 'be' + participle; 'did any of the figures be considered' is not a possible English structure. -->
- [x] B) could
  <!-- feedback: Correct. A modal inverts after 'Not until' and is followed by a passive infinitive: 'could any of the figures be considered official'. -->

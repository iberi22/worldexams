### Enunciado
Complete the following sentence about workplace evolution: 'Not until the redundancy package was announced __________ the unions agree to reopen talks.' (Topic: 'not until' with an inverted clause)

### Opciones
- [ ] A) had
  <!-- feedback: 'Not until' takes an inverted auxiliary before the subject. 'Had the unions agreed' would place the subject first, which is the pattern 'not until' forbids. -->

  <!-- feedback: 'Not until the package was announced did the unions agree'. The auxiliary is fronted to invert the clause, which is the defining feature of the structure. -->

  <!-- feedback: 'Were' would form a subjunctive or a passive. Here the main clause is active and the subject is 'the unions', so a plural form is not used. -->

## Question 20 [D9-D10]

**Contexto:** An employee in Pereira asks her manager about flexible hours.

### Enunciado
Read the conversation.

Juliana: I was wondering if I could start at seven and leave at four on Fridays. I'm taking an evening course at the university.
Manager: That should be fine, as long as the team knows how to reach you.

Why does Juliana use "I was wondering if I could..."?

### Opciones

  <!-- feedback: Incorrect. "Wondering" here does not show confusion; the structure is a conventional form for making a polite request. -->

  <!-- feedback: Incorrect. Although "was wondering" is in the past continuous, it refers to a present request; the past form makes it softer, not past in meaning. -->

### Explicacion Pedagogica
In English, past forms like "I was wondering" make requests more polite and less direct. This pragmatic use of tense is common in the workplace, where speakers soften requests to people in higher positions.

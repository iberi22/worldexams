### Enunciado
Complete the following sentence about education systems: 'The inspection covered forty schools; it __________ only twelve, all of them rural.' (Topic: ellipsis of a repeated subject)

### Opciones
- [x] A) covered
  <!-- feedback: After a semicolon the repeated subject is normally left out, so 'it covered only twelve' is understood and 'covered' alone supplies the verb. The elliptical subject points back to the one named before the semicolon. -->

  <!-- feedback: 'Was covering' would add a progressive reading that the sentence does not need, since the inspection of the first group is already complete and the second group is simply smaller. Ellipsis removes the subject, not the tense choice. -->

**Bloom:** Analyze
**ICFES:** Structural Cloze
**Expected_Success:** 0.49
**Contexto:** Discussing energy sources in Manizales.

### Enunciado
Complete the following sentence about energy sources: 'Not until the turbines were connected to the grid __________ the household see any benefit from the wind farm.' (Topic: 'not until' with an inverted clause)

### Opciones
- [ ] A) had
  <!-- feedback: 'Not until' inverts the main clause, placing the auxiliary before the subject 'the household'. 'Had the household seen' would leave the subject first, which the pattern forbids. -->
- [x] B) did
  <!-- feedback: 'Not until the turbines were connected did the household see'. The auxiliary is fronted to invert the clause, which is the defining feature of the structure. -->
- [ ] C) was
  <!-- feedback: 'Was' would suggest a passive or a singular subject. The subject is singular but the verb is active, so a passive reading is not available. -->
- [ ] D) do
  <!-- feedback: 'Do' is present tense and cannot invert a past sequence in which the connection had already been completed. -->

### Explicación Pedagógica
Inversion with 'not until' frontes the auxiliary so that the subject follows the verb: 'not until X did Y happen'. The auxiliary 'did' carries the tense of the main clause while the subject 'the household' is delayed. Selecting a perfect or passive auxiliary would introduce a relation of time or voice that the sentence does not state.

### Enunciado
A student writes, 'Despite the wind farm produces electricity without burning fuel, its output varies with wind conditions.' Which revision fixes the connector structure while preserving the intended contrast?

### Opciones
- [x] A) Although the wind farm produces electricity without burning fuel, its output varies with wind conditions.
  <!-- feedback: 'Although' can introduce a clause with a subject and finite verb, preserving the contrast between fuel-free generation and variable output. -->
- [ ] B) Despite of the wind farm produces electricity without burning fuel, its output varies with wind conditions.
  <!-- feedback: 'Despite of' is not the standard construction, and 'despite' cannot directly introduce this finite clause. -->
- [ ] C) In spite the wind farm produces electricity without burning fuel, its output varies with wind conditions.
  <!-- feedback: The expression requires 'in spite of' followed by a noun phrase or gerund construction, not this directly attached finite clause. -->
- [ ] D) Because the wind farm produces electricity without burning fuel, its output varies with wind conditions.
  <!-- feedback: 'Because' changes the contrast into a causal claim, incorrectly making fuel-free generation the reason for variable output. -->

### Explicacion Pedagogica
'Although' introduces a finite concessive clause. 'Despite' normally takes a noun phrase or gerund construction, so replacing it with 'although' repairs the sentence without changing the intended contrast.

### Enunciado
Complete the following sentence about comprehensive review 3: 'Not until the marking scheme was published in full __________ the candidates see how the rubric was weighted.' (Topic: 'not until' with an inverted clause)

### Opciones
- [x] A) did
  <!-- feedback: 'Not until the scheme was published did the candidates see'. The auxiliary is fronted to invert the clause, which is the defining feature of the structure. -->
- [ ] B) had
  <!-- feedback: 'Not until' inverts the main clause, placing the auxiliary before the subject 'the candidates'. 'Had the candidates seen' would leave the subject first, which the pattern forbids. -->
- [ ] C) was
  <!-- feedback: 'Was' would suggest a passive or a singular subject. The subject is plural but the verb is active, so a passive reading is not available. -->
- [ ] D) do
  <!-- feedback: 'Do' is present tense and cannot invert a past sequence in which the scheme had already been published. -->

### Explicación Pedagógica
Inversion with 'not until' frontes the auxiliary so that the subject follows the verb: 'not until X did Y happen'. The auxiliary 'did' carries the tense of the main clause while the subject is delayed. Selecting a perfect or passive auxiliary would state a relation of time or voice that the sentence does not express.

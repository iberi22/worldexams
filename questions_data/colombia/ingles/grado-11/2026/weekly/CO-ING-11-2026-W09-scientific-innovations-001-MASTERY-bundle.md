### Enunciado
In the context of scientific innovations, which word best completes the following sentence: 'The early prototype produced results so inconsistent that the team had to __________ the whole procedure and build the apparatus again from the ground up'?

### Opciones
- [x] B) discard
  <!-- feedback: 'To discard something is to throw it away as worthless. Inconsistent results mean the procedure cannot be trusted, and building the apparatus again is exactly what happens when the earlier version is discarded and replaced. -->
- [ ] A) retain
  <!-- feedback: 'To retain' is to keep something, the direct opposite of what the sentence describes. If the team had kept the procedure, there would be no reason to start again from the ground up. -->
- [ ] C) refine
  <!-- feedback: 'To refine' is to improve something that already works by making small adjustments. Inconsistent results are not a flaw to polish; they show the method is fundamentally unsound and has to be replaced. -->
- [ ] D) postpone
  <!-- feedback: 'To postpone' is to delay something to a later time. The sentence says the team started the work over immediately, which is the opposite of putting it off for another date. -->

### Explicacion Pedagogica
'Discard', 'retain' and 'refine' form a contrast C1+ students must learn together: discarding rejects a thing outright, retaining keeps it, and refining improves what already functions. The phrase 'from the ground up' is the grammatical evidence for which of the three applies.

### Opciones
- [x] A) attribute
  <!-- feedback: 'To attribute an effect to a change is to assign the cause. When two experiments differ in only one controlled way, that variable is the one the result can be attributed to, and the reviewers are saying the design no longer allows it. -->

### Enunciado
Complete the following sentence about globalization: 'Not until the tariff was removed __________ local manufacturers start competing with imports on price.' (Topic: 'not until' with an inverted clause)

### Opciones
- [ ] A) had
  <!-- feedback: 'Had' would form a past perfect and need the participle 'started', placing the competing before another past point. The sentence describes the moment competing began, which is a single past event. -->
- [x] B) did
  <!-- feedback: 'Not until' is a fronted negative that inverts the main clause, and 'did' carries the simple past: 'not until the tariff was removed did local manufacturers start'. The main verb returns to its base form 'start'. -->
- [ ] C) could
  <!-- feedback: 'Could' would add a sense of ability or possibility rather than fixing the time of the event. The clause names the condition under which competing started, which is a temporal trigger and not a capability. -->

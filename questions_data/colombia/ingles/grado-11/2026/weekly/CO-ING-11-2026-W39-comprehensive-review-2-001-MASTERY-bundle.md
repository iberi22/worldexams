Mother: "How was the English exam?"
Simón: "A piece of cake! I finished in forty minutes and had time to check all my answers."

What does Simón mean?

### Opciones
- [ ] A) He ate cake during the exam.
  <!-- feedback: Incorrect. This is a literal reading; 'a piece of cake' is an idiom about difficulty, not food. -->
- [x] B) The exam was very easy for him.
  <!-- feedback: Correct. 'A piece of cake' means something very easy, and finishing early with time to check supports this. -->
- [ ] C) The exam was too long for the time given.
  <!-- feedback: Incorrect. He finished early and had extra time, which shows the opposite. -->
- [ ] D) He received a prize for his result.
  <!-- feedback: Incorrect. The dialogue says nothing about a prize; he describes how the exam felt. -->

### Explicacion Pedagogica
'A piece of cake' is an informal idiom meaning that something is very easy. Simón's explanation, finishing in forty minutes with time to check his answers, gives evidence that matches the idiom's meaning.

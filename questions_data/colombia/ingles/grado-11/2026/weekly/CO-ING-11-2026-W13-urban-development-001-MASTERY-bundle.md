### Enunciado
Complete the following sentence about urban development: 'Not until the environmental impact assessment was published __________ the company withdraw its planning application.' (Topic: 'not until' with an inverted past clause)

### Opciones
- [x] C) did
  <!-- feedback: 'Not until' is a fronted negative that inverts the main clause, and 'did' carries the simple past: 'not until the assessment was published did the company withdraw'. The main verb returns to its base form 'withdraw'. -->
- [ ] A) had
  <!-- feedback: 'Had' would form a past perfect and need the participle 'withdrawn', placing the withdrawal before another past point. The sentence reports the decision as an event following the publication. -->
- [ ] B) must
  <!-- feedback: 'Must' expresses obligation or deduction about the present, and the clause describes a completed past action. Nothing in the sentence states an obligation or supports a deduction about now. -->
- [ ] D) should
  <!-- feedback: 'Should' expresses obligation or expectation about what ought to happen, and the withdrawal in the sentence is a completed fact rather than a recommendation. No advice is being given. -->

### Explicacion Pedagogica
Two independent decisions meet here: the fronted negative justifies the inversion, and the main clause fixes the auxiliary as 'did'. The modal options are the ones that appear when a student reads the withdrawal as advice rather than as a record of what the company did.

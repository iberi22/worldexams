## Part 6: Reading Comprehension

### Text: The Great Filter: Past or Future?
Robin Hanson's "Great Filter" theory provides a chilling framework for interpreting the Fermi Paradox. The filter represents an evolutionary or technological step that is so difficult that almost no species can surpass it. If the filter is behind us—meaning it was a step in our biological past like the transition from single-celled to multicellular life—then we may be one of the rare survivors of a cosmic lottery. However, if we discover evidence of complex, extinct civilizations on Mars or other planets, it would suggest that the filter lies ahead of us. This would imply that advanced life is common, but self-destruction (through nuclear war, AI, or ecological collapse) is the nearly inevitable "filter" that prevents interstellar expansion. For Hanson, "no news is good news": the lack of evidence for alien life suggests that we may have already passed the hardest hurdles.

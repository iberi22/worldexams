## Part 6: Reading Comprehension

### Text: The Gig Economy and the Precarity of Work
The "gig economy" is often celebrated for the autonomy it provides, allowing individuals to work when and where they choose. However, critics argue that this autonomy comes at a high price: the transfer of risk from capital to labor. In traditional employment, the company bears the cost of downtime, equipment, and insurance. In the gig economy, these costs are shifted to the worker. This leads to a state of "precarity"—a condition of existence without predictability or security. While some workers thrive as high-skilled freelancers, many find themselves trapped in low-wage "micro-tasks" mediated by algorithms that can terminate their livelihood with a single update. The challenge for modern society is to maintain the benefits of flexibility while ensuring that the digital frontier does not become a new era of exploitation.

## Part 6: Reading Comprehension

### Text: FOMO and the Social Comparison Trap
The ubiquity of social media has amplified a psychological phenomenon known as FOMO—the "Fear of Missing Out." As users scroll through carefully curated feeds of others' highlight reels, they often fall into the "social comparison trap," where they compare their own messy, everyday reality to the idealized versions of other people's lives. This can lead to feelings of inadequacy, anxiety, and loneliness. Psychologists argue that social media creates a "distorted mirror" that emphasizes superficial metrics like popularity and physical appearance over genuine connection. To combat this, experts recommend "digital hygiene" practices, such as setting boundaries on usage and focusing on high-quality, off-line interactions.

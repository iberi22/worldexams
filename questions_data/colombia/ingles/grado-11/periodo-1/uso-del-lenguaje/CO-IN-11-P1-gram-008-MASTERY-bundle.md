## Part 6: Reading Comprehension

### Text: The Rise of Surveillance Capitalism
Shoshana Zuboff's concept of "surveillance capitalism" describes a new economic order that claims human experience as free raw material for hidden commercial practices of extraction, prediction, and sales. In this model, the "product" is not just the service you use, but the behavioral data you leave behind. This data is converted into "prediction products" that are sold in a new kind of marketplace: behavioral futures markets. While traditional capitalism focused on the production of goods, surveillance capitalism focuses on the modification of behavior. The danger, Zuboff argues, is not just the loss of privacy, but the loss of our "right to the future tense"—our ability to act freely without being steered by invisible algorithms.

### Enunciado
In recent years, Artificial Intelligence (AI) has advanced at ____ unprecedented pace.

### Options
- [ ] A) a <!-- feedback: Incorrect. "Unprecedented" begins with a vowel sound, requiring "an". -->
- [x] B) an <!-- feedback: Correct. The indefinite article "an" is used before words starting with a vowel sound. -->

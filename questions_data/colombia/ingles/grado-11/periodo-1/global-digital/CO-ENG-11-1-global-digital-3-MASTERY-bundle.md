## Question 2 (Grammar: Past Simple vs Present Perfect - Difficulty 5)
**ID:** `CO-ENG-11-1-global-digital-3-MASTERY-v2`
**Bloom:** Apply
**ICFES:** Parte 4 (Gramática)

### Enunciado
"Colombia ________ the Paris Agreement on climate change in 2016, and since then, the government ________ various reforestation projects."

### Opciones
- [ ] A) has signed / started <!-- feedback: Incorrect. Specific dates (2016) require past simple, not present perfect. -->
- [x] B) signed / has started <!-- feedback: Correct. "Signed" for the specific past event (2016) and "has started" for ongoing actions. -->
- [ ] C) signs / is starting <!-- feedback: Incorrect. Using present for a 2016 event is grammatically wrong. -->
- [ ] D) was signing / will start <!-- feedback: Incorrect. Continuous for a signature is rare, and the second action is already happening. -->

**Rubric:** Differentiates between specific past events and ongoing trends or results.
**Justificación:** Contrasting these tenses is critical for accurately reporting political and environmental history.

- [ ] A) To send you a free gift for your birthday. <!-- feedback: Incorrect. The "gift" is usually a trap to steal information. -->
- [x] B) To trick you into giving away your private login details. <!-- feedback: Correct. To "obtain sensitive information" is the primary goal. -->

## Question 6 (Grammar: Passive Voice - Difficulty 6)
**ID:** `CO-ENG-11-1-global-digital-3-MASTERY-v6`
**Bloom:** Apply
**ICFES:** Parte 4 (Gramática)

### Enunciado
"The Amazon rainforest ________ 'the lungs of the planet' because of its ability to absorb carbon dioxide and release oxygen."

### Opciones
- [ ] A) calls <!-- feedback: Incorrect. The rainforest doesn't call; it is named or labeled by people. -->
- [x] B) is called <!-- feedback: Correct. Passive voice is used to describe a widely accepted title or definition. -->
- [ ] C) was calling <!-- feedback: Incorrect. This is past continuous active and doesn't fit a factual description. -->
- [ ] D) calling <!-- feedback: Incorrect. This is a participle and requires an auxiliary verb to function as a main verb. -->

**Rubric:** Uses passive voice for factual/definitional descriptions.
**Justificación:** Scientific facts are often expressed in the passive voice to maintain an objective tone.

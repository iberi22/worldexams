Read the sentence and choose the correct word:
"To protect our future, we must adopt ________ energy sources like solar and wind power to reduce our carbon footprint."

### Opciones
- [ ] A) finite <!-- feedback: Incorrect. Finite means limited, which is the opposite of what is needed for solar/wind energy. -->
- [x] B) renewable <!-- feedback: Correct. Renewable energy sources are those that can be replenished naturally. -->

**Rubric:** Identifies high-frequency academic vocabulary related to environmental issues.
**Justificación:** Understanding "renewable" is essential for discussing climate change and sustainability in English.

## Question 2 (Grammar: Zero Conditional - Difficulty 4)
**ID:** `CO-ENG-11-1-global-digital-1-MASTERY-v2`
**Bloom:** Apply
**ICFES:** Parte 5 (Comprensión literal de textos)

### Enunciado
Choose the correct structure to complete the digital safety rule:
"If you ________ your password with strangers, your personal account ________ at risk."

### Opciones
- [ ] A) shared / was <!-- feedback: Incorrect. This uses past tense, but rules are usually expressed in the present. -->
- [ ] B) will share / will be <!-- feedback: Incorrect. In a zero conditional for general facts, we use present simple in both clauses. -->
- [x] C) share / is <!-- feedback: Correct. Zero conditional (If + Present, Present) is used for facts and certainties. -->
- [ ] D) sharing / being <!-- feedback: Incorrect. These are gerunds and cannot function as main verbs here. -->

**Rubric:** Applies conditional structures to express general truths in digital contexts.
**Justificación:** Mastering conditionals allows students to describe consequences and safety protocols in a digital world.

## Question 3 (Reading Comprehension: Global Warming - Difficulty 5)
**ID:** `CO-ENG-11-1-global-digital-1-MASTERY-v3`
**Bloom:** Analyze
**ICFES:** Parte 6 (Comprensión inferencial de textos)

### Enunciado
"Recent data suggests that Arctic ice is melting at an unprecedented rate. This phenomenon not only threatens local wildlife but also contributes to the rise of sea levels globally, putting coastal cities at risk of flooding."
According to the text, what is a direct consequence of Arctic ice melting?

### Opciones
- [ ] A) A decrease in global temperatures. <!-- feedback: Incorrect. Ice melting is a result of warming, not a cause of cooling. -->
- [x] B) The potential flooding of cities near the ocean. <!-- feedback: Correct. The text explicitly mentions "rise of sea levels... putting coastal cities at risk." -->
- [ ] C) More snow in tropical countries like Colombia. <!-- feedback: Incorrect. The text mentions rising sea levels, not changes in tropical snowfall. -->
- [ ] D) The immediate extinction of all land animals. <!-- feedback: Incorrect. The text mentions a threat to wildlife, but not total extinction of all land animals. -->

**Rubric:** Extracts specific information from a short text about global issues.
**Justificación:** The ability to link "sea level rise" with "coastal flooding" is a key inferential skill.

**Bloom:** Evaluate
**ICFES:** Parte 7 (Comprensión de textos con intención comunicativa)

### Enunciado
What is the best piece of advice for a student who is experiencing online harassment?

### Opciones
- [ ] A) Delete all your social media accounts and never use the internet again. <!-- feedback: Incorrect. This is an extreme and impractical solution that isolates the victim. -->
- [x] B) Save evidence of the messages and report the behavior to a trusted adult or authority. <!-- feedback: Correct. Documentation and seeking help are the standard protocols for digital safety. -->

**Rubric:** Evaluates appropriate responses to common digital citizenship challenges.
**Justificación:** Citizenship in the digital age requires knowing how to handle conflict and seek institutional support.

## Question 5 (Grammar: Passive Voice in Reports - Difficulty 6)
**ID:** `CO-ENG-11-1-global-digital-1-MASTERY-v5`
**Bloom:** Apply
**ICFES:** Parte 4 (Conocimiento léxico y gramatical)

### Enunciado
Complete the sentence using the correct passive voice form:
"Millions of tons of plastic ________ into the ocean every year, causing severe damage to marine ecosystems."

### Opciones
- [ ] A) are throwing <!-- feedback: Incorrect. This is active voice; the plastic doesn't throw itself. -->
- [x] B) are thrown <!-- feedback: Correct. Passive voice (to be + past participle) focuses on the action being done to the object. -->
- [ ] C) have thrown <!-- feedback: Incorrect. This is present perfect active; it lacks the passive structure. -->
- [ ] D) will throw <!-- feedback: Incorrect. This is future active; the context describes a regular, current problem. -->

**Rubric:** Uses passive voice to describe objective processes or global problems.
**Justificación:** Passive voice is the standard for academic and scientific reporting in English.

Alex: "Did you know that everything you post online stays there forever?"

### Opciones
- [ ] A) shoes <!-- feedback: Incorrect. This is a literal interpretation of "footprint" that ignores the digital context. -->

**Rubric:** Identifies key terminology of digital citizenship in a conversational context.
**Justificación:** Understanding the permanence of online data is fundamental for responsible digital behavior.

## Question 11 (Grammar: Modals of Obligation - Difficulty 5)
**ID:** `CO-ENG-11-1-global-digital-1-MASTERY-v11`
**Bloom:** Apply
**ICFES:** Parte 4 (Conocimiento léxico y gramatical)

### Enunciado
Complete the advice for a digital citizen:
"You ________ post photos of your friends without their permission; it is a matter of privacy and respect."

### Opciones
- [ ] A) must <!-- feedback: Incorrect. This indicates an obligation to post, which contradicts the context of privacy. -->
- [x] B) shouldn't <!-- feedback: Correct. "Shouldn't" is used for advice against an action that is morally or socially wrong. -->
- [ ] C) can <!-- feedback: Incorrect. This indicates possibility or permission, but the sentence is a warning. -->
- [ ] D) have to <!-- feedback: Incorrect. This indicates a strong external obligation to do the action. -->

**Rubric:** Selects the appropriate modal verb to express social rules and ethics.
**Justificación:** Modals are essential for communicating norms, rights, and responsibilities.

## Question 12 (Reading: Social Media Impact - Difficulty 7)
**ID:** `CO-ENG-11-1-global-digital-1-MASTERY-v12`
**Bloom:** Analyze
**ICFES:** Parte 6 (Comprensión inferencial)

### Enunciado
"While social media facilitates global connection, it can also create 'echo chambers' where users only see information that confirms their existing beliefs, leading to increased social polarization."
What can be inferred from the term 'echo chambers'?

### Opciones
- [ ] A) People are literally shouting in empty rooms online. <!-- feedback: Incorrect. This is a literal interpretation of a metaphorical term. -->
- [x] B) Users are rarely exposed to diverse or opposing viewpoints. <!-- feedback: Correct. An echo chamber reinforces one's own voice/views without external input. -->
- [ ] C) Social media makes everyone smarter and more informed. <!-- feedback: Incorrect. The text suggests the opposite: it leads to polarization. -->
- [ ] D) Echo chambers are special groups for professional singers. <!-- feedback: Incorrect. In this context, the term is political and sociological. -->

**Rubric:** Interprets metaphors and academic terminology in social critiques.
**Justificación:** Critical reading in English involves understanding how technology affects our perception of truth and reality.

Which of the following scenarios best illustrates the 'algorithmic bias' described in the text?

### Opciones

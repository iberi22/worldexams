## Question 1 (Vocabulary: Fake News - Difficulty 5)
**ID:** `CO-ENG-11-1-global-digital-2-MASTERY-v1`
**Bloom:** Remember
**ICFES:** Parte 4 (Conocimiento léxico)

### Enunciado
"Critical thinkers should always check multiple sources to avoid being ________ by misinformation and sensationalist headlines."

### Opciones
- [ ] A) enlightened <!-- feedback: Incorrect. Enlightened means informed or wise; misinformation does the opposite. -->
- [x] B) misled <!-- feedback: Correct. To be misled is to be given a wrong idea or guided in the wrong direction. -->
- [ ] C) improved <!-- feedback: Incorrect. Misinformation does not improve one's knowledge. -->
- [ ] D) supported <!-- feedback: Incorrect. Misinformation undermines truth, it doesn't support the user. -->

**Rubric:** Recognizes vocabulary related to media literacy and truth in the digital age.
**Justificación:** Media literacy is a core component of digital citizenship, requiring students to identify deceptive content.

## Question 2 (Grammar: First Conditional - Difficulty 4)
**ID:** `CO-ENG-11-1-global-digital-2-MASTERY-v2`
**Bloom:** Apply
**ICFES:** Parte 4 (Gramática)

### Enunciado
Choose the correct future outcome:
"If we don't reduce plastic consumption now, the amount of waste in the ocean ________ by 2050."

### Opciones
- [ ] A) would double <!-- feedback: Incorrect. This is second conditional (hypothetical), but the sentence describes a real possibility. -->
- [x] B) will double <!-- feedback: Correct. First conditional (If + Present, Will) expresses a real possible future result. -->
- [ ] C) doubled <!-- feedback: Incorrect. This is past tense and doesn't fit a prediction. -->
- [ ] D) doubling <!-- feedback: Incorrect. This is a gerund and cannot be the main verb of the result clause. -->

**Rubric:** Applies the first conditional to environmental predictions.
**Justificación:** Scientific and environmental discourse frequently uses the first conditional to warn about future consequences.

**Bloom:** Understand
**ICFES:** Parte 3 (Comunicación)

### Enunciado
Teacher: "Your essay is good, but you forgot to cite your sources."
Student: "I'm sorry, I didn't know that using someone else's ideas without credit was ________."

### Opciones
- [ ] A) creative <!-- feedback: Incorrect. Taking ideas without credit is the opposite of original creativity. -->
- [x] B) plagiarism <!-- feedback: Correct. Plagiarism is the practice of taking someone else's work or ideas and passing them off as one's own. -->
- [ ] C) legal <!-- feedback: Incorrect. In academic contexts, it is an ethical and often legal violation. -->
- [ ] D) traditional <!-- feedback: Incorrect. While common, it is not a "tradition" but a violation of academic integrity. -->

**Rubric:** Identifies ethical violations in digital information management.
**Justificación:** Understanding plagiarism is vital for academic success and ethical digital production.

## Question 4 (Vocabulary: Climate Change Mitigation - Difficulty 6)
**ID:** `CO-ENG-11-1-global-digital-2-MASTERY-v4`
**Bloom:** Analyze
**ICFES:** Parte 4 (Léxico)

### Enunciado
"Countries around the world are implementing policies to ________ greenhouse gas emissions to slow down the process of global warming."

### Opciones
- [ ] A) increase <!-- feedback: Incorrect. Increasing emissions would accelerate global warming. -->
- [x] B) curb <!-- feedback: Correct. To curb means to restrain or keep in check, which fits the context of reduction. -->
- [ ] C) ignore <!-- feedback: Incorrect. Ignoring emissions is the opposite of implementing helpful policies. -->
- [ ] D) celebrate <!-- feedback: Incorrect. Emissions are a problem to be solved, not celebrated. -->

**Rubric:** Selects precise verbs for discussing policy and environmental solutions.
**Justificación:** Academic English requires specific verbs like "curb" or "mitigate" rather than simple words like "stop".

## Question 5 (Reading: E-waste - Difficulty 6)

### Enunciado
"Electronic waste, or e-waste, contains toxic materials such as lead and mercury. When discarded improperly in landfills, these chemicals leak into the soil and water supply, posing a health risk to nearby Colombian communities."
What is the main danger of e-waste according to the text?

### Opciones
- [ ] A) It makes old computers very expensive. <!-- feedback: Incorrect. The text doesn't mention prices. -->
- [ ] B) It helps plants grow faster. <!-- feedback: Incorrect. Toxic materials like lead usually harm plant life. -->
- [x] C) It can contaminate the water that people drink. <!-- feedback: Correct. The text mentions chemicals leaking into the "water supply." -->
- [ ] D) It makes internet connections slower. <!-- feedback: Incorrect. E-waste refers to physical garbage, not signal quality. -->

**Rubric:** Identifies cause-and-effect relationships in environmental texts.
**Justificación:** Linking "waste" to "water contamination" is a fundamental scientific and reading skill.

## Question 6 (Grammar: Present Perfect - Difficulty 5)
**ID:** `CO-ENG-11-1-global-digital-2-MASTERY-v6`
**Bloom:** Apply
**ICFES:** Parte 4 (Gramática)

### Enunciado
"Technology ________ the way we communicate in the last decade, making it easier to connect with people globally."

### Opciones
- [ ] A) changes <!-- feedback: Incorrect. Simple present doesn't capture the ongoing effect from the past until now. -->
- [ ] B) change <!-- feedback: Incorrect. Incorrect subject-verb agreement. -->
- [x] C) has changed <!-- feedback: Correct. Present perfect is used for actions that started in the past and have current relevance. -->
- [ ] D) changing <!-- feedback: Incorrect. Lacks the auxiliary verb "has." -->

**Rubric:** Uses present perfect to describe technological transformations.
**Justificación:** The present perfect is the primary tense for describing social and technological trends.

**ID:** `CO-ENG-11-1-global-digital-2-MASTERY-v20`
**Bloom:** Transfer
**ICFES:** Parte 7 (Comprensión global e intención)

### Enunciado
**MASTER CHALLENGE:**
"The concept of the '15-minute city' aims to reduce urban carbon emissions by ensuring that residents can access work, education, and health services within a short walk or bike ride. While Bogotá's 'Barrios Vitales' initiative aligns with this global trend, critics argue that such models may inadvertently lead to 'gentrification,' where rising costs of living displace the original low-income inhabitants."

Based on the text, what is the main tension or conflict described regarding the 15-minute city model?

### Opciones
- [ ] A) The conflict between people who like to walk and people who prefer to use TransMilenio. <!-- feedback: Incorrect. The text focuses on socio-economic displacement, not just transport preference. -->
- [ ] B) The technical difficulty of building more bike lanes in hilly areas. <!-- feedback: Incorrect. This is a topographical issue, not the socio-economic one mentioned in the text. -->
- [x] C) The balance between environmental urban improvement and the potential exclusion of poor residents due to higher costs. <!-- feedback: Correct. This summarizes the tension between the "vital" initiative and "gentrification." -->
- [ ] D) The lack of internet connection in the new urban parks. <!-- feedback: Incorrect. Digital access is not mentioned as a conflict in this specific urban model. -->

**Rubric:** Synthesizes complex socio-environmental arguments in an urban context.
**Justificación:** High-level mastery requires evaluating the pros and cons of global urban planning theories applied to local contexts.

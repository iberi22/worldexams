## Part 6: Reading Comprehension

### Text: The Silicon Curtain: Technological Decoupling
"Technological decoupling" refers to the growing trend of major powers (most notably the US and China) creating separate and independent supply chains for critical technologies like semiconductors and AI. This process is driven by concerns over national security and "data sovereignty." In the past, the global tech industry was highly integrated, with hardware from one country often running software from another. However, as AI becomes a tool for both economic growth and military intelligence, this integration is seen as a vulnerability. The "Silicon Curtain" represents a future where the world is divided into separate digital ecosystems that do not communicate or share data, potentially leading to a "splinternet" that undermines global collaboration and scientific progress.

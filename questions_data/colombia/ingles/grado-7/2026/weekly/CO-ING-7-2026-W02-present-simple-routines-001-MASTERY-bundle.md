# MASTERY Bundle - Inglés: Present Simple - Habits and Routines (W02)

This bundle focuses on using the Present Simple tense to describe daily habits and routines in a Colombian context, suitable for 7th-grade students (CEFR A2+).

---

**Bloom:** Remember
**ICFES:** Lexical Knowledge

**Contexto:** Juan is talking about his morning activities before going to school in Cali.

### Enunciado
What is the correct verb for the activity of cleaning your teeth?

### Opciones
- [ ] A) Wash my teeth
  <!-- feedback: Incorrect. We usually use "wash" for hands or face, but not for teeth. -->

## Question 8 [D8]
**ID:** CO-ING-7-2026-W02-present-simple-routines-008-v1
**Bloom:** Analyze
**ICFES:** Grammatical Knowledge
**Expected_Success:** 0.60
**Contexto:** Choosing the correct negative form for a routine.

### Enunciado
Which sentence is correct for someone who doesn't watch TV in the morning?

### Opciones
- [ ] A) I not watch TV in the morning.
  <!-- feedback: Incorrect. We need an auxiliary verb for negatives in Present Simple. -->
- [ ] B) I don't watches TV in the morning.
  <!-- feedback: Incorrect. After "don't", we use the base form of the verb without 's'. -->
- [x] C) I don't watch TV in the morning.
  <!-- feedback: Correct! "Don't" is the correct auxiliary for "I". -->
- [ ] D) I am not watch TV in the morning.
  <!-- feedback: Incorrect. "Am not" is used with adjectives or -ing verbs. -->

### Explicacion Pedagogica
The student analyzes the structure of negative sentences in the Present Simple.

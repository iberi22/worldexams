# MASTERY Bundle - Inglés: Possessive Adjectives and Saxon Genitive (W08)

This bundle focuses on expressing possession using possessive adjectives (my, your, his, her, its, our, their) and the Saxon Genitive ('s), applied to family and personal objects.

### Enunciado

### Opciones

  <!-- feedback: Incorrect. "Its" is a possessive adjective; "It" (subject) is needed here. -->

  <!-- feedback: Correct! "Brother's" shows possession and "It" refers to the room. -->

  <!-- feedback: Incorrect. Missing the apostrophe for possession. -->

  <!-- feedback: Incorrect. "Mine" is a possessive pronoun, and "brother" needs the genitive 's. -->

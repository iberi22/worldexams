# MASTERY Bundle - Inglés: Comparatives (Equality and Superiority) (W26)

This bundle focuses on comparing two people, objects, or places using comparative adjectives (Short adjectives + er, Long adjectives with more) and comparisons of equality (as...as).

---

**ICFES:** Grammatical Knowledge
**Expected_Success:** 0.85
**Contexto:** Identifying the comparative suffix for short adjectives.

### Enunciado
What suffix is added to short adjectives (like "tall" or "small") to form a comparative?

### Opciones
- [ ] A) -est
  <!-- feedback: Incorrect. This is for superlatives. -->
- [x] B) -er
  <!-- feedback: Correct! We add -er to short adjectives for comparisons. -->

  <!-- feedback: Incorrect. This is for continuous tenses. -->
- [ ] D) -ed

## Question 8 [D8]
**ID:** CO-ING-7-2026-W26-comparatives-008-v1
**Bloom:** Analyze
**ICFES:** Grammatical Knowledge
**Expected_Success:** 0.60
**Contexto:** Analyzing spelling rules for short adjectives.

### Enunciado
Choose the sentence with the correct spelling.

### Opciones
- [ ] A) My dog is biger than your cat.
  <!-- feedback: Incorrect. "Big" is CVC, so double the 'g'. -->
- [x] B) My dog is bigger than your cat.
  <!-- feedback: Correct! CVC adjectives double the last consonant before -er. -->
- [ ] C) My dog is more big than your cat.
  <!-- feedback: Incorrect. Short adjectives use -er. -->
- [ ] D) My dog is bigest than your cat.
  <!-- feedback: Incorrect. This is the superlative suffix. -->

### Explicacion Pedagogica
The student analyzes the spelling rules (doubling consonants) for short comparative adjectives with a CVC pattern.

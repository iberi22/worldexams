# Bundle Mastery: Past Perfect - Grado 7
## Semana W01 — Past Perfect: Structure and Use

## Pregunta 1 [D3]
**ID:** `CO-ENG-7-2026-W01-past-perfect-001-MASTERY-v1`
**Bloom:** Remember
**ICFES:** Uso comprensivo del conocimiento científico
**Context:** In English class at Colegio Colombo Británico in Bogotá, the teacher writes a sentence on the board: "She had already eaten when I arrived."
**Expected_Success:** 0.85

### Enunciado
What is the grammatical structure of the Past Perfect tense in the sentence above?

### Opciones
- [ ] A) Subject + past simple + complement <!-- feedback: Incorrect. The structure has "had" + past participle, not simple past. -->
- [ ] B) Subject + have + past participle <!-- feedback: Incorrect. "have" is for present perfect; past perfect uses "had". -->
- [x] C) Subject + had + past participle <!-- feedback: Correct. Past perfect uses "had" + past participle (eaten). -->
- [ ] D) Subject + was/were + present participle <!-- feedback: Incorrect. That is the past continuous structure. -->

### Explicacion Pedagogica
The Past Perfect tense is formed with the auxiliary verb "had" (for all persons) followed by the past participle of the main verb. In "She had already eaten," "had" is the auxiliary and "eaten" is the past participle of "eat." This tense shows that one past action happened before another past action.

## Pregunta 2 [D4]
**ID:** `CO-ENG-7-2026-W01-past-perfect-001-MASTERY-v2`
**Bloom:** Understand
**ICFES:** Explicación de fenómenos
**Context:** María is writing a story about her weekend in Medellín. She writes: "When my friends arrived, I had already prepared the snacks."
**Expected_Success:** 0.80

### Enunciado
What does María's sentence express?

### Opciones
- [ ] A) She prepared the snacks after her friends arrived. <!-- feedback: Incorrect. The past perfect shows the opposite order. -->
- [x] B) She prepared the snacks before her friends arrived. <!-- feedback: Correct. "Had already prepared" happened before "arrived." -->
- [ ] C) She and her friends prepared the snacks together. <!-- feedback: Incorrect. The sentence does not suggest collaboration. -->
- [ ] D) She is preparing the snacks now. <!-- feedback: Incorrect. Both actions are in the past. -->

### Explicacion Pedagogica
The Past Perfect (had prepared) indicates an action that was completed before another past action (arrived). The word "already" reinforces that the snack preparation was finished before the friends' arrival. This is the main use of Past Perfect: to establish the chronological order of two past events.

## Pregunta 3 [D4]
**ID:** `CO-ENG-7-2026-W01-past-perfect-001-MASTERY-v3`
**Bloom:** Remember
**ICFES:** Uso comprensivo del conocimiento científico
**Context:** Carlos is studying irregular verbs for his English test at school in Cali. He needs to use the correct past participle form.
**Expected_Success:** 0.82

### Enunciado
Choose the correct past participle of the verb "to write":

### Opciones
- [ ] A) Wrote <!-- feedback: Incorrect. "Wrote" is the simple past form, not the past participle. -->
- [ ] B) Writed <!-- feedback: Incorrect. "Write" is an irregular verb; "-ed" does not apply. -->
- [x] C) Written <!-- feedback: Correct. The past participle of "write" is "written." -->
- [ ] D) Writing <!-- feedback: Incorrect. "Writing" is the gerund or present participle. -->

### Explicacion Pedagogica
"Write" is an irregular verb. Its forms are: write (present) → wrote (past simple) → written (past participle). It is essential to memorize irregular past participles because they are used in all perfect tenses (present perfect, past perfect, future perfect). Common irregular verbs: go-went-gone, see-saw-seen, do-did-done.

## Pregunta 4 [D5]
**ID:** `CO-ENG-7-2026-W01-past-perfect-001-MASTERY-v4`
**Bloom:** Apply
**ICFES:** Indagación
**Context:** Laura is writing about her visit to Cartagena. She wants to express that before visiting the walled city (la ciudad amurallada), she had read about its history.
**Expected_Success:** 0.78

### Enunciado
Which sentence correctly expresses Laura's idea?

### Opciones
- [ ] A) I read about the history of Cartagena before I visited the walled city. <!-- feedback: Both simple pasts; the order is implied but not grammatically marked. -->
- [ ] B) I had read about the history of Cartagena before I visited the walled city. <!-- feedback: Correct. Past perfect clarifies that reading happened before visiting. -->
- [x] C) I have read about the history of Cartagena before I visited the walled city. <!-- feedback: Incorrect. Present perfect cannot be used with a specific past time. -->

### Explicacion Pedagogica
When we want to make clear that one past action happened before another, we use Past Perfect for the earlier action. While option A is grammatically correct, option B makes the time relationship explicit. Past Perfect is especially useful when the chronological order is not obvious from context.

## Pregunta 5 [D5]
**ID:** `CO-ENG-7-2026-W01-past-perfect-001-MASTERY-v5`
**Bloom:** Understand
**ICFES:** Explicación de fenómenos
**Context:** In class in Bucaramanga, Andrés reads: "The teacher explained that the students hadn't done their homework because they had forgotten about it."
**Expected_Success:** 0.76

### Enunciado
What is the negative form of the Past Perfect?

### Opciones
- [ ] A) Subject + didn't have + past participle <!-- feedback: Incorrect. "Didn't have" is not the correct negative structure. -->
- [ ] B) Subject + wasn't + past participle <!-- feedback: Incorrect. That is the negative past continuous. -->
- [x] C) Subject + hadn't + past participle <!-- feedback: Correct. "Hadn't" (had not) + past participle is the negative form. -->
- [ ] D) Subject + not had + past participle <!-- feedback: Incorrect. The auxiliary "had" must come before "not." -->

### Explicacion Pedagogica
To form the negative of Past Perfect, we add "not" after the auxiliary "had": Subject + had + not + past participle. The contracted form is "hadn't" (e.g., "I hadn't finished," "she hadn't arrived"). This structure is used to indicate that an action had NOT happened before another past action.

## Pregunta 6 [D6]
**ID:** `CO-ENG-7-2026-W01-past-perfect-001-MASTERY-v6`
**Bloom:** Apply
**ICFES:** Uso comprensivo del conocimiento científico
**Context:** Valentina is asking questions in English class at school in Pereira. She wants to ask if her friend had ever eaten bandeja paisa before coming to Colombia.
**Expected_Success:** 0.70

### Enunciado
Which question form is correct for Past Perfect?

### Opciones
- [ ] A) Did you had eaten bandeja paisa before? <!-- feedback: Incorrect. "Did" + "had" is a double auxiliary error. -->
- [x] B) Had you ever eaten bandeja paisa before coming to Colombia? <!-- feedback: Correct. "Had" + subject + past participle is the question form. -->
- [ ] C) Have you eaten bandeja paisa before coming to Colombia? <!-- feedback: Incorrect. Present perfect is used for experiences without a specific past time. -->
- [ ] D) Did you eat bandeja paisa before coming to Colombia? <!-- feedback: Incorrect. Simple past does not emphasize the order of events. -->

### Explicacion Pedagogica
To form Past Perfect questions, invert the auxiliary "had" and the subject: Had + subject + past participle + ...? For wh-questions: Wh-word + had + subject + past participle? Example: "Where had she studied before moving to Bogotá?" Questions with Past Perfect are common in narratives and when discussing experiences before a specific past moment.

**ID:** `CO-ENG-7-2026-W01-past-perfect-001-MASTERY-v7`
**Bloom:** Analyze
**ICFES:** Indagación
**Context:** Santiago is checking his friend's paragraph about a trip to Santa Marta. He finds: "We visited Tayrona Park. We had never been there before."
**Expected_Success:** 0.65

### Enunciado
Why did Santiago's friend use "had never been" instead of "were never"?

### Opciones
- [ ] A) Because "had never been" is the only correct form. <!-- feedback: Incorrect. "Were never" is also grammatically possible but has a different meaning. -->
- [x] B) Because Past Perfect emphasizes that the experience of never having been there was before the visit. <!-- feedback: Correct. Past perfect sets the time reference before "visited." -->
- [ ] C) Because simple past cannot be used with "never." <!-- feedback: Incorrect. Simple past can be used with "never" (e.g., "We never went"). -->

The Past Perfect ("had never been") establishes that the experience of not having visited Tayrona was before the specific past moment ("visited"). Using the simple past ("We were never there before") would be less precise about the time relationship. Past Perfect is particularly useful with adverbs like "never," "already," "ever," and "yet" to clarify the sequence of past events.

## Pregunta 11 [D8]
**ID:** `CO-ENG-7-2026-W01-past-perfect-001-MASTERY-v11`
**Bloom:** Analyze
**ICFES:** Explicación de fenómenos
**Context:** Daniel is comparing two sentences in his English class in Sincelejo: "I saw the movie." and "I had seen the movie before I read the book."
**Expected_Success:** 0.48

### Enunciado
What is the difference in meaning between "I saw the movie" and "I had seen the movie before I read the book"?

### Opciones
- [ ] A) They mean exactly the same thing. <!-- feedback: Incorrect. The first is ambiguous, the second specifies sequence. -->
- [ ] B) The first sentence is in present tense. <!-- feedback: Incorrect. Both are about past events. -->
- [x] C) The second sentence explicitly states that seeing the movie happened before reading the book. <!-- feedback: Correct. Past perfect establishes the temporal order. -->

### Explicacion Pedagogica

The Past Perfect ("I had seen the movie before I read the book") explicitly places "seeing the movie" before "reading the book" in the past timeline. This is the key function of Past Perfect: establishing a clear order between two past events.

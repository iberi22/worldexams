# MASTERY Bundle - Inglés: Greetings and Personal Info (W01)

This bundle focuses on reviewing and expanding greetings, introductions, and sharing personal information at a CEFR A2+ level for 7th-grade students in Colombia.

---

## Question 1 [D3-D4]
**ID:** CO-ING-7-2026-W01-greetings-personal-info-001-v1
**Bloom:** Remember
**ICFES:** Lexical Knowledge
**Expected_Success:** 0.85
**Contexto:** Two students, Mateo and Valentina, meet on their first day at a public school in Bogotá.

### Enunciado
What is the most appropriate formal greeting when meeting a teacher in the morning?

### Opciones
- [ ] A) What's up, teacher?
  <!-- feedback: Incorrect. This is very informal and not suitable for a teacher. -->
- [x] B) Good morning, teacher.
  <!-- feedback: Correct! This is a polite and formal greeting for the morning. -->
- [ ] C) Bye, teacher.
  <!-- feedback: Incorrect. This is used when leaving, not when greeting. -->
- [ ] D) See you later.
  <!-- feedback: Incorrect. This is a farewell, not a greeting. -->

### Explicacion Pedagogica
The student needs to identify the correct formal greeting for a morning school context. "Good morning" is the standard formal greeting for this time of day.

## Question 5 [D5-D6]
**ID:** CO-ING-7-2026-W01-greetings-personal-info-005-v1
**Bloom:** Apply
**ICFES:** Grammatical Knowledge
**Expected_Success:** 0.70
**Contexto:** Introducing a new student to the group.

### Enunciado
Choose the correct sentence to introduce your friend Sarah.

### Opciones
- [ ] A) I is Sarah.
  <!-- feedback: Incorrect. The verb "to be" is incorrectly conjugated for the first person. -->
- [ ] B) She name is Sarah.
  <!-- feedback: Incorrect. "She" is a subject pronoun; the possessive "Her" should be used. -->
- [x] C) This is my friend, Sarah.
  <!-- feedback: Correct! This is a common and correct way to introduce someone else. -->
- [ ] D) Sarah am my friend.
  <!-- feedback: Incorrect. The verb "am" only goes with "I". -->

### Explicacion Pedagogica
The student applies grammatical rules (demonstrative pronouns and verb to be) to perform a social introduction.

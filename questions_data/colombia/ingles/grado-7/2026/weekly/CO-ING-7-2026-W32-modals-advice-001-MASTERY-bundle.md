# MASTERY Bundle - Inglés: Modals of Advice (Should, Ought to) (W32)

This bundle focuses on giving and asking for advice using the modal verb "should" and its negative form "shouldn't", as well as an introduction to "ought to".

### Enunciado

### Opciones
- [ ] A) He shoulds study more.
  <!-- feedback: Incorrect. Modal verbs don't take 's' in the third person. -->
- [ ] B) He should to study more.
  <!-- feedback: Incorrect. We don't use "to" after "should". -->

  <!-- feedback: Correct! Modal + base form of the verb. -->

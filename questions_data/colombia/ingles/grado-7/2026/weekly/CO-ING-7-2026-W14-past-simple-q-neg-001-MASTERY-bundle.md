# MASTERY Bundle - Inglés: Past Simple - Questions and Negatives (W14)

This bundle focuses on forming negative sentences and questions in the Past Simple using the auxiliary "did", including the use of base forms of verbs.

---

**ID:** CO-ING-7-2026-W14-past-simple-q-neg-001-v1
**Bloom:** Remember
**ICFES:** Grammatical Knowledge
**Expected_Success:** 0.85
**Contexto:** Identifying the auxiliary for past negatives.

### Enunciado
Which auxiliary verb is used to form a negative sentence in the Past Simple?

### Opciones
- [ ] A) Do not
  <!-- feedback: Incorrect. This is for Present Simple. -->
- [ ] B) Does not
  <!-- feedback: Incorrect. This is for Present Simple third person. -->
- [x] C) Did not
  <!-- feedback: Correct! "Did not" (or "didn't") is the auxiliary for Past Simple. -->
- [ ] D) Was not
  <!-- feedback: Incorrect. This is only for the verb "to be" in the past. -->

### Explicacion Pedagogica
The student identifies the correct auxiliary for negative sentences in the Past Simple.

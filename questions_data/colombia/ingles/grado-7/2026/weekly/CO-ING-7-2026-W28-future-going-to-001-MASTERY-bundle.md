# MASTERY Bundle - Inglés: Future with "Going to" (W28)

This bundle focuses on using "be going to" to express intentions, plans, and predictions based on present evidence, suitable for 7th-grade students (CEFR A2+).

---

**ID:** CO-ING-7-2026-W28-future-going-to-001-v1
**Bloom:** Remember
**ICFES:** Grammatical Knowledge
**Expected_Success:** 0.85
**Contexto:** Identifying the auxiliary for "be going to".

### Enunciado
Which auxiliary verb is essential to form the "going to" future?

### Opciones
- [ ] A) Do
  <!-- feedback: Incorrect. "Do" is for present simple. -->
- [x] B) Be (Am, Is, Are)
  <!-- feedback: Correct! "Be going to" requires the verb to be conjugated correctly. -->
- [ ] C) Have
  <!-- feedback: Incorrect. "Have" is for perfect tenses. -->
- [ ] D) Will
  <!-- feedback: Incorrect. "Will" is a separate way to form the future. -->

### Explicacion Pedagogica
The student identifies the basic structural component of the "going to" future tense.

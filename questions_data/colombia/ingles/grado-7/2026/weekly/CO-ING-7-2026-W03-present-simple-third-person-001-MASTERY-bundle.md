# MASTERY Bundle - Inglés: Present Simple - 3rd Person and Spelling (W03)

This bundle focuses on the third-person singular (he, she, it) rules and spelling variations in the Present Simple tense, applied to Colombian daily contexts for 7th-grade students.

---

## Question 1 [D3]
**ID:** CO-ING-7-2026-W03-present-simple-third-person-001-v1
**Bloom:** Remember
**ICFES:** Grammatical Knowledge
**Expected_Success:** 0.85
**Contexto:** Talking about a sister's hobby.

### Enunciado
Which verb form is correct for "She"?

### Opciones
- [ ] A) She read books.
  <!-- feedback: Incorrect. Third person singular requires an 's' in the affirmative. -->
- [x] B) She reads books.
  <!-- feedback: Correct! "Reads" is the correct form for the subject "She". -->
- [ ] C) She reading books.
  <!-- feedback: Incorrect. This is the continuous form, not present simple. -->
- [ ] D) She reades books.
  <!-- feedback: Incorrect. "Read" only needs a simple 's', not 'es'. -->

### Explicacion Pedagogica
The student identifies the basic third-person singular rule for the Present Simple tense.

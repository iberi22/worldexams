# MASTERY Bundle - Inglés: Review P2 - Past Events (W15)

This bundle reviews the key concepts of Period 2: Past Simple of "to be", regular and irregular verbs, and negative and interrogative forms in the past.

---

## Question 1 [D3-D4]
**ID:** CO-ING-7-2026-W15-review-p2-past-001-v1
**Bloom:** Remember
**ICFES:** Grammatical Knowledge
**Expected_Success:** 0.85
**Contexto:** Past states of feelings.

### Enunciado
Complete: "I ________ very tired after the trip to the Amazonas."

### Opciones
- [x] A) was
  <!-- feedback: Correct! "Was" is the past of "to be" for "I". -->
- [ ] B) were
  <!-- feedback: Incorrect. "Were" is for plural subjects. -->
- [ ] C) am
  <!-- feedback: Incorrect. "Am" is present tense. -->
- [ ] D) did
  <!-- feedback: Incorrect. "Did" is an auxiliary for other verbs, not for states. -->

### Explicacion Pedagogica
The student identifies the correct past form of the verb "to be" for the first person singular.

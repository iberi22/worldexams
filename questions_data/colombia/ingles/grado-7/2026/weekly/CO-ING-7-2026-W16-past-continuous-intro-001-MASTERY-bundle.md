# MASTERY Bundle - Inglés: Past Continuous (Form and Use) (W16)

This bundle introduces the Past Continuous tense (was/were + verb-ing) to describe actions that were in progress at a specific time in the past.

---

**ID:** CO-ING-7-2026-W16-past-continuous-intro-001-v1
**Bloom:** Remember
**ICFES:** Grammatical Knowledge
**Expected_Success:** 0.85
**Contexto:** Identifying the structure of the Past Continuous.

### Enunciado
Which two components are used to form the Past Continuous?

### Opciones
- [ ] A) Was/Were + Base form of the verb.
  <!-- feedback: Incorrect. The second verb needs -ing. -->
- [x] B) Was/Were + Verb with -ing.
  <!-- feedback: Correct! Past continuous requires "to be" in the past and the -ing form. -->
- [ ] C) Am/Is/Are + Verb with -ing.
  <!-- feedback: Incorrect. This is for Present Continuous. -->
- [ ] D) Did + Verb with -ing.
  <!-- feedback: Incorrect. "Did" is the auxiliary for Past Simple. -->

### Explicacion Pedagogica
The student identifies the basic structural elements of the Past Continuous tense.

## Question 2 [D3-D4]
**ID:** CO-ING-7-2026-W16-past-continuous-intro-002-v1
**Bloom:** Remember
**ICFES:** Grammatical Knowledge
**Expected_Success:** 0.80
**Contexto:** Using the correct auxiliary for "They".

### Enunciado
What is the correct form for the subject "They"?

### Opciones
- [ ] A) They was dancing.
  <!-- feedback: Incorrect. "Was" is for singular subjects. -->
- [x] B) They were dancing.
  <!-- feedback: Correct! "Were" is the past plural auxiliary. -->
- [ ] C) They are dancing.
  <!-- feedback: Incorrect. This is present tense. -->
- [ ] D) They were danceing.
  <!-- feedback: Incorrect. Spelling error; verbs ending in 'e' drop the 'e' before -ing. -->

### Explicacion Pedagogica
The student identifies the correct past conjugation of "to be" for plural subjects in the continuous form.

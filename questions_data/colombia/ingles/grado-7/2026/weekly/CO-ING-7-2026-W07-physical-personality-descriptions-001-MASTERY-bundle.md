## Question 4 [D5-D6]
**ID:** CO-ING-7-2026-W07-physical-personality-descriptions-004-v1
**Bloom:** Understand
**ICFES:** Grammatical Knowledge
**Expected_Success:** 0.75
**Contexto:** Describing eyes using the verb "have".

### Enunciado
What is the correct way to describe your eyes?

### Opciones
- [ ] A) I am green eyes.
  <!-- feedback: Incorrect. You don't "be" a color of eyes; you "have" them. -->
- [x] B) I have green eyes.
  <!-- feedback: Correct! We use the verb "have" for body features like eyes and hair. -->
- [ ] C) My eyes is green.
  <!-- feedback: Incorrect. "Eyes" is plural, so it should be "are". -->
- [ ] D) I have eyes greens.
  <!-- feedback: Incorrect. In English, the adjective (green) comes before the noun (eyes). -->

### Explicacion Pedagogica
The student understands the grammatical structure for describing physical features (verb have + adjective + noun).

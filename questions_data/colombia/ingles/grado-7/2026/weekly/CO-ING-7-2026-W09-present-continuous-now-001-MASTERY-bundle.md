# MASTERY Bundle - Inglés: Present Continuous for Actions Now (W09)

This bundle focuses on the Present Continuous tense (be + verb-ing) to describe actions happening at the moment of speaking.

---

## Question 1 [D3]
**ID:** CO-ING-7-2026-W09-present-continuous-now-001-v1
**Bloom:** Remember
**ICFES:** Grammatical Knowledge
**Expected_Success:** 0.85
**Contexto:** Identifying the structure of the Present Continuous.

### Enunciado
Which two elements are necessary to form the Present Continuous?

### Opciones
- [ ] A) Verb "to do" and verb with -s.
  <!-- feedback: Incorrect. This is for Present Simple. -->
- [x] B) Verb "to be" and verb with -ing.
  <!-- feedback: Correct! Present Continuous requires the auxiliary "be" and the principal verb in the -ing form. -->
- [ ] C) Verb "to have" and verb with -ed.
  <!-- feedback: Incorrect. This is for Present Perfect. -->
- [ ] D) Only a verb with -ing.
  <!-- feedback: Incorrect. Without the verb "to be", the sentence is incomplete. -->

### Explicacion Pedagogica
The student identifies the basic components of the Present Continuous tense.

## Question 2 [D4]
**ID:** CO-ING-7-2026-W09-present-continuous-now-002-v1
**Bloom:** Remember
**ICFES:** Grammatical Knowledge
**Expected_Success:** 0.80
**Contexto:** Describing a current action of a friend.

### Enunciado
Complete the sentence: "Mateo ________ a sandwich right now."

### Opciones
- [ ] A) is eat
  <!-- feedback: Incorrect. The second verb must have -ing. -->
- [x] B) is eating
  <!-- feedback: Correct! "Is" for Mateo (he) and "eating" for the current action. -->
- [ ] C) am eating
  <!-- feedback: Incorrect. "Am" is only for "I". -->
- [ ] D) are eating
  <!-- feedback: Incorrect. "Are" is for plural subjects or "you". -->

### Explicacion Pedagogica
The student remembers the correct conjugation of the verb "to be" and the -ing form in a simple sentence.

## Question 9 [D9]
**ID:** CO-ING-7-2026-W09-present-continuous-now-009-v1
**Bloom:** Analyze
**ICFES:** Lexico-grammatical Knowledge
**Expected_Success:** 0.55
**Contexto:** Analyzing spelling rules for -ing (consonant-vowel-consonant).

### Enunciado
Choose the sentence with the correct spelling for the verb "run".

### Opciones
- [ ] A) The dog is runing in the garden.
  <!-- feedback: Incorrect. For C-V-C verbs, the last consonant must be doubled. -->
- [x] B) The dog is running in the garden.
  <!-- feedback: Correct! Double 'n' before adding -ing for "run". -->
- [ ] C) The dog is runneing in the garden.
  <!-- feedback: Incorrect. You don't need to add an 'e' before 'ing'. -->
- [ ] D) The dog is runs in the garden.
  <!-- feedback: Incorrect. You cannot use the present simple 's' form with "is". -->

### Explicacion Pedagogica
The student analyzes the CVC rule (consonant-vowel-consonant) for doubling consonants in the -ing form.

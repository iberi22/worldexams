# Bundle Mastery: English Grade 7 — Period 3

This bundle covers present perfect for experiences, travel vocabulary, and making requests and offers. Topics for the third period of Grade 7, CEFR A2 level.

---

## Question 1 (Difficulty D3)

**ID:** `CO-ING-7-P3-comprehensive-001-MASTERY-v1`
**Bloom:** Remember
**ICFES:** Comunicación Lingüística

### Contexto
In English class at Colegio San Felipe in Bogotá, the teacher explains the present perfect tense.

### Enunciado
How do we form the present perfect tense?

### Options
- [ ] A) Subject + past simple + complement. <!-- feedback: Incorrect. That's past simple. -->
- [ ] B) Subject + have/has + past participle. <!-- feedback: Correct! Present perfect = have/has + past participle. -->
- [ ] C) Subject + am/is/are + verb-ing. <!-- feedback: Incorrect. That's present continuous. -->
- [ ] D) Subject + will + base verb. <!-- feedback: Incorrect. That's future. -->

### Explicación Pedagógica
Present perfect: have/has + past participle. For regular verbs, the past participle is the same as past simple (played, visited). For irregular verbs, memorize the third form (seen, eaten, gone).

### Contexto
A student wants to ask a classmate to lend her a pencil.

### Enunciado
Which is a polite request?

### Options
- [ ] A) "Give me your pencil." <!-- feedback: Incorrect. This is an order, not a request. -->
- [ ] B) "Pencil!" <!-- feedback: Incorrect. This is not a full sentence. -->
- [x] C) "Can I borrow your pencil, please?" <!-- feedback: Correct! Polite request with "can" and "please". -->

### Enunciado
What does this sentence mean?

### Options
- [ ] A) She visited the Amazon River yesterday. <!-- feedback: Incorrect. Present perfect doesn't specify when. -->
- [ ] B) She made a plan to visit the Amazon River. <!-- feedback: Incorrect. That's future. -->

## Question 12 (Difficulty D8)

**ID:** `CO-ING-7-P3-comprehensive-001-MASTERY-v12`
**Bloom:** Evaluate
**ICFES:** Comunicación Lingüística

### Contexto
A student wrote: "I have went to the Museo del Oro last week."

### Enunciado
What is wrong with this sentence?

### Options
- [ ] A) Nothing. It is correct. <!-- feedback: Incorrect. There are two problems. -->
- [ ] B) "Have went" → should be "have gone". Also, don't use "last week" with present perfect. <!-- feedback: Correct! Two errors: wrong past participle and specific past time. -->
- [ ] C) "Museo del Oro" should be in Spanish. <!-- feedback: Incorrect. Proper nouns can stay. -->
- [ ] D) "I" should be "Me". <!-- feedback: Incorrect. "I" is correct as the subject. -->

### Explicación Pedagógica
Two rules: (1) "Go" is irregular: went (past) → gone (past participle). (2) Present perfect cannot be used with specific past time expressions (yesterday, last week, in 2020). Use past simple for specific times.

## Question 15 (Difficulty D10)

**ID:** `CO-ING-7-P3-comprehensive-001-MASTERY-v15`
**Bloom:** Evaluate
**ICFES:** Comunicación Lingüística

### Contexto
A student wrote a travel paragraph:
"I have never fly in a plane. My family has travel to Medellín by bus many times. I want to visit the Eje Cafetero one day."

### Enunciado
Find and correct the errors in present perfect.

### Options
- [ ] A) No errors. <!-- feedback: Incorrect. There are errors. -->
- [ ] B) "have never fly" → "have never flew". <!-- feedback: Incorrect. "Flew" is past, not past participle. -->
- [x] C) "have never fly" → "have never flown", "has travel" → "has travelled". <!-- feedback: Correct! Fly → flown, travel → travelled (regular). -->
- [ ] D) "has travel" → "has traveld". <!-- feedback: Incorrect. "Travel" follows consonant + l doubling. -->

### Explicación Pedagógica
To form present perfect correctly: (1) Use the correct past participle (fly → flown, not "fly" or "flew"). (2) For regular verbs, add -ed (travel → travelled in British English, traveled in American). Always check both auxiliary and participle.

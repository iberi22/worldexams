# Bundle Mastery: English Grade 7 — Period 1

This bundle covers past simple (regular and irregular verbs), comparatives and superlatives, and giving directions. Topics for the first period of Grade 7, CEFR A2 level.

---

## Question 1 (Difficulty D3)

**ID:** `CO-ING-7-P1-comprehensive-001-MASTERY-v1`
**Bloom:** Remember
**ICFES:** Comunicación Lingüística

### Contexto
In English class at Colegio San Jorge in Bogotá, the teacher writes on the board: "Yesterday, I _____ (play) soccer."

### Enunciado
What is the correct past simple form of the verb "play"?

### Options
- [ ] A) playd <!-- feedback: Incorrect. The correct ending for regular verbs in past is "-ed", not "-d". -->
- [ ] B) played <!-- feedback: Correct! "Play" is a regular verb. The past form is "played". -->
- [ ] C) plaied <!-- feedback: Incorrect. For verbs ending in consonant + y, we change y to i and add -ed, but "play" ends in vowel + y. -->
- [ ] D) was play <!-- feedback: Incorrect. "Was" is used with the -ing form, not the base form. -->

### Explicación Pedagógica
Regular verbs in English form the past simple by adding "-ed". "Play" is regular: play → played. Some spelling rules: verbs ending in "e" just add "-d" (like → liked), verbs ending in consonant + "y" change to "i" + "ed" (study → studied).

## Question 2 (Difficulty D3)

**ID:** `CO-ING-7-P1-comprehensive-001-MASTERY-v2`
**Bloom:** Remember
**ICFES:** Comunicación Lingüística

### Contexto
In class, a student says: "Yesterday, I go to the museum."

### Enunciado
Is this sentence correct? If not, what is the correct past simple form of "go"?

### Options
- [ ] A) Yes, it is correct. <!-- feedback: Incorrect. "Go" is an irregular verb. -->
- [ ] B) "Goed" <!-- feedback: Incorrect. "Go" does not follow the regular -ed rule. -->
- [ ] C) "Went" <!-- feedback: Correct! "Go" is irregular: go → went. -->
- [ ] D) "Went" is only for plural subjects. <!-- feedback: Incorrect. "Went" works for all subjects (I went, she went, they went). -->

### Explicación Pedagógica
"Go" is an irregular verb: its past form is "went", not "goed". Irregular verbs don't follow the -ed rule. Common irregular verbs: go → went, eat → ate, see → saw, have → had.

## Question 3 (Difficulty D4)

**ID:** `CO-ING-7-P1-comprehensive-001-MASTERY-v3`
**Bloom:** Apply
**ICFES:** Comunicación Lingüística

### Contexto
Two students at Colegio La Salle in Medellín are comparing cities:
Student A: "Bogotá is big."
Student B: "Medellín is bigger than Bogotá."

### Enunciado
What is the comparative form of "big"?

### Options
- [ ] A) more big <!-- feedback: Incorrect. Short adjectives don't use "more". -->
- [ ] B) biger <!-- feedback: Incorrect. For CVC (consonant-vowel-consonant) words, double the last consonant. -->
- [ ] C) bigger <!-- feedback: Correct! For one-syllable adjectives ending in CVC, double the last consonant + -er. -->
- [ ] D) biggest <!-- feedback: Incorrect. This is the superlative form, not comparative. -->

### Explicación Pedagógica
One-syllable adjectives: add -er (tall → taller). If the adjective ends in CVC (consonant-vowel-consonant), double the final consonant: big → bigger, hot → hotter.

## Question 4 (Difficulty D4)

**ID:** `CO-ING-7-P1-comprehensive-001-MASTERY-v4`
**Bloom:** Understand
**ICFES:** Comunicación Lingüística

### Contexto
A tourist in Cartagena asks a student: "Excuse me, how do I get to the Castillo de San Felipe?"

### Enunciado
Which response correctly gives directions?

### Options
- [ ] A) "Is very far." <!-- feedback: Incorrect. This is not a proper direction, it's missing a subject. -->
- [ ] B) "Go straight ahead for two blocks, then turn left." <!-- feedback: Correct! This gives clear, sequential directions. -->
- [ ] C) "Yesterday I went there." <!-- feedback: Incorrect. This is past simple, not giving directions. -->
- [ ] D) "It is bigger than the other castle." <!-- feedback: Incorrect. This is a comparative, not a direction. -->

### Explicación Pedagógica
To give directions in English, use imperative verbs: go straight, turn left/right, walk, take the first street, etc. Common phrases: "Go straight ahead", "Turn right at the corner", "It's on your left."

## Question 6 (Difficulty D5)

**ID:** `CO-ING-7-P1-comprehensive-001-MASTERY-v6`
**Bloom:** Understand
**ICFES:** Comunicación Lingüística

### Contexto
In class, students compare three Colombian cities:
- Cartagena: hot
- Bogotá: cold
- Medellín: warm

### Enunciado
What is the superlative form of "hot"?

### Options
- [ ] A) more hot <!-- feedback: Incorrect. Short adjectives use -est, not "more". -->
- [ ] B) hottiest <!-- feedback: Incorrect. Double consonant for CVC, then add -est. -->
- [ ] C) hottest <!-- feedback: Correct! CVC rule: hot → hottest. -->
- [ ] D) most hot <!-- feedback: Incorrect. "Most" is for long adjectives (most beautiful). -->

### Explicación Pedagógica
Superlative of short adjectives: add -est (cold → coldest). For CVC adjectives, double the last consonant: hot → hottest. Use "the" before superlatives: "Cartagena is the hottest city."

## Question 7 (Difficulty D5)

**ID:** `CO-ING-7-P1-comprehensive-001-MASTERY-v7`
**Bloom:** Apply
**ICFES:** Comunicación Lingüística

### Contexto
A student at Colegio San Simón in Ibagué is comparing two phones:
"The iPhone is expensive. The Samsung is more expensive than the iPhone."

### Enunciado
What is the correct comparative form of "expensive"?

### Options
- [ ] A) expensiver <!-- feedback: Incorrect. Long adjectives don't take -er. -->
- [ ] B) more expensive <!-- feedback: Correct! For adjectives with 2+ syllables, use "more" + adjective. -->
- [ ] C) most expensive <!-- feedback: Incorrect. "Most" is for superlatives, not comparatives. -->
- [ ] D) more expensiver <!-- feedback: Incorrect. Use either -er or "more", not both. -->

### Explicación Pedagógica
For long adjectives (2+ syllables), form the comparative with "more": more expensive, more difficult, more interesting. Never use "more" + -er together (don't say "more expensiver").

## Question 8 (Difficulty D6)

**ID:** `CO-ING-7-P1-comprehensive-001-MASTERY-v8`
**Bloom:** Analyze
**ICFES:** Comunicación Lingüística

### Contexto
Read this paragraph about a trip to Salento, Quindío:

"Last weekend, my family and I visited Salento. We _____ (see) the Valle del Cocora. The palm trees _____ (be) very tall. We _____ (take) many photos."

### Enunciado
What are the correct past simple forms?

### Options
- [ ] A) seed, was, taked <!-- feedback: Incorrect. All three verbs need correction. -->
- [ ] B) saw, were, took <!-- feedback: Correct! See → saw, be → were (plural), take → took. -->
- [ ] C) seen, was, took <!-- feedback: Incorrect. "Seen" needs an auxiliary. -->
- [ ] D) saw, was, taked <!-- feedback: Incorrect. "The palm trees" is plural → "were". -->

### Explicación Pedagógica
Be is irregular and changes with subject: I/he/she/it → was, you/we/they → were. "The palm trees" is plural, so use "were". See → saw, take → took.

## Question 9 (Difficulty D6)

**ID:** `CO-ING-7-P1-comprehensive-001-MASTERY-v9`
**Bloom:** Apply
**ICFES:** Comunicación Lingüística

### Contexto
A tourist in Bogotá asks for directions to the Museo del Oro. You want to say: "Walk two blocks, then turn right. The museum is on your left."

### Enunciado
Which option gives these directions correctly in English?

### Options
- [ ] A) "Walk two blocks and then right turn. The museum left." <!-- feedback: Incorrect. Word order is wrong. -->
- [ ] B) "Walking two blocks, turning right. Museum is in left." <!-- feedback: Incorrect. Use imperative form. -->
- [ ] C) "Walk two blocks, then turn right. The museum is on your left." <!-- feedback: Correct! Clear imperative + location. -->
- [ ] D) "To walk two blocks and turn to the right. Museum left side." <!-- feedback: Incorrect. Not imperative and incomplete. -->

### Explicación Pedagógica
Giving directions in English: use the imperative (Walk, Turn, Go), then give the location using prepositions: on your left, on the corner, next to, across from.

## Question 10 (Difficulty D7)

**ID:** `CO-ING-7-P1-comprehensive-001-MASTERY-v10`
**Bloom:** Analyze
**ICFES:** Comunicación Lingüística

### Contexto
Read:
"The Sierra Nevada de Santa Marta is the _____ (high) coastal mountain in the world. It is _____ (tall) than the Alps in some parts."

### Enunciado
Complete the sentences with the correct forms.

### Options
- [ ] A) most high, taller <!-- feedback: Incorrect. "High" is one syllable. -->
- [ ] B) highest, taller <!-- feedback: Correct! Superlative: highest. Comparative: taller. -->
- [ ] C) higher, more tall <!-- feedback: Incorrect. First blank needs superlative (the...), second needs comparative. -->
- [ ] D) highest, more tall <!-- feedback: Incorrect. "Tall" is one syllable → "taller". -->

### Explicación Pedagógica
Comparative vs Superlative: comparative compares two things (-er or more), superlative compares one thing with a group (-est or most). Use "the" before superlatives.

**Bloom:** Analyze
**ICFES:** Comunicación Lingüística

### Contexto
Read: "Yesterday was a busy day. I _____ (have) breakfast, then I _____ (go) to school. After school, I _____ (do) my homework."

### Enunciado
What are the correct irregular past simple forms?

### Options
- [ ] A) haved, went, doed <!-- feedback: Incorrect. "Have" and "do" are irregular. -->
- [ ] B) had, went, did <!-- feedback: Correct! Have → had, go → went, do → did. -->

**ID:** `CO-ING-7-P1-comprehensive-001-MASTERY-v12`
**Bloom:** Evaluate
**ICFES:** Comunicación Lingüística

### Contexto
Two students are comparing Colombia to other countries.
Student A: "Colombia is beautiful."
Student B: "Yes, but I think Colombia is _____ (beautiful) country in the world."

### Enunciado
Complete Student B's sentence with the correct superlative form.

### Options
- [ ] A) most beautiful country <!-- feedback: Incorrect. Don't forget the article "the". -->
- [ ] B) the beautifulest <!-- feedback: Incorrect. Long adjectives don't use -est. -->
- [ ] C) the most beautiful <!-- feedback: Correct! Long adjective: most + adjective + "the" before. -->
- [ ] D) more beautiful <!-- feedback: Incorrect. "More" is comparative, and the sentence needs a superlative. -->

### Explicación Pedagógica
For superlatives of long adjectives (2+ syllables): "the most + adjective". Example: beautiful → the most beautiful. Always use "the" before superlatives.

## Question 13 (Difficulty D9)

**ID:** `CO-ING-7-P1-comprehensive-001-MASTERY-v13`
**Bloom:** Evaluate
**ICFES:** Comunicación Lingüística

### Contexto
A student wrote: "Yesterday, I buyed a souvenir in the Old Town of Cartagena. I also swimmed in the beach."

### Enunciado
How many errors are there in this text?

### Options
- [ ] A) 0 errors. <!-- feedback: Incorrect. Both verbs are incorrect. -->
- [ ] B) 1 error. <!-- feedback: Incorrect. There are two errors. -->
- [ ] C) 2 errors. <!-- feedback: Correct! "Buyed" → "bought", "swimmed" → "swam". -->

### Explicación Pedagógica
"Buy" is irregular: buy → bought. "Swim" is irregular: swim → swam. Always check if a verb is regular or irregular before using it in past simple.

## Question 14 (Difficulty D9)

**ID:** `CO-ING-7-P1-comprehensive-001-MASTERY-v14`
**Bloom:** Evaluate
**ICFES:** Comunicación Lingüística

### Contexto
Read this conversation:
A: "Excuse me, is there a supermarket near here?"
B: "Yes. Go straight for one block. Then turn left. The supermarket is between the bank and the pharmacy."

### Enunciado
If you are at the starting point, where do you find the supermarket?

### Options
- [ ] A) On the right at the first corner. <!-- feedback: Incorrect. The directions say turn left. -->
- [ ] B) Behind the pharmacy. <!-- feedback: Incorrect. It says "between the bank and the pharmacy". -->
- [ ] C) One block straight, then left, between a bank and a pharmacy. <!-- feedback: Correct! This matches the directions given. -->
- [ ] D) Across from the school. <!-- feedback: Incorrect. The directions don't mention a school. -->

### Explicación Pedagógica
To follow directions, listen for: sequence words (first, then), action verbs (go, turn), and prepositions of place (between, next to, on the corner). Practice following multi-step directions.

## Question 15 (Difficulty D10)

**ID:** `CO-ING-7-P1-comprehensive-001-MASTERY-v15`
**Bloom:** Evaluate
**ICFES:** Comunicación Lingüística

### Contexto
Read and correct: "My trip to San Andrés was amazing. I swim in the sea, I eat fresh fish, and I take a boat tour. Everything was perfect!"

### Enunciado
All the past simple verbs in this text are:

### Options
- [ ] A) Correct: swim, eat, take. <!-- feedback: Incorrect. These are base forms, not past. -->
- [ ] B) Incorrect: should be swimmed, eated, taked. <!-- feedback: Incorrect. Those are not the correct past forms. -->
- [x] C) Incorrect: should be swam, ate, took. <!-- feedback: Correct! These are the correct irregular past forms. -->
- [ ] D) Correct: swam, ate, took are used in the text. <!-- feedback: Incorrect. The text uses base forms. -->

### Explicación Pedagógica
When narrating past events, ALL main verbs must be in past simple. Mixing base forms with past is a common error. Always check that your verbs are all in the correct tense when telling a story about the past.

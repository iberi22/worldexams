# Bundle Mastery: English Grade 7 — Period 4

This bundle covers past continuous, present perfect vs past simple, and reading comprehension at A2 level. Topics for the fourth period of Grade 7, CEFR A2 level.

---

## Question 1 (Difficulty D3)

**ID:** `CO-ING-7-P4-comprehensive-001-MASTERY-v1`
**Bloom:** Remember
**ICFES:** Comunicación Lingüística

### Contexto
In English class at Colegio San José in Pasto, the teacher explains a new tense.

### Enunciado
How do we form the past continuous tense?

### Options
- [ ] A) Subject + was/were + verb-ing. <!-- feedback: Correct! Past continuous = was/were + verb-ing. -->
- [ ] B) Subject + have/has + past participle. <!-- feedback: Incorrect. That's present perfect. -->
- [ ] C) Subject + past simple. <!-- feedback: Incorrect. That's past simple. -->
- [ ] D) Subject + am/is/are + verb-ing. <!-- feedback: Incorrect. That's present continuous. -->

### Explicación Pedagógica
Past continuous: was/were + verb-ing. Use "was" for I/he/she/it, and "were" for you/we/they. Example: "I was reading" (Yo estaba leyendo).

## Question 2 (Difficulty D3)

**ID:** `CO-ING-7-P4-comprehensive-001-MASTERY-v2`
**Bloom:** Remember
**ICFES:** Comunicación Lingüística

### Contexto
A student is confused about when to use present perfect vs past simple.

### Enunciado
Which time expression CANNOT be used with present perfect?

### Options
- [ ] A) Ever. <!-- feedback: Incorrect. "Ever" is used with present perfect. -->
- [ ] B) Never. <!-- feedback: Incorrect. "Never" is used with present perfect. -->
- [x] C) Yesterday. <!-- feedback: Correct! Specific past times (yesterday, last week, in 2010) use past simple, not present perfect. -->
- [ ] D) Already. <!-- feedback: Incorrect. "Already" is used with present perfect. -->

### Explicación Pedagógica
Present perfect cannot be used with specific past time expressions (yesterday, last year, in 2020, when I was a child). Use past simple for those. Present perfect uses: ever, never, already, yet, just, so far.

## Question 3 (Difficulty D4)

**ID:** `CO-ING-7-P4-comprehensive-001-MASTERY-v3`
**Bloom:** Understand
**ICFES:** Comunicación Lingüística

### Contexto
Read: "I was watching TV when my mom arrived."

### Enunciado
What does the past continuous ("was watching") express?

### Options
- [ ] A) A completed action in the past. <!-- feedback: Incorrect. Past simple expresses completed actions. -->
- [ ] B) An action that was in progress when another action happened. <!-- feedback: Correct! Past continuous = longer action in progress; past simple = shorter action. -->
- [ ] C) A future plan. <!-- feedback: Incorrect. Not future. -->
- [ ] D) A habit in the present. <!-- feedback: Incorrect. Not present. -->

### Explicación Pedagógica
Past continuous sets the background (longer action). Past simple is the main event (shorter action). "I was watching TV (background) when my mom arrived (main event)."

## Question 4 (Difficulty D4)

**ID:** `CO-ING-7-P4-comprehensive-001-MASTERY-v4`
**Bloom:** Understand
**ICFES:** Comunicación Lingüística

### Contexto
Read: "She has lived in Bogotá since 2015."

### Enunciado
What does this sentence mean?

### Options
- [ ] A) She lived in Bogotá in 2015 but moved away. <!-- feedback: Incorrect. That would be past simple. -->
- [x] B) She moved to Bogotá in 2015 and still lives there now. <!-- feedback: Correct! Present perfect + "since" shows an action that started in the past and continues to the present. -->
- [ ] C) She will move to Bogotá in 2015. <!-- feedback: Incorrect. Future can't use 2015. -->
- [ ] D) She is planning to visit Bogotá. <!-- feedback: Incorrect. "Has lived" is not a plan. -->

### Explicación Pedagógica
Present perfect with "since" (point in time) or "for" (duration) shows an action that started in the past and continues now. "She has lived in Bogotá since 2015" = she moved there in 2015 and still lives there.

## Question 5 (Difficulty D5)

**ID:** `CO-ING-7-P4-comprehensive-001-MASTERY-v5`
**Bloom:** Apply
**ICFES:** Comunicación Lingüística

### Contexto
Read the sentence: "They _____ (play) soccer when it _____ (start) to rain."

### Enunciado
Complete the sentence with the correct tenses.

### Options
- [ ] A) played, started <!-- feedback: Incorrect. Both actions in past simple loses the connection. -->
- [ ] B) were playing, started <!-- feedback: Correct! Past continuous for the longer action (playing), past simple for the interruption (started). -->
- [ ] C) have played, started <!-- feedback: Incorrect. Mix of present perfect and past. -->
- [ ] D) were playing, was starting <!-- feedback: Incorrect. Both as continuous doesn't show the interruption. -->

### Explicación Pedagógica
When one action interrupts another: past continuous (ongoing) + past simple (interruption). "They were playing soccer (in progress) when it started to rain (interruption)."

## Question 6 (Difficulty D5)

**ID:** `CO-ING-7-P4-comprehensive-001-MASTERY-v6`
**Bloom:** Understand
**ICFES:** Comunicación Lingüística

### Contexto
Read the following short text:

"Last weekend, my family visited Cartagena. We walked through the Old Town. The weather was hot. I bought a souvenir."

### Enunciado
Why does this text use only past simple?

### Options
- [x] A) Because it describes completed actions in a finished time period (last weekend). All events are finished. <!-- feedback: Correct! Past simple for completed past actions. -->
- [ ] B) Because the writer doesn't know present perfect. <!-- feedback: Incorrect. The choice is grammatical. -->
- [ ] C) Because it's about experiences. <!-- feedback: Incorrect. If it were about life experiences, present perfect would work. -->
- [ ] D) Because the events are in the future. <!-- feedback: Incorrect. "Last weekend" is past. -->

### Explicación Pedagógica
Use past simple when the time period is finished (yesterday, last weekend, in 2019) and the actions are complete. Present perfect would be wrong here because "last weekend" is a specific finished time.

### Enunciado
Both actions were happening at the same time. Choose the correct tense.

### Options
- [ ] A) did, played <!-- feedback: Incorrect. Past simple doesn't show simultaneous ongoing actions. -->
- [x] B) was doing, was playing <!-- feedback: Correct! Both actions in past continuous = simultaneous ongoing actions. -->

## Question 8 (Difficulty D6)

**ID:** `CO-ING-7-P4-comprehensive-001-MASTERY-v8`
**Bloom:** Analyze
**ICFES:** Comunicación Lingüística

### Contexto
Read the reading comprehension text:

"María has traveled to many places. She has visited the Amazon, the Andes, and the Caribbean coast. Last year, she went to the Coffee Region. She loved the landscapes."

### Enunciado
Which sentence uses present perfect and which uses past simple? Why?

### Options
- [ ] A) All are present perfect. <!-- feedback: Incorrect. "Went" and "loved" are past simple. -->
- [ ] B) "Has traveled" and "has visited" are present perfect (experiences). "Went" and "loved" are past simple (specific time: last year). <!-- feedback: Correct! Correct analysis of the tenses. -->
- [ ] C) All are past simple. <!-- feedback: Incorrect. "Has traveled" and "has visited" are present perfect. -->
- [ ] D) "Went" is present perfect. <!-- feedback: Incorrect. "Went" is past simple of "go". -->

### Explicación Pedagógica
In reading comprehension, the choice of tense gives information. Present perfect = experiences, no specific time. Past simple = completed actions at specific past times. Both can appear in the same text.

# Bundle MASTERY: Means of Transport - Grado 5 (W26)

Este bundle enseña vocabulario sobre medios de transporte, preguntas para llegar a lugares y preposiciones de lugar en viajes por ciudades colombianas.

**Bloom:** Remember
**ICFES:** Lexico
**Expected_Success:** 0.90
**Contexto:** At a bus stop in Bogotá, a boy waits for the school bus in the morning.
### Enunciado
Which transport has four wheels and carries many people in the city?
### Opciones
- [ ] A) Bicycle.
  <!-- feedback: A bicycle has two wheels and carries one person. -->
- [ ] B) Airplane.
  <!-- feedback: An airplane flies in the sky, not on city streets. -->
- [x] C) Bus.
  <!-- feedback: Bus is correct because it carries many people in the city. -->

### Explicacion Pedagogica
La palabra bus es esencial para hablar del transporte escolar y urbano. También es útil asociarla con bus stop para pedir direcciones.

### Enunciado

### Opciones
- [ ] A) on foot only, never by bus.
  <!-- feedback: The sentence needs by plus transport, not on foot here. -->
- [x] B) by
  <!-- feedback: By is correct because we say by bus, by taxi, by bike. -->

La estructura by más transporte expresa el medio utilizado para viajar. También contrasta con on foot, que significa caminando.

## Question 6 [D7-D8]
**ID:** CO-ING-5-2026-W26-means-of-transport-travel-001-MASTERY-bundle-v6
**Bloom:** Analyze
**ICFES:** Part 6 - Reading Comprehension
**Expected_Success:** 0.70
**Contexto:** At a school in Pereira, Laura reads a travel text about two cities in Colombia.
### Enunciado
Text: I go from Manizales to Pereira by bus. The bus is behind the school. Where is the bus and how does she travel?
### Opciones
- [ ] A) The bus is in front of the school and she flies.
  <!-- feedback: The text says behind, not in front, and by bus, not by plane. -->
- [x] B) The bus is behind the school and she travels by bus.
  <!-- feedback: This is correct because it matches both place and transport. -->
- [ ] C) The bus is on the bus and she walks.
  <!-- feedback: She does not walk, she travels by bus. -->
- [ ] D) The bus is under the park and she swims.
  <!-- feedback: Under the park and swimming do not match the text. -->
### Explicacion Pedagogica
Analizar exige unir el medio de transporte con la preposición de lugar. También es útil subrayar by bus y behind para responder con precisión.

## Question 8 [D9-D10]

**Bloom:** Evaluate
**ICFES:** Lingüística
**Expected_Success:** 0.62
**Contexto:** In a travel agency in Bogotá, a boy compares two trip options to Cartagena.
### Enunciado
Option 1: By airplane, 1 hour, $180.000 COP. Option 2: By bus, 18 hours, $90.000 COP. He wants the fastest trip. Which is the best choice and why?
### Opciones
- [ ] A) Option 2, because 18 hours is faster than 1 hour.
  <!-- feedback: Eighteen hours is much slower than one hour. -->
- [ ] B) Both are equal, because bus and airplane have the same speed.
  <!-- feedback: An airplane is much faster than a bus. -->
- [x] C) Option 1, because one hour by airplane is faster although it costs more.
  <!-- feedback: This is correct because it evaluates speed versus price well. -->
- [ ] D) Neither, because airplanes cannot fly in Colombia.
  <!-- feedback: Airplanes fly between Colombian cities every day. -->
### Explicacion Pedagogica
Evaluar implica comparar tiempo y precio en COP antes de decidir. Asimismo, la justificación con because demuestra una elección razonada del transporte.

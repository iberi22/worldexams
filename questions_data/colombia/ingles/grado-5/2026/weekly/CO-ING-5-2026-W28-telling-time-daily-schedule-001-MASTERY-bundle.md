# Bundle MASTERY: Telling Time - Grado 5 (W28)

Este bundle enseña a decir la hora con o clock, half past y quarter, y a describir la rutina diaria y el horario escolar con claridad.

## Question 1 [D3-D4]
**ID:** CO-ING-5-2026-W28-telling-time-daily-schedule-001-MASTERY-bundle-v1
**Bloom:** Remember
**ICFES:** Lexico
**Expected_Success:** 0.90
**Contexto:** In a classroom in Bogotá, a clock on the wall shows 3:00 during English class.
### Enunciado
The clock shows 3:00. How do you say the time in English?
### Opciones
- [ ] A) It is half past three.
  <!-- feedback: Half past three means 3:30, not 3:00. -->
- [x] B) It is three o clock.
  <!-- feedback: Three o clock is correct because the clock shows 3:00. -->
- [ ] C) It is quarter past three.
  <!-- feedback: Quarter past three means 3:15, not 3:00. -->
- [ ] D) It is three apples.
  <!-- feedback: Apples are fruit and do not tell the time. -->
### Explicacion Pedagogica
La expresión o clock se usa para horas exactas. También es fundamental practicar con un reloj real para fijar el vocabulario.

  <!-- feedback: Half past seven means 7:30, thirty minutes late. -->

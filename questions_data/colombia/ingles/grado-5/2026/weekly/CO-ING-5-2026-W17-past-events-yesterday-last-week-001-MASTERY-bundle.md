# Bundle MASTERY: Past Events with Yesterday and Last Week - Grado 5 (W17)

Past simple with was/were and regular verbs (-ed), plus time expressions yesterday and last week.

## Question 1 [D3-D4]
**ID:** CO-ING-5-2026-W17-past-events-yesterday-last-week-001-MASTERY-bundle-v1
**Bloom:** Remember
**ICFES:** Lingüística
**Expected_Success:** 0.90
**Contexto:** In a classroom in Tunja, Boyacá, the teacher writes "yesterday" on the board and asks when it happened.

### Enunciado
The word "yesterday" refers to:

### Opciones
- [x] A) The day before today.
  <!-- feedback: Correct: yesterday is the past time marker for the previous day. -->
- [ ] B) The day after today.
  <!-- feedback: Incorrect: the day after today is tomorrow, a future marker. -->
- [ ] C) Every day of the week.
  <!-- feedback: Incorrect: every day is always, not a past marker. -->
- [ ] D) A day in the next month.
  <!-- feedback: Incorrect: that is future time, not yesterday. -->

### Explicacion Pedagogica
Yesterday es un marcador de pasado: señala el día anterior al de hoy y suele acompañar al pasado simple.

## Question 3 [D5-D6]

**ICFES:** Comunicación y representación

**Contexto:** In Neiva, Huila, Sara writes in her diary: "Yesterday I ___ football in the park". She needs the past of "play".

### Enunciado
Which is the correct sentence?

### Opciones
- [ ] A) Yesterday I play football in the park.
  <!-- feedback: Incorrect: play is present; with yesterday we need the past form. -->
- [x] B) Yesterday I played football in the park.
  <!-- feedback: Correct: regular verbs add -ed in the past: played. -->
- [ ] C) Yesterday I playing football in the park.
  <!-- feedback: Incorrect: playing needs was/were, not yesterday alone. -->
- [ ] D) Yesterday I plays football in the park.
  <!-- feedback: Incorrect: plays is present for he/she, never past. -->

### Explicacion Pedagogica
Los verbos regulares forman el pasado agregando -ed: play → played. Con yesterday siempre se usa el pasado.

## Question 4 [D5-D6]
**ID:** CO-ING-5-2026-W17-past-events-yesterday-last-week-001-MASTERY-bundle-v4
**Bloom:** Apply
**ICFES:** Lingüística
**Expected_Success:** 0.78
**Contexto:** In Sincelejo, Sucre, two brothers talk: "We ___ at the stadium last week". The verb is "was/were".

### Enunciado
Which is the correct sentence?

### Opciones
- [ ] A) We was at the stadium last week.
  <!-- feedback: Incorrect: was goes with I/he/she, not with we. -->
- [ ] B) We is at the stadium last week.
  <!-- feedback: Incorrect: is is present; last week needs past. -->
- [ ] C) We are at the stadium last week.
  <!-- feedback: Incorrect: are is present plural, not past. -->
- [x] D) We were at the stadium last week.
  <!-- feedback: Correct: were goes with you/we/they in the past. -->

### Explicacion Pedagogica
Was se usa con I/he/she/it y were con you/we/they. Como el sujeto es we, lo correcto es were.

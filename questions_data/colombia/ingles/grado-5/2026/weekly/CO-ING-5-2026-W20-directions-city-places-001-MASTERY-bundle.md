# Bundle MASTERY: Asking for Directions in the City - Grado 5 (W20)

Asking for and giving directions in English to find places in a Colombian city.

## Question 1 [D3-D4]
**ID:** CO-ING-5-2026-W20-directions-city-places-001-MASTERY-bundle-v1
**Bloom:** Remember
**ICFES:** Lexico
**Expected_Success:** 0.90
**Contexto:** In Cartagena, a tourist asks a student how to find the museum.

### Enunciado
Which word is the opposite of "turn left"?

### Opciones
- [ ] A) Go straight.
  <!-- feedback: "Go straight" means to continue forward, not the opposite of "turn left". -->
- [x] B) Turn right.
  <!-- feedback: "Turn right" is the opposite direction of "turn left". -->
- [ ] C) Next to.
  <!-- feedback: "Next to" is a location phrase, not a direction opposite to "left". -->
- [ ] D) Stop.
  <!-- feedback: "Stop" means to halt, not the opposite of "turn left". -->

### Explicacion Pedagogica
Direction words come in pairs: left and right, up and down, forward and back. Knowing the opposite of "turn left" helps students understand and give directions.

**Contexto:** In Bogota, a student reads a line in a dialogue: "The park is next to the church."

### Enunciado
What does "next to" mean in this sentence?

### Opciones
- [ ] A) Far away from.
  <!-- feedback: "Far away from" is the opposite of "next to". -->
- [ ] B) Inside.
  <!-- feedback: "Inside" describes being within something, not beside it. -->
- [x] C) Beside or very close to.
  <!-- feedback: "Next to" means one place is beside or very close to another. -->
- [ ] D) Above.
  <!-- feedback: "Above" describes a higher position, not a position beside. -->

### Explicacion Pedagogica
Location phrases such as "next to", "in front of", "between" and "behind" describe where a place is. "Next to" means the two places are side by side.

### Opciones
- [x] A) Go straight for two blocks; the museum is on your right.
  <!-- feedback: "Go straight" tells the person to continue ahead to reach the place. -->
- [ ] B) Turn left and stop at the first house.
  <!-- feedback: The museum is straight ahead, so turning left would lead the wrong way. -->

# Bundle MASTERY: Future Plans with Going To and Weekend - Grado 5 (W18)

Going to plus activity and place verbs to talk about weekend plans.

## Question 1 [D3-D4]
**ID:** CO-ING-5-2026-W18-future-plans-going-to-weekend-001-MASTERY-bundle-v1
**Bloom:** Remember
**ICFES:** Lingüística
**Expected_Success:** 0.90
**Contexto:** In a classroom in Tunja, Boyacá, the teacher explains how to talk about plans for Saturday.

### Enunciado
Which structure expresses a future plan?

### Opciones
- [x] A) I am going to visit my grandmother.
  <!-- feedback: Correct: be + going to + verb expresses a future plan. -->
- [ ] B) I visit my grandmother every day.
  <!-- feedback: Incorrect: that is a present routine, not a future plan. -->
- [ ] C) I visited my grandmother yesterday.
  <!-- feedback: Incorrect: that is past, not a future plan. -->
- [ ] D) I visiting my grandmother now.
  <!-- feedback: Incorrect: that is incomplete and points to the present, not the future. -->

### Explicacion Pedagogica
Going to sirve para hablar de planes futuros: se forma con el verbo be (am/is/are) + going to + verbo principal.

## Question 3 [D5-D6]
**ID:** CO-ING-5-2026-W18-future-plans-going-to-weekend-001-MASTERY-bundle-v3
**Bloom:** Apply
**ICFES:** Comunicación y representación
**Expected_Success:** 0.82
**Contexto:** In Neiva, Huila, Camilo tells his friend about Saturday: "I ___ play football in the stadium". He needs am/is/are.

### Enunciado
Which is the correct sentence?

### Opciones
- [ ] A) I is going to play football in the stadium.
  <!-- feedback: Incorrect: is goes with he/she, never with I. -->
- [x] B) I am going to play football in the stadium.
  <!-- feedback: Correct: I always goes with am in the going to structure. -->
- [ ] C) I are going to play football in the stadium.
  <!-- feedback: Incorrect: are goes with you/we/they, not with I. -->
- [ ] D) I be going to play football in the stadium.
  <!-- feedback: Incorrect: be alone is not conjugated; with I we need am. -->

### Explicacion Pedagogica
El verbo be debe concordar: I am, he/she is, you/we/they are. Con I, la forma correcta es am going to.

## Question 8 [D9-D10]

**Bloom:** Evaluate
**ICFES:** Comunicación y representación
**Expected_Success:** 0.60
**Contexto:** In Yopal, Casanare, a student presents: "I is going to visit the library, they is going to play football and we am going to swim".

### Enunciado
How should the presentation be evaluated?

### Opciones
- [ ] A) It is perfect because is and am agree with every subject.
  <!-- feedback: Incorrect: is does not go with I or they, and am does not go with we. -->
- [ ] B) It is perfect because going to never needs be.
  <!-- feedback: Incorrect: going to always needs be conjugated before it. -->
- [ ] C) Only the verbs visit, play and swim are wrong.
  <!-- feedback: Incorrect: those main verbs are correct in base form; the errors are in be. -->
- [x] D) It has three errors in be: I requires am, they require are and we require are; the main verbs are correct.
  <!-- feedback: Correct: evaluates each subject-be pair and keeps the base verbs visit, play and swim. -->

### Explicacion Pedagogica
Evaluar exige revisar cada pareja sujeto-be: I am, they are, we are. Los verbos principales van siempre en forma base después de going to.

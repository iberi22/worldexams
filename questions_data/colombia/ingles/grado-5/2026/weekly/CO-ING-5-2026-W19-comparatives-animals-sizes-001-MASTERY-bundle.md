### Enunciado

### Opciones
- [ ] A) Biggest.
  <!-- feedback: "Biggest" is the superlative form, used to compare three or more things. -->
- [ ] B) Big.
  <!-- feedback: "Big" is the base adjective, not the comparative form. -->

Short adjectives usually form the comparative by adding "-er": big becomes bigger. The comparative is used to compare two people, animals or things.

## Question 3 [D5-D6]

**Bloom:** Apply
**ICFES:** Lingüística
**Expected_Success:** 0.82
**Contexto:** In Medellin, a student describes the jaguar and the sloth for a class project.

### Enunciado
Choose the correct comparative sentence about the jaguar (fast) and the sloth (slow).

### Opciones
- [ ] A) The jaguar is more fast than the sloth.
  <!-- feedback: Short adjectives like "fast" form the comparative with "-er", not "more". -->
- [x] B) The jaguar is faster than the sloth.
  <!-- feedback: "Faster than" is the correct comparative form for the short adjective "fast". -->
- [ ] C) The jaguar is fastest than the sloth.
  <!-- feedback: "Fastest" is the superlative and does not take "than". -->
- [ ] D) The jaguar is fast more than the sloth.
  <!-- feedback: The word order is incorrect for a comparative sentence. -->

### Explicacion Pedagogica
To compare two animals, use the comparative form plus "than": The jaguar is faster than the sloth. Short adjectives add "-er" instead of using "more".

## Question 4 [D5-D6]

**Contexto:** In a school in Cali, students write about the pink dolphin, one of the largest freshwater dolphins in the world.

### Enunciado
Choose the correct superlative sentence about the pink dolphin among three river animals.

### Opciones
- [x] A) The pink dolphin is the largest of the three river animals.
  <!-- feedback: "The largest" is the correct superlative form to compare three or more animals. -->
- [ ] B) The pink dolphin is larger of the three river animals.
  <!-- feedback: "Larger" is comparative and needs "than"; the superlative is "the largest". -->
- [ ] C) The pink dolphin is the more large of the three river animals.
  <!-- feedback: "More large" is not the correct superlative form of "large". -->
- [ ] D) The pink dolphin is largest than the three river animals.
  <!-- feedback: The superlative does not use "than"; it uses "the largest". -->

### Explicacion Pedagogica
The superlative compares three or more things and usually takes "the" before the adjective: the largest, the fastest, the smallest. It does not use "than".

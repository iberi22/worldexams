# Bundle MASTERY: Abilities with "can / can't" and Sports - Grado 5 (W21)

Use of modal verbs "can" and "cannot" to talk about abilities in sports, basic sports vocabulary, and short affirmative and negative sentences in English.

**ID:** CO-ING-5-2026-W21-abilities-can-cannot-sports-001-MASTERY-bundle-v1
**Bloom:** Remember
**ICFES:** Comunicación y representación
**Expected_Success:** 0.90
**Contexto:** In a fifth-grade class in Medellin, the teacher shows flashcards of sports like soccer, swimming, and basketball.

### Enunciado
Which word in English means the ability to do something?

### Opciones
- [x] A) "Can".
  <!-- feedback: "Can" expresses ability in English (e.g., I can swim). -->
- [ ] B) "Must".
  <!-- feedback: "Must" expresses obligation, not ability. -->
- [ ] C) "Should".
  <!-- feedback: "Should" is used to give advice, not to talk about ability. -->
- [ ] D) "May".
  <!-- feedback: "May" is mainly used for permission or possibility, not ability. -->

### Explicacion Pedagogica
Reconocer el modal "can" como el verbo que expresa habilidad en inglés.

### Opciones
- [x] A) Juan cannot ride a bike.
  <!-- feedback: "Cannot" shows that Juan does not have the ability to ride a bike. -->
- [ ] B) Juan can ride a bike.
  <!-- feedback: According to the text, Juan cannot ride a bike. -->

  <!-- feedback: "Can" does not take a final -s; we say "Juan can ride". -->

  <!-- feedback: The correct negative is "cannot" or "can't", not "don't can". -->

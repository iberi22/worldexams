# Bundle MASTERY: At the Doctor - Grado 5 (W25)

Este bundle desarrolla vocabulario sobre el médico, los síntomas como headache y fever, las partes del cuerpo y los consejos con should y should not en situaciones cotidianas de salud.

## Question 1 [D3-D4]
**ID:** CO-ING-5-2026-W25-at-the-doctor-health-001-MASTERY-bundle-v1
**Bloom:** Remember
**ICFES:** Lexico
**Expected_Success:** 0.90
**Contexto:** In a clinic in Medellín, a girl talks to the doctor about her headache.
### Enunciado
Which body part hurts when you have a headache?
### Opciones
- [ ] A) Foot.
  <!-- feedback: The foot is in the leg, not in the head. -->
- [x] B) Head.
  <!-- feedback: Head is correct because a headache is pain in the head. -->
- [ ] C) Hand.
  <!-- feedback: The hand is for writing, not related to headache. -->
- [ ] D) Knee.
  <!-- feedback: The knee is in the leg, not related to headache. -->
### Explicacion Pedagogica
La palabra headache combina head y ache, que significa dolor. También es útil recordar que el médico pregunta Where does it hurt para localizar el dolor.

## Question 2 [D3-D4]
**ID:** CO-ING-5-2026-W25-at-the-doctor-health-001-MASTERY-bundle-v2
**Bloom:** Understand
**ICFES:** Reading Comprehension
**Expected_Success:** 0.88
**Contexto:** At a school in Cali, the nurse checks a boy with a thermometer in the morning.
### Enunciado
Tom feels very hot and the thermometer shows 39 degrees. What symptom does Tom have?
### Opciones
- [x] A) He has a fever.
  <!-- feedback: Fever is correct because a hot body and high temperature mean fever. -->
- [ ] B) He has a toothache.
  <!-- feedback: A toothache is pain in a tooth, not a hot body. -->
- [ ] C) He has a cut.
  <!-- feedback: A cut is an injury on the skin, not a high temperature. -->
- [ ] D) He is hungry.
  <!-- feedback: Being hungry means you need food, not that your body is hot. -->
### Explicacion Pedagogica
La comprensión lectora exige conectar thermometer y hot con fever. Además, la expresión have a fever es fija y no se dice make a fever.

## Question 8 [D9-D10]

**ICFES:** Lingüística

**Contexto:** In a clinic in Santa Marta, a doctor evaluates three health tips on a wall chart.
### Enunciado
Tip 1: You should wash your hands. Tip 2: You should not eat too much candy. Tip 3: You should drink dirty water. Which evaluation is correct?
### Opciones
- [ ] A) Tip 3 is good because dirty water is healthy.
  <!-- feedback: Dirty water causes disease, so this tip is dangerous. -->
- [x] B) Tips 1 and 2 are good, but Tip 3 is bad.
  <!-- feedback: This is correct because hygiene and less candy help, but dirty water harms. -->
- [ ] C) All tips are good for your health.
  <!-- feedback: Tip 3 is not good, so not all tips are healthy. -->
- [ ] D) Tips 1 and 3 are good, but Tip 2 is bad.
  <!-- feedback: Less candy is good advice and dirty water is bad advice. -->
### Explicacion Pedagogica
Evaluar implica juzgar qué consejos protegen la salud y cuáles la ponen en riesgo. Asimismo, el uso correcto de should y should not distingue un buen consejo de uno peligroso.

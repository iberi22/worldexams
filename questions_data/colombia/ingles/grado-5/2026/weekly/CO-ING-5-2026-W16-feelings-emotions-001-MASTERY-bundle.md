### Opciones
- [x] A) Happy.
  <!-- feedback: "Happy" is the feeling of joy shown when you smile and laugh. -->

## Question 3 [D5-D6]

**ICFES:** Reading Comprehension
**Expected_Success:** 0.78
**Contexto:** A boy in Bucaramanga says: "I am scared because there is a big storm outside."

### Enunciado
Which sentence correctly uses the verb "be" to describe how the boy feels?

### Opciones
- [x] A) He is scared.
  <!-- feedback: "Is" is the correct form of "be" for the third person singular subject "he". -->
- [ ] B) He are scared.
  <!-- feedback: "Are" is used with plural subjects like "they", not with "he". -->
- [ ] C) He scared is.
  <!-- feedback: In English the verb "be" comes before the adjective, not after it. -->
- [ ] D) He am scared.
  <!-- feedback: "Am" is used only with "I", not with "he". -->

### Explicacion Pedagogica
Applying the correct form of the verb "be" with the third person singular to describe feelings.

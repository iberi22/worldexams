# Bundle MASTERY: Present Continuous for Actions Happening Now - Grado 5 (W22)

Use of the Present Continuous tense to describe actions that are happening at the moment of speaking, including affirmative, negative, and interrogative forms.

## Question 1 [D3-D4]
**ID:** CO-ING-5-2026-W22-present-continuous-actions-now-001-MASTERY-bundle-v1
**Bloom:** Remember
**ICFES:** Comunicación y representación
**Expected_Success:** 0.90
**Contexto:** In a fifth-grade class in Barranquilla, the teacher explains how to describe what classmates are doing right now.

### Enunciado
What is the correct structure of the Present Continuous tense in English?

### Opciones
- [x] A) Subject + am/is/are + verb-ing.
  <!-- feedback: The Present Continuous uses the verb "to be" plus the main verb with -ing. -->
- [ ] B) Subject + verb in past tense.
  <!-- feedback: The past simple is used for finished actions, not for actions happening now. -->
- [ ] C) Subject + do/does + verb.
  <!-- feedback: "Do/does" is used in the present simple, not in the continuous. -->
- [ ] D) Subject + will + verb.
  <!-- feedback: "Will" is used for the future, not for actions happening now. -->

### Explicacion Pedagogica
Reconocer la estructura del Presente Continuo en inglés: sujeto + am/is/are + verbo en -ing.

Este paquete enseña vocabulario de la naturaleza, acciones de reciclaje y el uso de there is y there are. También presenta ecosistemas colombianos como el páramo y la Amazonía en lecturas sencillas en inglés.

**Contexto:** In a classroom in Cali, there is one recycling box for paper near the door.
### Enunciado
Choose the correct sentence about the box.
### Opciones
- [ ] A) There are one box for paper.
  <!-- feedback: "There are" is for plural nouns, not for "one box". -->
- [x] B) There is one box for paper.
  <!-- feedback: "There is" is correct with the singular noun "one box". -->
- [ ] C) There is three boxes for paper.
  <!-- feedback: "There is" is not correct with the plural "three boxes". -->

  <!-- feedback: "There am" is never correct in English grammar. -->
### Explicacion Pedagogica
There is se usa con sustantivos singulares como one box. También se refuerza la diferencia básica entre singular y plural en inglés.

## Question 7 [D7-D8]
**ID:** CO-ING-5-2026-W31-environment-recycling-nature-001-MASTERY-bundle-v7
**Bloom:** Analyze
**ICFES:** Lingüística
**Expected_Success:** 0.68
**Contexto:** In Santa Marta, a teacher writes two sentences: "There is a jaguar in the Amazon." "There are pink dolphins in the Amazon river."
### Enunciado
Which rule explains the difference between the two sentences?
### Opciones
- [x] A) "There is" is for one animal, "there are" is for more than one.
  <!-- feedback: Singular "a jaguar" uses "is" and plural "dolphins" uses "are". -->
- [ ] B) "There is" is for rivers, "there are" is for forests.
  <!-- feedback: The place does not decide between "is" and "are". -->
- [ ] C) "There is" is for big animals, "there are" is for small animals.
  <!-- feedback: Size does not decide the verb; number decides it. -->
- [ ] D) "There is" is for the past, "there are" is for the future.
  <!-- feedback: Both sentences are in the present, not past or future. -->
### Explicacion Pedagogica
La elección entre there is y there are depende del número del sustantivo. También se conecta la gramática con animales emblemáticos de la Amazonía colombiana.

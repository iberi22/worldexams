# Weekly Pack W31: Reason and Result Connectors (B1)

This bundle focuses on connectors that explain why things happen (reason) and what happens as a consequence (result), such as "because", "due to", "therefore", and "as a result". Aligned with CEFR B1.

---

**Bloom:** Remember
**ICFES:** Lingüística
**Expected_Success:** 0.85
**Contexto:** Basic reason with "because".

### Enunciado
I stayed at home ________ it was raining heavily outside.

### Opciones
- [x] A) because <!-- feedback: Correct! "Because" introduces a clause showing the reason. -->
- [ ] B) so <!-- feedback: Incorrect. "So" introduces the result, not the reason. -->
- [ ] C) although <!-- feedback: Incorrect. -->
- [ ] D) but <!-- feedback: Incorrect. -->

### Explicación Pedagógica
"Because" (porque) se usa para introducir la causa o razón de una situación. Va seguido de una oración completa (sujeto + verbo).

### Opciones

## Question 10 [D9-D10]
**ID:** `CO-ING-8-2026-W31-connectors-reason-result-001-MASTERY-v10`
**Bloom:** Evaluate
**ICFES:** Lingüística
**Expected_Success:** 0.40
**Contexto:** Identifying errors in logical connections.

### Enunciado
Identify the error:
"**As (A)** the price of gas has **risen (B)**, **therefore (C)** many people are **starting (D)** to use bicycles."

### Opciones
- [ ] A) As <!-- feedback: Incorrect. -->
- [ ] B) risen <!-- feedback: Incorrect. -->
- [x] C) therefore <!-- feedback: Correct! Error found. You cannot use "As" (reason) and "therefore" (result) in the same sentence to connect the same two clauses. It's redundant. -->
- [ ] D) starting <!-- feedback: Incorrect. -->

### Explicación Pedagógica
La redundancia de conectores es un error común. Si una oración ya empieza con "As" o "Because", no se debe poner "so" o "therefore" en medio.

## Question 12 [D9-D10]
**ID:** `CO-ING-8-2026-W31-connectors-reason-result-001-MASTERY-v12`
**Bloom:** Evaluate
**ICFES:** Lingüística
**Expected_Success:** 0.30
**Contexto:** Selecting the best summary with cause and effect.

### Enunciado
"The region experienced a severe drought. Crops failed, and many farmers lost their income. This led to a significant increase in food prices in the cities."
Choose the best summary:

### Opciones
- [x] A) The severe drought caused crop failure and financial loss for farmers, resulting in higher food prices for urban consumers. <!-- feedback: Correct! It captures the chain of cause and effect accurately. -->
- [ ] B) Farmers moved to cities because they liked the food prices there. <!-- feedback: Incorrect. Misinterprets the text. -->
- [ ] C) Due to the high food prices, there was a drought in the region. <!-- feedback: Incorrect. Logic is reversed. -->
- [ ] D) As farmers had no money, it started to rain again. <!-- feedback: Incorrect. -->

### Explicación Pedagógica
Sintetizar cadenas de eventos requiere identificar el "primer motor" (causa inicial) y seguir lógicamente todos los "eslabones" (resultados intermedios) hasta la conclusión final.

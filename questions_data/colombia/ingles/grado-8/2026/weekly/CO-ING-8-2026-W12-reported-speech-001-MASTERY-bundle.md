# Weekly Pack W12: Reporting What People Say (B1)

This bundle focuses on Reported Speech (Indirect Speech) for statements, focusing on tense shifts from present to past, aligned with CEFR B1 level.

---

## Question 1 [D3-D4]
**ID:** `CO-ING-8-2026-W12-reported-speech-001-MASTERY-v1`
**Bloom:** Remember
**ICFES:** Lingüística
**Expected_Success:** 0.85
**Contexto:** Reporting a present simple statement.

### Enunciado
**Direct Speech:** "I like coffee," said Maria.
**Reported Speech:** Maria said that she ________ coffee.

### Opciones
- [ ] A) like <!-- feedback: Incorrect. We usually shift the tense back in reported speech. -->
- [x] B) liked <!-- feedback: Correct! Present simple shifts to past simple in reported speech. -->
- [ ] C) likes <!-- feedback: Incorrect. -->
- [ ] D) is liking <!-- feedback: Incorrect. -->

### Explicación Pedagógica
En el estilo indirecto (Reported Speech), cuando el verbo introductorio está en pasado (said), los tiempos verbales dentro de la oración suelen "retroceder" un paso en el tiempo. El presente simple cambia a pasado simple.

### Enunciado

### Opciones
- [ ] A) said <!-- feedback: Incorrect. "Say" is not followed by a personal object without "to". -->

### Enunciado
If someone says "I will do it **tomorrow**" on Tuesday, and you report it on Wednesday, you should say:
"He said he would do it ________."

### Opciones
- [ ] A) tomorrow <!-- feedback: Incorrect. This would mean Thursday. -->
- [x] B) today <!-- feedback: Correct! Since it is now Wednesday, "tomorrow" (from Tuesday) is today. -->

### Explicación Pedagógica

## Question 10 [D9-D10]
**ID:** `CO-ING-8-2026-W12-reported-speech-001-MASTERY-v10`
**Bloom:** Evaluate
**ICFES:** Lingüística
**Expected_Success:** 0.40
**Contexto:** Identifying errors in complex reporting.

### Enunciado
Identify the error in this report:
"Last night, Luis **told (A)** that he **was (B)** going to visit us **the following (C)** week, but he **doesn't (D)** know the exact day."

### Opciones
- [x] A) told <!-- feedback: Correct! Error found. "Told" must have a personal object. It should be "Luis said" or "Luis told us". -->
- [ ] B) was <!-- feedback: Incorrect. Correct tense shift. -->
- [ ] C) the following <!-- feedback: Incorrect. Correct change for "next". -->
- [ ] D) doesn't <!-- feedback: Incorrect. This could stay in present if it's still true, though "didn't" is safer. -->

### Explicación Pedagógica
El uso de "say" vs "tell" es un error común. "Tell" siempre necesita a quién se le dice la información (un objeto indirecto).

### Enunciado

### Opciones
- [ ] A) He said we gotta spend less cash now. <!-- feedback: Incorrect. Too informal. -->
- [x] B) The manager stated that the company had to reduce its expenses immediately. <!-- feedback: Correct! Formal, uses "stated", and correct tense shift. -->
- [ ] C) He says they had to reduce expenses then. <!-- feedback: Incorrect. Tense mismatch between "says" and "had". -->
- [ ] D) He told to reduce expenses. <!-- feedback: Incorrect. Grammatically incomplete. -->

### Explicación Pedagógica
En el nivel B1, se debe saber elegir verbos introductorios más precisos que "say" (como "state", "claim", "explain") para contextos formales.

## Question 12 [D9-D10]
**ID:** `CO-ING-8-2026-W12-reported-speech-001-MASTERY-v12`
**Bloom:** Evaluate
**ICFES:** Lingüística
**Expected_Success:** 0.30
**Contexto:** Summarizing reported information.

### Enunciado
**Contexto:** During a news broadcast, the scientist said: "We have discovered a new species of frog in the Chocó region. This is a significant find for biodiversity."
Which is the best summary of her words?

### Opciones
- [x] A) The scientist announced that they had discovered a new frog species and explained that it was important for biodiversity. <!-- feedback: Correct! Good use of "announced" and "explained" with correct reporting. -->
- [ ] B) She says they discover a frog in Chocó and it is important. <!-- feedback: Incorrect. No tense shifts. -->
- [ ] C) She told that frogs are important in Chocó now. <!-- feedback: Incorrect. Missing object for "told" and poor summary. -->
- [ ] D) The news said that frogs were found by scientists in a region. <!-- feedback: Incorrect. Too vague. -->

### Explicación Pedagógica
Resumir lo que alguien dijo requiere no solo aplicar reglas gramaticales de estilo indirecto, sino también capturar la esencia y el propósito del mensaje original.

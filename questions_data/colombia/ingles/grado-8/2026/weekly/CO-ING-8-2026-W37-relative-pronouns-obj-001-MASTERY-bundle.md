# Weekly Pack W37: Who, Whom, That - Object Relative Pronouns (B1)

This bundle focuses on relative pronouns when they function as the object of the relative clause, including the formal use of "whom" and the rules for omitting pronouns. Aligned with CEFR B1.

---

## Question 1 [D3-D4]
**ID:** `CO-ING-8-2026-W37-relative-pronouns-obj-001-MASTERY-v1`
**Bloom:** Remember
**ICFES:** Lingüística
**Expected_Success:** 0.85
**Contexto:** Identifying an object relative pronoun for things.

### Enunciado
The present ________ you gave me is fantastic!

### Opciones
- [ ] A) who <!-- feedback: Incorrect. Present is not a person. -->
- [x] B) that <!-- feedback: Correct! "That" (or "which") can be the object of the clause (you gave it). -->
- [ ] C) whose <!-- feedback: Incorrect. -->
- [ ] D) whom <!-- feedback: Incorrect. -->

### Explicación Pedagógica
En esta oración, "that" es el objeto del verbo "gave". Se refiere al regalo (the present).

## Question 2 [D3-D4]

**Bloom:** Remember
**ICFES:** Lingüística
**Expected_Success:** 0.80
**Contexto:** Formal use of "whom".

### Enunciado
The person to ________ you were speaking is the manager.

### Opciones
- [ ] A) who <!-- feedback: Incorrect. After a preposition, "whom" is the correct formal form. -->
- [x] B) whom <!-- feedback: Correct! Formal object pronoun used after prepositions. -->
- [ ] C) which <!-- feedback: Incorrect. -->
- [ ] D) whose <!-- feedback: Incorrect. -->

### Explicación Pedagógica
En inglés formal, después de una preposición (to, with, for), usamos "whom" en lugar de "who" para referirnos a personas que son el objeto de la acción.

## Question 4 [D5-D6]
**ID:** `CO-ING-8-2026-W37-relative-pronouns-obj-001-MASTERY-v4`
**Bloom:** Understand
**ICFES:** Lingüística
**Expected_Success:** 0.70
**Contexto:** Identifying subject vs. object pronouns.

### Enunciado
In which sentence is the relative pronoun the **object**?

### Opciones
- [ ] A) The man who lives here is a doctor. <!-- feedback: Incorrect. "Who" is the subject of "lives". -->
- [x] B) The man (who) I saw here is a doctor. <!-- feedback: Correct! "I" is the subject, "who" is the object of "saw". -->
- [ ] C) The dog that barked is mine. <!-- feedback: Incorrect. "That" is the subject. -->
- [ ] D) The woman who won is happy. <!-- feedback: Incorrect. -->

### Explicación Pedagógica
Para saber si un pronombre es objeto, mira si hay otro sujeto (yo, tú, Luis) inmediatamente después del pronombre. Si lo hay, el pronombre es el objeto.

### Opciones

**Bloom:** Evaluate
**ICFES:** Lingüística
**Expected_Success:** 0.40
**Contexto:** Identifying redundancy errors.

### Enunciado
Identify the error:
"The **movie (A)** that **we (B)** watched **it (C)** last night **was (D)** great."

### Opciones
- [ ] A) movie <!-- feedback: Incorrect. -->
- [ ] B) we <!-- feedback: Incorrect. -->
- [x] C) it <!-- feedback: Correct! Error found. The relative pronoun "that" already represents the movie. You cannot have "it" (the movie) again in the same clause. -->
- [ ] D) was <!-- feedback: Incorrect. -->

### Explicación Pedagógica
Un error típico en B1 es repetir el pronombre de objeto (it, him, them) dentro de la oración de relativo. El pronombre relativo ya cumple esa función.

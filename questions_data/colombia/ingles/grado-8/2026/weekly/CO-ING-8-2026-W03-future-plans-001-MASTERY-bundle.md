# Weekly Pack W03: Future Plans and Predictions (B1)

This bundle focuses on expressing the future using "will", "be going to", and Present Continuous for arrangements, aligned with CEFR B1 level for Grade 8.

---

## Question 1 [D3-D4]
**ID:** `CO-ING-8-2026-W03-future-plans-001-MASTERY-v1`
**Bloom:** Remember
**ICFES:** Lingüística
**Expected_Success:** 0.85
**Contexto:** Making a prediction about the weather.

### Enunciado
I think it ________ rain later this afternoon, so take an umbrella.

### Opciones
- [x] A) will <!-- feedback: Correct! We use "will" for predictions based on opinions or thoughts. -->
- [ ] B) is going to <!-- feedback: Incorrect. While common, "will" is more standard for "I think" predictions. -->
- [ ] C) rains <!-- feedback: Incorrect. This is present tense. -->
- [ ] D) is raining <!-- feedback: Incorrect. This describes an action happening now. -->

### Explicación Pedagógica
Usamos "will" para hacer predicciones basadas en lo que creemos o pensamos (frecuentemente después de "I think").

## Question 7 [D7-D8]
**ID:** `CO-ING-8-2026-W03-future-plans-001-MASTERY-v7`
**Bloom:** Analyze
**ICFES:** Lingüística
**Expected_Success:** 0.55
**Contexto:** Distinguishing nuances of future forms.

### Enunciado
Which sentence expresses a **spontaneous offer**?

### Opciones
- [ ] A) I am going to help you with that heavy bag. <!-- feedback: Incorrect. This sounds like a previous intention. -->
- [x] B) I'll help you with that heavy bag. <!-- feedback: Correct! "I'll" (I will) is used for immediate offers. -->
- [ ] C) I am helping you with that heavy bag. <!-- feedback: Incorrect. This means you are doing it right now. -->

## Question 10 [D9-D10]
**ID:** `CO-ING-8-2026-W03-future-plans-001-MASTERY-v10`
**Bloom:** Evaluate
**ICFES:** Lingüística
**Expected_Success:** 0.40
**Contexto:** Evaluating correctness in a complex dialogue.

### Enunciado
Identify the grammatically **incorrect** part of this dialogue:
**Teacher:** "What are your plans for the science fair?"
**Student:** "I **am going to (A)** build a robot. My partner **is bringing (B)** the parts tomorrow. I think we **are winning (C)** the first prize because our design **is (D)** unique."

### Opciones
- [ ] A) am going to <!-- feedback: Incorrect. This is a correct expression of intention. -->
- [ ] B) is bringing <!-- feedback: Incorrect. Correct use of Present Continuous for a future arrangement. -->
- [x] C) are winning <!-- feedback: Correct! This is an error. For an opinion/prediction, it should be "will win". -->
- [ ] D) is <!-- feedback: Incorrect. Simple present for a fact about the design. -->

### Explicación Pedagógica
Las predicciones basadas en opiniones personales ("I think...") deben usar "will", no el Presente Continuo ni "going to" (a menos que haya evidencia abrumadora).

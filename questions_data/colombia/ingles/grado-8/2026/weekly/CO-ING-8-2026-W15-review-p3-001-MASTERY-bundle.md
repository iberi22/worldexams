This bundle provides a comprehensive review of the topics covered in weeks 11 to 14: Passive Voice, Reported Speech, Past Perfect, and Second Conditional. Aligned with CEFR B1 level.

## Question 1 [D3-D4]
**ID:** `CO-ING-8-2026-W15-review-p3-001-MASTERY-v1`
**Bloom:** Remember
**ICFES:** Lingüística
**Expected_Success:** 0.85
**Contexto:** Reviewing Passive Voice.

### Enunciado
The Mona Lisa ________ by Leonardo da Vinci in the 16th century.

### Opciones
- [ ] A) painted <!-- feedback: Incorrect. Active voice needs a subject first. -->
- [x] B) was painted <!-- feedback: Correct! Past passive: was + past participle. -->
- [ ] C) is painted <!-- feedback: Incorrect. It happened in the past. -->
- [ ] D) were painted <!-- feedback: Incorrect. "The Mona Lisa" is singular. -->

### Explicación Pedagógica
La voz pasiva se usa para resaltar el objeto de la acción. Para eventos históricos, usamos el pasado pasivo (was/were + participio).

## Question 10 [D9-D10]
**ID:** `CO-ING-8-2026-W15-review-p3-001-MASTERY-v10`
**Bloom:** Evaluate
**ICFES:** Lingüística
**Expected_Success:** 0.40
**Contexto:** Identifying errors in a multi-tense paragraph.

### Enunciado
Identify the error:
"Maria **said (A)** that she **had (B)** a great time yesterday. If she **had (C)** more free time, she **will (D)** visit us again."

### Opciones
- [ ] A) said <!-- feedback: Incorrect. -->
- [ ] B) had <!-- feedback: Incorrect. Correct shift from "I had" or "I am having". -->
- [ ] C) more <!-- feedback: Incorrect. Correct adjective in the condition. -->
- [x] D) will <!-- feedback: Correct! Error found. The result of a second conditional must be "would", not "will". -->

### Explicación Pedagógica
La mezcla de estructuras es un reto en B1. El estudiante debe detectar que "had" (pasado simple) en la condición requiere "would" en el resultado.

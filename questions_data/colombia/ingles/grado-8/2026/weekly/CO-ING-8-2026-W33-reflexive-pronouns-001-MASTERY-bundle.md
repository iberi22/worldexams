# Weekly Pack W33: Myself, Yourself, Themselves - Reflexive Pronouns (B1)

This bundle focuses on reflexive pronouns used when the subject and the object of the verb are the same person or thing. Aligned with CEFR B1.

---

## Question 1 [D3-D4]
**ID:** `CO-ING-8-2026-W33-reflexive-pronouns-001-MASTERY-v1`
**Bloom:** Remember
**ICFES:** Lingüística
**Expected_Success:** 0.85
**Contexto:** Identifying reflexive pronoun for "I".

### Enunciado
I cut ________ while I was chopping vegetables for the salad.

### Opciones
- [ ] A) me <!-- feedback: Incorrect. Use reflexive when subject and object are the same. -->
- [x] B) myself <!-- feedback: Correct! Reflexive pronoun for "I". -->
- [ ] C) mine <!-- feedback: Incorrect. -->
- [ ] D) my <!-- feedback: Incorrect. -->

### Explicación Pedagógica
Usamos los pronombres reflexivos cuando la persona que realiza la acción es la misma que la recibe. El reflexivo de "I" es "myself".

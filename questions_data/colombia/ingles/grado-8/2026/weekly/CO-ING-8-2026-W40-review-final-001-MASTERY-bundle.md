### Opciones
- [x] A) had <!-- feedback: Correct! Error found. Since the result is "would have finished" (3rd conditional), the condition must be "had had" (Past Perfect). -->

- [ ] D) yesterday <!-- feedback: Incorrect. -->

### Explicación Pedagógica

## Question 12 [D9-D10]
**ID:** `CO-ING-8-2026-W40-review-final-001-MASTERY-v12`
**Bloom:** Evaluate
**ICFES:** Lingüística
**Expected_Success:** 0.30
**Contexto:** Selecting the best comprehensive summary.

### Enunciado
"The factory was closed last year due to a strike. Hundreds of workers lost their jobs. If the management had listened to them earlier, the crisis would have been avoided. Now, the building is being converted into a library."
What is the best summary?

### Opciones
- [x] A) A past failure in communication led to a crisis that could have been prevented, resulting in a current change of purpose for the site. <!-- feedback: Correct! It synthesizes the passive voice (was closed), the 3rd conditional (could have been prevented), and the present continuous passive (is being converted). -->
- [ ] B) The strike was good for everyone because they have a library now. <!-- feedback: Incorrect. Simplistic. -->
- [ ] C) Workers are happy because they don't work in the factory. <!-- feedback: Incorrect. -->
- [ ] D) The management must have listened to the workers because they liked libraries. <!-- feedback: Incorrect logic. -->

### Explicación Pedagógica
La evaluación final requiere que el estudiante decodifique múltiples capas de significado (voz pasiva, condicionales hipotéticos y cambios temporales) para comprender una narrativa compleja en su totalidad.

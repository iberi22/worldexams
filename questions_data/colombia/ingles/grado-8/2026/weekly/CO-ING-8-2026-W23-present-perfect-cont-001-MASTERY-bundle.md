# Weekly Pack W23: Ongoing Actions - Present Perfect Continuous (B1)

This bundle focuses on the Present Perfect Continuous tense to talk about actions that started in the past and are still continuing or have just finished, aligned with CEFR B1 level.

---

## Question 1 [D3-D4]
**ID:** `CO-ING-8-2026-W23-present-perfect-cont-001-MASTERY-v1`
**Bloom:** Remember
**ICFES:** Lingüística
**Expected_Success:** 0.85
**Contexto:** Identifying the structure.

### Enunciado
I ________ English for three years, and I still have a lot to learn.

### Opciones
- [ ] A) have study <!-- feedback: Incorrect. -->
- [x] B) have been studying <!-- feedback: Correct! Present Perfect Continuous: have/has + been + -ing. -->
- [ ] C) am studying <!-- feedback: Incorrect. This is only for now. -->
- [ ] D) has been studying <!-- feedback: Incorrect. "I" takes "have". -->

### Explicación Pedagógica
El Presente Perfecto Continuo se usa para acciones que comenzaron en el pasado y continúan en el presente. La estructura es: have/has + been + verbo con -ing.

**ICFES:** Lingüística
**Expected_Success:** 0.65
**Contexto:** Continuous action vs. state.

### Enunciado
Which sentence is **NOT** correct in the continuous form?

### Opciones
- [ ] A) I have been playing soccer. <!-- feedback: Incorrect. This is correct. -->
- [x] B) I have been knowing her for years. <!-- feedback: Correct! "Know" is a stative verb and shouldn't be used in continuous forms. -->

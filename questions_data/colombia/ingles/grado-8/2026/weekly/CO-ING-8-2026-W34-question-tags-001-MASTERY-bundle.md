# Weekly Pack W34: Don't You? - Question Tags (B1)

This bundle focuses on Question Tags, the short questions at the end of sentences used to check information or ask for agreement. Aligned with CEFR B1.

---

## Question 1 [D3-D4]
**ID:** `CO-ING-8-2026-W34-question-tags-001-MASTERY-v1`
**Bloom:** Remember
**ICFES:** Lingüística
**Expected_Success:** 0.85
**Contexto:** Question tag for "to be" (affirmative).

### Enunciado
You are a student at this school, ________?

### Opciones
- [ ] A) are you <!-- feedback: Incorrect. If the sentence is positive, the tag must be negative. -->
- [x] B) aren't you <!-- feedback: Correct! Negative tag for a positive "to be" sentence. -->
- [ ] C) don't you <!-- feedback: Incorrect. -->
- [ ] D) you aren't <!-- feedback: Incorrect. -->

### Explicación Pedagógica
Las "Question Tags" funcionan como espejos opuestos: si la oración es afirmativa, la pregunta final debe ser negativa. Con el verbo "to be", repetimos el mismo verbo.

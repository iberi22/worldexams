## Question 7 [D7-D8]
**ID:** `CO-ING-8-2026-W21-relative-clauses-adv-001-MASTERY-v7`
**Bloom:** Analyze
**ICFES:** Lingüística
**Expected_Success:** 0.55
**Contexto:** Non-defining vs. Defining.

### Enunciado
Which sentence is a **non-defining** relative clause?

### Opciones
- [ ] A) The car that I bought is blue. <!-- feedback: Incorrect. This is defining (which car?). -->
- [x] B) My car, which I bought last month, is blue. <!-- feedback: Correct! The commas and "which" show this is extra information. -->
- [ ] C) The man who is standing there is my uncle. <!-- feedback: Incorrect. Defining. -->
- [ ] D) This is the house where I live. <!-- feedback: Incorrect. Defining. -->

### Explicación Pedagógica
Las oraciones explicativas (non-defining) añaden información sobre algo que ya está identificado (por ejemplo, "mi carro" ya sabemos cuál es). Siempre llevan comas.

### Opciones
- [ ] A) Yes, because it is the object. <!-- feedback: Incorrect. -->
- [ ] B) Yes, because it's a non-defining clause. <!-- feedback: Incorrect. -->

## Question 10 [D9-D10]
**ID:** `CO-ING-8-2026-W21-relative-clauses-adv-001-MASTERY-v10`
**Bloom:** Evaluate
**ICFES:** Lingüística
**Expected_Success:** 0.40
**Contexto:** Identifying punctuation errors in relative clauses.

### Enunciado
Identify the grammatically **incorrect** sentence:

### Opciones
- [ ] A) My mother, who is a chef, loves spicy food. <!-- feedback: Incorrect. This is correct. -->
- [ ] B) The house where I was born is now a museum. <!-- feedback: Incorrect. This is correct. -->
- [x] C) Albert Einstein that was a genius discovered relativity. <!-- feedback: Correct! Error found. For a specific famous person, we need a non-defining clause with "who" and commas. -->
- [ ] D) This is the reason why I didn't come. <!-- feedback: Incorrect. This is correct. -->

### Explicación Pedagógica
Cuando hablamos de nombres propios únicos (como Einstein), la información adicional debe ir en una oración explicativa (comas + who/which), no una especificativa con "that".

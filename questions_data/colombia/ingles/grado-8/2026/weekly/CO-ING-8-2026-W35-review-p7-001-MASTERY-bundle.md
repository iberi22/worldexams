**ICFES:** Pragmática
**Expected_Success:** 0.35

### Enunciado
Which sentence is the best way to explain a delay in a professional report?

### Opciones
- [ ] A) We were late because the rain was bad. <!-- feedback: Incorrect. Too simple. -->
- [x] B) The project was delayed due to unforeseen technical difficulties; therefore, the deadline has been extended. <!-- feedback: Correct! Excellent use of "due to" and "therefore" in a formal context. -->

- [ ] D) Everything is bad, haven't it? <!-- feedback: Incorrect grammar. -->

### Explicación Pedagógica

**Contexto:** Selecting the best summary of a group action.

### Enunciado
"Nobody expected the surprise party. Everybody arrived at different times. Because of the lack of coordination, some people brought the same gifts. As a result, there were many duplicates."
Choose the best summary:

### Opciones
- [x] A) Due to a lack of coordination, everyone's independent arrivals resulted in duplicate gifts that nobody had anticipated. <!-- feedback: Correct! It synthesizes all the indefinite pronouns and connectors perfectly. -->
- [ ] B) Someone brought gifts to everyone because they were coordinated. <!-- feedback: Incorrect. Contradicts the text. -->

- [ ] D) They arrived themselves and brought nothing. <!-- feedback: Incorrect. -->

### Explicación Pedagógica

# Weekly Pack W26: Past Hypotheses - Third Conditional (B1)

This bundle focuses on the Third Conditional to talk about imaginary situations in the past (regrets or what-if scenarios), aligned with CEFR B1 level.

---

## Question 1 [D3-D4]
**ID:** `CO-ING-8-2026-W26-third-conditional-001-MASTERY-v1`
**Bloom:** Remember
**ICFES:** Lingüística
**Expected_Success:** 0.85
**Contexto:** Identifying the structure.

### Enunciado
If I ________ more, I would have passed the exam.

### Opciones
- [ ] A) studied <!-- feedback: Incorrect. -->
- [x] B) had studied <!-- feedback: Correct! Third conditional uses Past Perfect in the "if" clause. -->
- [ ] C) have studied <!-- feedback: Incorrect. -->
- [ ] D) would study <!-- feedback: Incorrect. -->

### Explicación Pedagógica
El Tercer Condicional se usa para imaginar un pasado diferente. La estructura de la condición es: If + Pasado Perfecto (had + participio).

## Question 7 [D7-D8]
**ID:** `CO-ING-8-2026-W26-third-conditional-001-MASTERY-v7`
**Bloom:** Analyze
**ICFES:** Lingüística
**Expected_Success:** 0.55
**Contexto:** Distinguishing Second and Third Conditional.

### Enunciado
Which sentence refers to a **past** event that cannot be changed?

### Opciones
- [ ] A) If I had a car, I would drive to work. <!-- feedback: Incorrect. This is about the present situation. -->
- [x] B) If I had had a car last year, I would have driven to work. <!-- feedback: Correct! The use of past perfect and "would have" points to the past. -->
- [ ] C) If I have a car, I will drive to work. <!-- feedback: Incorrect. Future possibility. -->
- [ ] D) I have a car and I drive to work. <!-- feedback: Incorrect. Fact. -->

### Explicación Pedagógica
El Segundo Condicional (A) habla de situaciones imaginarias hoy. El Tercer Condicional (B) habla de situaciones que ya pasaron y no se pueden cambiar.

## Question 12 [D9-D10]
**ID:** `CO-ING-8-2026-W26-third-conditional-001-MASTERY-v12`
**Bloom:** Evaluate
**ICFES:** Lingüística
**Expected_Success:** 0.30
**Contexto:** Selecting the best summary of a hypothetical story.

### Enunciado
"Elena forgot to set her alarm. She woke up late and missed her flight to San Andrés. Because of this, she couldn't attend her best friend's wedding."
What is the best hypothetical summary?

### Opciones
- [x] A) If Elena hadn't forgotten her alarm, she would have been able to attend the wedding. <!-- feedback: Correct! It correctly connects the first cause with the final consequence in the past. -->
- [ ] B) Elena will go to the wedding if she wakes up early tomorrow. <!-- feedback: Incorrect. The wedding already happened. -->
- [ ] C) If she missed the flight, she wouldn't have a wedding. <!-- feedback: Incorrect. Logic is reversed. -->
- [ ] D) She used to attend weddings when she had an alarm. <!-- feedback: Incorrect. -->

### Explicación Pedagógica
Sintetizar una cadena de eventos pasados en una sola oración condicional requiere identificar la causa raíz y el resultado final, aplicando la gramática del Tercer Condicional.

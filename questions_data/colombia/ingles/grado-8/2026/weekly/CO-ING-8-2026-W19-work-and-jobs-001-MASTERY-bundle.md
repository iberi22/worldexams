# Weekly Pack W19: Work and Jobs — professions, job interviews, "going to" for professional plans and the curriculum vitae (B2)

This pack works with the vocabulary of professions and with the language of recruitment, and practises "going to" for plans and the sections of a curriculum vitae.

---

## Question 1 [D3-D4]
**ID:** CO-ING-8-2026-W19-work-and-jobs-001-MASTERY-bundle-v1
**Bloom:** Remember
**ICFES:** Lingüística
**Expected_Success:** 0.85
**Contexto:** A group of students in grade 8 are preparing a small business project for the school fair.

### Enunciado
Look at those boxes in the hall. The students are going to ________ a small business this term.

### Opciones
- [x] A) start
  <!-- feedback: Correct. "Be going to" is followed by the base form of the verb, so the sentence needs "going to start". -->
- [ ] B) to start
  <!-- feedback: Incorrect. The "to" is already part of "going to", so adding a second "to" is not allowed. -->
- [ ] C) starting
  <!-- feedback: Incorrect. Modals are never followed by the "-ing" form, so "going to starting" is impossible. -->
- [ ] D) starts
  <!-- feedback: Incorrect. The third person ending "-s" cannot appear after a modal, which always takes the base form. -->

### Explicacion Pedagogica
"Be going to" is a modal structure and follows the same rule as "must" or "will": modal + base form of the verb. It expresses a plan that has already been decided, which is exactly what starting a business this term means.

## Question 2 [D3-D4]

**Bloom:** Remember
**ICFES:** Structural Cloze
**Expected_Success:** 0.80
**Contexto:** A student in Medellín has finished high school and is looking for a first job.

### Enunciado
After graduating, she sent her CV and ________ for a job in a local company. Use "apply".

### Opciones
- [ ] A) signed
  <!-- feedback: Incorrect. "Sign for a job" does not exist; signing is done for documents or contracts, not for a position. -->
- [ ] B) asked
  <!-- feedback: Incorrect. "Asked for a job" is informal and informal usage, but the standard recruitment verb in English is "apply". -->
- [x] C) applied
  <!-- feedback: Correct. The standard phrase is "apply for a job", and the past form "applied" is required in this context. -->
- [ ] D) paid
  <!-- feedback: Incorrect. "Pay for a job" is meaningless, because employers pay salaries to workers and not the other way round. -->

### Explicacion Pedagogica
Recruitment uses fixed phrases: "apply for a job", "fill in an application form" and "attend an interview". The preposition "for" is part of the phrasal verb "apply for", and it cannot be replaced by another preposition such as "to".

## Question 5 [D5-D6]
**ID:** CO-ING-8-2026-W19-work-and-jobs-001-MASTERY-bundle-v5
**Bloom:** Apply
**ICFES:** Grammatical Knowledge
**Expected_Success:** 0.75
**Contexto:** A newspaper announces the expansion of a company and the plans of its managers.

### Enunciado
Next week the company ________ open a new branch in the coastal city.

### Opciones
- [x] A) is going to
  <!-- feedback: Correct. "Be going to" expresses a plan that has already been decided, so it fits the announcement of a new branch. -->
- [ ] B) goes to
  <!-- feedback: Incorrect. "Goes to" means travelling to a place, which is a completely different meaning from opening a branch. -->
- [ ] C) is opening to
  <!-- feedback: Incorrect. The infinitive "to" cannot be used after the continuous form of the verb in this structure. -->
- [ ] D) will opening
  <!-- feedback: Incorrect. "Will" is never followed by the "-ing" form; a modal always takes the base form of the verb. -->

### Explicacion Pedagogica
Three structures express future plans and each has a fixed form: "will" + base form, "am/is/are going to" + base form and the present continuous with an "-ing" form. Mixing the parts of two different structures produces errors such as "will opening".

## Question 6 [D5-D6]
**ID:** CO-ING-8-2026-W19-work-and-jobs-001-MASTERY-bundle-v6
**Bloom:** Apply
**ICFES:** Structural Cloze
**Expected_Success:** 0.75
**Contexto:** A graduate describes her working experience so far.

### Enunciado
She ________ never worked in a factory, but she has worked in two offices. Use "has".

### Opciones
- [ ] A) have
  <!-- feedback: Incorrect. "Have" is used with "I", "you", "we" and "they", but the subject of this sentence is "she". -->
- [x] B) has
  <!-- feedback: Correct. The third person singular takes "has" plus the past participle "worked" in the present perfect. -->
- [ ] C) had
  <!-- feedback: Incorrect. The past perfect would require a past reference point, and there is none in this sentence. -->
- [ ] D) would
  <!-- feedback: Incorrect. "Would worked" is not a form of the verb at all, since a modal never takes a past participle. -->

### Explicacion Pedagogica
The present perfect is "have/has" plus participle, and the choice between the two depends only on the subject: "she has" and "they have". Modal forms such as "would" cannot be combined with a participle.

## Question 8 [D5-D6]
**ID:** CO-ING-8-2026-W19-work-and-jobs-001-MASTERY-bundle-v8
**Bloom:** Apply
**ICFES:** Pragmatic Competence
**Expected_Success:** 0.75
**Contexto:** A candidate is at a job interview for a position in a technology company in Medellín.

### Enunciado
Which is the best way to answer "What is your greatest strength?" at a job interview?

### Opciones
- [ ] A) "I do not really know. I am good at many things."
  <!-- feedback: Incorrect. Saying you do not know your own strength answers nothing, and the second half contradicts the first. -->
- [ ] B) "I am lazy in the morning but I work hard later."
  <!-- feedback: Incorrect. It opens with a weakness, which is not what the interview question asks for. -->
- [ ] C) "Nobody has told me. I prefer not to say."
  <!-- feedback: Incorrect. Refusing to answer makes the candidate look unprepared for the job. -->
- [x] D) "I am organised and I always meet the deadlines I agree to, as I did in my last job."
  <!-- feedback: Correct. It names a specific strength, gives evidence from real experience and stays relevant to the job. -->

### Explicacion Pedagogica
A good interview answer follows the pattern strength plus evidence: say what you are good at and then show it with a concrete example from a previous job. Answers that deny the question, list unrelated qualities or begin with a weakness leave the employer with nothing to evaluate.

**Contexto:** A student has bought the materials for a project and a classmate draws a conclusion from that evidence.

### Enunciado
She has bought the paints, the brushes and the canvas, so she clearly is going to ________ a new picture.

### Opciones
- [x] A) paint
  <!-- feedback: Correct. After "is going to" the base form "paint" is required, and the materials already bought point to that plan. -->
- [ ] B) paint in
  <!-- feedback: Incorrect. "Paint in" needs a place or a colour after it, which is not the meaning of this sentence. -->

  <!-- feedback: Incorrect. "Is going to be painting" mixes two future structures and changes the meaning of the plan. -->

The researchers say the main reason is not the lack of demand but the lack of training in managing money. What do the researchers identify as the main reason?

**Contexto:** A guide explains how a curriculum vitae should describe past experience with clear results.

### Enunciado
Look at the sentence in the CV: "During 2023 I ________ the sales team of five people and increased sales by twenty per cent." Use "managed".

### Opciones
- [ ] A) managing
  <!-- feedback: Incorrect. "Managing" would need an auxiliary such as "was managing" or a continuous structure in order to complete the sentence. -->

  <!-- feedback: Incorrect. "Manages" is the present simple, and it describes a current job rather than a role carried out in 2023. -->

## Question 12 [D7-D8]
**ID:** CO-ING-8-2026-W19-work-and-jobs-001-MASTERY-bundle-v12
**Bloom:** Evaluate
**ICFES:** Reading Comprehension
**Expected_Success:** 0.70
**Contexto:** A school careers workshop asks students to plan a professional path before leaving secondary school.

### Enunciado
Ana has decided to become a pastry chef. She will study cooking for two years, and while she studies she will work on Saturdays in a bakery in her neighbourhood. When she gets her certificate she will apply to larger hotels, because experience counts as much as the diploma. According to the plan, what will Ana do first?

### Opciones
- [ ] A) Apply to a large hotel.
  <!-- feedback: Incorrect. Applying to hotels comes after she gets the certificate, so it cannot be the first step. -->
- [ ] B) Get the certificate.
  <!-- feedback: Incorrect. The certificate comes after the two years of study, so it is a later step in the plan. -->
- [x] C) Work on Saturdays in the neighbourhood bakery.
  <!-- feedback: Correct. The text gives the order clearly: study, work on Saturdays, get the certificate, and then apply to hotels. -->
- [ ] D) Choose the hotels where she will apply.
  <!-- feedback: Incorrect. Choosing the hotels comes after the certificate, so it is not the first action in the plan. -->

### Explicacion Pedagogica
A plan is read as a sequence, and the words "while she studies", "when she gets her certificate" and "she will apply" mark the order of the stages. The first concrete action is working on Saturdays while she studies, and everything else comes later.

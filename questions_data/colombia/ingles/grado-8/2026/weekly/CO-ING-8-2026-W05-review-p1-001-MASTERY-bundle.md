# Weekly Pack W05: Review Period 1 (B1)

This bundle provides a comprehensive review of the topics covered in weeks 1 to 4: Daily Routines, Past Stories, Future Plans, and Comparisons. Aligned with CEFR B1 level.

## Question 1 [D3-D4]
**ID:** `CO-ING-8-2026-W05-review-p1-001-MASTERY-v1`
**Bloom:** Remember
**ICFES:** Lingüística
**Expected_Success:** 0.85
**Contexto:** Reviewing daily habits vs. current actions.

### Enunciado
My father ________ a uniform to work every day, but today he ________ a casual shirt.

### Opciones
- [x] A) wears / is wearing <!-- feedback: Correct! Simple Present for habit, Present Continuous for today's exception. -->
- [ ] B) is wearing / wears <!-- feedback: Incorrect. The order of habit and exception is swapped. -->
- [ ] C) wear / wearing <!-- feedback: Incorrect. Missing third person 's' and auxiliary 'is'. -->
- [ ] D) wore / wears <!-- feedback: Incorrect. "Every day" implies present habit, not past. -->

### Explicación Pedagógica
Recordamos que las rutinas diarias usan el Presente Simple, mientras que las excepciones o acciones ocurriendo en el momento usan el Presente Continuo.

### Enunciado

## Question 4 [D5-D6]
**ID:** `CO-ING-8-2026-W05-review-p1-001-MASTERY-v4`
**Bloom:** Understand
**ICFES:** Lingüística
**Expected_Success:** 0.70
**Contexto:** Reviewing comparisons of superiority.

### Enunciado
A flight from Bogotá to Medellín is ________ than a bus trip, but it's also ________ expensive.

### Opciones
- [x] A) faster / more <!-- feedback: Correct! Fast is short (-er), expensive is long (more). -->
- [ ] B) more fast / more <!-- feedback: Incorrect. "Fast" is a short adjective, so it uses "-er". -->
- [ ] C) faster / expensiver <!-- feedback: Incorrect. "Expensive" is a long adjective and doesn't take "-er". -->
- [ ] D) fastest / most <!-- feedback: Incorrect. These are superlatives, not comparatives. -->

### Explicación Pedagógica
En comparaciones, los adjetivos cortos usan el sufijo "-er" y los largos usan la palabra "more".

**Bloom:** Understand
**ICFES:** Lingüística
**Expected_Success:** 0.65
**Contexto:** Reviewing interrupted actions in the past.

### Enunciado
I ________ a shower when the electricity ________ off.

### Opciones
- [ ] A) took / went <!-- feedback: Incorrect. One action was in progress when the other happened. -->
- [x] B) was taking / went <!-- feedback: Correct! Past continuous for the background action and past simple for the interruption. -->
- [ ] C) were taking / go <!-- feedback: Incorrect. Subject-verb agreement and tense errors. -->

### Explicación Pedagógica
Cuando una acción continua en el pasado es interrumpida, usamos el Pasado Continuo para la acción larga y el Pasado Simple para la corta.

## Question 12 [D9-D10]
**ID:** `CO-ING-8-2026-W05-review-p1-001-MASTERY-v12`
**Bloom:** Evaluate
**ICFES:** Lingüística
**Expected_Success:** 0.30
**Contexto:** Summarizing a story with multiple tenses.

### Enunciado
Read and choose the best summary:
"Last week, I planned to go hiking. While I was packing, it started to rain. I decided to stay home. Now, I am reading a book about mountains. Next month, I'll try again."

### Opciones
- [x] A) A past plan was cancelled due to weather, leading to a present activity and a future intention. <!-- feedback: Correct! It covers past, present, and future mentioned in the text. -->
- [ ] B) The person always goes hiking when it rains and is reading now. <!-- feedback: Incorrect. The hike was cancelled. -->
- [ ] C) Hiking is better than reading books about mountains in the future. <!-- feedback: Incorrect. Misinterprets the tenses and facts. -->
- [ ] D) He is going to hike next month because he doesn't like books. <!-- feedback: Incorrect. The text doesn't say he doesn't like books. -->

### Explicación Pedagógica
Sintetizar un texto que utiliza múltiples tiempos verbales requiere una comprensión clara de la cronología de los eventos.

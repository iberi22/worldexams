### Enunciado

## Question 12 [D9-D10]
**ID:** `CO-ING-8-2026-W10-review-p2-001-MASTERY-v12`
**Bloom:** Evaluate
**ICFES:** Lingüística
**Expected_Success:** 0.30
**Contexto:** Summarizing a complex situation using relative clauses.

### Enunciado
"I have a friend. Her name is Carla. She lives in a house. The house is very old. She has visited many countries."
Choose the best combined sentence:

### Opciones
- [x] A) Carla, who has visited many countries, lives in a house that is very old. <!-- feedback: Correct! Perfectly combines the information using relative clauses. -->
- [ ] B) Carla is a friend where lives in a very old house and has visited countries. <!-- feedback: Incorrect. "Where" is used incorrectly for a person. -->
- [ ] C) The old house who Carla lives in has visited many countries. <!-- feedback: Incorrect. Illogical (houses don't visit countries). -->
- [ ] D) Carla has visited countries since she lived in an old house who is hers. <!-- feedback: Incorrect. Grammatically weak and uses "who" for a house. -->

### Explicación Pedagógica
La creación de oraciones complejas mediante la subordinación (relative clauses) es una marca distintiva de la competencia en nivel B1.

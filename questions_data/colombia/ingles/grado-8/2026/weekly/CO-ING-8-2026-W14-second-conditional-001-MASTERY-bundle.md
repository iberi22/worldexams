# Weekly Pack W14: Imaginary Situations - Second Conditional (B1)

This bundle focuses on the Second Conditional to talk about hypothetical, imaginary, or unlikely situations in the present or future, aligned with CEFR B1 level.

---

## Question 1 [D3-D4]
**ID:** `CO-ING-8-2026-W14-second-conditional-001-MASTERY-v1`
**Bloom:** Remember
**ICFES:** Lingüística
**Expected_Success:** 0.85
**Contexto:** Identifying the structure of Second Conditional.

### Enunciado
If I ________ a lot of money, I ________ around the world.

### Opciones
- [ ] A) have / will travel <!-- feedback: Incorrect. This is First Conditional (possible). -->
- [x] B) had / would travel <!-- feedback: Correct! Second conditional: If + Past Simple, Would + base form. -->
- [ ] C) have / would travel <!-- feedback: Incorrect. Tense mismatch. -->
- [ ] D) had / will travel <!-- feedback: Incorrect. -->

### Explicación Pedagógica
El Segundo Condicional se usa para situaciones imaginarias. La estructura es: If + Pasado Simple, "would" + verbo en forma base.

## Question 7 [D7-D8]
**ID:** `CO-ING-8-2026-W14-second-conditional-001-MASTERY-v7`
**Bloom:** Analyze
**ICFES:** Lingüística
**Expected_Success:** 0.55
**Contexto:** Distinguishing First and Second Conditional.

### Enunciado
**Sentence A:** If I win the lottery, I will buy a car.
**Sentence B:** If I won the lottery, I would buy a car.

What is the difference?

### Opciones
- [ ] A) Sentence A is in the past. <!-- feedback: Incorrect. -->
- [x] B) Sentence A is more likely; Sentence B is purely imaginary. <!-- feedback: Correct! First conditional is for real possibilities; Second is for dreams or unlikely events. -->
- [ ] C) Sentence B is more likely. <!-- feedback: Incorrect. -->
- [ ] D) There is no difference. <!-- feedback: Incorrect. -->

### Explicación Pedagógica
La diferencia entre el Primer y Segundo condicional no es de tiempo (ambos se refieren al presente/futuro), sino de probabilidad y actitud del hablante hacia la realidad.

### Opciones

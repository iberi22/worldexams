# Weekly Pack W11: Focus on the Action - Passive Voice (B1)

This bundle introduces the Present and Past Passive Voice, focusing on actions where the object is more important than the subject. Aligned with CEFR B1 level.

---

## Question 1 [D3-D4]
**ID:** `CO-ING-8-2026-W11-passive-voice-001-MASTERY-v1`
**Bloom:** Remember
**ICFES:** Lingüística
**Expected_Success:** 0.85
**Contexto:** Identifying a present passive action.

### Enunciado
Coffee ________ in many regions of Colombia.

### Opciones
- [ ] A) grows <!-- feedback: Incorrect. Coffee doesn't grow itself; it is grown by people. -->
- [x] B) is grown <!-- feedback: Correct! Present passive: am/is/are + past participle. -->
- [ ] C) are grown <!-- feedback: Incorrect. "Coffee" is uncountable and takes "is". -->
- [ ] D) is grow <!-- feedback: Incorrect. We need the past participle "grown". -->

### Explicación Pedagógica
La voz pasiva se usa cuando el objeto (el café) es más importante que quien realiza la acción. La estructura es: verbo "to be" + participio pasado.

## Question 10 [D9-D10]
**ID:** `CO-ING-8-2026-W11-passive-voice-001-MASTERY-v10`
**Bloom:** Evaluate
**ICFES:** Lingüística
**Expected_Success:** 0.40
**Contexto:** Identifying errors in a complex passive text.

### Enunciado
Identify the error in this report:
"The results of the study **were analyzed (A)** by the team. Then, a final report **was wrote (B)** and **it was presented (C)** to the director **last Tuesday (D)**."

### Opciones
- [ ] A) were analyzed <!-- feedback: Incorrect. Plural subject, correct participle. -->
- [x] B) was wrote <!-- feedback: Correct! Error found. The past participle of "write" is "written", not "wrote". -->
- [ ] C) it was presented <!-- feedback: Incorrect. Correct passive form. -->
- [ ] D) last Tuesday <!-- feedback: Incorrect. -->

### Explicación Pedagógica
El conocimiento de los participios pasados irregulares es fundamental para usar correctamente la voz pasiva en nivel B1.

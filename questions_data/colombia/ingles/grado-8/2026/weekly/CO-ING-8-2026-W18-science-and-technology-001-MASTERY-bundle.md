# Weekly Pack W18: Science and Technology — inventions, scientific discoveries and the "-ing" and "-ed" adjectives (B2)

This pack works with the language of inventions, laboratory equipment and scientific discoveries, and practises the "-ing" and "-ed" adjectives that describe causes and feelings.

---

## Question 1 [D3-D4]
**ID:** CO-ING-8-2026-W18-science-and-technology-001-MASTERY-bundle-v1
**Bloom:** Remember
**ICFES:** Lingüística
**Expected_Success:** 0.85
**Contexto:** A group of students at home in Bogotá is discussing a film they watched together.

### Enunciado
The film was very ________; I nearly fell asleep.

### Opciones
- [ ] A) bored
  <!-- feedback: Incorrect. "Bored" describes how a person feels, but the sentence describes the kind of film, not the viewer's state. -->
- [x] B) boring
  <!-- feedback: Correct. An "-ing" adjective describes the quality of the thing itself, and this film made the viewer want to sleep. -->
- [ ] C) interest
  <!-- feedback: Incorrect. "Interest" is a noun here, so it cannot modify the noun "film" before it. -->
- [ ] D) interesting
  <!-- feedback: Incorrect. "Interesting" has the opposite meaning, and the speaker clearly did not enjoy the film. -->

### Explicacion Pedagogica
An "-ing" adjective describes what a thing or a situation is like, while an "-ed" adjective describes how a person feels because of it. A film that makes you sleep is "boring", and the person who feels bored is "bored". Applying this rule prevents the two forms from being confused.

## Question 2 [D3-D4]
**ID:** CO-ING-8-2026-W18-science-and-technology-001-MASTERY-bundle-v2
**Bloom:** Remember
**ICFES:** Structural Cloze
**Expected_Success:** 0.80
**Contexto:** A school science fair describes what the students felt when they saw a reaction in a beaker.

### Enunciado
The students were ________ when they saw the experiment with the coloured liquid change from blue to red.

### Opciones
- [x] A) excited
  <!-- feedback: Correct. An "-ed" adjective describes the feeling of the people, so the students themselves were excited. -->
- [ ] B) exciting
  <!-- feedback: Incorrect. An "-ing" adjective describes the cause of the feeling, which would be the experiment and not the students. -->
- [ ] C) excite
  <!-- feedback: Incorrect. "Excite" is the verb, and a verb cannot follow "were" in the adjective position of this sentence. -->
- [ ] D) excitement
  <!-- feedback: Incorrect. "Excitement" is a noun, and nouns cannot complete the structure "were" + adjective. -->

### Explicacion Pedagogica
The subject of the sentence decides the form: people take "-ed" adjectives for their feelings, and things take "-ing" adjectives for their qualities. In this sentence the subject is "the students", so the correct form is "were excited".

## Question 5 [D5-D6]

**ICFES:** Grammatical Knowledge
**Expected_Success:** 0.75
**Contexto:** A student complains about the noise produced during an experiment in the school laboratory.

### Enunciado
The experiment was so ________ that we could not hear the music in the next room.

### Opciones
- [ ] A) noised
  <!-- feedback: Incorrect. "Noised" is not an English adjective; the correct word is built from the noun "noise". -->
- [ ] B) noisily
  <!-- feedback: Incorrect. "Noisily" is an adverb and would have to modify a verb of action, not follow "was so". -->
- [x] C) noisy
  <!-- feedback: Correct. "Noisy" is the adjective that describes the experiment, and it follows "was so" correctly. -->

## Question 6 [D5-D6]
**ID:** CO-ING-8-2026-W18-science-and-technology-001-MASTERY-bundle-v6
**Bloom:** Apply
**ICFES:** Structural Cloze
**Expected_Success:** 0.75
**Contexto:** A newspaper reports on a medical discovery made in a research institute.

### Enunciado
The first vaccine against this disease was ________ in a laboratory in the capital in 1985. Use "developed".

### Opciones
- [ ] A) develop
  <!-- feedback: Incorrect. The base form "develop" cannot follow "was" in a passive structure; the past participle is required. -->
- [x] B) developed
  <!-- feedback: Correct. The passive is formed with "was" plus the past participle, so the sentence needs "was developed". -->
- [ ] C) developing
  <!-- feedback: Incorrect. A continuous passive would need "was being developed", which describes an action in progress, not a completed one. -->
- [ ] D) developer
  <!-- feedback: Incorrect. "Developer" is a person or a computer program, and it is not a past participle. -->

### Explicacion Pedagogica
The passive voice has a fixed shape in the past: "was" or "were" plus the past participle. Knowing the three forms of the verb (develop, was developing, developed) makes it easy to build each structure correctly.

## Question 10 [D7-D8]
**ID:** CO-ING-8-2026-W18-science-and-technology-001-MASTERY-bundle-v10
**Bloom:** Analyze
**ICFES:** Structural Cloze
**Expected_Success:** 0.70
**Contexto:** A university project explains the steps that remain after the funding is confirmed.

### Enunciado
If enough funds are raised, the prototype can be built and ________ in less than a year.

### Opciones
- [x] A) tested
  <!-- feedback: Correct. After the modal "can be" the second passive requires the past participle, so the sentence needs "can be tested". -->
- [ ] B) testing
  <!-- feedback: Incorrect. A modal passive always takes the participle; "can be testing" is not a possible form. -->
- [ ] C) test
  <!-- feedback: Incorrect. "Can be test" is wrong because the passive of "test" requires the participle "tested". -->
- [ ] D) tests
  <!-- feedback: Incorrect. A modal never takes the third person "-s", and the passive would need "be tested" in any case. -->

### Explicacion Pedagogica
Modal passive structures follow one rule: modal + "be" + past participle. Nothing changes when the passive has two verbs joined by "and"; each of them must be in the same form, so both halves need the participle.

## Question 12 [D7-D8]
**ID:** CO-ING-8-2026-W18-science-and-technology-001-MASTERY-bundle-v12
**Bloom:** Evaluate
**ICFES:** Lexical Knowledge
**Expected_Success:** 0.70
**Contexto:** A scientific writing workshop reviews how results are reported in journals.

### Enunciado
Which sentence is correct in a scientific report?

### Opciones
- [ ] A) "The results were surprising for the team of researchers."
  <!-- feedback: Incorrect. The preposition "for" is not used here; a structure like "X surprised Y" needs two different subjects or objects. -->
- [ ] B) "The results were surprising to the team of researchers."
  <!-- feedback: Incorrect. This structure describes a quality that surprises people, but "the results" are an action here, not a quality. -->
- [ ] C) "The results surprised for the team of researchers."
  <!-- feedback: Incorrect. The active verb "surprised" is followed directly by its object, so the preposition "for" cannot be placed there. -->
- [x] D) "The results surprised the team of researchers."
  <!-- feedback: Correct. An active verb can take the affected people as its object: "The results surprised the team". -->

### Explicacion Pedagogica
Deciding between the active and the passive voice depends on the focus: if the people are important we use "The results surprised the team", and if the focus is the quality we use "were surprising to". Mixing an active verb with a preposition produces the ungrammatical option C.

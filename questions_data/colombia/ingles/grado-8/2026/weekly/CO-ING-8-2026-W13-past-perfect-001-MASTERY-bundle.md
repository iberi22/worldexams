# Weekly Pack W13: The Past before the Past - Past Perfect (B1)

This bundle focuses on the Past Perfect tense (had + past participle) to talk about actions that happened before another action in the past, aligned with CEFR B1 level.

---

## Question 1 [D3-D4]
**ID:** `CO-ING-8-2026-W13-past-perfect-001-MASTERY-v1`
**Bloom:** Remember
**ICFES:** Lingüística
**Expected_Success:** 0.85
**Contexto:** Identifying the structure of Past Perfect.

### Enunciado
When I arrived at the cinema, the movie ________ already ________.

### Opciones
- [ ] A) has / started <!-- feedback: Incorrect. This is present perfect. -->
- [x] B) had / started <!-- feedback: Correct! Past perfect structure: had + past participle. -->
- [ ] C) was / started <!-- feedback: Incorrect. -->
- [ ] D) did / start <!-- feedback: Incorrect. -->

### Explicación Pedagógica
El Pasado Perfecto se utiliza para hablar de una acción que ocurrió antes que otra acción en el pasado. Su estructura es el auxiliar "had" seguido del participio pasado.

**Bloom:** Evaluate
**ICFES:** Lingüística
**Expected_Success:** 0.40
**Contexto:** Identifying errors in narrative tenses.

### Enunciado
Which sentence is grammatically **incorrect**?

### Opciones
- [ ] A) By the time we got home, the rain had stopped. <!-- feedback: Incorrect. This is correct. -->
- [ ] B) I had already eaten when they invited me to dinner. <!-- feedback: Incorrect. This is correct. -->
- [x] C) When I had seen him, I recognized him immediately. <!-- feedback: Correct! Error found. Recognizing happens after/at the same time as seeing; it should be "When I saw him". -->

### Explicación Pedagógica

# Weekly Pack W38: Asking Clearly - Indirect and Subject Questions (B1)

This bundle focuses on indirect questions (polite) and subject questions (where we ask about the person doing the action), aligned with CEFR B1.

---

## Question 1 [D3-D4]
**ID:** `CO-ING-8-2026-W38-question-formation-adv-001-MASTERY-v1`
**Bloom:** Remember
**ICFES:** Lingüística
**Expected_Success:** 0.85
**Contexto:** Basic indirect question structure.

### Enunciado
Could you tell me where ________?

### Opciones
- [ ] A) is the library <!-- feedback: Incorrect. In indirect questions, we use statement order. -->
- [x] B) the library is <!-- feedback: Correct! Indirect questions don't use inversion in the second part. -->
- [ ] C) does the library is <!-- feedback: Incorrect. -->
- [ ] D) the library it is <!-- feedback: Incorrect. -->

### Explicación Pedagógica
Cuando empezamos una pregunta con una frase cortés (Could you tell me...), la parte de la información vuelve al orden normal de una afirmación (sujeto + verbo).

## Question 2 [D3-D4]
**ID:** `CO-ING-8-2026-W38-question-formation-adv-001-MASTERY-v2`
**Bloom:** Remember
**ICFES:** Lingüística
**Expected_Success:** 0.80
**Contexto:** Subject question (asking about the doer).

### Enunciado
"Who ________ the window?" - "I did, by accident."

### Opciones
- [ ] A) did break <!-- feedback: Incorrect. Subject questions don't use "did" in the affirmative. -->
- [x] B) broke <!-- feedback: Correct! When "Who" is the subject, we use the past form directly without "did". -->
- [ ] C) breaks <!-- feedback: Incorrect. -->
- [ ] D) breaking <!-- feedback: Incorrect. -->

### Explicación Pedagógica
En las "Subject Questions", cuando la palabra interrogativa (Who/What) es el sujeto de la oración, no usamos el auxiliar "do/did". El verbo va directamente en su tiempo correspondiente.

**Contexto:** Distinguishing subject and object questions.

### Enunciado
**Question A:** Who did you see?
**Question B:** Who saw you?

In which question are you asking about the **attacker** in a crime? (Someone saw you).

### Opciones
- [ ] A) Question A <!-- feedback: Incorrect. This asks who YOU saw. -->
- [x] B) Question B <!-- feedback: Correct! "Who" is the subject who performed the action of seeing. -->
- [ ] C) Both. <!-- feedback: Incorrect. -->
- [ ] D) Neither. <!-- feedback: Incorrect. -->

### Explicación Pedagógica
Es vital distinguir: "Who did you..." (tú eres el sujeto) de "Who ...ed you" (la otra persona es el sujeto).

### Enunciado

### Opciones
- [ ] A) How much is the ticket? <!-- feedback: Okay, but a bit direct. -->
- [ ] B) Tell me the price now. <!-- feedback: Very rude. -->
- [x] C) Could you please tell me how much the ticket costs? <!-- feedback: Correct! Perfect indirect question for a formal service interaction. -->

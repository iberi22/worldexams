# Weekly Pack W17: Action Verbs - Phrasal Verbs (B1)

This bundle introduces common phrasal verbs used in daily life (look for, turn on/off, wake up, etc.), aligned with CEFR B1 level for Grade 8.

---

## Question 1 [D3-D4]
**ID:** `CO-ING-8-2026-W17-phrasal-verbs-001-MASTERY-v1`
**Bloom:** Remember
**ICFES:** Lingüística
**Expected_Success:** 0.85
**Contexto:** Daily routine phrasal verb.

### Enunciado
I usually ________ at 6:00 AM, but I stay in bed until 6:15 AM.

### Opciones
- [ ] A) get up <!-- feedback: Incorrect. This means leaving the bed. -->
- [x] B) wake up <!-- feedback: Correct! "Wake up" means to stop sleeping. -->
- [ ] C) look for <!-- feedback: Incorrect. -->
- [ ] D) turn on <!-- feedback: Incorrect. -->

### Explicación Pedagógica
Un "phrasal verb" es un verbo seguido de una preposición o adverbio que cambia su significado original. "Wake up" es el acto de despertar.

## Question 9 [D7-D8]
**ID:** `CO-ING-8-2026-W17-phrasal-verbs-001-MASTERY-v9`
**Bloom:** Analyze
**ICFES:** Lingüística
**Expected_Success:** 0.45
**Contexto:** Separable phrasal verbs.

### Enunciado
Which sentence is correct?

### Opciones
- [ ] A) Please turn off it. <!-- feedback: Incorrect. With pronouns, they must go in the middle of separable phrasal verbs. -->
- [x] B) Please turn it off. <!-- feedback: Correct! If the object is a pronoun (it, them), it must go between the verb and the particle. -->
- [ ] C) Please turning off it. <!-- feedback: Incorrect. -->
- [ ] D) Please off turn the light. <!-- feedback: Incorrect. -->

### Explicación Pedagógica
En los phrasal verbs separables, si el objeto es un pronombre (it, me, you, etc.), este **obligatoriamente** debe ir en medio del verbo y la partícula.

### Opciones
- [ ] A) looking for <!-- feedback: Incorrect. Correct use for searching. -->

- [ ] D) sofa <!-- feedback: Incorrect. -->

### Explicación Pedagógica
Diferenciar entre "find" (encontrar un objeto) y "find out" (descubrir un dato o información) es un detalle de precisión necesario en B1.

## Question 12 [D9-D10]

**Expected_Success:** 0.30

### Enunciado
"Juan was tired of his old car because it always broke down. He decided to look for a new one, but he didn't want to give up his savings easily."
What is the summary of Juan's situation?

### Opciones
- [x] A) He is searching for a replacement for his unreliable vehicle while trying to be careful with his money. <!-- feedback: Correct! It paraphrases the phrasal verbs correctly. -->
- [ ] B) He broke his car and is now giving presents to everyone. <!-- feedback: Incorrect. Misinterprets "broke down" and "give up". -->

### Explicación Pedagógica
La capacidad de parafrasear phrasal verbs usando verbos de una sola palabra (search, quit, stop working) demuestra un dominio sólido del vocabulario en B1.

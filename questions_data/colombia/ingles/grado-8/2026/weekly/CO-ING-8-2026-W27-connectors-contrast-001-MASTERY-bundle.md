# Weekly Pack W27: Connecting Ideas - Contrast (B1)

This bundle focuses on connectors of contrast such as "however", "although", "but", and "despite", aligned with CEFR B1 level for Grade 8.

### Enunciado

## Question 7 [D7-D8]
**ID:** `CO-ING-8-2026-W27-connectors-contrast-001-MASTERY-v7`
**Bloom:** Analyze
**ICFES:** Lingüística
**Expected_Success:** 0.55
**Contexto:** Changing structures.

### Enunciado
Which sentence is grammatically correct?

### Opciones
- [ ] A) Despite it was cold, we went swimming. <!-- feedback: Incorrect. "Despite" cannot be followed by a clause. -->
- [x] B) Despite the cold, we went swimming. <!-- feedback: Correct! "Despite" + noun. -->
- [ ] C) Although the cold, we went swimming. <!-- feedback: Incorrect. "Although" needs a verb. -->
- [ ] D) However the cold, we went swimming. <!-- feedback: Incorrect. -->

### Explicación Pedagógica
El estudiante debe saber distinguir cuándo usar un conector que requiere una oración completa (although/even though) y cuándo uno que requiere un sustantivo (despite/in spite of).

### Opciones

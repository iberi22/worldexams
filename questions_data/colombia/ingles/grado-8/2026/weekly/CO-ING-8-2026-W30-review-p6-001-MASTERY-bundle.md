## Question 12 [D9-D10]
**ID:** `CO-ING-8-2026-W30-review-p6-001-MASTERY-v12`
**Bloom:** Evaluate
**ICFES:** Lingüística
**Expected_Success:** 0.30
**Contexto:** Selecting the best summary of a past regret.

### Enunciado
"I didn't take an umbrella because the sun was shining. Then it rained suddenly and my clothes got completely wet. I wish I had been more careful."
Choose the best summary:

### Opciones
- [x] A) If I had taken an umbrella, I wouldn't have gotten wet despite the sudden rain. <!-- feedback: Correct! It combines Third Conditional and a contrast connector perfectly. -->
- [ ] B) Because it was such a sunny day, I didn't need an umbrella. <!-- feedback: Incorrect. Doesn't mention the rain consequence. -->
- [ ] C) Although I got wet, I had an umbrella. <!-- feedback: Incorrect. Contradicts the text. -->
- [ ] D) I used to have an umbrella but I lost it so I got wet. <!-- feedback: Incorrect. -->

### Explicación Pedagógica
La capacidad de integrar múltiples herramientas gramaticales (condicionales y conectores) en un solo resumen demuestra un dominio avanzado de la lengua en el nivel B1.

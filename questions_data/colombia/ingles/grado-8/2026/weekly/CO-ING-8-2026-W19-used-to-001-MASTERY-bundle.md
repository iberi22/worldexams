# Weekly Pack W19: Past Habits - Used To (B1)

This bundle focuses on "used to" to talk about past habits or states that are no longer true, aligned with CEFR B1 level for Grade 8.

---

**ID:** `CO-ING-8-2026-W19-used-to-001-MASTERY-v1`
**Bloom:** Remember
**ICFES:** Lingüística
**Expected_Success:** 0.85
**Contexto:** Past habit with "used to".

### Enunciado
When I was a child, I ________ play with dolls every afternoon.

### Opciones
- [x] A) used to <!-- feedback: Correct! We use "used to" for past habits that don't happen now. -->
- [ ] B) use to <!-- feedback: Incorrect. Positive form needs the "-d". -->

"Used to" se utiliza para describir hábitos o estados que eran frecuentes en el pasado pero que ya no ocurren en el presente.

## Question 7 [D7-D8]
**ID:** `CO-ING-8-2026-W19-used-to-001-MASTERY-v7`
**Bloom:** Analyze
**ICFES:** Lingüística
**Expected_Success:** 0.55
**Contexto:** "Used to" vs. "Would" for past habits.

### Enunciado
In which case can we NOT use "would" as a substitute for "used to"?

### Opciones
- [ ] A) When I lived in the country, I used to walk to school. <!-- feedback: Incorrect. Walking is an action, so "would walk" is possible. -->
- [x] B) I used to be very shy when I was a teenager. <!-- feedback: Correct! "Would" cannot be used for past states (like "be" or "have"). -->
- [ ] C) We used to visit my grandparents every Sunday. <!-- feedback: Incorrect. Visiting is an action. -->
- [ ] D) I used to eat a lot of candy. <!-- feedback: Incorrect. -->

### Explicación Pedagógica
Aunque "would" puede describir hábitos pasados, solo funciona con verbos de acción. Para verbos de estado (be, have, live, believe), solo podemos usar "used to".

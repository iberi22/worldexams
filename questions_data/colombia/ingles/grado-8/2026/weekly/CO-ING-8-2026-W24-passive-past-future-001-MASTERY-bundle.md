## Question 1 [D3-D4]
**ID:** `CO-ING-8-2026-W24-passive-past-future-001-MASTERY-v1`
**Bloom:** Remember
**ICFES:** Lingüística
**Expected_Success:** 0.85
**Contexto:** Reviewing Past Passive.

### Enunciado
The first computers ________ in the mid-20th century.

### Opciones
- [ ] A) are developed <!-- feedback: Incorrect. This is present. -->
- [x] B) were developed <!-- feedback: Correct! Past passive for a plural subject. -->
- [ ] C) was developed <!-- feedback: Incorrect. "Computers" is plural. -->
- [ ] D) developed <!-- feedback: Incorrect. Active voice. -->

### Explicación Pedagógica
La voz pasiva en pasado se forma con was/were + participio pasado. Se usa cuando el objeto (los computadores) es el foco de la oración histórica.

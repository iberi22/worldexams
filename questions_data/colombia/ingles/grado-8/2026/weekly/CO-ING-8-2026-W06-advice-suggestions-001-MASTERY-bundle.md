# Weekly Pack W06: Advice and Suggestions (B1)

This bundle focuses on giving advice and suggestions using modal verbs like "should", "shouldn't", "must", and "have to", aligned with CEFR B1 level for Grade 8.

---

## Question 1 [D3-D4]
**ID:** `CO-ING-8-2026-W06-advice-suggestions-001-MASTERY-v1`
**Bloom:** Remember
**ICFES:** Lingüística
**Expected_Success:** 0.85
**Contexto:** Giving simple health advice.

### Enunciado
If you have a very bad headache, you ________ see a doctor.

### Opciones
- [x] A) should <!-- feedback: Correct! "Should" is used to give advice or suggestions. -->
- [ ] B) shouldn't <!-- feedback: Incorrect. "Shouldn't" would mean it's a bad idea to see a doctor. -->
- [ ] C) must to <!-- feedback: Incorrect. "Must" is not followed by "to". -->
- [ ] D) had better not <!-- feedback: Incorrect. This means you shouldn't do it. -->

### Explicación Pedagógica
"Should" es el verbo modal estándar para dar consejos o sugerencias en inglés. Va seguido del verbo en su forma base.

## Question 7 [D7-D8]
**ID:** `CO-ING-8-2026-W06-advice-suggestions-001-MASTERY-v7`
**Bloom:** Analyze
**ICFES:** Lingüística
**Expected_Success:** 0.55
**Contexto:** Using "ought to" as an alternative to "should".

### Enunciado
Which sentence has the same meaning as "You should study more"?

### Opciones
- [ ] A) You must study more. <!-- feedback: Incorrect. "Must" is stronger than "should". -->
- [x] B) You ought to study more. <!-- feedback: Correct! "Ought to" is a synonym of "should". -->
- [ ] C) You have study more. <!-- feedback: Incorrect. Grammatically incomplete. -->
- [ ] D) You better study more. <!-- feedback: Incorrect. Needs "had" (You'd better) to be correct. -->

### Explicación Pedagógica
"Ought to" es un modal que funciona casi exactamente como "should", aunque es un poco más formal. Ambos se usan para dar consejos.

### Enunciado
**A:** "I'm always tired."
**B:** "Maybe you ________ go to bed earlier. What ________ I do to help you?"

### Opciones
- [ ] A) must / should <!-- feedback: Incorrect. "Must" is too strong for a friendly suggestion. -->
- [x] B) should / can <!-- feedback: Correct! "Should" for advice and "can" for offering help. -->

**ID:** `CO-ING-8-2026-W06-advice-suggestions-001-MASTERY-v10`
**Bloom:** Evaluate
**ICFES:** Lingüística
**Expected_Success:** 0.40
**Contexto:** Identifying the strongest degree of obligation.

### Enunciado
Which sentence expresses the **strongest** obligation?

### Opciones
- [ ] A) You should finish the report tonight. <!-- feedback: Incorrect. This is just advice. -->
- [ ] B) You ought to finish the report tonight. <!-- feedback: Incorrect. Similar to "should". -->
- [x] C) You must finish the report tonight; it's a matter of life or death! <!-- feedback: Correct! "Must" expresses the highest level of necessity or obligation. -->
- [ ] D) You might finish the report tonight if you have time. <!-- feedback: Incorrect. This is just a possibility. -->

### Explicación Pedagógica
El estudiante debe evaluar la carga semántica de cada modal. "Must" es el más fuerte, seguido de "have to", mientras que "should" y "ought to" son para consejos.

## Question 12 [D9-D10]
**ID:** `CO-ING-8-2026-W06-advice-suggestions-001-MASTERY-v12`
**Bloom:** Evaluate
**ICFES:** Sociolingüística
**Expected_Success:** 0.30
**Contexto:** Providing a logical conclusion based on modal logic.

### Enunciado
"I have a terrible toothache, and the dentist's office is already closed. I have an important presentation tomorrow."
What is the most logical and helpful advice?

### Opciones
- [x] A) You should take a painkiller now and you must call the dentist first thing tomorrow morning. <!-- feedback: Correct! Balanced advice with immediate and future steps. -->
- [ ] B) You don't have to worry because teeth don't matter for presentations. <!-- feedback: Incorrect. Dismissive and unhelpful. -->
- [ ] C) You mustn't go to the dentist ever. <!-- feedback: Incorrect. Dangerous advice. -->
- [ ] D) You should go to the cinema to forget the pain. <!-- feedback: Incorrect. Doesn't solve the problem. -->

### Explicación Pedagógica
Crear una respuesta lógica requiere priorizar necesidades (aliviar el dolor vs. solucionar el problema) usando los modales adecuados ("should" para sugerir, "must" para la acción necesaria).

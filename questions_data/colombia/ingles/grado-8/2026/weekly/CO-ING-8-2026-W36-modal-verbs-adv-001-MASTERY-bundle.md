# Weekly Pack W36: Advise, Request, Offer - Advanced Modals (B1)

This bundle explores advanced uses of modal verbs including "shall" for suggestions, "would" for polite requests/offers, and "should have" for past criticism. Aligned with CEFR B1.

---

**ID:** `CO-ING-8-2026-W36-modal-verbs-adv-001-MASTERY-v1`
**Bloom:** Remember
**ICFES:** Lingüística
**Expected_Success:** 0.85
**Contexto:** Making a polite suggestion with "shall".

### Enunciado
________ we go to the park this afternoon? The weather is beautiful.

### Opciones
- [ ] A) Will <!-- feedback: Incorrect. "Will" is for future facts or predictions. -->
- [x] B) Shall <!-- feedback: Correct! "Shall we...?" is a standard formal way to make a suggestion. -->
- [ ] C) Must <!-- feedback: Incorrect. -->
- [ ] D) Do <!-- feedback: Incorrect. -->

### Explicación Pedagógica
"Shall" se utiliza frecuentemente en preguntas con "I" o "we" para hacer sugerencias o proponer planes de manera cortés.

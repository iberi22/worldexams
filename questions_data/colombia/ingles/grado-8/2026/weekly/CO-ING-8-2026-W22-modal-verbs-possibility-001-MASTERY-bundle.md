# Weekly Pack W22: Modals of Possibility and Ability (B1)

This bundle explores modal verbs for expressing ability, permission, and possibility in different tenses (can, could, be able to, may), aligned with CEFR B1 level.

---

## Question 1 [D3-D4]
**ID:** `CO-ING-8-2026-W22-modal-verbs-possibility-001-MASTERY-v1`
**Bloom:** Remember
**ICFES:** Lingüística
**Expected_Success:** 0.85
**Contexto:** Present ability.

### Enunciado
Juan ________ speak three languages: Spanish, English, and French.

### Opciones
- [x] A) can <!-- feedback: Correct! "Can" expresses general ability in the present. -->
- [ ] B) could <!-- feedback: Incorrect. This is for past ability. -->
- [ ] C) is able <!-- feedback: Incorrect. Missing "to". -->
- [ ] D) may <!-- feedback: Incorrect. This is for permission or possibility. -->

### Explicación Pedagógica
El verbo modal "can" es la forma más común de expresar que alguien tiene la capacidad o habilidad de hacer algo en el presente.

## Question 10 [D9-D10]
**ID:** `CO-ING-8-2026-W22-modal-verbs-possibility-001-MASTERY-v10`
**Bloom:** Evaluate
**ICFES:** Lingüística
**Expected_Success:** 0.40
**Contexto:** Identifying errors in modal usage across tenses.

### Enunciado
Identify the error:
"I **haven't could (A)** finish the work yet, but I **hope (B)** I **will be able (C)** to do it **tomorrow (D)**."

### Opciones
- [x] A) haven't could <!-- feedback: Correct! Error found. "Can/could" cannot be used in the Present Perfect. It should be "haven't been able to". -->
- [ ] B) hope <!-- feedback: Incorrect. -->
- [ ] C) will be able <!-- feedback: Incorrect. -->
- [ ] D) tomorrow <!-- feedback: Incorrect. -->

### Explicación Pedagógica
Dado que los modales como "can" son "defectivos" (no tienen todas las formas), debemos usar "be able to" en tiempos compuestos como el Presente Perfecto.

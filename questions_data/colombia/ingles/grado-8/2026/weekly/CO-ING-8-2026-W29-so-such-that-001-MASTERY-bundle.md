# Weekly Pack W29: Cause and Effect - So and Such (B1)

This bundle focuses on the structures "so... that" and "such... that" to express consequence or emphasis, aligned with CEFR B1 level for Grade 8.

---

## Question 1 [D3-D4]
**ID:** `CO-ING-8-2026-W29-so-such-that-001-MASTERY-v1`
**Bloom:** Remember
**ICFES:** Lingüística
**Expected_Success:** 0.85
**Contexto:** Using "so" with an adjective.

### Enunciado
The movie was ________ interesting that I watched it twice.

### Opciones
- [x] A) so <!-- feedback: Correct! We use "so" before an adjective. -->
- [ ] B) such <!-- feedback: Incorrect. "Such" needs a noun. -->
- [ ] C) very <!-- feedback: Incorrect. "Very" doesn't connect with "that" to show consequence. -->
- [ ] D) too <!-- feedback: Incorrect. -->

### Explicación Pedagógica
Usamos "so" antes de un adjetivo o adverbio para enfatizar una cualidad que produce un resultado (expresado con "that").

### Enunciado
Which sentence has the same meaning as "The exam was so long that I couldn't finish it"?

### Opciones
- [ ] A) It was so a long exam that I couldn't finish it. <!-- feedback: Incorrect grammar. -->
- [x] B) It was such a long exam that I couldn't finish it. <!-- feedback: Correct! Grammatically equivalent. -->

## Question 12 [D9-D10]
**ID:** `CO-ING-8-2026-W29-so-such-that-001-MASTERY-v12`
**Bloom:** Evaluate
**ICFES:** Lingüística
**Expected_Success:** 0.30
**Contexto:** Selecting the best summary of cause and effect.

### Enunciado
"The noise from the construction site was constant and extremely loud. As a result, the neighbors were unable to sleep or concentrate during the entire day."
Choose the most effective summary using "so" or "such":

### Opciones
- [x] A) The construction was so loud that the neighbors couldn't sleep or concentrate. <!-- feedback: Correct! It summarizes the cause and the two effects accurately using "so... that". -->
- [ ] B) It was very loud construction and the neighbors were sad. <!-- feedback: Incorrect. Does not show the consequence link clearly. -->
- [ ] C) The neighbors didn't sleep because the construction was such a noise. <!-- feedback: Incorrect grammar. -->
- [ ] D) Such a loud noise made the neighbors sleep so much. <!-- feedback: Incorrect. Contradicts the text. -->

### Explicación Pedagógica
Sintetizar relaciones de causa y efecto requiere el uso preciso de "so... that" para integrar los hechos de manera coherente y compacta.

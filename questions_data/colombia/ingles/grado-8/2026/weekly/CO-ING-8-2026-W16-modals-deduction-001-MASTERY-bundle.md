# Weekly Pack W16: Making Guesses - Modals of Deduction (B1)

This bundle focuses on modal verbs of deduction (must, might, could, can't) to express how certain we are about a situation in the present, aligned with CEFR B1 level.

---

## Question 1 [D3-D4]
**ID:** `CO-ING-8-2026-W16-modals-deduction-001-MASTERY-v1`
**Bloom:** Remember
**ICFES:** Lingüística
**Expected_Success:** 0.85
**Contexto:** Expressing certainty (positive).

### Enunciado
He has three Ferraris and a huge mansion. He ________ be very rich.

### Opciones
- [ ] A) can't <!-- feedback: Incorrect. "Can't" is for when we are sure something is impossible. -->
- [x] B) must <!-- feedback: Correct! We use "must" when we are almost certain something is true. -->
- [ ] C) might <!-- feedback: Incorrect. "Might" is for low probability, but here the evidence is strong. -->
- [ ] D) shouldn't <!-- feedback: Incorrect. -->

### Explicación Pedagógica
Usamos "must" para hacer deducciones cuando estamos casi seguros de que algo es cierto basándonos en la evidencia disponible.

## Question 7 [D7-D8]
**ID:** `CO-ING-8-2026-W16-modals-deduction-001-MASTERY-v7`
**Bloom:** Analyze
**ICFES:** Lingüística
**Expected_Success:** 0.55
**Contexto:** Distinguishing "mustn't" from "can't".

### Enunciado
Which sentence expresses a **logical deduction** of impossibility?

### Opciones
- [ ] A) You mustn't enter this room. <!-- feedback: Incorrect. This is a prohibition (rule). -->
- [x] B) That can't be Carlos; he is in London right now. <!-- feedback: Correct! This is a logical conclusion based on a fact. -->
- [ ] C) You shouldn't eat that. <!-- feedback: Incorrect. This is advice. -->
- [ ] D) I can't swim very well. <!-- feedback: Incorrect. This is lack of ability. -->

### Explicación Pedagógica
Es vital para un estudiante B1 distinguir entre los diferentes usos de los modales. "Mustn't" nunca se usa para deducciones, solo para reglas o prohibiciones.

# Bundle Mastery: Present Perfect, Modal Verbs and Travel Vocabulary

This bundle covers the present perfect tense (regular verbs), modal verbs (must, should), and travel-related vocabulary at CEFR A2-B1 level.

---

## Question 1 [D3]

**ID:** `CO-INGLES-8-2026-P1-comprehensive-001-MASTERY-v1`
**Bloom:** Remember
**ICFES:** Comunicación
**Context:** A teacher in Bogotá asks students about the present perfect tense.

### Enunciado
Complete the sentence: "She _____ (visit) Cartagena three times."

### Options
- [ ] A) visit <!-- feedback: Incorrect. This is the base form. -->
- [ ] B) visited <!-- feedback: Incorrect. This is simple past. -->
- [x] C) has visited <!-- feedback: Correct. Present perfect: has + past participle. -->
- [ ] D) is visiting <!-- feedback: Incorrect. This is present continuous. -->

### Explicación Pedagógica
El present perfect se forma con have/has + past participle. Se usa para acciones que ocurrieron en un tiempo no especificado del pasado pero que tienen relevancia en el presente. "She has visited Cartagena three times" = tiene esa experiencia.

### Enunciado
Which modal verb expresses obligation (something you have to do)?

### Options
- [ ] A) Can <!-- feedback: Incorrect. "Can" expresses ability. -->
- [x] B) Must <!-- feedback: Correct. "Must" expresses obligation or necessity. -->
- [ ] C) Might <!-- feedback: Incorrect. "Might" expresses possibility. -->

## Question 7 [D5]

**ID:** `CO-INGLES-8-2026-P1-comprehensive-001-MASTERY-v7`
**Bloom:** Understand
**ICFES:** Razonamiento
**Context:** A teacher explains the difference between simple past and present perfect.

### Enunciado
Which sentence is in present perfect?

### Options
- [ ] A) I visited Bogotá last year. <!-- feedback: Incorrect. This is simple past (specific time: last year). -->
- [ ] B) I visit Bogotá every year. <!-- feedback: Incorrect. This is simple present (routine). -->
- [x] C) I have visited Bogotá. <!-- feedback: Correct. Present perfect, no specific time. -->
- [ ] D) I am visiting Bogotá now. <!-- feedback: Incorrect. This is present continuous. -->

### Explicación Pedagógica
El present perfect se usa cuando el tiempo NO es específico (I have visited Bogotá). El simple past se usa cuando el tiempo SÍ es específico (I visited Bogotá last year). Esta diferencia es fundamental para elegir el tiempo correcto.

## Question 9 [D6]

**ID:** `CO-INGLES-8-2026-P1-comprehensive-001-MASTERY-v9`
**Bloom:** Analyze
**ICFES:** Razonamiento
**Context:** A student compares two sentences: "You must study" and "You should study."

### Enunciado
What is the difference in meaning between these two sentences?

### Options
- [ ] A) They mean exactly the same thing. <!-- feedback: Incorrect. They have different strength. -->
- [x] B) "Must" expresses obligation (it's required); "should" expresses advice (it's recommended but not required). <!-- feedback: Correct. "Must" is stronger than "should." -->
- [ ] C) "Must" is past tense, "should" is present. <!-- feedback: Incorrect. Both refer to present/future. -->
- [ ] D) "Should" is more formal. <!-- feedback: Incorrect. Both can be formal or informal. -->

### Explicación Pedagógica
"Must" = obligación (es necesario/requerido). "Should" = recomendación (es aconsejable pero no obligatorio). Ej: "You must take this medicine" (orden médica). "You should exercise" (recomendación de salud).

## Question 10 [D6]

**ID:** `CO-INGLES-8-2026-P1-comprehensive-001-MASTERY-v10`
**Bloom:** Apply
**ICFES:** Indagación
**Context:** A student in Cartagena writes a postcard using present perfect.

### Enunciado
Which sentence is correct?

### Options
- [ ] A) We have visited the Castillo de San Felipe yesterday. <!-- feedback: Incorrect. "Yesterday" requires simple past. -->
- [x] B) We have visited the Castillo de San Felipe. It was amazing! <!-- feedback: Correct. Present perfect without specific time. -->
- [ ] C) We have visit the Castillo de San Felipe. <!-- feedback: Incorrect. "Visit" should be "visited" (past participle). -->
- [ ] D) We has visited the Castillo de San Felipe. <!-- feedback: Incorrect. "Has" is for he/she/it. -->

### Explicación Pedagógica
Present perfect no se usa con expresiones de tiempo específico como "yesterday" (ayer), "last week" (la semana pasada), "in 2023". Con esas expresiones se usa simple past. Present perfect se usa con "ever", "never", "already", "yet", "just".

**ICFES:** Razonamiento
**Context:** A student reads: "I have worked here for five years." vs. "I worked here for five years."

### Enunciado
What is the difference in meaning between these two sentences?

### Options
- [ ] A) They mean exactly the same thing. <!-- feedback: Incorrect. They have different meanings. -->
- [ ] B) The first one means the job is definitely over. <!-- feedback: Incorrect. Present perfect implies connection to present. -->
- [x] C) The first sentence (present perfect) implies the person still works there; the second (simple past) implies the job is finished. <!-- feedback: Correct. Present perfect connects past action to present. -->

**ICFES:** Razonamiento
**Context:** A student says: "I must to study for the English exam."

### Enunciado
Is this sentence grammatically correct?

### Options
- [ ] A) Yes, it's correct. <!-- feedback: Incorrect. "Must" is followed by base verb without "to." -->
- [ ] B) No, it should be: "I must studied." <!-- feedback: Incorrect. Wrong form. -->

## Question 15 [D9]

**ID:** `CO-INGLES-8-2026-P1-comprehensive-001-MASTERY-v15`
**Bloom:** Evaluate
**ICFES:** Pensamiento Reflexivo
**Context:** A teacher says: "To communicate effectively in English while traveling, you must know key vocabulary, but you should also practice listening comprehension."

### Enunciado
Why does the teacher use both "must" and "should" in this sentence?

### Options
- [ ] A) The teacher doesn't know the difference. <!-- feedback: Incorrect. The teacher uses them correctly. -->
- [x] B) "Must" indicates essential knowledge (vocabulary), while "should" indicates advisable but less essential skill (listening practice). <!-- feedback: Correct. The modals show different levels of importance. -->
- [ ] C) Both words mean the same thing here. <!-- feedback: Incorrect. They have different strengths. -->
- [ ] D) "Should" is used because listening is more important. <!-- feedback: Incorrect. "Should" is weaker than "must." -->

### Explicación Pedagógica
La elección entre "must" y "should" comunica el nivel de importancia. "Must" (esencial/obligatorio) se usa para lo fundamental. "Should" (aconsejable/recomendable) se usa para lo complementario. Esta distinción permite matizar el mensaje.

### Enunciado
Which adjective describes a very big object?

### Opciones
- [ ] A) small
  <!-- feedback: small means little in size and is the opposite of what the question asks for. -->
- [ ] B) long
  <!-- feedback: long measures the length along the object and not its overall size. -->
- [x] D) big
  <!-- feedback: big means large and describes the overall size of an object. -->

  <!-- feedback: The question asks which element is old, and the word that names it on its own is not a complete answer. -->

## Question 7 [D3-D4]
**ID:** CO-ING-3-2026-W38-describing-objects-001-MASTERY-bundle-v7
**Bloom:** Apply
**ICFES:** Uso de la lengua
**Expected_Success:** 0.85
**Contexto:** Ejercicio de descripcion comparada entre dos objetos de uso escolar.

### Enunciado
Which sentence describes the correct comparison?

### Opciones
- [ ] A) A pen is bigger than a pencil case.
  <!-- feedback: A pen cannot be bigger than a pencil case, so the comparison is impossible. -->
- [x] B) A pencil case is bigger than a pen.
  <!-- feedback: A pencil case really is bigger than a pen, and the comparative bigger is used correctly. -->
- [ ] C) A pencil case is more big than a pen.
  <!-- feedback: Comparatives in English are formed with -er or with more, but never with both at once. -->
- [ ] D) A pencil case is the big than a pen.
  <!-- feedback: The comparative is bigER and not big, because the -er form is used with short adjectives. -->

### Explicacion Pedagogica
Los comparativos en ingles tienen dos caminos: anadir -er en adjetivos cortos como big, o anteponer more con adjetivos largos como interesting. Mezclar ambas formas o usar el grado positivo es el error tipico cuando el espanol resuelve todo con el mismo termino.

## Question 8 [D3-D4]

**Contexto:** Lectura de un articulo sobre los materiales con que se fabrican los objetos cotidianos.

### Enunciado
Read: "This chair is made of wood and that cup is made of glass." What is the cup made of?

### Opciones
- [x] C) Glass
  <!-- feedback: The text says literally that that cup is made of glass. -->
- [ ] A) Wood
  <!-- feedback: Wood is the material of the chair according to the first part of the text. -->
- [ ] B) Plastic
  <!-- feedback: Plastic is not mentioned in the text. -->
- [ ] D) Metal
  <!-- feedback: Metal does not appear in the sentence either, so it cannot be the answer. -->

### Explicacion Pedagogica
En un texto con dos objetos y dos materiales, el lector debe emparejar cada elemento con su pareja correspondiente. Confundir los pares es el error tipico, y el estrategia preventiva es subrayar cada pareja en cuanto aparece en el texto.

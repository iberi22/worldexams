### Enunciado
Which sentence describes a morning routine correctly?

### Opciones
- [ ] A) She get up at six and eat breakfast.
  <!-- feedback: Both verbs need the -s of the third person singular. -->
- [ ] B) She is get up at six and eat breakfast.
  <!-- feedback: The present continuous does not describe a daily habit. -->
- [ ] C) She get up at six and is eat breakfast.

  <!-- feedback: The -s would be required only for he, she or it. -->

  <!-- feedback: 'Friends' is plural, so the base form 'play' is correct. -->

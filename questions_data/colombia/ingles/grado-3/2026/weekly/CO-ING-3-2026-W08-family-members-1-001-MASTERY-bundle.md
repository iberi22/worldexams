# Bundle MASTERY: La familia nuclear - Grado 3

Este bundle evalúa el vocabulario de la familia cercana (mother, father, brother, sister, baby, parents) y la frase "This is my ..." para presentar a los familiares.

## Question 1 [D3-D4]
**ID:** CO-ING-3-2026-W08-family-members-1-001-MASTERY-bundle-v1
**Bloom:** Remember
**ICFES:** Lexico
**Expected_Success:** 0.85
**Contexto:** En una clase de inglés en Manizales, los niños dibujan a la persona que los cuida en casa: su mamá.

### Enunciado
Which word means "mom"?

### Opciones
- [x] A) mother
  <!-- feedback: Correct. "Mother" is the word for mom, the woman who is your parent. -->
- [ ] B) father
  <!-- feedback: Incorrect. "Father" means dad, the man who is your parent, not your mom. -->
- [ ] C) brother
  <!-- feedback: Incorrect. "Brother" is a boy who has the same parents as you, not your mom. -->
- [ ] D) baby
  <!-- feedback: Incorrect. "Baby" is a very young child in the family, so it cannot be your mom. -->

### Explicacion Pedagogica
La palabra "mother" significa mamá o madre. "Mom" es la forma corta y cariñosa de decir "mother". Las otras palabras nombran a otros miembros de la familia: "father" (papá), "brother" (hermano) y "baby" (bebé).

### Enunciado

### Opciones
- [ ] A) This is my father. She is a teacher.
  <!-- feedback: Incorrect. A father is a man, so we say "He", not "She". -->

  <!-- feedback: Incorrect. A mother is a woman, so we say "She", not "He". -->

  <!-- feedback: Incorrect. A brother is a boy, so the pronoun must be "He", not "She". -->

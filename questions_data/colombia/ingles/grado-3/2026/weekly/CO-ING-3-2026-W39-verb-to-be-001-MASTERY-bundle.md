**ICFES:** Lingüística

### Enunciado
What verb do we use with **"I"**?

### Options
- [ ] A) Is <!-- feedback: Incorrect. "Is" goes with he, she, it. -->
- [x] B) Am <!-- feedback: Correct! "I am" = yo soy/estoy. -->
- [ ] C) Are <!-- feedback: Incorrect. "Are" goes with you, we, they. -->
- [ ] D) Be <!-- feedback: Incorrect. "Be" is the base form. -->

### Explicación Pedagógica

### Options

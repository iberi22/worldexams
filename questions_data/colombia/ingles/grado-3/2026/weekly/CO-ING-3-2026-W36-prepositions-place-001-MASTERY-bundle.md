### Enunciado
The pencil is **in** the pencil case. What does "in" mean?

### Options
- [ ] A) Sobre / encima de <!-- feedback: Incorrect. That's "on" or "above." -->

- [ ] C) Debajo de <!-- feedback: Incorrect. That's "under." -->

# Bundle MASTERY: Telling Time (Decir la hora) - Grado 3

Preguntar y decir la hora en inglés: "What time is it?", o'clock, half past, quarter past y el uso de a.m. y p.m. en la rutina diaria.

## Question 1 [D3-D4]
**ID:** CO-ING-3-2026-W31-telling-time-001-MASTERY-bundle-v1
**Bloom:** Remember
**ICFES:** Lexico
**Expected_Success:** 0.85
**Contexto:** En el salón de un colegio de Manizales hay un reloj de manecillas encima del tablero.

### Enunciado
Look at the clock: the short hand is on 3 and the long hand is on 12. What time is it?

### Opciones
- [ ] A) It's twelve o'clock.
  <!-- feedback: Incorrect. This reads the long hand as the hour; the short hand on 3 shows the hour. -->
- [ ] B) It's half past three.
  <!-- feedback: Incorrect. For half past three the long hand must be on 6, not on 12. -->
- [ ] C) It's two o'clock.
  <!-- feedback: Incorrect. For two o'clock the short hand must be on 2, but it is on 3. -->
- [x] D) It's three o'clock.
  <!-- feedback: Correct. The short hand shows the hour, 3, and the long hand on 12 means o'clock. -->

### Explicacion Pedagogica
En un reloj de manecillas, la manecilla corta ("short hand") marca la hora y la larga ("long hand") marca los minutos. Si la corta está en 3 y la larga en 12, decimos "It's three o'clock" (las tres en punto).

### Enunciado

### Opciones

  <!-- feedback: Incorrect. He gets up at 6:00 a.m., in the morning, not at four in the afternoon. -->

  <!-- feedback: Correct. The text says he does his homework at 4:00 p.m., four in the afternoon. -->

### Enunciado
The clock says 10:15. Which sentence is correct?

### Opciones

  <!-- feedback: Correct. Fifteen minutes after ten o'clock is quarter past ten. -->
- [ ] B) It's quarter to ten.
  <!-- feedback: Incorrect. Quarter to ten is 9:45, fifteen minutes before ten. -->

## Question 8 [D9-D10]

**Expected_Success:** 0.45
**Contexto:** La profesora de un colegio de Tunja pide escribir oraciones sobre la rutina con a.m. y p.m.

### Enunciado
Which sentence uses a.m. or p.m. correctly?

### Opciones
- [ ] A) I eat breakfast at 7:00 p.m.
  <!-- feedback: Incorrect. 7:00 p.m. is in the evening; breakfast is in the morning, so it needs a.m. -->
- [ ] B) I go to bed at 9:00 a.m.
  <!-- feedback: Incorrect. 9:00 a.m. is in the morning; bedtime at night needs p.m. -->
- [x] C) I eat dinner at 7:00 p.m.
  <!-- feedback: Correct. Dinner is in the evening, and p.m. is for times after midday. -->
- [ ] D) I have lunch at 12:30 a.m.
  <!-- feedback: Incorrect. 12:30 a.m. is after midnight; lunch at midday is 12:30 p.m. -->

### Explicacion Pedagogica
"a.m." se usa desde la medianoche hasta el mediodía y "p.m." desde el mediodía hasta la medianoche. La cena ("dinner") es en la noche, por eso "7:00 p.m." es correcto. El desayuno sería a.m.

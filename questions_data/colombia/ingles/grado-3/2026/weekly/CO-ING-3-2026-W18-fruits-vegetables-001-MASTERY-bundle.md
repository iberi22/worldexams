### Enunciado
Which word is a fruit?

### Opciones
- [ ] A) carrot
  <!-- feedback: A carrot is a root vegetable that grows underground, so it is not a fruit. -->
- [ ] B) potato
  <!-- feedback: A potato is a tuber and belongs to the group of vegetables. -->
- [x] D) banana
  <!-- feedback: A banana grows on a plant and is the fruit of that plant, so it is a fruit. -->

  <!-- feedback: Milk is uncountable, so it cannot take the numeral two in front of it. -->

  <!-- feedback: Rice is also an uncountable food and does not accept the numeral two. -->

## Question 8 [D3-D4]
**ID:** CO-ING-3-2026-W18-fruits-vegetables-001-MASTERY-bundle-v8
**Bloom:** Apply
**ICFES:** Uso de la lengua
**Expected_Success:** 0.80
**Contexto:** Actividad final en la que los estudiantes preparan una fruta para un compañero.

### Enunciado
Choose the correct sentence.

### Opciones
- [ ] A) I eat an apple and an banana.
  <!-- feedback: After the vowel a, n is used before the article, so it should be written an apple and not a banana. -->
- [ ] B) I eat a apple and a banana.
  <!-- feedback: Before a noun that starts with a vowel you use an and not a, because that avoids the harder sound. -->
- [x] C) I eat an apple and a banana.
  <!-- feedback: An goes before the noun apple because of its initial vowel, and a goes before banana because of its initial consonant. -->
- [ ] D) I eat the a apple and the a banana.
  <!-- feedback: The article the cannot be combined with a, and the sentence would also be redundant. -->

### Explicacion Pedagogica
La eleccion entre a y an depende de como se pronuncia la palabra, no de como se escribe. Si el sonido inicial es vocal se usa an y si es consonante se usa a, una regla que se verifica leyendo la palabra en voz alta y no solo mirandola.

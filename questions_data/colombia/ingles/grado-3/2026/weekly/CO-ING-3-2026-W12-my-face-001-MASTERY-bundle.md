# Bundle MASTERY: Mi cara - Grado 3

Este bundle evalúa las partes de la cara en inglés (eyes, nose, mouth, cheeks, eyebrows) y oraciones sencillas para describir una cara con "has".

## Question 1 [D3-D4]
**ID:** CO-ING-3-2026-W12-my-face-001-MASTERY-bundle-v1
**Bloom:** Remember
**ICFES:** Lexico
**Expected_Success:** 0.85
**Contexto:** En el Festival de las Flores de Medellín, los niños se acercan a oler las orquídeas de las silletas.

### Enunciado
Complete: "I smell the flowers with my ______."

### Opciones
- [x] A) nose
  <!-- feedback: Correct. The nose is the part of the face we use to smell things like flowers. -->
- [ ] B) ears
  <!-- feedback: Incorrect. Ears are for hearing sounds, not for smelling flowers. -->
- [ ] C) eyebrows
  <!-- feedback: Incorrect. Eyebrows are the lines of hair above the eyes, and they cannot smell. -->
- [ ] D) cheeks
  <!-- feedback: Incorrect. Cheeks are the soft sides of the face, and we do not smell with them. -->

### Explicacion Pedagogica
La palabra "nose" significa nariz, y con la nariz sentimos los olores. Por eso la oración correcta es "I smell the flowers with my nose" (huelo las flores con mi nariz).

## Question 2 [D3-D4]

**ICFES:** Lexico
**Expected_Success:** 0.80
**Contexto:** En una clase de arte en Duitama, los niños dibujan un autorretrato y la profesora nombra cada parte de la cara.

### Enunciado
Which word names the two lines of hair above your eyes?

### Opciones
- [ ] A) teeth
  <!-- feedback: Incorrect. Teeth are white and hard and live inside the mouth, not above the eyes. -->
- [ ] B) cheeks
  <!-- feedback: Incorrect. Cheeks are under the eyes, on the sides of the nose, and they have no lines of hair. -->
- [ ] C) mouth
  <!-- feedback: Incorrect. The mouth is under the nose, and it is for eating and talking. -->
- [x] D) eyebrows
  <!-- feedback: Correct. Eyebrows are the two lines of hair just above the eyes. -->

### Explicacion Pedagogica
"Eyebrows" significa cejas. La palabra tiene dentro "eye" (ojo) porque las cejas están justo encima de los ojos. Las mejillas ("cheeks") están debajo y la boca ("mouth") más abajo.

## Question 5 [D7-D8]
**ID:** CO-ING-3-2026-W12-my-face-001-MASTERY-bundle-v5
**Bloom:** Analyze
**ICFES:** Uso de la lengua
**Expected_Success:** 0.65
**Contexto:** En Riohacha, Samuel describe a su amiga Ana para el juego "¿Quién es quién?" de la clase de inglés.

### Enunciado
Complete: "Look at Ana. She ______ big brown eyes."

### Opciones
- [ ] A) have
  <!-- feedback: Incorrect. "Have" goes with I, you, we and they; with "She" the verb needs an -s ending. -->
- [ ] B) is
  <!-- feedback: Incorrect. "Is" describes what a person is, but "She is big brown eyes" says Ana is a pair of eyes. -->
- [ ] C) are
  <!-- feedback: Incorrect. "Are" goes with you, we and they, and it does not express that Ana owns her eyes. -->
- [x] D) has
  <!-- feedback: Correct. With "She" we use "has" to say what a person's face is like: "She has big brown eyes". -->

### Explicacion Pedagogica
Para describir la cara usamos el verbo "have" (tener). Con "I" y "you" se dice "have", pero con "He" o "She" cambia a "has". Por eso decimos "She has big brown eyes" (ella tiene ojos grandes y cafés).

## Question 6 [D7-D8]
**ID:** CO-ING-3-2026-W12-my-face-001-MASTERY-bundle-v6
**Bloom:** Analyze
**ICFES:** Comprension lectora
**Expected_Success:** 0.60
**Contexto:** Andres, un niño de Quibdó, escribe una descripción de su cara para su amigo por correspondencia.

### Enunciado
Read: "My name is Andres. I have short black hair and green eyes. I have a small nose and a big smile."
What color are Andres's eyes?

### Opciones
- [ ] A) black
  <!-- feedback: Incorrect. In the text "black" describes his hair, not his eyes. -->
- [x] B) green
  <!-- feedback: Correct. The text says "green eyes", so the eyes of Andres are green. -->
- [ ] C) brown
  <!-- feedback: Incorrect. The word "brown" does not appear in the text about Andres. -->
- [ ] D) blue
  <!-- feedback: Incorrect. The text never says "blue"; the color next to "eyes" is green. -->

### Explicacion Pedagogica
En inglés el color va antes de la palabra que describe: "black hair" es cabello negro y "green eyes" es ojos verdes. Hay que fijarse qué palabra acompaña a "eyes": es "green", entonces los ojos de Andres son verdes.

## Question 8 [D9-D10]
**ID:** CO-ING-3-2026-W12-my-face-001-MASTERY-bundle-v8
**Bloom:** Evaluate
**ICFES:** Uso de la lengua
**Expected_Success:** 0.45
**Contexto:** En Yopal, la profesora muestra cuatro descripciones de caras para un mural y pide encontrar la que está bien escrita.

### Enunciado
Which sentence is correct?

### Opciones
- [x] A) He has a small nose and two big eyes.
  <!-- feedback: Correct. "He" goes with "has", and "two eyes" uses the plural because there are two of them. -->
- [ ] B) He has one eyes.
  <!-- feedback: Incorrect. With "one" we need the singular "eye"; "eyes" is the plural for two or more. -->
- [ ] C) She have two ears.
  <!-- feedback: Incorrect. With "She" the verb must be "has", so "She have" is a grammar mistake. -->
- [ ] D) I has a big mouth.
  <!-- feedback: Incorrect. With "I" we use "have", because "has" only goes with he, she and it. -->

### Explicacion Pedagogica
Para describir la cara recordamos dos reglas: "I have" pero "He/She has", y una sola cosa va en singular ("one eye", "one nose") mientras dos o más van en plural ("two eyes", "two ears"). Solo la opción A cumple las dos reglas.

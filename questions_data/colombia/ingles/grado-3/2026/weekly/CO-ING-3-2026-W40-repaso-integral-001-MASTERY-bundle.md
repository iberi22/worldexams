It is 8 p.m. Daniela sees her neighbor. What does she say?

### Opciones

  <!-- feedback: Incorrect. Good morning is for the morning, before 12 p.m., and it is 8 p.m. -->

### Enunciado

### Opciones
- [x] A) twenty
  <!-- feedback: Correct. Twelve + eight = 20, and 20 is twenty. -->
- [ ] B) eighteen
  <!-- feedback: Incorrect. Eighteen is 18, but 12 + 8 = 20. -->

  <!-- feedback: Incorrect. Twelve is the number before her aunt gives her more; 12 + 8 = 20. -->

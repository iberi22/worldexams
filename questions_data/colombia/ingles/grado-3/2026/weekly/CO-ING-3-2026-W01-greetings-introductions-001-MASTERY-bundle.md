## Question 1 [D2]

**ID:** `CO-ING-3-2026-W01-greetings-introductions-001-v1`
**Bloom:** Remember
**ICFES:** Competencia Lingüistica
**Context:** Meeting a friend

### Enunciado
When you see a friend in the morning at school, what is the most common way to say hello?

### Options
- [ ] A) Goodbye <!-- feedback: Incorrect. "Goodbye" is used when you are leaving, not when you arrive. -->
- [x] B) Hello <!-- feedback: Correct! "Hello" or "Hi" are standard ways to greet someone. -->
- [ ] C) Goodnight <!-- feedback: Incorrect. "Goodnight" is used before going to sleep or late at night. -->
- [ ] D) Thank you <!-- feedback: Incorrect. "Thank you" is used to show gratitude, not to say hello. -->

### Explicacion Pedagogica
Los saludos (greetings) son las palabras que usamos para iniciar una conversacion. "Hello" es el saludo mas comun y universal en ingles.

**Bloom:** Remember
**ICFES:** Competencia Lingüistica
**Context:** Morning greeting

### Enunciado
It is 8:00 AM. How do you greet your teacher in English?

### Options
- [ ] A) Good afternoon <!-- feedback: Incorrect. "Afternoon" is after 12:00 PM. -->
- [x] B) Good morning <!-- feedback: Correct! "Good morning" is the greeting used from sunrise until noon. -->
- [ ] C) Good evening <!-- feedback: Incorrect. "Evening" is used when it starts to get dark. -->

**Context:** Introducing yourself

### Enunciado
Read the conversation:
- Tom: "Hi, I am Tom."
- Sara: "Hello, ________ Sara."

What is the correct way for Sara to complete her sentence?

### Options
- [ ] A) You are <!-- feedback: Incorrect. Sara is talking about herself, not Tom. -->
- [x] B) I am <!-- feedback: Correct! "I am" (o la contraccion "I'm") se usa para presentarse a uno mismo. -->
- [ ] C) He is <!-- feedback: Incorrect. "He" refers to another boy. -->
- [ ] D) Goodbye <!-- feedback: Incorrect. They are introducing themselves, not saying goodbye. -->

### Explicacion Pedagogica
Usamos el pronombre "I" (Yo) seguido del verbo "am" (soy/estoy) para decir nuestro nombre: "I am Sara" (Yo soy Sara).

### Enunciado

**ID:** `CO-ING-3-2026-W01-greetings-introductions-007-v1`
**Bloom:** Apply
**ICFES:** Competencia Lingüistica
**Context:** Afternoon greeting

### Enunciado
You see your neighbor at 3:00 PM. What do you say?

### Options
- [ ] A) Good morning <!-- feedback: Incorrect. 3:00 PM is too late for morning. -->
- [x] B) Good afternoon <!-- feedback: Correct! Usamos "Good afternoon" desde el mediodia (12:00 PM) hasta el atardecer. -->
- [ ] C) Good night <!-- feedback: Incorrect. We say this when leaving or going to bed. -->

## Question 9 [D6]

**ID:** `CO-ING-3-2026-W01-greetings-introductions-009-v1`
**Bloom:** Apply
**ICFES:** Competencia Lingüistica
**Context:** Organizing a dialogue

### Enunciado
What is the correct order for this short dialogue?
1. Nice to meet you too.
2. Hi, I am Alex. Nice to meet you.
3. Hello, my name is Kim.

### Options
- [ ] A) 1 - 2 - 3 <!-- feedback: Incorrect. "Too" cannot come before the first greeting. -->
- [ ] B) 3 - 1 - 2 <!-- feedback: Incorrect. The response "too" must be at the end. -->
- [x] C) 3 - 2 - 1 <!-- feedback: Correct! First Kim introduces herself, then Alex introduces himself and says "Nice to meet you", and Kim responds. -->
- [ ] D) 2 - 3 - 1 <!-- feedback: Incorrect. Although it's possible, C is more natural for a first interaction. -->

### Explicacion Pedagogica
En una conversacion, primero se saluda y se dice el nombre. Luego, la otra persona hace lo mismo. Finalmente, se intercambian frases de cortesia como "Nice to meet you".

**Bloom:** Understand
**ICFES:** Competencia Lingüistica
**Context:** Formal vs Informal

### Enunciado
Which word is an **informal** way to say "Hello"?

### Options
- [x] A) Hi <!-- feedback: Correct! "Hi" is shorter and more informal than "Hello", used with friends and family. -->
- [ ] B) Good morning <!-- feedback: Incorrect. This is more formal. -->
- [ ] C) Good afternoon <!-- feedback: Incorrect. This is more formal. -->
- [ ] D) Goodbye <!-- feedback: Incorrect. This is for leaving, not for greeting. -->

### Explicacion Pedagogica
En ingles existen diferentes niveles de formalidad. "Hello" es adecuado para cualquier situacion, mientras que "Hi" es mas relajado y comun entre compañeros de clase.

## Question 1 (Difficulty D3)
**ID:** `CO-ING-10-2026-P3-comprehensive-001-MASTERY-v1`
**Bloom:** Remember
**ICFES:** Gramatica B1
**Context:** In English class in Bucaramanga, the teacher introduces perfect modal verbs for the first time.
### Enunciado
Which structure is used to express a past action that was necessary but did NOT happen?
### Options
- [ ] A) Must have + past participle <!-- feedback: Incorrecto. 'Must have' expresses a logical deduction about the past, not an unfulfilled obligation. -->
- [x] B) Should have + past participle <!-- feedback: Correcto. 'Should have + past participle' expresses a past action that was advisable or expected but did NOT happen. -->
- [ ] C) Could have + past participle <!-- feedback: Incorrecto. 'Could have' expresses a past possibility that did not happen, not an obligation. -->
- [ ] D) Might have + past participle <!-- feedback: Incorrecto. 'Might have' expresses uncertainty about a past event, not an unfulfilled obligation. -->

### Explicacion Pedagogica
Perfect modals combine a modal verb + 'have' + past participle. 'Should have + past participle' expresses regret or criticism about a past action that was expected but did not happen. Example: 'You should have studied for the exam' (you did NOT study, and that was a mistake).

What level of certainty does 'might have' express?

## Question 1 (Difficulty D3)
**ID:** `CO-ING-10-2026-P2-comprehensive-001-MASTERY-v1`
**Bloom:** Remember
**ICFES:** Gramatica B1
**Context:** In English class in Medellin, the teacher introduces phrasal verbs.
### Enunciado
What is a phrasal verb?
### Options
- [ ] A) A single verb with no preposition <!-- feedback: Incorrecto. A phrasal verb consists of a verb + one or two particles (preposition or adverb). -->
- [ ] B) A verb followed by a noun <!-- feedback: Incorrecto. A verb + noun is just a verb with a direct object, not a phrasal verb. -->
- [x] C) A verb combined with a preposition or adverb that changes its meaning <!-- feedback: Correcto. Phrasal verbs are multi-word verbs where the particle changes the meaning of the base verb. -->
- [ ] D) An adjective that functions as a verb <!-- feedback: Incorrecto. Phrasal verbs are verbs, not adjectives. -->

### Explicacion Pedagogica
Phrasal verbs combine a verb with one or more particles (prepositions or adverbs) to create a new meaning. For example, 'give up' (stop trying) has a different meaning from 'give' (donate or offer). Phrasal verbs are very common in English and essential for B1 level communication.

## Question 2 (Difficulty D3)
**ID:** `CO-ING-10-2026-P2-comprehensive-001-MASTERY-v2`
**Bloom:** Remember
**ICFES:** Gramatica B1
**Context:** The teacher explains the structure of an argumentative essay.
### Enunciado
Which of the following is the correct structure for an argumentative essay?
### Options
- [ ] A) Introduction, conclusion, and bibliography only <!-- feedback: Incorrecto. An argumentative essay needs body paragraphs with arguments and evidence. -->
- [x] B) Introduction, body paragraphs with arguments, counterargument, and conclusion <!-- feedback: Correcto. An argumentative essay presents arguments, addresses counterarguments, and concludes with the writer's position. -->
- [ ] C) Only body paragraphs without introduction or conclusion <!-- feedback: Incorrecto. An essay must have an introduction that presents the thesis and a conclusion that summarizes. -->
- [ ] D) Counterargument first, then introduction <!-- feedback: Incorrecto. The introduction comes first to present the topic and thesis statement. -->

### Explicacion Pedagogica
A standard argumentative essay structure includes: (1) Introduction with a hook, background, and thesis statement; (2) Body paragraphs presenting arguments with evidence; (3) A counterargument paragraph addressing opposing views; (4) A conclusion restating the thesis and summarizing key points.

**ID:** `CO-ING-10-2026-P2-comprehensive-001-MASTERY-v4`
**Bloom:** Understand
**ICFES:** Escritura B1
**Context:** A student must write an argumentative paragraph about recycling in Colombia.
### Enunciado
Which linking word is MOST appropriate to introduce a counterargument?
### Options
- [ ] A) Firstly <!-- feedback: Incorrecto. 'Firstly' is used to introduce the first point in a sequence, not a counterargument. -->
- [x] B) However <!-- feedback: Correcto. 'However' is used to introduce a contrasting point or counterargument, showing a different perspective. -->
- [ ] C) Moreover <!-- feedback: Incorrecto. 'Moreover' adds supporting information; it does not introduce an opposing view. -->

## Question 8 (Difficulty D6)
**ID:** `CO-ING-10-2026-P2-comprehensive-001-MASTERY-v8`
**Bloom:** Analyze
**ICFES:** Lectura B1
**Context:** Read the argumentative text: 'While some argue that school uniforms reduce bullying, I believe they limit students' freedom of expression. Furthermore, uniforms are expensive for low-income families. However, it is true that uniforms create a sense of equality.'
### Enunciado
What is the writer's MAIN position on school uniforms?
### Options
- [ ] A) School uniforms should be mandatory everywhere. <!-- feedback: Incorrecto. The writer states 'I believe they limit students' freedom,' indicating opposition. -->
- [ ] B) The writer is neutral and has no opinion. <!-- feedback: Incorrecto. The phrase 'I believe' indicates a clear position. -->
- [x] C) The writer is against mandatory school uniforms. <!-- feedback: Correcto. The writer gives reasons against uniforms (limit freedom, expensive) and only acknowledges the opposing view. -->

The writer uses 'While some argue... I believe' to contrast the opposing view with their own position. 'Furthermore' adds a supporting argument. 'However' introduces a concession (acknowledging a valid point from the other side) before restating the main argument. This is a classic argumentative essay structure.

## Question 10 (Difficulty D6)
**ID:** `CO-ING-10-2026-P2-comprehensive-001-MASTERY-v10`
**Bloom:** Analyze
**ICFES:** Escritura B1
**Context:** A student wrote: 'Firstly, recycling helps reduce pollution in Colombian cities. Moreover, it creates jobs in the recycling industry. On the other hand, some people say recycling is expensive. In conclusion, the benefits outweigh the costs.'
### Enunciado
What is the function of 'Moreover' in this text?
### Options
- [ ] A) To introduce a counterargument <!-- feedback: Incorrecto. 'Moreover' adds supporting information, it does not introduce an opposing view. -->
- [x] B) To add a new supporting point <!-- feedback: Correcto. 'Moreover' adds another argument that supports the writer's main position. -->
- [ ] C) To conclude the essay <!-- feedback: Incorrecto. 'In conclusion' introduces the conclusion, not 'Moreover.' -->
- [ ] D) To show cause and effect <!-- feedback: Incorrecto. 'Therefore' or 'as a result' shows cause and effect; 'Moreover' adds information. -->

### Explicacion Pedagogica
'Moreover' is a formal linker used to add additional supporting information. It is more formal than 'also' or 'besides.' Other additive linkers: 'furthermore,' 'in addition,' 'additionally.' The essay structure: Firstly (first point) -> Moreover (second point) -> On the other hand (counterargument) -> In conclusion (summary).

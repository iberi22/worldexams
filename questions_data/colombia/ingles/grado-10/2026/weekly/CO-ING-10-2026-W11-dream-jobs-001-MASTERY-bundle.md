# Bundle Mastery: Dream Jobs - Grade 10

This bundle explores career aspirations and job prospects using B2-level grammar, focusing on the First Conditional and the use of 'when' and 'unless'.

---

## Question 1 [D3]
**ID:** `CO-ING-10-2026-W11-dream-jobs-001-MASTERY-v1`
**Bloom:** Understand
**ICFES:** Part 4 - Grammatical Cloze
**Expected_Success:** 0.88
**Contexto:** Talking about future job interviews.

### Enunciado
"If I ____ the job interview tomorrow, I ____ celebrating with my family in the evening."

### Opciones
- [ ] A) pass / was <!-- feedback: Incorrect tenses. -->
- [x] B) pass / will be <!-- feedback: Correct. First Conditional (present simple + will). -->
- [ ] C) passed / would be <!-- feedback: This is Second Conditional. -->
- [ ] D) will pass / am <!-- feedback: Incorrect. 'Will' is not used in the 'if' clause. -->

### Explicación Pedagógica
The First Conditional is used for real or possible situations in the future. Structure: If + Present Simple, Will + Verb.

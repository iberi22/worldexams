**ICFES:** Part 4 - Grammatical Cloze
**Expected_Success:** 0.86
**Contexto:** A student talking about a difficult exam.

### Enunciado
"I ____ extremely nervous before the exam began, but I realized that I ____ enough."

### Opciones
- [ ] A) felt / study <!-- feedback: Incorrect. -->

### Explicación Pedagógica
Past Perfect is used to describe an action that happened before another action in the past.

# Bundle Mastery: Mixed Realities - Grade 10

This bundle explores complex hypothetical scenarios using B2-level grammar, focusing on Mixed Conditionals (Past condition with Present result, and vice versa).

---

## Question 1 [D3-D4]
**ID:** `CO-ING-10-2026-W16-mixed-realities-001-MASTERY-v1`
**Bloom:** Understand
**ICFES:** Part 4 - Grammatical Cloze
**Expected_Success:** 0.85
**Contexto:** Reflecting on a past study choice.

### Enunciado
"If I ____ more attention in French class three years ago, I ____ a better job in Paris now."

### Opciones
- [ ] A) paid / would have <!-- feedback: 'paid' in the if-clause would be the second conditional, which treats the French class as a present hypothetical rather than a past one. -->
- [x] C) had paid / would have <!-- feedback: The condition is in the past ('three years ago'), so the if-clause takes the past perfect 'had paid', and the present result takes 'would have'. -->
- [ ] B) had paid / would have had <!-- feedback: 'Would have had' would put the result in the past too, but the sentence ends in 'now', which is present. -->
- [ ] D) paid / will have <!-- feedback: 'Will have' states a future certainty, and 'paid' ignores the past time reference. -->

### Explicación Pedagógica
Mixed Conditional 1: If + Past Perfect (Past hypothetical condition), Would + Verb (Present hypothetical result).

## Question 1 [D3]
**ID:** `CO-ING-10-2026-W13-historical-what-ifs-001-MASTERY-v1`
**Bloom:** Understand
**ICFES:** Part 4 - Grammatical Cloze
**Expected_Success:** 0.88
**Contexto:** Speculating about a missed flight.

### Enunciado
"If he ____ the taxi earlier, he ____ his flight to the conference."

### Opciones
- [ ] A) takes / wouldn't miss <!-- feedback: Incorrect. -->
- [x] B) had taken / wouldn't have missed <!-- feedback: Correct. Third Conditional (past hypothetical). -->
- [ ] C) took / wouldn't miss <!-- feedback: Second Conditional (present hypothetical). -->
- [ ] D) has taken / won't miss <!-- feedback: Incorrect. -->

### Explicación Pedagógica
The Third Conditional is used to talk about things that didn't happen in the past and their hypothetical results. Structure: If + Past Perfect, Would have + Past Participle.

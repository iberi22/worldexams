# Bundle Mastery: Life Landmarks - Grade 10

This bundle explores significant life events using B2-level grammar, focusing on 'used to', 'would', and 'be/get used to'.

---

## Question 1 [D3-D4]
**ID:** `CO-ING-10-2026-W02-life-landmarks-001-MASTERY-v1`
**Bloom:** Understand
**ICFES:** Part 4 - Grammatical Cloze
**Expected_Success:** 0.88
**Contexto:** Discussing childhood habits.

### Enunciado
"When I was a child, we ____ live in a small town, and my father ____ take us to the river every Sunday."

### Opciones
- [x] A) used to / would <!-- feedback: Correct. 'Used to' for a past state (live) and 'would' for a repeated past action (take). -->
- [ ] B) would / used to <!-- feedback: Incorrect. 'Would' is not used for past states like 'live'. -->
- [ ] C) used to / used to <!-- feedback: Also grammatically correct, but less varied. -->
- [ ] D) were / did <!-- feedback: Incorrect. -->

### Explicación Pedagógica
'Used to' can describe both past states and actions. 'Would' only describes repeated past actions, not states.

# Bundle Mastery: Journalism Ethics - Grade 10

This bundle explores ethical issues in journalism using B2-level grammar, focusing on Modals of Deduction and Speculation about the past (must have, could have, might have, can't have).

**Contexto:** Deducing why a story was published.

### Enunciado

### Opciones

- [x] B) can't have had <!-- feedback: Correct. Logical impossibility in the past (based on the evidence). -->

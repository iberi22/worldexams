# Bundle Mastery: Journalism Ethics - Grade 10

This bundle explores ethical issues in journalism using B2-level grammar, focusing on Modals of Deduction and Speculation about the past (must have, could have, might have, can't have).

---

## Question 1 [D3-D4]
**ID:** `CO-ING-10-2026-W33-journalism-ethics-001-MASTERY-v1`
**Bloom:** Understand
**ICFES:** Part 4 - Grammatical Cloze
**Expected_Success:** 0.88
**Contexto:** Deducing why a story was published.

### Enunciado
"The newspaper ____ very high standards; they published the story without verifying the sources."

### Opciones
- [ ] B) must have had <!-- feedback: "Must have had" says the standards were definitely there, which contradicts the fact that the story was published unverified. -->
- [x] A) can't have had <!-- feedback: Correct. Logical impossibility in the past (based on the evidence). -->
- [ ] C) should have had <!-- feedback: Correct as criticism, but B is a stronger deduction of fact. -->
- [ ] D) might have had <!-- feedback: Too weak. -->

### Explicación Pedagógica
'Can't have' + past participle is used to express a strong belief that something was impossible in the past.

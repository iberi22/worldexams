**ICFES:** Part 4 - Grammatical Cloze
**Expected_Success:** 0.85
**Contexto:** Discussing a long-term project.

### Enunciado
"I ____ at the local animal shelter for six months, and I love seeing the progress the pets make."

### Opciones
- [ ] A) volunteered <!-- feedback: Incorrect. -->
- [ ] B) am volunteering <!-- feedback: Incorrect. -->
- [x] C) have been volunteering <!-- feedback: Correct. Present Perfect Continuous shows an action that started in the past and continues. -->
- [ ] D) have volunteered <!-- feedback: Possible, but less idiomatic for ongoing actions with duration. -->

### Explicación Pedagógica
Present Perfect Continuous is used for actions that started in the past and are still happening.

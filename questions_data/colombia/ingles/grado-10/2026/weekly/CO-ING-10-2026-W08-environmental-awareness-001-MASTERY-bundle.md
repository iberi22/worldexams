## Question 1 [D3]
**ID:** `CO-ING-10-2026-W08-environmental-awareness-001-MASTERY-v1`
**Bloom:** Understand
**ICFES:** Part 4 - Grammatical Cloze
**Expected_Success:** 0.88
**Contexto:** Reporting a scientist's claim.

### Enunciado
"The scientist said that global temperatures ____ rising at an alarming rate."

### Opciones
- [ ] A) are <!-- feedback: Incorrect backshifting. -->
- [x] B) were <!-- feedback: Correct. Backshift from present continuous to past continuous. -->
- [ ] C) have been <!-- feedback: Incorrect. -->
- [ ] D) will be <!-- feedback: Incorrect. -->

### Explicación Pedagógica
In Reported Speech, verbs in the statement usually shift one tense back (backshifting).

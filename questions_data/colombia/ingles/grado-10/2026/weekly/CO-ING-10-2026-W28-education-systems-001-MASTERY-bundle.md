# Bundle Mastery: Education Systems - Grade 10

This bundle explores educational models and reforms using B2-level grammar, focusing on Inversion after negative and restrictive adverbials.

**Expected_Success:** 0.88
**Contexto:** Discussing traditional teaching methods.

### Enunciado
"Never ____ the students ____ so much autonomy in choosing their own research projects."

### Opciones
- [ ] A) did / had <!-- feedback: Incorrect. -->

# Question

Chidi works at a shop in Lagos and earns ₦5,000 per day plus a 10% commission on all his sales. If his total earnings for one day were ₦8,000, what was the value of his sales that day?

# Choices

- [ ] A) ₦20,000
- [ ] B) ₦25,000
- [x] C) ₦30,000
- [ ] D) ₦35,000

# Explanation

**Correct Answer: C) ₦30,000**

To solve this problem:

1. Set up the equation: Total earnings = Fixed salary + Commission
2. Fixed salary = ₦5,000
3. Commission = 10% of sales = 0.10 × Sales
4. Equation: ₦8,000 = ₦5,000 + 0.10 × Sales
5. Solve: 0.10 × Sales = ₦8,000 - ₦5,000 = ₦3,000
6. Sales = ₦3,000 ÷ 0.10 = ₦30,000

**Why other options are wrong:**
- A) ₦20,000: Error in percentage calculation
- B) ₦25,000: Forgot to subtract fixed salary
- D) ₦35,000: Added instead of subtracted

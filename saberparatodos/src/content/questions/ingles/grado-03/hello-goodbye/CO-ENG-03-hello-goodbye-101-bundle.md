# Topic: Hello & Goodbye! 👋 (Pre-A1)

## Q1 - Vocabulary
**When you meet someone, say:** / *Cuando conoces a alguien, di:*
- [x] Hello! 👋 / *¡Hola!*

| English | Español | When |
|---------|---------|------|
| Hello/Hi | Hola | Meeting |
| Goodbye/Bye | Adiós | Leaving |
| Good morning | Buenos días | ☀️ Morning |
| Good night | Buenas noches | 🌙 Night |

**Practice:** 👋 "Hello! My name is ___." = "¡Hola! Me llamo ___."

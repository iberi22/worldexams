# Topic: Hobbies & Free Time (A2+)

---

## Question 1-10 Bundle

**Q1 - Vocabulary:** What do you call pictures you take with a camera? → **Photos/Photographs**

**Q2 - Pragmatics:** "What are you into?" means... → Asking about your interests/hobbies.

**Q3 - Conversation:** "Want to play video games?" Complete: → "Sure! I'll bring an extra controller."

**Q4 - Grammar:** "I (enjoy) reading fantasy novels. I (read) three books this month."
→ (1) enjoy, (2) have read

**Q5 - Literal Reading:** "Studies show hobbies reduce stress and improve creativity."
Q: What benefit do hobbies provide? → Reduce stress and improve creativity.

**Q6 - Inferential Reading:** "He spent hours building model planes alone in his room. His friends thought it was boring. Years later, he became an aerospace engineer."
Meaning: → Childhood hobbies can shape future careers.

**Q7 - Grammar:** "If you (try) something new, you (might) discover a hidden talent."
→ (1) try, (2) might

**Q8 - Challenge Vocab:** "Collecting" means → Gathering items as a hobby (stamps, coins, cards).

**Q9 - Scenario:** Friend mocks your unusual hobby. Best response? → "It makes me happy. You don't have to like it, but please respect it."

**Q10 - Critical Thinking:** "Hobby time is never wasted time" means → Time spent on things you love has value for well-being.

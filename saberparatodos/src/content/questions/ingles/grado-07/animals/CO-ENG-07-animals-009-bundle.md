# Topic: Animals & Pets (A2+)

---

## Question 1 - Vocabulary
**A domestic animal that lives in your house and you care for is called:**
- [x] A) Pet *(Mascota)*
- [ ] B) Wild animal
- [ ] C) Insect
- [ ] D) Farm animal

**Explanation:** Pet = animal in your home (dog, cat, fish, hamster).
*Mascota = animal en tu casa (perro, gato, pez, hámster).*

---

## Question 2 - Pragmatics
**Sign at zoo:** "Do not feed the animals 🦁"
**Why?** → Human food can make animals sick; it disrupts their diet.

---

## Question 3 - Conversation
**Friend:** "My dog ran away yesterday."
**Best response:** → "I'm so sorry! Have you checked the neighborhood? I can help you look."

---

## Question 4 - Grammar
"There (1)______ many species of birds in Colombia. It (2)______ the most bird species in the world."
→ (1) are, (2) has

**Explanation:** "Many species" = plural → are. Colombia (singular) has.

---

## Question 5 - Literal Reading
**Text:** "Colombia has more bird species than any other country—over 1,900. The Andean condor is a national symbol, while hummingbirds are abundant in cloud forests."

**Q: How many bird species does Colombia have?** → Over 1,900.

---

## Question 6 - Inferential Reading
**Passage:** "The zoo announced it would release its captive dolphins. 'They deserve the ocean,' the director said. Critics worried they couldn't survive. The dolphins were trained for shows, not survival."

**Q: What concern does this passage raise?** → Captive animals may lose survival skills for the wild.

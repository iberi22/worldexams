## Question 1 - Vocabulary
**A place where you can borrow books for free:**
- [x] A) Library *(Biblioteca)*
- [ ] B) Bookstore
- [ ] C) Museum
- [ ] D) School

**Explanation:** Library = borrow for free. Bookstore = buy books.

## Question 3 - Conversation
**Tourist:** "Excuse me, is there a bank nearby?"
**Best response:** → "Yes! There's one two blocks ahead, next to the pharmacy."

## Question 4 - Grammar
"The hospital is (1)______ the park. The bank is (2)______ the corner."
→ (1) opposite/across from, (2) on

**Explanation:** Prepositions of place: opposite/across from, next to, on the corner.

**Text:** "TransMilenio, Bogotá's Bus Rapid Transit system, carries over 2 million passengers daily. It has dedicated lanes that allow buses to bypass traffic. However, overcrowding during rush hours remains a major challenge."

**Q: What problem does TransMilenio have?** → Overcrowding during rush hours.

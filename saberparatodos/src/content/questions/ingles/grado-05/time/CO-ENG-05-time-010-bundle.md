# Topic: Telling Time (A1+)

---

## Q1 - Vocabulary
**What time is 3:00?**
- [x] Three o'clock *(Las tres en punto)*
- [ ] Three thirty
- [ ] Three fifteen
- [ ] Half past three

---

## Q2 - Pragmatics
**Question:** "What time is it?"
**Clock shows 9:30**
**Answer:** → "It's nine thirty" or "It's half past nine."

---

## Q3 - Conversation
**Mom:** "What time does school start?"
**Answer:** → "School starts at [time]."
Example: "School starts at seven thirty."

---

## Q4 - Grammar
"The class starts (1)______ 8:00. We finish (2)______ 3:00."
- [x] (1) at, (2) at

**Time preposition:** Always use "at" with clock times.

---

## Q5 - Reading
**Text:** "In most Colombian schools, classes start at 7:00 AM and finish around 1:00 PM or 3:00 PM."

**Q: What time do classes start in most Colombian schools?** → 7:00 AM

---

## Q6 - Inferential
**Story:** "The alarm rang. She jumped out of bed, looked at the clock, and ran. She was late for school!"
**Q: What time word helped you understand?** → "Late" (tarde)

---

## Q7 - Grammar
"It (1)______ 10 o'clock now. Class (2)______ at 10:30."
- [x] (1) is, (2) starts

---

## Q8 - Challenge
**Time expressions:**
| English | Meaning | Español |
|---------|---------|---------|
| o'clock | Exact hour | En punto |
| half past | 30 minutes | Y media |
| quarter past | 15 minutes | Y cuarto |
| quarter to | 15 before | Menos cuarto |

---

## Q9 - Scenario
**Setting a reminder:**
→ "Please wake me up at [time]."
Example: "Please wake me up at six thirty."

---

## Q10 - Critical Thinking
**Why is being on time important?**
→ It shows respect, helps you not miss things, and builds good habits.
*Muestra respeto, te ayuda a no perder cosas y crea buenos hábitos.*

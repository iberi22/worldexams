# Topic: Days & Months (A2)

---

## Q1 - Vocabulary
**Which day comes after Thursday?**
- [x] Friday *(Viernes)*
- [ ] Wednesday
- [ ] Saturday
- [ ] Monday

**Days of Week:** Sunday, Monday, Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday, Friday, Saturday

---

## Q2 - Pragmatics
**Appointment reminder:** "Your appointment is scheduled for Tuesday, March 15th at 2 PM."
**Q: What day is the appointment?** → Tuesday, March 15th

---

## Q3 - Conversation
**Friend:** "When is your birthday?"
**Response:** → "My birthday is on [month + day]. / It's on July 20th."

---

## Q4 - Grammar
"My birthday is (1)______ July. It's (2)______ the 20th."
- [x] (1) in, (2) on

**Prepositions:** in + month/year (in July). on + specific date (on the 20th).

---

## Q5 - Reading
**Text:** "The Colombian Independence Day is celebrated on July 20th. It commemorates the Cry of Independence in 1810."

**Q: What month is Colombian Independence Day?** → July

---

## Q6 - Inferential
**Calendar:** "December 24... December 25... December 31... January 1..."
**Q: What holiday season is this?** → Christmas and New Year's.

---

## Q7 - Grammar
"We have school (1)______ Monday (2)______ Friday."
- [x] (1) from, (2) to

**From... to...** = start and end (from Monday to Friday).

---

## Q8 - Challenge

January, February, March, April, May, June, July, August, September, October, November, December.
*(Enero, Febrero, Marzo, Abril, Mayo, Junio, Julio, Agosto, Septiembre, Octubre, Noviembre, Diciembre)*

## Q9 - Scenario
**Setting up a meeting:**
→ "Let's meet on [day], [month] [number]. Is 3 PM okay?"
Example: "Let's meet on Friday, October 15th. Is 3 PM okay?"

---

## Q10 - Critical Thinking
**Quote:** "The days are long, but the years are short."
**Meaning:** → Individual days feel slow, but time passes quickly overall.
*Los días individuales parecen largos, pero el tiempo pasa rápido en general.*

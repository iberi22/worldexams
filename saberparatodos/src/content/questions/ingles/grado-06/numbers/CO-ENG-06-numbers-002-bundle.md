# Topic: Numbers & Counting (A2)

---

## Q1 - Vocabulary
**What comes after "twelve"?**
- [x] Thirteen *(Trece)*
- [ ] Eleven
- [ ] Twenty
- [ ] Ten

---

## Q2 - Pragmatics
**Price tag:** "$25,000"
**Salesperson:** "That will be twenty-five thousand pesos."
**Q: What number is $25,000?** → Twenty-five thousand *(Veinticinco mil)*

---

## Q3 - Conversation
**Friend:** "What's your phone number?"
**Response:** → "It's 3-1-5, 8-2-4, 7-6-9-0." (Say each digit individually)

---

## Q4 - Grammar
"There (1)______ fifteen students in my class. Today there (2)______ only ten."
- [x] (1) are, (2) are

**Explanation:** Numbers plural = are (except "there is one").

---

## Q5 - Reading
**Text:** "Colombia's population is approximately 52 million people. The capital, Bogotá, has over 8 million residents."

**Q: How many people live in Bogotá?** → Over 8 million.

---

## Q6 - Inferential
**Math problem:** "If I have fifteen apples and give away eight, I have... 'not enough for my recipe.'"

**Q: How many does the recipe need?** → More than 7 (15-8=7, still not enough).

---

## Q7 - Grammar
"My birthday is on the (1)______ of July." [15]
- [x] fifteenth

**Ordinal numbers:** 1st=first, 2nd=second, 3rd=third, 4th=fourth... 15th=fifteenth.

---

## Q8 - Challenge
**How do you say "2024" for a year?**
- [x] "Twenty twenty-four" or "Two thousand twenty-four"

---

## Q9 - Scenario
**You need to give your address: "Calle 45 #12-34"**
**In English:** → "Forty-five street, number twelve, dash thirty-four"

---

## Q10 - Critical Thinking
**Quote:** "Mathematics is the language of the universe."
**Meaning:** → Numbers are universal—they work the same everywhere.
*Los números son universales—funcionan igual en todas partes.*

# Topic: Colors & Descriptions (A2)

---

## Q1 - Vocabulary
**The color of the sky on a sunny day:**
- [x] Blue *(Azul)*
- [ ] Red
- [ ] Green
- [ ] Yellow

---

## Q2 - Pragmatics
**Shopping:** "Do you have this shirt in a different color?"
**What are you asking?** → If the shirt comes in another color option.

---

## Q3 - Conversation
**Friend:** "What's your favorite color?"
**Response:** → "My favorite color is [color] because [reason]."
Example: "I love green because it reminds me of nature."

---

## Q4 - Grammar
"The flag is yellow, blue, and red. The yellow stripe (1)______ the biggest. The stripes (2)______ horizontal."
- [x] (1) is, (2) are

---

## Q5 - Reading
**Text:** "The Colombian flag has three horizontal stripes. Yellow represents gold and natural resources, blue represents the oceans, and red represents the blood of those who fought for independence."

**Q: What does blue represent?** → The oceans.

---

## Q6 - Inferential
**Art class:** "Mix red and blue to make this color. Add white to make it lighter."

**Q: What color are they making?** → Purple/Violet (and lighter = lavender).

---

## Q7 - Grammar
"This is (1)______ orange. That is (2)______ apple colored red."
- [x] (1) an, (2) an

**Explanation:** Use "an" before vowel sounds (an orange, an apple).

---

## Q8 - Challenge

- [x] Blue and green

Colors: teal, turquoise, magenta, maroon, navy, beige, crimson...

---

## Q9 - Scenario
**Describing someone to help find them:**
**Include:** → "She's wearing a [color] shirt and [color] pants."

---

## Q10 - Critical Thinking
**Quote:** "Colors, like features, follow the changes of emotions." — Picasso
**Meaning:** → Colors express feelings. We choose colors based on mood.
*Los colores expresan sentimientos. Elegimos colores según el ánimo.*

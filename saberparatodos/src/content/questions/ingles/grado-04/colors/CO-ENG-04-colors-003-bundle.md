# Topic: Basic Colors (A1)

---

## Q1 - Vocabulary
**The sun is:**
- [ ] Blue *(Azul)*
- [x] Yellow *(Amarillo)*
- [ ] Green *(Verde)*
- [ ] Red *(Rojo)*

---

## Q2 - Pragmatics
**Teacher points to an apple and asks:** "What color is this?"
**You say:** → "Red!" or "It's red."

---

## Q3 - Conversation
**Friend:** "What color is the sky?"
**Answer:** → "The sky is blue."

---

## Q4 - Grammar
"The apple (1)______ red. The bananas (2)______ yellow."
- [x] (1) is, (2) are

---

## Q5 - Reading
**Text:** "Strawberries are red. Oranges are orange. Bananas are yellow."

**Q: What color are oranges?** → Orange

---

## Q6 - Inferential
**Clues:** "This fruit is yellow. Monkeys love it."
**Q: What fruit is it?** → Banana

---

## Q7 - Grammar
"I like (1)______ blue color. She likes (2)______ pink color."
- [x] (1) the, (2) the

---

## Q9 - Scenario
**Describe your clothes:**
→ "My shirt is [color]."
Example: "My shirt is white."

## Q10 - Critical Thinking
**Why are colors important to learn?**
→ To describe things, art, nature, and communicate clearly!

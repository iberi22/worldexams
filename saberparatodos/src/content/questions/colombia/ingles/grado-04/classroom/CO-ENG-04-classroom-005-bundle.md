# Topic: In the Classroom (A1)

---

## Q1 - Vocabulary
**You sit on a:**
- [ ] Desk
- [x] Chair *(Silla)*
- [ ] Window
- [ ] Door

---

## Q2 - Pragmatics
**Teacher says:** "Sit down, please."
**What do you do?** → Sit in your chair.

---

## Q3 - Conversation
**Teacher:** "What is this?" [points to book]
**Answer:** → "It's a book."

---

## Q4 - Grammar
"The book (1)______ on the desk. The pencils (2)______ in the box."
- [x] (1) is, (2) are

---

## Q5 - Reading
**Text:** "In our classroom, we have desks, chairs, a board, and books. We learn and play here."

**Q: What do we have in the classroom?** → Desks, chairs, a board, and books.

---

## Q6 - Inferential
**Riddle:** "You write on me with markers. Students look at me to learn. What am I?"
**Answer:** → Board *(Tablero/Pizarra)*

---

## Q7 - Grammar
"This (1)______ a pencil. These (2)______ erasers."
- [x] (1) is, (2) are

**This/That = singular. These/Those = plural.**

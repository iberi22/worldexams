## Q2 - Pragmatics
**Friend shows a toy:** "Look at my new toy!"
**You say:** → "Wow, it's cool!" or "That's nice!"

---

## Q3 - Conversation
**Friend:** "What's your favorite toy?"
**Answer:** → "My favorite toy is my [toy]."

---

## Q4 - Grammar
"The ball (1)______ red. The toys (2)______ fun."
- [x] (1) is, (2) are

---

## Q5 - Reading
**Text:** "María has a doll. Carlos has a car. They play together every day."

**Q: What does María have?** → A doll

---

## Q6 - Inferential
**Riddle:** "I have four wheels. You push me on the floor. What am I?"
**Answer:** → Toy car *(Carro de juguete)*

## Q7 - Grammar

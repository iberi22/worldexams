## Question 1 - Vocabulary
ID: CO-ENG-07-sports-007-v1

**Which sport uses a racket and a net?**
*¿Cuál deporte usa una raqueta y una red?*

- [ ] A) Soccer *(Fútbol)*
- [x] B) Tennis *(Tenis)*
- [ ] C) Swimming *(Natación)*
- [ ] D) Running *(Correr)*

**Explanation:** Tennis uses a racket to hit a ball over a net.
*El tenis usa una raqueta para golpear una pelota sobre una red.*

## Question 6 - Inferential Reading

**What does this story emphasize?**

- [ ] A) Natural talent is everything.
- [x] B) Persistence and hard work can overcome obstacles.
- [ ] C) Height determines success.

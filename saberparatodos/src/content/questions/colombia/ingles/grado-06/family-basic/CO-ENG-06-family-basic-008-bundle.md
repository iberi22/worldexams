# Topic: Family Members (A2)

---

## Q1 - Vocabulary
**Your mother and father together are your:**
- [x] Parents *(Padres)*
- [ ] Siblings
- [ ] Grandparents
- [ ] Cousins

---

## Q2 - Pragmatics
**Question:** "How many siblings do you have?"
**Answer if you have 1 brother and 1 sister:** → "I have two siblings—one brother and one sister."

---

## Q3 - Conversation
**Friend:** "Tell me about your family."
**Response:** → "I have [#] people in my family. My mom, my dad, my [siblings], and me."

---

## Q4 - Grammar
"My mother (1)______ a teacher. My parents (2)______ very kind."
- [x] (1) is, (2) are

---

## Q5 - Reading
**Text:** "In Colombian culture, extended family often lives nearby or together. It's common for grandparents to help raise children while parents work."

**Q: Who often helps raise children in Colombia?** → Grandparents

---

## Q6 - Inferential
**Story:** "Every Sunday, the house fills with noise—uncles, aunts, cousins, grandparents all around the table. It's chaotic, but she wouldn't have it any other way."

**Q: How does she feel about family gatherings?** → Happy, despite the chaos.

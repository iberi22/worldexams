# Topic: Telling Time (A2)

---

## Q1 - Vocabulary
**3:30 in English is:**
- [x] Three thirty / Half past three *(Tres y media)*
- [ ] Three thirteen
- [ ] Thirty three
- [ ] Three o'clock

---

## Q2 - Pragmatics
**Question:** "What time is it?"
**Clock shows 7:45**
**Answer:** → "It's seven forty-five" or "It's quarter to eight."

---

## Q3 - Conversation
**Friend:** "What time does the movie start?"
**Response:** → "It starts at 8:15. We should leave at 7:30."

---

## Q4 - Grammar
"The class starts (1)______ 9 o'clock. We finish (2)______ noon."
- [x] (1) at, (2) at

**Time preposition:** at + specific time (at 9:00, at noon, at midnight).

---

## Q5 - Reading
**Text:** "Colombia uses the 12-hour clock in conversation (8 PM) but often the 24-hour clock officially (20:00)."

**Q: How would 3 PM be written in 24-hour format?** → 15:00

---

## Q6 - Inferential
**Schedule:** "Bus arrives at 6:45. She left home at 7:00. She stood at the empty bus stop, wondering where she went wrong."

**Q: What's the problem?** → She left too late and missed the bus.

---

## Q7 - Grammar
"It (1)______ 10 AM right now. The meeting (2)______ at 10:30."
- [x] (1) is, (2) starts

---

## Q8 - Challenge
**"A.M." stands for:**
- [x] Ante Meridiem (before midday/noon) = 12:01 AM - 11:59 AM
**"P.M."** = Post Meridiem (after noon) = 12:01 PM - 11:59 PM

---

## Q9 - Scenario
**You need to set an alarm for 6:30 AM.**
→ Say: "Please set an alarm for six thirty A.M."

---

## Q10 - Critical Thinking
**Quote:** "Time is what we want most, but what we use worst."
**Meaning:** → We want more time, but often waste what we have.
*Queremos más tiempo, pero frecuentemente desperdiciamos el que tenemos.*

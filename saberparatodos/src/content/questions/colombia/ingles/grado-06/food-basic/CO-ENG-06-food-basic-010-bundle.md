## Q2 - Pragmatics
**Restaurant:** "Would you like something to drink?"
**Response:** → "Yes, I'd like a [juice/water/soda], please."

---

## Q3 - Conversation
**Friend:** "What's your favorite food?"
**Response:** → "My favorite food is [food] because [reason]."
Example: "I love pizza because I like cheese."

---

## Q4 - Grammar
"I (1)______ pizza. She (2)______ apples."
- [x] (1) like, (2) likes

**He/She/It + verb+s:** She likes, He eats, It tastes.

---

## Q5 - Reading
**Text:** "Arepa is a traditional Colombian food made from ground maize. It's eaten for breakfast, lunch, or dinner with cheese, butter, or hogao sauce."

**Q: What is arepa made from?** → Ground maize (corn).

---

## Q6 - Inferential
**Scene:** "Mom placed vegetables on his plate. He ate everything around them, but the broccoli remained untouched until Mom looked at him."

**Q: How does the child feel about broccoli?** → He doesn't like it / avoids eating it.

---

## Q7 - Grammar
"There (1)______ milk in the fridge. There aren't (2)______ apples."
- [x] (1) is, (2) any

**Uncountable nouns (milk, water, rice):** There is + no/some/any.

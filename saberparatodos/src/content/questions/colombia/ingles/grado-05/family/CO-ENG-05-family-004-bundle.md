# Topic: My Family (A1+)

---

## Q1 - Vocabulary
**Your mom's mom is your:**
*La mamá de tu mamá es tu:*

- [ ] A) Aunt *(Tía)*
- [x] B) Grandmother *(Abuela)*
- [ ] C) Sister *(Hermana)*
- [ ] D) Cousin *(Prima)*

**Family tree / Árbol familiar:**
- Grandmother = Abuela
- Grandfather = Abuelo
- Mother/Mom = Madre/Mamá
- Father/Dad = Padre/Papá

---

## Q2 - Pragmatics
**Question:** "How many brothers do you have?"
**Answer:** → "I have [number] brother(s)." or "I don't have any brothers."

---

## Q3 - Conversation
**Friend:** "Tell me about your family."
**Answer:** → "I have a [mom/dad/brother/sister]. My mom's name is..."

---

## Q4 - Grammar
"My father (1)______ tall. My parents (2)______ kind."
- [x] (1) is, (2) are

---

## Q5 - Reading
**Text:** "María has a big family. She has two brothers and one sister. Her grandmother lives with them."

**Q: How many brothers does María have?** → Two (2)

---

## Q6 - Inferential
**Story:** "Every Sunday, we visit my grandmother. She always makes my favorite food: ajiaco."

**Q: Who cooks for the family on Sundays?** → The grandmother

---

## Q7 - Grammar
"I (1)______ my family. We (2)______ dinner together every day."
- [x] (1) love, (2) have

# Topic: Colors (A1+)

---

## Q1 - Vocabulary
**The color of grass is:**
*El color del pasto es:*

- [ ] A) Blue *(Azul)*
- [x] B) Green *(Verde)*
- [ ] C) Red *(Rojo)*
- [ ] D) Yellow *(Amarillo)*

---

## Q2 - Pragmatics
**Teacher shows an object and asks:** "What color is this?"
**Answer format:** → "It is [color]." or "It's [color]."

---

## Q3 - Conversation
**Friend:** "What's your favorite color?"
**Answer:** → "My favorite color is [color]."
Example: "My favorite color is blue because it's the color of the sky."

---

## Q4 - Grammar
"The apple (1)______ red. The bananas (2)______ yellow."
- [x] (1) is, (2) are

**Singular:** The apple IS. **Plural:** The bananas ARE.

---

## Q5 - Reading
**Text:** "Traffic lights have three colors. Red means stop, yellow means slow down, and green means go."

**Q: What color means 'go'?** → Green *(Verde)*

---

## Q6 - Inferential
**Mix colors:** "Blue + Yellow = ?"
**Answer:** → Green (Mixing colors creates new colors!)

**Color mixing / Mezcla de colores:**
- Red + Blue = Purple
- Red + Yellow = Orange
- Blue + Yellow = Green

---

## Q7 - Grammar
"I like (1)______ blue shirt. Those (2)______ pink flowers."
- [x] (1) the, (2) are

---

## Q8 - Challenge
**The Colombian flag colors are:**
- [x] Yellow, Blue, Red *(Amarillo, Azul, Rojo)*

**Fun fact:** Yellow is the biggest stripe!

---

## Q9 - Scenario
**Describing your clothes:**
→ "I am wearing a [color] shirt and [color] pants."
Example: "I am wearing a white shirt and blue pants."

---

## Q10 - Critical Thinking
**Why do we learn colors?**
→ To describe things, follow instructions (traffic lights!), art, clothing, nature.
*Para describir cosas, seguir instrucciones, arte, ropa, naturaleza.*

## Q2 - Pragmatics
**Weather forecast:** "Tomorrow will be sunny."
**What should you wear?** → Light clothes, maybe sunscreen!

---

## Q3 - Conversation
**Friend:** "What's the weather like today?"
**Answer:** → "It's [sunny/rainy/cloudy/cold/hot]."
Example: "It's sunny and hot today."

---

## Q4 - Grammar
"It (1)______ hot today. It (2)______ yesterday."
- [x] (1) is, (2) was

---

## Q5 - Reading
**Text:** "In Bogotá, the weather is often cold and rainy. In Cartagena, it's almost always hot and sunny. Colombia has many different climates!"

**Q: How is the weather in Bogotá?** → Cold and rainy

---

## Q6 - Inferential
**Clue:** "People are carrying umbrellas and wearing raincoats."
**Q: What's the weather like?** → It's rainy. *(Está lloviendo.)*

## Q9 - Scenario
**Planning an outdoor activity:**
→ "If it's sunny, we can go to the park. If it rains, we can play inside."

---

## Q10 - Critical Thinking
**Why is it important to know the weather?**
→ To dress appropriately, plan activities, and stay safe.
*Para vestirse apropiadamente, planificar actividades y estar seguro.*

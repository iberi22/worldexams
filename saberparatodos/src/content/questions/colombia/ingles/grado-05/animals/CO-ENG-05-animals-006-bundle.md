## Q9 - Scenario
**Describing an animal:**
→ "It is a [size] [color] [animal]. It has [features]."
Example: "It is a big gray elephant. It has big ears and a long trunk."

## Q10 - Critical Thinking
**Why should we protect animals?**
→ They are part of nature, help the environment, and deserve respect.
*Son parte de la naturaleza, ayudan al ambiente y merecen respeto.*

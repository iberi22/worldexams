# Topic: The Alphabet & Spelling (A1+)

---

## Q1 - Vocabulary
**How many letters are in the English alphabet?**
*¿Cuántas letras tiene el alfabeto inglés?*

- [ ] A) 24
- [ ] B) 25
- [x] C) 26
- [ ] D) 27

**Tip:** The English alphabet has 26 letters: A-Z. Spanish has more because of ñ and variations.
*El alfabeto inglés tiene 26 letras: A-Z. El español tiene más por la ñ.*

---

## Q2 - Pragmatics
**Someone asks:** "How do you spell your name?"
*Alguien pregunta: "¿Cómo deletreas tu nombre?"*

**What should you do?** → Say each letter of your name.
Example: "Carlos" → C-A-R-L-O-S

---

## Q3 - Conversation
**Teacher:** "What letter comes after M?"
**Your answer:**
- [x] N *(The letter after M is N)*

**Memory tip:** L-M-N-O-P (sing it like a song!) 🎵

---

## Q4 - Grammar
"The word 'apple' starts with (1)______ 'A'. It (2)______ five letters."
- [x] (1) an, (2) has

**Rule:** Use "an" before vowel sounds (A, E, I, O, U).
*Usa "an" antes de sonidos vocales.*

---

## Q5 - Reading
**Text:** "The letter 'W' is called 'double-u' because it looks like two U letters together (UU → W)."

**Q: Why is W called 'double-u'?** → It looks like two U's together.

---

## Q6 - Inferential
**Puzzle:** "I'm the first letter of 'apple' and the last letter of 'banana.'"

**Q: What letter am I?** → A (apple starts with A, banana ends with A)

---

## Q7 - Grammar
"There (1)______ vowels and consonants. A, E, I, O, U (2)______ vowels."
- [x] (1) are, (2) are

**Vowels / Vocales:** A, E, I, O, U (5 letters)
**Consonants / Consonantes:** All the rest (21 letters)

---

## Q8 - Challenge
**Vowels in English are:**
- [x] A, E, I, O, U *(sometimes Y)*

**Spanish cognate:** Vocales = Vowels. Same letters!
*Las vocales son las mismas en español e inglés.*

---

## Q9 - Scenario
**Spelling your email address:**
→ "My email is maria123@gmail.com"
→ "M-A-R-I-A-one-two-three-at-gmail-dot-com"

**Key words:** @ = "at", . = "dot"

---

## Q10 - Critical Thinking
**Question:** "Why is learning to spell important?"
**Answer:** → So people understand what you write. Spelling helps communication.
*Para que la gente entienda lo que escribes. La ortografía ayuda a comunicarse.*

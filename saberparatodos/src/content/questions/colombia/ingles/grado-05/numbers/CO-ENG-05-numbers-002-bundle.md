# Topic: Numbers 1-100 (A1+)

---

## Q1 - Vocabulary
**What number is "twenty-five"?**
*¿Qué número es "twenty-five"?*

- [ ] A) 15
- [x] B) 25
- [ ] C) 52
- [ ] D) 35

**Pattern:** twenty (20) + five (5) = 25. Also: twenty-one, twenty-two...
*Patrón: twenty (20) + five (5) = 25.*

---

## Q2 - Pragmatics
**Price tag:** "50,000 pesos"
**Say in English:** → "Fifty thousand pesos"

**Big numbers:** 100 = one hundred, 1,000 = one thousand

---

## Q3 - Conversation
**Teacher:** "What's 7 plus 8?"
**Answer:** → "Fifteen" (7 + 8 = 15)

**Math vocabulary:** plus (+), minus (-), equals (=)
*Vocabulario matemático: más (+), menos (-), igual (=)*

---

## Q4 - Grammar
"There (1)______ twelve months in a year. There (2)______ one sun."
- [x] (1) are, (2) is

**Rule:** There is + singular. There are + plural.

---

## Q5 - Reading
**Text:** "A soccer team has eleven players. Two teams together have twenty-two players on the field."

**Q: How many players are on a soccer team?** → Eleven (11)

---

## Q6 - Inferential
**Problem:** "I have ten candies. I give five to my friend. How many do I have now?"

**Answer:** → Five (10 - 5 = 5)

---

## Q7 - Grammar
"I have (1)______ pencils. She has (2)______ pencil."
- [x] (1) three, (2) one

**Singular/Plural:** one pencil (singular) vs. two pencils, three pencils (plural)

## Q8 - Challenge

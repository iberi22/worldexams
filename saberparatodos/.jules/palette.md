## 2024-05-24 - Accessibility on Search Modals\n**Learning:** When adding ARIA expanded states to disclosure toggles (like search modals), it's important to link them with `aria-controls` to the modal `id` to ensure screen readers can announce the state and target of the interaction properly.\n**Action:** Always include `aria-expanded` and `aria-controls` on the trigger, and a matching `id` on the container.

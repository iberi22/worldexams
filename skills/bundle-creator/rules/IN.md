# IN - India Bundle Creation Rules

## Official Exam Framework
- **Exam:** Central Board of Secondary Education (CBSE) Class 12 Board Exams / various state boards
- **Agency:** CBSE (Central Board of Secondary Education), NCERT (National Council of Educational Research and Training)
- **Subjects (Science stream):** Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics/Biology, English
- **Subjects (Commerce stream):** Accountancy, Business Studies, Economics, English
- **Subjects (Humanities stream):** History, Political Science, Geography, English, Sociology
- **Grade:** Class 12 (12th grade / Senior Secondary)
- **Reference:** https://www.cbse.gov.in/ | https://ncert.nic.in/

## Language & Cultural Rules
- Use English (primary examination language for CBSE)
- Use INR ₹ (Rupee) for currency
- Reference Indian cities: Delhi, Mumbai, Bengaluru, Chennai, Hyderabad, Kolkata
- Reference Indian cultural, historical, and geographic contexts
- Never reference ICFES or Colombian exam names

# JP - Japan Bundle Creation Rules

## Official Exam Framework
- **Exam:** Common Test for University Admissions (大学入学共通テスト) / Center Test
- **Agency:** DNC (National Center for University Entrance Examinations)
- **Subjects:** Mathematics, Japanese, English, Science, Geography/History/Civics
- **Grade:** 12 (3rd year of High School / Kōtōgakkō)
- **Reference:** https://www.dnc.ac.jp/

## Curriculum Alignment
Aligned with the MEXT (Ministry of Education, Culture, Sports, Science and Technology) curriculum guidelines.

## Language & Cultural Rules
- **Linguistic Variant:** Use Japanese (Nihongo).
- **Currency:** Use Japanese Yen (JPY ¥).
- **Local Context:** Use names of Japanese cities (Tokyo, Osaka, Kyoto) and cultural references (Mt. Fuji, Sushi, Sakura).
- **Note:** Currently only `matematicas` (Mathematics) is implemented in the repository structure.
- Never reference ICFES or Colombia-specific exam names.

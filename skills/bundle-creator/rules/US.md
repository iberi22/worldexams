- **Exam:** SAT / ACT
- **Agency:** College Board / ACT, Inc.
- **Subjects:** Mathematics, Reading, Writing and Language, Science
- **Grade:** 11 (Junior Year) / 12 (Senior Year)

## Language & Cultural Rules
- **Linguistic Variant:** Use American English spelling and terminology.
- **Currency:** Use US Dollars (USD $).
- **Local Context:** Use names of US cities (New York, Los Angeles, Chicago) and cultural references (Baseball, Thanksgiving, Fourth of July).
- Never reference ICFES or Colombia-specific exam names.

# KR - South Korea Bundle Creation Rules

## Official Exam Framework
- **Exam:** College Scholastic Ability Test (CSAT / Suneung)
- **Agency:** KICE (Korea Institute for Curriculum and Evaluation)
- **Subjects:** Korean Language, Mathematics, English, Korean History, Science/Social Studies
- **Grade:** 12 (3rd year of High School / Godeung-hakgyo)
- **Reference:** https://www.kice.re.kr/

## Curriculum Alignment
Aligned with the national curriculum developed by KICE.

## Language & Cultural Rules
- **Linguistic Variant:** Use Korean (Hangul).
- **Currency:** Use South Korean Won (KRW ₩).
- **Local Context:** Use names of Korean cities (Seoul, Busan, Incheon) and cultural references (Hagwon, Kimchi, Hanbok).
- **Note:** Currently only `matematicas` (Mathematics) is implemented in the repository structure.
- Never reference ICFES or Colombia-specific exam names.

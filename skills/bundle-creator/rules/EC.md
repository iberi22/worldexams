# EC - Ecuador Bundle Creation Rules

## Official Exam Framework
- **Exam:** Ser Bachiller / Nueva Evaluación (Transitioning to university-specific exams but following BGU curriculum)
- **Agency:** Senescyt / Ministerio de Educación
- **Subjects:**
    - Lengua y Literatura
    - Matemática
    - Ciencias Naturales (Biología, Física, Química)
    - Estudios Sociales (Historia, Filosofía, Educación para la Ciudadanía)
- **Grade:** 11 (Tercer Año de Bachillerato General Unificado - BGU)
- **Reference:** https://educacion.gob.ec/curriculo-bachillerato/

## Curriculum Alignment
- **Matemática:** focus on Algebra, Functions, Geometry, and Statistics.
- **Lengua y Literatura:** focus on critical reading, Ecuadorian and Latin American literature, and formal writing.
- **Ciencias Naturales:** Biology (cell, genetics, ecosystems), Physics (mechanics, energy, electromagnetism), Chemistry (organic, atomic structure, reactions).
- **Estudios Sociales:** History (Ecuadorian history, global history), Citizenship Education, and Philosophy.

## Language & Cultural Rules
- Use Ecuadorian Spanish (formal, "tú" or "usted" depending on context, but standard academic Spanish for exams).
- Currency: US Dollar (USD $).
- Cultural references: Provinces (Pichincha, Guayas, Azuay, etc.), historical figures (Eloy Alfaro, Eugenio Espejo), and geographical landmarks (Chimborazo, Galápagos, Amazonas).
- Terminology: "Bachillerato General Unificado (BGU)", "Ser Bachiller", "Senescyt".
- NEVER reference ICFES or Colombia.

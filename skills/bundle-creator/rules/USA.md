# USA - United States Bundle Creation Rules

## Official Exam Framework
- **Exam:** SAT (Scholastic Assessment Test), ACT, state-specific assessments
- **Agency:** College Board (SAT), ACT Inc. (ACT)
- **Subjects:** Evidence-Based Reading and Writing (SAT), Mathematics (SAT), English (ACT), Math (ACT), Reading (ACT), Science (ACT)
- **Grade:** 11-12 (Junior/Senior year of high school)
- **Reference:** https://www.collegeboard.org/sat | https://www.act.org/

## Language & Cultural Rules
- Use American English spelling and terminology
- Use USD $ for currency
- Reference US cities and states
- Reference US cultural and historical contexts
- Never reference ICFES or Colombian exam names

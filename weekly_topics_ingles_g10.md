# Weekly Topics - Grade 10 English (Colombia 2026)
**CEFR Level: B2**

## Period 1: Experiences (W01-W10)
- **W01: Travel Experiences** - Present Perfect vs Past Simple (Focus on Duration vs Finished time)
- **W02: Life Landmarks** - Used to / Would / Get used to
- **W03: Cultural Exchanges** - Passive Voice (Present/Past)
- **W04: Volunteer Work** - Present Perfect Continuous
- **W05: Overcoming Challenges** - Past Perfect vs Past Simple
- **W06: Academic Achievements** - Modals of Ability (Past/Present/Future)
- **W07: Childhood Memories** - Past Continuous vs Past Simple vs Past Perfect
- **W08: Environmental Awareness** - Reported Speech (Statements)
- **W09: Personal Growth** - Gerunds and Infinitives
- **W10: Review P1** - Cumulative Grammar

## Period 2: Hypothetical (W11-W20)
- **W11: Dream Jobs** - First Conditional / When / Unless
- **W12: Future Dilemmas** - Second Conditional
- **W13: Historical "What Ifs"** - Third Conditional
- **W14: Regrets** - I Wish / If Only (Past)
- **W15: Future Aspirations** - I Wish / If Only (Future/Present)
- **W16: Mixed Realities** - Mixed Conditionals
- **W17: Scientific Predictions** - Future Continuous / Future Perfect
- **W18: Urban Planning** - Passive Voice (Future/Modals)
- **W19: Ethical Choices** - Modals of Deduction (Present)
- **W20: Review P2** - Cumulative Conditionals

## Period 3: Opinions (W21-W30)
- **W21: Debating Public Issues** - Advanced Connectors (Contrast/Addition)
- **W22: Art and Society** - Relative Clauses (Defining/Non-defining)
- **W23: Global Citizenship** - Reported Speech (Questions/Commands)
- **W24: Healthy Lifestyles** - Modals of Advice (Should/Ought to/Had better)
- **W25: Consumerism** - Cleft Sentences (It is... that...)
- **W26: Sports and Identity** - Comparison (Adjectives/Adverbs)
- **W27: Animal Rights** - Passive Voice (Reporting Verbs: It is said that...)
- **W28: Education Systems** - Inversion after negative adverbials
- **W29: Cultural Stereotypes** - Phrasal Verbs (Social context)
- **W30: Review P3** - Cumulative Opinions

## Period 4: Media & Tech (W31-W40)
- **W31: Social Media** - Passive Voice (Causative: Have/Get something done)
- **W32: Artificial Intelligence** - Future Perfect Continuous
- **W33: Journalism Ethics** - Modals of Deduction (Past: Must have, Could have)
- **W34: Cyber Security** - Conditionals (review) + Phrasal Verbs (tech)
- **W35: Digital Footprint** - Relative Clauses (review)
- **W36: Entertainment Industry** - Participle Clauses (-ing / -ed)
- **W37: Scientific Breakthroughs** - Articles (A/An, The, Zero Article)
- **W38: Globalization** - Advanced Connectors (Reason/Result/Purpose)
- **W39: Final Project Prep** - Review of complex structures
- **W40: Final Review** - Comprehensive Test

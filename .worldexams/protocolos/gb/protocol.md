# Protocolo: Reino Unido / England (GB) - GCSE/A-Levels

## Sistema Educativo
- **Estructura:** Primary (Y1-Y6, ages 5-11), Secondary (Y7-Y11, ages 11-16), Sixth Form (Y12-Y13, ages 16-18)
- **Curriculum:** National Curriculum for England (DfE)
- **Exámenes clave:**
  - **GCSE** (Year 11, age 15-16) — General Certificate of Secondary Education
  - **A-Levels** (Year 13, age 17-18) — Advanced Level qualifications
  - **SATs** (Year 2, Year 6) — Standard Assessment Tests
- **Equivalente ICFES:** A-Levels (Year 13 / Grade 13)
- **Ciclo:** Years 1-13 (Key Stages 1-5)

## Calendario Académico UK
- **Inicio:** Septiembre
- **Términos:** Autumn (Sep-Dec), Spring (Jan-Apr), Summer (Apr-Jul)
- **Año académico:** 3 terms × ~13 weeks = 39 weeks
- **Half-term breaks:** Oct, Feb, May (1 week each)
- **Descansos:** Christmas (2 weeks), Easter (2 weeks), Summer (6 weeks)
- **Semanas lectivas:** 39 semanas (W01-W39)

## Coding Guidelines
- **Country code:** `GB` → directory `questions_data/gb/`
- **Subjects:** `mathematics`, `english`, `science`, `humanities`, `mfl-french`, `mfl-spanish`
- **Year folders:** `year-10`, `year-11`, `year-12`, `year-13`
- **ID format:** `GB-{SUBJ}-Y{YY}-2026-W{NN}-{topic}-001-MASTERY`
- **CEFR field:** Set for MFL subjects only (A1, A2, B1, B2, C1)
- **Language:** English (UK) for all subjects except MFL
- **Modern contexts:** UK cities, British culture, real-world scenarios
- **MFL bundles:** Include `cefr_level` in frontmatter
